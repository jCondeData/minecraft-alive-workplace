Cobblemon has no breeding of its own; the Daycare Keeper brings it. Leave her a pair of your Pokémon and she finds
their eggs.

## Getting a keeper

Stand a villager by a **Pasture Block** and sneak-right-click them with an **egg**. A [Daycare](daycare-build.md) has a
paddock round a pasture for her.

## Leaving a pair

Right-click her with an empty hand (sneak to trade) to open the daycare. Click a Pokémon in your party, then its
partner: the screen says how well they'd get along, and the chance of an egg at dawn. Each player may leave one pair,
and she looks after three at most. Keep at least one Pokémon with you.

How well a pair gets along:

- **very well**: the same species, from different original trainers (70% a dawn);
- **well**: the same species, or one egg group in common (50%);
- **so-so**: Ditto with anything that breeds (20%);
- **not at all**: no egg group in common, or one that can't breed. No eggs.

From Expert she finds eggs 10% more often.

![Eggs waiting](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/daycare_keeper/still-3.jpg)

## Eggs

Each dawn she may find an egg with each pair and keeps up to three for you. Collect them on her screen for **4 emeralds
each** (or their worth in CobbleDollars); collect the eggs before you take the pair back.

- With **Cobbreeding** installed, it's a real Cobbreeding egg, hatching by its rules.
- Without it, you get the hatchling itself at level 1: the mother's base form (or the parent that isn't Ditto), 3 IVs
  from the parents (5 when one holds a Destiny Knot), the nature of a parent holding an Everstone, the mother's ball, a
  1 in 5 chance of a hidden ability the mother has, and the egg moves both parents know. It goes to your party or PC.

If she dies, the pairs go back to their trainers' PCs.

## Trading

Exp. Candy XS and wheat bought (Novice), eggs bought and hay (Apprentice), an Everstone and the **Daycare** blueprint for
14 emeralds (Journeyman), Exp. Candy S (Expert), a Destiny Knot (Master).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Fed by**: your pairs, held items (an Everstone, a Destiny Knot from her own trades), and Normal or Fairy Pokémon
  pastured nearby, who keep the pairs company ([Pokémon partners at work](partners-at-work.md)).
- **Not the same as** the [Rancher](rancher.md)'s daycare, where Pokémon you leave gain experience.
- **Stops her**: the **Daycare Keepers** setting off (keepers already hired find no eggs).
