The Fire Station is the [Firewarden](firewarden.md)'s home: a stone engine room with water on tap, and a lookout loft
to watch the roofs from.

## What it looks like

A stone ground floor on a cobbled plinth, with buttresses down the sides and a wide red door under a chiseled lintel,
lamp posts either side. Inside, the Firewarden's grindstone stands at the back, a water basin fills one corner and
buckets hang on two racks. A ladder climbs to a timber watch loft with windows on every side, under a dark oak roof.

## Building it

Take the Fire Station from the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md), place it and stock the chests by the builder's
table with what its tooltip lists; the basin needs a water bucket. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md). It's a
single build with no upgrade.

## Putting it to work

Stand a villager by its grindstone and sneak-right-click them with a **water bucket**: the village has its Firewarden,
with water right beside them to fill their buckets. The station has the grindstone and no other job block, so nobody
wanders in and takes another job there.

## How it fits in

- **What it answers:** the [village fire](fire.md). A wooden village with no Firewarden gets a tip in **What next?**
  at the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) to build it.
- **The village map:** it's marked as a defense on the [village map](village-map.md).
- **What stops it:** **Firewardens** off in the settings screen. The station still stands, but its grindstone no
  longer offers the job.
