Your builders furnish what they build with the furniture mods your server has. Where a plain build has a stair for a
chair and a fence post with a pressure plate for a table, a server with **Handcrafted** gets Handcrafted chairs round
a Handcrafted table, and the same goes for beds, nightstands, couches, shelves of cups, lamps, clocks, pictures and
blinds. You don't have to do anything: install the mods and build.

![A furnished bedroom corner](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/furnished_home/still-2.jpg)

## Which mod gives what

- **Handcrafted**: chairs, tables, fancy beds, nightstands, couches, shelves and crockery, and the counters and desks
  in shops, the Library and the Print Shop.
- **Lucky's Cozy Home**: sofas, chairs and tables, lamps and grandfather clocks.
- **CobbleFurnies**: its chairs, tables, sofas, lamps and curtains, and the Poké Ball chair and desk. A home built
  near a Pokémon you keep in a Pasture Block gets a Trainer's room with the Poké Ball chair and desk, and the Healing
  Center's and Schoolhouse's desks are Poké Ball desks.
- **Beautify**: picture frames, blinds, candelabras, rose trellises and hanging pots.

With several of them installed, each piece comes from the first mod in the list that makes it: Handcrafted first, then
Cozy Home, CobbleFurnies and Beautify. A server owner can change that order in the mod's settings file (the furniture
mods list), for example to put Cozy Home first.

## What the builder needs

The furniture is part of the build's materials: the Blueprint Table's preview, its supply report and the material list
show the modded pieces, and the builder waits for them like any other block. Put a modded chair in his chest, not a
stair.

**Any mod's piece will do.** A spruce chair is a spruce chair whichever furniture mod made it: if the build asks for
Handcrafted's spruce chair and you bring CobbleFurnies' (or Cozy Home's), the builder builds that one, in the same
place and facing the same way. The material list book shows the piece once, as "Spruce Chair (any furniture mod)";
point at it to see each mod's piece that will do. With several mods' pieces in the chest, he takes the first mod's in
the list. A piece he has already put up stays as it is. Chairs, tables, couches, desks, counters, cupboards,
nightstands, drawers and lamps work this way; a piece no installed mod makes is the plain one, as before.

![Two cottages: Handcrafted chairs from one chest, CobbleFurnies chairs from the other](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/furniture_any_mod/still-3.jpg) Every home in the starter set is furnished (the Starter Cottage, Stone House, Townhouse, Manor, Artisan's
House and Inn), and so are the Supply Shop, Library, Schoolhouse, Healing Center, Print Shop, Village Hall and
Kitchen.

Villagers use the furniture: from supper until bedtime an idle villager sits on a free chair, bench or sofa nearby,
and they claim and sleep in Handcrafted's fancy beds.

![Supper on Handcrafted chairs](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/seats_and_beds/still-1.jpg)

## The modded build library

With **Rechiseled, Supplementaries, Carved Wood, Chipped and Handcrafted** all installed (as in the pack), homes and
workshops come from the **modded library**: the same buildings, dressed with those mods. Plaster walls become
Supplementaries daub with wooden braces, wooden floors become herringbone boards, chests and bookshelves are Carved
Wood's in the house's own wood, and there is crockery on the tables and books on the shelves. The Blueprint Table and the
builder's shopping list show those blocks. If one of those mods is missing, you get the plain build instead.

![The Starter Cottage from the vanilla and the modded library](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/modded_library/still-1.jpg)

## Without the mods

Nothing changes: every build is the plain build, with its stair chairs, fence tables, banner pictures and trapdoor
blinds. A building already standing keeps the pieces it was built with, even if you change the order or add a mod. If
you take a furniture mod out, its pieces are put back as the plain ones when the builder next repairs the building.

## Storage mods

The **Storehouse** reads **Sophisticated Storage** chests and barrels of every tier, and the board shows when a slot
holds more than a stack. Put a Sophisticated **storage controller**, or a **Tom's Simple Storage** inventory
connector, in the Storehouse's area and everything it links counts as one store: porters put goods in through it,
builders take what they need through it, and Tom's terminal shows the village's goods.

![The Storehouse with a storage controller](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/modded_storehouse/still-1.jpg)

## Settings

- **Furnished Builds** (on): builders use the furniture mods you have. Off: every build is the plain build.
- **Modded Build Library** (on): homes and workshops are built from the modded library when all its mods are
  installed (needs Furnished Builds on). Off: the plain builds.
- **Storage Mod Networks** (on): a storage controller or an inventory connector in the Storehouse counts as one store.
  Off: each linked chest is its own store.
