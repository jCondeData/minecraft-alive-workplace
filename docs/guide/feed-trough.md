The Feed Trough is the Rancher's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Rancher works at a **smoker**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a saddle or a golden carrot.
The Feed Trough is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:feed_trough`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Feed Trough takes it by themselves and becomes a **Rancher**: no item needed.
They break in the wild horses, donkeys and llamas nearby, put on the saddles and armor you leave in the chests, and breed them. With Cobblemon they groom pastured Pokémon.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Feed Trough to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** saddles and horse armor in the chests.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Feed Trough or a smoker); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
