A square stone tower for a wall's end or corner, with a ladder up inside to a battlemented top.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two).

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Put it where two Stone Walls meet, or at the end of a run.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Wall Tower, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Wall Tower II** gets a roofed top with an archers' gallery, two stations a side.

## What it does for the village

It finishes off the corners of your wall and gives you a high place to shoot from. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a black banner (towers, barracks and gates).
