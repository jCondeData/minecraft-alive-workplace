The guild of the village's guards.

## Perks

- Guards train on the [Training Dummies](training-dummy.md) **up to Master** instead of Expert.
- Guards **hit 10% harder**.

The Wardens' Guild has no speed-up: its perks are all for the fight.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Guard with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder finish a
[Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a Master guard, and Training Dummies to spar on.
- **What it affects**: [raids](raids.md), [bandit camps](bandit-camps.md) and the village's [defense](defence.md).
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
