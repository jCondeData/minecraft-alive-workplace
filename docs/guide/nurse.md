The Nurse patches everyone up: you, the village's sick and wounded, and with Cobblemon, your Pokémon.

## Getting a nurse

Stand a villager by a **brewing stand** and sneak-right-click them with a **honey bottle**. (A glass bottle brings the
[Cleric](cleric.md) back, a golden apple picks the [Undertaker](undertaker.md).) Put a chest within 8 blocks of the
stand with honey bottles, milk or healing potions in it. Build a **Healing Center** from the Blueprint Table and a nurse
moves in by herself.

## What she does

- **You.** Right-click her with an empty hand: your health comes back and bad effects are cleared. With Cobblemon your
  whole team is healed too. Once a minute per player at Novice, less as she levels up. Sneak to trade instead.
- **The sick.** Now and then a villager falls ill (see [Village life](village-life.md)): they sneeze, walk slowly and work
  at half pace. The nurse finds the ill within 32 blocks and cures them at once with a honey bottle, a bucket of milk or
  a potion of healing or regeneration from her chest. With none there, she asks for one on the Storehouse's requests
  board.
- **The hurt.** Hurt villagers and iron golems nearby, guards included, get tended.

Over her head: "right-click to be treated", whom she just cured, or how many are ill and what she needs.

## How the nurse fits in

- **Fed by:** the [Beekeeper](beekeeper.md)'s honey bottles, the [Butcher](butcher.md)'s milk.
- **Feeds:** a healthy village works at full pace; the ill work at half. The [Guard](guard.md)s she tends go back to the
  fight.
- **Less work with:** the **Medicine** research (villagers a third less likely to fall ill a level; see
  [Scholar](scholar.md)), and well-fed villagers who sleep in a bed.
- **Off switch:** the **Sickness** setting (see [Settings](settings.md)).

## Trading

Nurses buy glistering melon slices, and sell potions of healing and regeneration, honey bottles, a golden apple and, at
Master, golden carrots.
