The Fisherman is vanilla's fisherman, put to work: hand them a rod and they fish for you.

## Getting a fisherman

A villager without a job takes a free **barrel** as a Fisherman, as in vanilla. To set them to work, right-click them
with a **fishing rod**. Put spare rods in the barrel or a chest next to it for when one breaks.

**Their rod wears like yours:** one point a catch, so a plain rod lasts 64 catches. Enchant it and the enchantments
work for them as for you: **Unbreaking** makes it last longer, **Mending** repairs it with the experience of each catch,
**Luck of the Sea** brings better catches and **Lure** shortens the wait for a bite.

## What they do

They walk to the water near their barrel, cast, wait for a bite (you see the bobber dip) and reel in a catch from the
same table as yours: fish and some junk. A few catches later they take them back to the barrel. Water off a jetty, like
the end of a Fisher's Hut's, is fished first.

**Out in a boat.** Put a boat in their chests, and if the water is big enough they row out to open water within 24
blocks of the barrel and fish from the boat. Out there, one catch in twenty is **treasure**, just as for you. They row
back to bring the catch in, and the boat goes back in the chest. Nightfall brings them ashore.

![A fisher out in a boat](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/extras/still-1.jpg)

Over their head: how many fish they've caught, and whether they're fishing, out in the boat or bringing in the catch.
Sneak-right-click them with an empty hand for the full status.

## How the fisherman fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Toolsmith](toolsmith.md) makes a rod when theirs breaks and the chests have none.
- **Feeds:** a smoker with coal beside the barrel and the fish come back cooked. The [Porter](porter.md) takes the catch
  to the storehouse (leaving fish that wait for the smoker), where the village eats it and the [Chef](chef.md) cooks it.
- **Stops when:** there's no rod ("needs a fishing rod"), or no water nearby.

See also [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).
