A battle ring for the village: where the [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md) is fought. A 15 by 9 ring of packed mud inside
white lines with a center circle, a trainer's box on a dais at each end with a lamp post, benches for 12 along the
back, two banner poles and a notice board out front.

![Arena](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/arena/still-1.jpg)

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** when Cobblemon is installed: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. An Expert [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md) sells it for 20 emeralds. A Cobblemon village of Village rank
with a Trainer Leader and no Arena gets "Build an Arena" on the hall's **What next?** page.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front; the way in is
there), stock the chests by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. It
takes a style like any blueprint. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Arena, they sell the next tier's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too).
Right-click the finished Arena with the upgrade and it lines up over it; the builder only builds what's new.

- **Arena II** adds stands on both long sides (30 seats), a stone gate arch over the way in, lanterns round the ring
  and, behind the back stand, a **fair lane**: a cobbled lane with two striped kiosks.
- **Arena III** turns the back stand into a covered grandstand on stone terraces (four rows of ten under a slate roof),
  adds a **trainers' room** at each end with a Healing Machine, a bench and a barrel, and a tall **champion's pole**
  before the gate.

![Arena III from above](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/arena/still-4.jpg)

## What it does for the village

- **Hosts the Cup**: a village with a hall, a finished Arena (any tier) and at least Village rank holds its festivals
  as the Festival Cup. Bouts are fought at the ring, the trainers stand in the boxes and the villagers watch from the
  seats ([Cup day](cup-day.md)).
- **The fair lane** (II and III) is where the fair's traders set up on Cup day.
- **The champion's pole** (III) flies the Cup banner when the village holds the Cup
  ([Champions](cup-champions.md)).
- **The notice board** has **[Watch]** for a player bout being fought.
- Like every finished building, it counts toward the village's [rank](ranks.md).

Take the Arena down and the next Cup is a plain festival.
