# Camp Kitchen (52.2): the critic's scorecard

Sheets: `camp_kitchen_sheet.png` (Level I) and `camp_kitchen_2_sheet.png` (Level II), OLD and NEW from four sides and
the top. Eye-level shots: not filmed by the critic (scene `rebuild_camp_kitchen` films them).

| Score | OLD | NEW |
|---|---|---|
| Silhouette | 3 (a plain gable pavilion, front and back the same shape) | 4 (a steep four-sided deepslate roof with a flared spruce eave and a copper lantern cupola, recognisable in outline; Level II adds a gabled smokehouse with its own chimney) |
| Depth | 3 (posts, a one-block eave, a cobble plinth, no walls) | 4 (spruce logs proud of birch infill, shutters, a flared eave band, a stone plinth with stairs, a lean-to over the bales) |
| Interior | 3 (an open kitchen with counters and the pot) | 3 (one planned 7 x 7 room: the pot centered on a brick hearth, a bench facing it, counters on two sides, chests in the back corners, hanging lanterns) |
| Detail | 3 (flower boxes, lamp posts, a fenced plot with a channel) | 4 (raised planters by stone steps, potted mushrooms, lamp posts, shutters, the cupola lantern, the chimney fire, a curbed plot with a channel) |

Weak points: top-heavy, and Level I's four sides nearly identical; Level II's back (the raised plot) is a flat stone
box; no table, and the smokehouse holds only a smoker, a chest and a counter; no eye-level shots. The bright copper cap
is the one loud color; the cupola didn't vent (solid deepslate at y = 11).

Brief: no requirement missed.

Verdict: **SHIP** (better by one on silhouette, depth and detail; loses on none).

Optional notes: open the flue; furnish the smokehouse; add a table and film eye-level shots.

Applied at ship: the flue is open (the louvre's floor over the pot is a propped-open spruce trapdoor, a damper, so
`holes.py` doesn't count it an open hole), and the smokehouse has a spruce tie beam wall to wall as its smoking rack,
its lamp and meat hooks hanging from it (the lamp had floated: the save's furniture check caught it). No table (a seat
beside one must face it, and the bench already faces the fire).
