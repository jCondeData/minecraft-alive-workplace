A tax on trade: the village gets richer, and so do its prices.

## What it does

While the Tithe is in force, **a tenth of the emeralds players pay villagers in trades goes into the village's
treasury**. In return, the villagers' **emerald prices are 10% higher**.

## Reforming it

While it's in force, a Librarian or Scholar asks to keep a fair ledger: 24 books and quills, then 192 gold ingots, then
a Pokémon battle against one of the village's trainers (or 32 monsters slain without one)
([The Fair Ledger](the-fair-ledger.md)). After that the tithe comes out of the villagers' takings instead of your
price: the treasury keeps its tenth, and prices are back to normal. The hall's owner also gets a book of the treasury's
takings.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: players trading with your villagers.
- **What it affects**: the [treasury](village-hall.md), which pays for mercenaries, festivals and more.
- **Good with**: [Festival Season](festival-season.md), which needs a full treasury.
