"I'm writing my thesis on Life in the Cold. Will you help me with the field work?" The village's professor needs field
notes no book can give, and you're the one with the Poké Balls.

The professor is the village's best Scholar at Expert or above. The thesis has one of six
topics: **Life in the Cold**, **Fire and Stone**, **Creatures of the Night**, **The Forest's Web**, **Sea and Shore** or
**Living Fossils**.

## Field Notes

Three quests, one for each catch the theory needs, made where and when it says. For Life in the Cold: an Ice type in a
snowy biome, a Water type in a frozen ocean or river, and any Pokémon above height 150. For Creatures of the Night:
a Dark type and a Ghost type at night, and a Psychic type under a full moon. Living Fossils asks for a fossil revived by
a Fossil Scientist. Each catch counts where the ball landed, and chat says so: "Snorunt goes in the field notes."

![A catch counts in the field notes](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/professors_thesis/still-2.jpg)

## A Closer Look

The professor wants to see how a Pokémon grows. Right-click them with one of the thesis' catches that has evolved since
in your party, or any Pokémon that trusts you completely (friendship 200 or more).

## Peer Review

A strong trainer has to test the ideas in battle: beat the **Trainer Leader** of another village nearby. With no other
village's leader in reach, the rival scholar **Thessaly** comes to the hall herself, a Master trainer: "Beat me in
battle and I'll admit it holds up."

![The published thesis](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/professors_thesis/still-3.jpg)

## How it ends

The thesis is published. Every helper gets the book, with a page for each catch and you as co-author, plus 3 Rare
Candies and an Ability Capsule, 150 standing and the honor **Co-author**. The village's scholars gain a free research
level.

## How it fits with the rest

- The professor comes from the village's [scholars](jobs-services.md), so research feeds the story and the story feeds
  research back.
- A [Fossil Scientist](pokemon.md) makes the Living Fossils topic possible, and the village's
  [trainers](pokemon.md) are the peer reviewers.
- Without Cobblemon this story never comes.
