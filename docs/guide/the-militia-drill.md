Under [Conscription](conscription.md) everyone drops their tools whenever a raid comes. A Guard asks to train them
properly: "Drafted folk with no training are no help in a fight. Arm them and drill them, and the work won't stop when
trouble comes."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Talk to the guard about the conscripts.
2. **The First Step.** Swords for the militia: bring **32 iron swords**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **32 shields**.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: real practice, clearing out the monsters round the village: **slay 48 monsters**.

Each step pays emeralds ("Shields up! That's the way.").

## How it ends

Conscription is **reformed** for good: everyone still fights in a raid, but work stops only near a raider, and the
morning after is a normal day. The next day at noon is **drill day**: every grown villager spars a while at the
village's Training Dummies (with no dummies, just the guards drill), and the chronicle notes it. Everyone who helped
gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Conscription](conscription.md) is in force and the village has a guard.
- The swords come from your [weaponsmith](jobs-crafts.md), the shields from a carpenter.
- Put up [Training Dummies](defence.md#guards) near the guards' grindstones before drill day.
