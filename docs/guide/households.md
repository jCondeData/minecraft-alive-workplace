Classes go by **household**, not by villager. A household is one grown villager living alone, or a married couple
(see [Couples and weddings](couples-and-weddings.md)). Each household has one [class](classes.md), takes one of each
[luxury](luxuries.md) it's due, and rises or falls together.

## Who's in a household

- **A couple is one household.** A need holds for a couple only when it holds for both: both fed three days, both on a
  varied diet, and their home counts at the lower of their two beds.
- **Children** take the class of the grown-ups they live with, and carry their family name (see
  [Family names](family-names.md)). Grown up, they are a household of their own.

## The home

A household's home is the finished building its bed is in, and the **grade** of that home is what the classes look at:

| Grade | What counts | Needed by |
|---|---|---|
| I | any home a builder built | [Artisans](artisan.md) |
| II | a home at its first upgrade (a Starter Cottage II, say), or a [Burgher's Townhouse](townhouse-build.md) | [Burghers](burgher.md) |
| III | a home at its second upgrade, a Townhouse II or III, or a [Noble's Manor](manor-build.md) | [Nobles](noble.md) |

A grander home also lifts the mood: +5 for grade II, +10 for grade III. A Manor II's garden counts as beauty for the
people who live there.

The hall's **What next?** page names households that want a grander home: "Dara and Tomas are Burghers and want a grade
II home", or "Bo could be an Artisan and wants a grade I home" when the home is all that holds them back.

## Where the grander homes come from

- the [Artisan's House](artisans-house-build.md): builders sell it from Apprentice on;
- the [Burgher's Townhouse](townhouse-build.md): builders sell it once the village has a household of Artisans or
  better;
- the [Noble's Manor](manor-build.md): in the Blueprint Table, and builders sell it once the village has Burghers.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** your [builder](builder.md) (homes and upgrades), the village store (food and luxuries) and the
  services near home (a chapel, school, clinic, library or tavern).
- **What it affects:** taxes into the [treasury](treasury.md), work speed (Artisans speed up the crafters, Burghers the
  scholars), and with a Noble household, the Noble's Ball every other festival.
- **What breaks it:** a bed broken or moved out of a finished building, an empty store, or a partner who lacks
  something the other has.
