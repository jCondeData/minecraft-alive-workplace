A note to another player, sent through the village post. It arrives as a signed book, "Letter from Jesse", with your
words on its page.

## Sending one

Open your [Mailbox](mailbox.md), type the player's name in **To**, and write in the **Letter** line ("a note to go with
it"). Put items in the top row to send with it if you like, and press **Send**. The letter goes first in the parcel. A
[Postman](postman.md) picks it up: within their 64-block round it's delivered on their next round; further away it
goes with the night mail and arrives at dawn.

## Getting one

It lands in your mailbox (the red flag goes up). With no mailbox of your own, collect it at a
[Postal Desk](postal-desk.md) or at a mailbox a postman works at. [/workplace mail](command-mail.md) tells you where your
parcels are. Right-click the letter to read it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it needs:** a postman. With no postman working near your mailbox, the parcel waits there until one starts work.
- Only you, your friends and operators can open your mailbox, so your letters stay yours.
