Open a door to the Nether and something may come through it. A village with a lit portal, or a finished
[Nether Gate](nether-gate-build.md) for its [Netherworkers](netherworker.md), can draw a piglin warlord.

## The Blackstone Outpost

56 to 80 blocks from the hall: a broken platform of blackstone with a lit portal on a dais at the back, a basalt pillar
flying the black piglin banner, a hoglin pen, a campfire, and the hoard of gold blocks with the loot chest. Chat says
so: "A portal has been lit beyond Thornholm: Warlord Grukk the Gilded has raised a blackstone outpost..."

## The Warlord and his band

The **Warlord** is a piglin brute in full gold armor with a golden axe and 50 more health. He stays at his outpost. By
night his raiders step out of the outpost's portal in a flash of light and march on the village: piglins with golden
swords or crossbows, piglin brutes with golden axes, and hoglins as rams. They never turn zombified while they raid,
and they go for your villagers and guards. The outpost starts 10 strong and grows by 2 a day up to 16.

## Plunder

A piglin that comes within 4 blocks of a chest, barrel or other storage in the village takes the gold in it: ingots,
nuggets, blocks, raw gold, golden tools, weapons and armor, one stack at most, carried on its head where you can see it.
Kill it and it drops the stack. If it's still alive at dawn it carries the gold home to the hoard, and the next
morning's siege report says how much was carried off.

## The Warlord's fall

Kill him and the outpost is broken: its portal goes out, and every piglin and hoglin of his band left in the Overworld
turns zombified on the spot. Those already raiding stay in the raid until it ends.

## How it fits in

- **What draws it:** a lit Nether portal in the village's area, or a finished Nether Gate.
- **What stops it:** walls and gates against the hoglin rams (see [Sieges](sieges.md)), and keeping gold out of
  reach of the street. A [war party](war-party.md) can break the outpost.
- **The hoard:** gold ingots and blocks, gold nuggets, blackstone, gilded blackstone, crying obsidian, golden carrots or
  an enchanted golden axe, now and then netherite scrap and the snout banner pattern, plus whatever gold they carried
  off.
- **Switched off:** `piglin_incursion` under `raiderCultures` in the config file. With **Sieges** off, no rams at the
  gate.
