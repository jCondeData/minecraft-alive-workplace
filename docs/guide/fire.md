A village built of wood can burn. Lightning in a thunderstorm can set a roof alight, a chimney spark can do it on a
long dry spell, and a pirate gunner's burning arrows light the wooden roofs they hit. The more of the village is wood,
the likelier it is. When a fire starts, everyone in the village (and the hall's owner, wherever they are) reads
"Fire in the Starter Cottage at Oakvale!".

## Your own builds are safe

The fire spreads the way fire always does, but only through what your builders built. A wall, a roof or a fence you
placed yourself never catches, even nailed right onto a burning house, and a fire with nothing of the builders' left
to burn goes out on its own. Whatever burned, your builders put back on their next round of the village, once the
fire is out.

![A roof catches and the Firewarden puts it out](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/fire/still-2.jpg)

## The Firewarden

Stand a villager by a **grindstone** and sneak-right-click them with a **water bucket**: they become the village's
**Firewarden**. (A sword at the same grindstone still makes a Guard.) Day or night, the Firewarden runs to the nearest
fire and throws a bucket over it: everything burning within two blocks goes out in a cloud of steam. They carry two
buckets and fill them again at water or a water cauldron near their grindstone, so dig them a little pond.

From **Journeyman** a Firewarden rings the village bell when a fire starts, and two villagers who aren't at work run
over with buckets of their own to help: a bucket brigade.

With **Cobblemon**, keep Water-type Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the grindstone: they douse the fire from where
they stand, a splash every two seconds.

When the last flame is out, the hall's chronicle remembers it: "Fire in the Starter Cottage: 18 blocks burned; Rowan
the Firewarden put it out."

## The Fire Station

The **Blueprint Table** has a **Fire Station** for your builder to put up: a stone ground floor with the Firewarden's
grindstone, a water basin to fill buckets at and buckets on racks, under a timber watch loft with windows on every
side. Stand a villager by its grindstone and hand them a water bucket, and the village has its Firewarden. When a
wooden village has no Firewarden, **What next?** at the hall suggests it.

![The Fire Station among the walls and gates](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/defences/anim.gif)

## Settings

**Firewardens** in the settings screen turns the job off (Firewardens already hired stand idle). Turn the
`mobGriefing` game rule off and raiders' arrows light nothing; with **Siege Damage** off a fire burns nothing at all.
