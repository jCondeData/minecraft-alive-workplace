The Sieve is the Sifter's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Sifter works at a **cauldron**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding gravel, sand, red sand or soul sand.
The Sieve is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:sieve`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Sieve takes it by themselves and becomes a **Sifter**: no item needed.
Put gravel, sand, red sand or soul sand in the chests and they shake out flint, nuggets, clay, seeds, quartz and now and then an emerald or a diamond.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Sieve to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** gravel and sand; a [Mason](jobs-crafts.md) crushes cobblestone into both.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Sieve or a cauldron); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
