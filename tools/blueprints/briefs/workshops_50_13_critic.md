## Village Train: BACK three times, so it goes to the owner as it stands

- Round 1, "the Steam Tram" (funnel, cab, lamp): 2/3, 2/2, 2/2, 2/2. Chest lid blocked by the casing over it, ragged
  roof line, a block outside the test stock.
- Round 2, the tram redrawn: 2/2, 2/2, 3/2, 3/2. Cargo stacked on the bogey, seat at rail level, lamp outboard.
- Round 3, "the Railcar": 2/2, 1/1, 2/2, 2/2. It converged on the old layout (the chest and one casing swapped; the
  same 15 blocks), so it is not better.

The brief leaves very little room: 4 long, 1 wide, at most 5 tall, a fixed bogey, seat and controls arrangement and a
test stock of 15 parts. The critic's round-3 notes if anyone tries again: a massing the old train doesn't have (with
the three test barrels updated to the new bill), a profile from the ends (the free fifth layer, agreed with the
station's 4 clear layers), worked canopy ends, a front and a tail. Sheets: `briefs/village_train_r3_sheet.png`,
`briefs/village_train_r3_standin_sheet.png`; design `js/village_train/` (round 3 in the files; rounds 1-2 described
in plan.mjs).
