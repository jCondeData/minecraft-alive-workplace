# Great Cathedral

```infobox
type: Wonder
expansion: M35
Needs: a Town, the Seer (or a Master Cleric), 3 finished Chapels, the Master Architect
Size: 39 blocks across the transepts, 55 long, its spires 52 high
Stages: The Foundations and the West Front; The Nave; The Transepts and the Choir; The Towers and the Rose Window
While it stands: undertakers bring the village's dead back by morning; weddings are blessed; the village is more beautiful
categories: Wonders
related: wonders.md, jobs-services.md, village-life.md
```

A Gothic cathedral on a cross plan: two west towers under stone spires, a rose window of white, light blue, yellow and
red glass over a deep west door, flying buttresses along the nave. Inside, pews either side of a red runner, an altar
of gold and candles, three side chapels and open belfries with three bells.

## What it needs

A **Town** with **three finished Chapels**, the **Seer** (on a server without Legends, a Master Cleric stands in) and
the Master Architect. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## Its stages and goods

| Stage | Found by exploring (and the rare worker who finds it) | From a workshop |
|---|---|---|
| The Foundations and the West Front | A Prize Pottery Sherd (a Master Sifter, 9 days), a Ghast Tear (a Master Netherworker, 7 days) | 2 Fine Clothes (Tailor) |
| The Nave | The music disc otherside (the Bard Laureate, 18 days), an Echo Shard (the Old Sage, 18 days), a Heart of the Sea (the Pathfinder, 11 days) | 2 Illuminated Books (Printer) |
| The Transepts and the Choir | A Totem of Undying (the Merchant Prince, 22 days), an Enchanted Golden Apple (the Grand Chef, 22 days), an Eye Armor Trim Template (the Old Sage, 18 days) | 2 Noble Robes (Tailor) |
| The Towers and the Rose Window | An Elytra (the Pathfinder, 45 days), a Nether Star (the Golem Smith, 36 days), the music disc Relic (the Bard Laureate, 16 days) | 2 Gold Circlets (Jeweller) |

## While it stands

- The village's **Undertakers** bring its dead back by morning, from anywhere in the village.
- **Weddings** are blessed, with a good mood of 15 that lasts 3 days.
- The whole village is the more **beautiful** for it, which the better [classes](classes.md) look for.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Chapels from your [builder](builder.md), a Master Cleric, rare things found by exploring (a rare
  worker in the village finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: the [Undertaker](jobs-services.md), weddings (see [Village life](village-life.md)) and the
  village's decorations.
