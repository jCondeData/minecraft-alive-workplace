"A tinkerer who stopped mending golems and started making them." Give the **Golem Smith** iron, and the village will
never lack strong hands again.

## How they come

In a happy village with four iron golems, a Master [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md) may be taken by a
[strange mood](strange-moods.md). They ask for three of these: a diamond, a block of copper, a block of redstone, a
blaze rod, a breeze rod, an amethyst shard, an echo shard. Bring them within three days and they make their Masterwork,
a heavy core called "The Heart of" your village, and become the Golem Smith.

## Golems that work

Every 2 days, while the village has fewer than one forged golem per 5 villagers, the Smith builds an iron golem from
the chests by the smithing table near their workstation: **4 iron blocks, a carved pumpkin and one more thing** for the
golem's role. Sneak-right-click them with an empty hand to choose which comes next:

- **Hauler Golem** (a chest): carries goods from every worker's chest in the village to the nearest
  [Storehouse](storehouse.md) (with a Golem Smith about, from the workers who answer to the Smith's people). If it
  can't get there (walled in, or up on a ledge), it gives up after about 10 seconds and takes the load to the next nearest Storehouse,
  trying the first one again a couple of minutes later. If no Storehouse can be reached, it puts the load back into
  the nearest Drop Box instead of standing around with it. A Drop Box or worker's chest it can't get to is skipped the
  same way, and it fetches from the next one.
- **Farmhand Golem** (an iron hoe): tends the fields and carries the harvest in.
- **Wall Sentry** (a shield): a tougher golem that holds a spot on the wall. Sneak-right-click it with a
  [Patrol Map](patrol-map.md) to give it its post.

Each golem has its name over its head, and the hall's list says what it's doing. The Smith also mends golems twice as
fast as a Tinkerer.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: iron (lots of it), pumpkins, and the miners and smiths who keep the chests full.
- **What they affect**: hauling, farming and the village's defense ([raids](raids.md)).
- **What stops them**: a strike, or empty chests by the smithing table.
