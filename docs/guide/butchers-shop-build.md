A brick-and-plaster shop under a dark oak roof, with a red-and-white striped awning over its shop windows and sides of
meat hanging inside them. Inside are a stone counter and a cleaver's block, and the **smoker** sits in a brick chimney
breast against the east wall. Behind the shop is a fenced pen with hay for the animals.

Under the shop, down a ladder behind the counter, is the **freezer**: walls of packed ice, a blue-ice path down the
middle, sides of meat and bones hanging on chains along the walls, chests for the frozen stock and snow heaped in a
corner. The ice never melts.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**. It starts locked: each time one of your village's builders levels up, the village
unlocks the next builds, and the Butcher's Shop comes among them. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
near your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the
builder with it. The builder digs the freezer out of the ground himself, four blocks under the floor, before he builds
the shop. You'll need packed ice and some blue ice for the freezer.

## What it does for the village

**A Butcher's workplace.** A villager without a job takes the free smoker as a [Butcher](butcher.md), who looks after
the animals round it: put the cows, pigs and chickens in the pen behind the shop.

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank, and a Village Map drawn
at the hall marks it as a workshop.
