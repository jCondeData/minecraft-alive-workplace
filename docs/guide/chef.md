The Chef feeds the village. Everything they cook goes into the store the villagers eat from, and a varied store makes a
happier village.

## Getting a chef

Stand a villager by a **smoker** and sneak-right-click them with raw meat (beef, pork, chicken, mutton or rabbit), raw
fish (cod or salmon) or a **potato**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the smoker.

## What they do

The chef works through their menu one dish after another: bread, cookies, pumpkin pie, cake, mushroom stew, beetroot
soup, rabbit stew and everything a smoker can cook. With Cobblemon installed the menu adds Cobblemon's Poké Bait, Poké
Snacks, Poké Cakes, candied apples and Aprijuice. The makings come from the chests by the smoker, from the village's
storehouse (where the farms' and fishermen's spare harvest ends up) and from the butcher's chests (milk and eggs). Each
dish is cooked up to eight at a time, and the chef stops making it once the chests hold 16.

**Stock orders.** Between dishes the chef also keeps the storehouse in any food you ordered on its board (see
[Porter](porter.md)).

Over their head: how much they've cooked, and what's cooking right now.

## How the chef fits in

- **Fed by:** the [Farmer](farmer.md)'s wheat, potatoes and pumpkins, the [Fisherman](fisherman.md)'s catch, the
  [Butcher](butcher.md)'s milk and eggs, the [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md)'s fruit.
- **Feeds:** every grown villager eats once a day, from the chests by the smokers first (the chef's cooking), then the
  storehouse. Bread, baked potatoes, cooked fish and pies together keep a village happier than a store full of one thing
  (see [Village life](village-life.md)). Good food is also what a [Netherworker](netherworker.md) or a
  [Cartographer](cartographer.md) packs for a trip.
- **Faster with:** the **Craftsmanship** research (see [Scholar](scholar.md)).

## Trading

Chefs buy potatoes and sugar, and sell bread, pumpkin pie, cooked beef, cake and, at Master, golden carrots.
