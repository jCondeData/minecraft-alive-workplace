The Music Stand is the Bard's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Bard works at a **jukebox**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a music disc.
The Music Stand is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:music_stand`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Music Stand takes it by themselves and becomes a **Bard**: no item needed.
Put music discs in the chests nearby and the bard plays them every morning and evening. No discs? They make up a tune on the harp.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Music Stand to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** the discs in the chests round the stand.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Music Stand or a jukebox); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
