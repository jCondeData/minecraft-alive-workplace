Villages with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) celebrate, and once a week traders come to town.

## Festivals

Every eight days, a village with a hall and at least six villagers holds a **festival**. After work the villagers
gather round the bell for a feast from the store, music and dancing, and at dusk fireworks go up over the square.
Everyone who came is in a better mood for two days. While it's on, players in the village are **Heroes of the
Village**: cheaper trades, and the villagers throw you gifts.

Can't wait? Click the hall's festival button with a **cake** to hold one sooner (at most every three days).

![The festival on the hall's screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall_quests/still-4.jpg)

## The calendar

The world has four seasons, each sixteen days long, and every season has a festival on its middle day. The hall's
**calendar** shows the season, the day and when the next festival is.

![The calendar](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall/still-3.jpg)

## Market days

A village with a hall and a **Market Square** (it's in the Blueprint Table) holds a **market** once a week. In the
morning two traveling traders come to the square with their wares and stay until nightfall; from Town rank a third
comes. Each also sells a blueprint, a starter building (often in another style) or a decoration, for 6 emeralds.
Everyone in the village is told when it's market day.
