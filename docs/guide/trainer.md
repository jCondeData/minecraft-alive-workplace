Trainers are villagers who battle you with Pokémon, and get better the more people battle them. They need **Cobblemon**
installed; without it there are no trainers.

## Getting a trainer

Craft a **Training Post** (a target block on planks) and place it near a villager without a job: they become a
Trainer. A villager already at a Training Post becomes one with a **Poké Ball**. Many villages already have a
**Trainer's House**.

## Battling

Right-click a trainer with an empty hand and their page opens (over their head you see "right-click to battle" and how
many battles they've fought). On the left: their name and rank, your record against them, whether you've battled today
and the rules. On the right: the prize, their team with the real Pokémon models (only half of it until you beat them
once, the rest "???"), your party with a line each (ready, fainted, or over your level cap), and three buttons.
**Challenge** starts the battle; **Trade** opens their trades (as sneaking and right-clicking does); **Close** closes.
When they can't battle you right now, Challenge is gray and the reason is under it: they're asleep ("come back in the
morning"), busy with someone else, or away at the Cup, or you have no Pokémon with you. The battle heals your team
first.

![The Trainer screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/battle/still-1.jpg)

Every trainer starts as a **Novice** with two young Pokémon and ranks up as people battle them (more when the trainer
wins), until a **Master** fields six fully evolved Pokémon and plays smart. Beating one pays **CobbleDollars**
(emeralds without CobbleDollars), once a day per trainer; beat them again the same day for the fun of it. With Radical
Cobblemon Trainers installed, their teams follow your level cap; with Mega Showdown, a Master Mega Evolves their ace.

![Mid-battle](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/battle/still-3.jpg)

## How the trainer fits in

- **Ranks up with:** every battle anyone fights with them, so a village's trainers get tougher as the server plays.
- **Leads to:** the village's [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md), who battles at Expert strength and pays three times
  the prize.
- **Quests:** the village may post a quest to beat one of its trainers (see [Quests](quests.md)).
- **Needs a heal?** A [Nurse](nurse.md) heals your whole team.

See also [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).
