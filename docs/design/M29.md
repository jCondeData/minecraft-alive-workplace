# M29 design note: Legends, rare villagers (1.3)

Written for ROADMAP 29.1 (lane-a-1004-1832, 2026-10-04). The roadmap items 29.2 to 29.23 hold the full specs and
their tests; this note is the overview the owner reviews and the map the lanes build from. Lanes don't wait for the
owner's reply; anything he changes goes back into the items. Decisions taken here are in section 7.

**In one line:** about one villager in thirty is born with a gift, and a village that has earned it may gain one of
twelve named Legends, each with powers that change what the village can do, who wants a fine home, a luxury and a
happy village, and goes on strike (never away) without them.

## 1. What the player sees

### The Gifted (29.6, 29.7)

About 1 in 30 villagers has a rare trait as well as their ordinary ones. It shows in gold under their traits on the
hall's list, they sparkle when they level up, and the chronicle notes it when a Gifted villager joins or grows up
("Wren, Dara and Tom's daughter, has grown up a Prodigy").

| Gift | What it does | Effect (toolbox) |
|---|---|---|
| Prodigy | learns three times as fast (Clever on top: still three times) | `xp` |
| Iron Will | never panics, keeps working through raids and the bell | `no_panic` |
| Silver Tongue | every trade with a player 20% cheaper | `trade_discount` |
| Night Owl | works dusk to dawn, sleeps mid-morning to mid-afternoon (builders and miners too) | `night_shift` |
| Lucky | luck +3 on every loot roll their work makes (explorer finds, Netherworker trips, sifting, fishing) | `loot_luck` |
| Hardy | never falls ill, twice a villager's health | `no_illness`, `health` |
| Beloved | everyone whose bed is within 16 blocks of theirs is +5 mood, "a beloved neighbour" | `mood` |
| Born Leader | workers of their own trade within 16 blocks work 10% faster | `pace` |

Inn travellers are Gifted 1 time in 10 (shown on the hire screen, at twice the price); a child of two schooled
Masters grows up Gifted 1 time in 4 when they don't grow up a Legend.

### The twelve Legends

A Legend is one named villager, always a Master of their trade, in a Legend outfit drawn over the job's outfit, with
their name in gold over their head and a soft end-rod sparkle every 10 seconds. On the hall's list they come first:
"Ada Stonewright, Master Architect", rarity, each power on a line, each need with a tick or a cross.

| Legend | Rarity | Trade | Comes (29.8 to 29.10) | Likes | Powers, in short |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Master Architect (29.12) | Legendary | Builder | guest at the inn: a Town with finished buildings in 3 styles | jewels | builders within 32 blocks twice as fast; every 3 days a finished building upgraded, or redrawn in the new **Grand** style |
| Pathfinder (29.13) | Rare | Cartographer | found at a ruined portal: an Expert explorer; born to a Cartographer | clothes | expeditions twice as far with their own loot; leads the player to a Stronghold, Ancient City or Trial Chambers and brings both home |
| Old Sage (29.14) | Rare | (none) | found in a hermit's hut after three riddles: 5 research levels; born to a Scholar | books | the **Ancient Lore** research tree (six topics and one of three last picks for good) |
| Golem Smith (29.15) | Legendary | Tinkerer | inspired: a Master Tinkerer, 4 iron golems | wine | builds Hauler, Farmhand and Wall Sentry golems, mends golems twice as fast |
| Seer (29.16) | Rare | (none) | guest at the Chapel at a full-moon midnight; born to a Cleric | jewels | foretells raids (always right), festivals, market days and guests at dawn; blesses Chapel weddings |
| Merchant Prince (29.17) | Legendary | (none) | found as a castaway by a shipwreck: 500 emeralds taken in, caravans on 3 routes | wine | a bank (2% a day on the treasury, player deposits at 5% a week); trade fairs every 10 days; caravans paid for wanted goods |
| Grand Chef (29.18) | Rare | Chef | inspired, or guest at the inn: 8 kinds of meal; born to a Chef | wine | a banquet every 5 days (+20 mood, two babies a day for 3 days); chefs 25% faster |
| Bard Laureate (29.19) | Rare | Bard | guest at a festival of 30; born to a Bard | books | the village's own anthem, played at every big moment; work songs (+25% pace while singing) |
| Beastmaster (29.20) | Rare | Rancher | found as a prisoner in a pillager outpost: a ranch of 10 animals; born to a Rancher | clothes | war dogs for the guards; foals that take their parents' best |
| Pokémon Professor (29.21) | Legendary | (none) | guest at the inn: 25 pastured Pokémon of 10 types (Cobblemon) | books | IV, nature, ability and EV hints; the village Pokédex and its research tree |
| Pokémon Ranger (29.22) | Rare | (none) | guest at the hall while an Alpha is within 96 blocks (Cobblemon) | clothes | calms Alphas for good; befriends a wild Pokémon a day into the pastures |
| Founder (29.23) | Mythic | their own | inspired, always, at the village's first rise to City | books | raises the Founder's statue (+5 mood, 5 beauty); a Founder's Wagon every 7 days (M33's colonies later) |

"(none)" is the profession `aliveworkplace:legend`: no workstation, never reset to a jobless Novice, a plain outfit.

**Rarity** (29.3). Rare: one of each per village. Legendary: one of each per world, in any dimension. Mythic: as
many as the server earns, but each village holds at most its rank's cap (Hamlet 0, Village 0, Town 1, City 2); a
village that falls back a rank keeps the ones it has. Rare and Legendary arrivals are told to the players in the
village and to the hall's owner wherever they are; Mythic ones to every player on the server, in gold, with the
challenge sound, the village's name and its direction from spawn. Everything goes in the chronicle under a new kind,
`LEGEND` (a nether star).

### How Legends arrive: the four ways

Every way first asks `Legends.canCome(level, hall, legend)`: the village meets the Legend's `conditions` and the
Legend's slot is free (rarity above).

| Way | Where it happens | What the player does |
|---|---|---|
| **visit** (29.8, Terraria) | once a day, at the way's place: `inn` (morning, instead of the traveller), `market` (market day), `festival` (at the fireworks, crowd of N), `chapel` (full-moon midnight), `hall` (morning); 1 in 4 by default, not twice in 7 days | right-click the guest for their terms (who they are, what they bring, their needs with ticks and crosses, days left); they settle the round every need is met, else leave on the third evening |
| **found** (29.9) | a camp placed when a qualifying player stands in a ruined portal (traveller's camp), a pillager outpost (prisoner's cage) or a shipwreck (castaway's camp on the nearest beach); each structure used once | talk to the traveller, break the cage's bars, hand the castaway a cooked meal; they arrive at the hall next morning as a guest (3 days to settle) |
| **born** (29.7) | a child of two schooled Masters grows up a Legend 1 time in 20 (a Rare whose `born` way names a parent's trade) | nothing: the chronicle says "... has grown up to be the village's Bard Laureate" |
| **inspired** (29.10, Dwarf Fortress) | once a day in a happy village, a Master of a named trade is seized by a strange mood 1 time in 8 | fill a chest by their workstation with the three rare materials they ask for within 3 days; success makes a named **Masterwork** and the villager becomes the Legend; failure is a week of sulking and no mood for 10 days |

The Old Sage is found in a hermit's hut placed 150-250 blocks from the hall (a rumour gives its direction) and the
Founder's mood always comes at the first rise to City; both are the same ways with their own settings.

A player can always see what a village needs: the hall's **Legends page** (a nether star on the hall's screen) lists
the village's Legends, then every other Legend as a card with its rarity, how it comes ("visits the inn", "found at a
ruined portal"), each condition with its progress ("kinds of meal in the store: 5 of 8"), "lives in Thornholm" for a
Legendary taken elsewhere, and the Mythic line ("Mythic Legends: 0 of 1, as a Town"). "What next?" gives a tip when a
Legend lacks only one condition, and villagers chat about their Legends and guests.

### Needs and strikes (29.5)

Checked once a day in the hall's round for every settled Legend:

- **a home of their own**: their bed is in a finished tier III (or higher) building, shared with nobody but their
  spouse;
- **a liked luxury**: every 7 days they take one item of their kind (wine, jewels, books or clothes) from a chest in
  their home, else the village store, and are +10 mood;
- **a happy village**: the grown-ups' average mood is 60 or more (wellbeing 60% with `villagerMoods` off).

After a 3-day grace from settling, a need unmet two days running starts a **strike**: their powers stop, their
trade's work stops, they picket by the Village Hall by day under a red line saying what they want ("On strike: a home
of my own"), and the hall and chronicle show it in red. The day every need is met they go back to work. Legends never
leave: inn departures, despawning and the hall's call-home all pass them by.

**Death** (29.3): a Legend's grave holds their slot and the Undertaker brings them back as they were; a Legend turned
into a zombie villager keeps the slot and is themselves again when cured; with no grave (or the grave broken) the
slot frees after 7 days and the record and chronicle say "fallen". If their hall is broken and placed again, they join
the nearest hall at its next round.

## 2. Data formats (all under `data/aliveworkplace/`)

One file per entry, loaded through `Platform.get().onDataReload` as `ranch/PokemonChores` loads
`pokemon_interactions/`. A malformed file is logged with its name and the bad field and skipped; the rest still load.
Every text a player reads is a lang key in `en_us.json`. Item, block, structure and profession fields take registry
ids or `#tags`, so packs can add their own.

### `legends/<id>.json` (29.2)

```json
{
  "rarity": "legendary",
  "job": "aliveworkplace:builder",
  "title": "legend.aliveworkplace.master_architect.title",
  "lore": "legend.aliveworkplace.master_architect.lore",
  "names": ["legend.aliveworkplace.master_architect.name.1", "... six in all"],
  "conditions": [
    {"type": "rank_at_least", "rank": "town"},
    {"type": "finished", "styles": 3}
  ],
  "arrive": [
    {"way": "visit", "place": "inn", "chance": 0.25}
  ],
  "needs": {"luxury": "jewels"},
  "powers": [
    {"type": "pace", "trades": ["aliveworkplace:builder"], "radius": 32, "factor": 2.0},
    {"type": "grand_rebuild", "every_days": 3}
  ],
  "outfit": "aliveworkplace:textures/entity/villager/legend/master_architect.png"
}
```

- `rarity`: `rare`, `legendary`, `mythic`. `job`: a profession id (always a Master, every level's trades through
  `Schools.headStart`), or `aliveworkplace:legend`.
- `names` are used only for guests and found Legends; a villager who becomes a Legend keeps their own name.
- `conditions` (the new `rules/` package, shared with M31 and M35; each type is one class with a test and a progress
  line for the Legends page): `rank_at_least` (`rank`), `villagers` (`count`), `finished` (`count`, optional
  `blueprint`, or `styles`), `job_level` (`job`, `level`, `count`), `treasury_total` (`emeralds`), `caravan_routes`
  (`count`), `meal_kinds` (`count`), `festival_crowd` (`count`), `animals_at_job` (`jobs`, `count`, radius 16),
  `research_levels` (`count`, optional `tree`), `iron_golems` (`count`), `full_moon`, `first_city`, `legend` (`id`);
  with Cobblemon, `pastured_pokemon` (`count`, `types`) and `alpha_near` (`radius`). An unknown type makes the file
  fail to load (a typo must never quietly make a Legend free).
- `arrive`: a list of ways, each `{"way": ..., settings}`:
  - `visit`: `place` (`inn`, `market`, `festival`, `chapel`, `hall`), `chance` (default 0.25), `crowd` for festivals;
  - `found`: `site` (a `legend_sites` tag name, or `hermit_hut`);
  - `born`: `trades` (the parents' trades that may bring them);
  - `inspired`: `trades`, `chance` (default 0.125), or `"founder": true` for the City rise.
- `needs`: `luxury` (`wine`, `jewels`, `books`, `clothes`); the home and happy-village needs are the same for all.
- `powers`: `{"type": ..., settings}`. Built in 29.2: `pace` (`trades` or all, `radius` or the village, `factor`,
  always under the shared 2x cap read in `BuilderLevels.delay`) and `mood` (`points`, `radius`). Each Legend item adds
  its named power (`grand_rebuild`, `expedition`, `golem_forge`, `foretell`, `bless_weddings`, `bank`, `trade_fair`,
  `caravan_pay`, `banquet`, `anthem`, `work_songs`, `war_dogs`, `horse_breeding`, `pokemon_hints`, `pokedex`,
  `calm_alphas`, `befriend`, `statue`, `found_villages`). An unknown power type also fails the file.
- `masterwork` (inspired Legends): `item`, `name` (a lang key with the village and maker as arguments), `materials`
  (the pool; three are asked for).

A Legend whose file needs a missing mod (Cobblemon for the Professor and Ranger) doesn't load, without an error.

### `gifted/<id>.json` (29.6)

```json
{
  "name": "gifted.aliveworkplace.prodigy",
  "description": "gifted.aliveworkplace.prodigy.desc",
  "weight": 10,
  "effects": [{"type": "xp", "factor": 3.0}]
}
```

Effects come from the same toolbox: `xp`, `no_panic`, `trade_discount`, `night_shift`, `loot_luck`, `no_illness`,
`health`, `mood`, `pace`. The eight files ship with equal weights.

### `research_trees/<tree>.json` (29.11)

```json
{
  "legend": "aliveworkplace:old_sage",
  "icon": "minecraft:enchanted_book",
  "topics": {
    "star_charts": {
      "levels": 2,
      "cost": [{"item": "minecraft:paper", "count": 16}, {"item": "minecraft:emerald", "count": 8}],
      "points": 200,
      "needs": ["old_tongues"],
      "effects": [{"type": "flag", "name": "explorer_range", "per_level": 0.25}]
    },
    "the_undying_flame": {"levels": 1, "exclusive": "last_pick", "needs": ["*"], "...": "..."}
  }
}
```

Topics may also have `unlock` (`{"counter": "pokedex_species", "at": 15}`). Effects added in 29.11: `wellbeing`,
`illness`, `raid_chance`, village-wide `xp` and `loot_luck`, and `flag` (a named switch other code reads). Levels go
in the hall's existing `Research.State.levels` map under `<tree>/<topic>` keys, so the first tree's keys and save are
untouched.

## 3. Config switches (`config/aliveworkplace.json`, all in the Mod Menu screen)

| Option | Default | Range | Off or 0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `legends` | true | | no Legend loads, nothing ticks, `/workplace legend` says so; Legends already settled stay as Master villagers of their trade without powers or needs |
| `giftedChance` | 30 (1 in 30) | 0 to 1000 | 0: nobody is Gifted (the gifts stop; nothing is erased) |
| `legendSites` | true | | no camps at ruined portals, outposts or shipwrecks, and no hermit's hut; no structure lookups at all |
| `strangeMoods` | true | | no strange moods; inspired Legends (Golem Smith, Founder) can't come |
| `legendNeeds` | true | | needs aren't checked and nobody strikes; a strike in progress ends at the next round |
| `mythicLegendCap` | 2 | 0 to 10 | the most Mythic Legends a City holds; a Town holds half of it (rounded down), a Hamlet or Village none |

`mythicLegendCap` is a single number, not the list 29.3 describes: the config file and its Mod Menu screen take only
switches and whole numbers (`WorkplaceConfig.optionNames`, `isSwitch`, `RANGES`), and a list would need a new kind of
option for one setting. With the default 2 it gives exactly 29.3's list (0, 0, 1, 2). See section 7.

Notes for the builders of 29.2 and 29.3:

- Graves already keep the whole villager (`Graves` saves `saveWithoutId`, attachments and UUID included), so a revived
  Legend is the same Legend with nothing new.
- A zombie villager is a new entity with a new UUID: 29.3 copies `LEGEND`, `GIFTED` and `STRANGE_MOOD` onto the zombie
  and back on curing, and writes the `GIFTED` roll into the attachment at that moment (the cured villager's new UUID
  would otherwise roll a different gift).
- The record lives on the overworld so Legendary and Mythic counts span dimensions, as `aliveworkplace_calendar` and
  the other `aliveworkplace_*` saved data do.

## 5. What an existing world sees after the update

- **Some villagers turn out Gifted at once.** The gift is rolled from the villager's UUID, as `Traits.of` rolls traits
  (with a different salt, so traits and gifts don't go together), so about one in thirty of the villagers already
  living there has one, with nothing to migrate. The hall's list shows it in gold. The chronicle does not announce
  them (no flood of lines on the first day); it notes only Gifted villagers who join or grow up from then on.
- **Nobody is a Legend** until a village earns one. The Legends page is there from the first day, so the owner sees
  what each Legend needs and how far each village has got.
- **What starts from zero:** `treasuryTotal` counts from the update (the Merchant Prince's 500 emeralds are earned
  after it), `festivalCrowd` from the next festival, and children already born have no schooled-Master parents on
  record, so the first born Legends come from babies born after the update.
- **What works at once:** guests, found Legends (structures already generated qualify, each used once) and strange
  moods, wherever the village already meets the conditions. A village that is already a City gets the Founder's mood
  at its first round after the update (`founderMoodDay` 0), once.
- **Unchanged:** research levels, every existing field, every config option. The new options are added to the config
  file with their defaults the first time it loads.
- **Going back to an older version** (not supported, but harmless): the new attachments and hall fields are ignored,
  and `LEGEND` chronicle lines read as `FOUNDED` (`Chronicle.Kind.parse`'s fallback).

## 6. Build order and hooks

29.2 (engine, `rules/`, profession, `LEGEND`, `legends` switch) → 29.3 (record, caps, announcements, death) → 29.4
(hall page and look) and 29.5 (needs, strikes) → 29.6 and 29.7 (Gifted, born) → 29.8 to 29.10 (the other three ways)
→ 29.11 (research trees) → the twelve Legends, each needing the way it arrives by. The Legends are independent of
one another and split across lanes.

Hooks other milestones use: `Legends.visitFactor(hall)` (M30's Open Gates), the `rules/` conditions (M31, M35 with
`legend`), `Legends.foretold(hall, kind)` (M32), `Legends.in(level, hall, id)` (M35). Fallbacks until they land:
the Founder's Wagon for M33's colonies, the stand-in luxuries for M34, the Merchant Prince's caravan pay for M33's
price board.

Budgets: auras read a per-dimension list of settled Legends refreshed every 200 ticks, never an entity scan; site
lookups are three `getStructureWithPieceAt` calls per player every 5 seconds and none with nobody online; needs,
visits and moods run once a day in the hall's round.

## 7. Decisions taken (the owner may change any)

1. **`mythicLegendCap` is one number** (the City cap, default 2; a Town half of it, Hamlets and Villages none), not a
   list, so it fits the config file and screen as they are. Same defaults as 29.3.
2. **`giftedChance` is a threshold on the UUID's hash, not a fresh roll**, so raising it keeps everyone already Gifted
   Gifted and lowering it only removes those past the new threshold; the gift itself comes from a second hash, so changing
   the chance never swaps one gift for another. Adding a gift file reshuffles the weighted pick, so the first time a
   Gifted villager is seen in a hall round their gift is written into `GIFTED` and kept.
3. **A villager who becomes a Legend keeps their gift** (it shows under their Legend title); guests and found Legends
   arrive without one.
4. **Existing Gifted villagers are not announced** in the chronicle on the update's first day.
5. **An existing City gets the Founder's mood once** after the update, rather than never.
6. **`legends` off keeps the people**: settled Legends stay in the village as ordinary Masters of their trade; turning
   it back on restores them as they were (the attachment is kept).
7. **An unknown condition or power type fails the whole Legend file** (logged), so a typo never makes a Legend come
   for free.
8. **`treasuryTotal` starts at 0** in old halls, as 29.2 says, rather than at the treasury's current balance.
