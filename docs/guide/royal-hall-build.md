A realm deserves a seat. The Royal Hall is a great stone hall by the [capital](capital.md), with a throne, banners and a
vault behind the dais.

## What it looks like

A stone great hall with pilasters and buttresses, tall windows four to a side, and a deepslate roof with a rose window
in the front gable over a gabled porch hung with red-and-gold banners. Inside, a red runner leads to a two-step dais and
the seat of state under banners, with benches down the hall, a minstrels' gallery over the door and lanterns hanging
from the beams. Behind the dais, a lower annex is the vault, with four chests.

**Royal Hall II** adds a four-floor keep tower with a crenellated top and the realm's banner on a pole, and a treasury
wing with chests along both walls, a counting table and barred windows.

![Royal Hall II from behind: the keep tower, the treasury wing and the vault](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/realm_builds/still-3.jpg)

## Getting the blueprint

It isn't on the Blueprint Table. On the capital's Realm tab, the **Royal Hall** button (a lectern) draws it for the
capital's ruler: click it with a [Blank Blueprint](blank-blueprint.md) in your inventory and you get the Royal Hall in
the capital's style. With a Royal Hall standing, it draws the Royal Hall II. Have a [builder](builder.md) put it up
within 32 blocks of the capital's hall.

## How it fits in

- **The treasury:** finished near the capital, it doubles the [realm treasury](realm-treasury.md)'s cap; the Royal
  Hall II triples it.
- **Realm edicts:** a finished Royal Hall II adds one more realm edict slot to the capital's Book of Edicts (see
  [Edicts](edicts.md)).
- **Relief:** when the capital is raided, [relief riders](relief-riders.md) muster before it.
- **The village map:** it's marked as a hall on the [village map](village-map.md).
- **What breaks it:** taking it down, or moving the capital to another village, lowers the cap at once. A treasury
  already over the new cap keeps what it holds but takes no more until it's under.
