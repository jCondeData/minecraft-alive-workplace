The Quarry Marker tells a Miner where to dig.

## Getting one

Craft a **stick**, **red dye** and **paper** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

1. Right-click one corner block, then the opposite corner (up to 32 by 32).
2. Sneak-right-click the air to choose the depth, or a **strip mine**: 2-high tunnels with 2 blocks of rock between
   them, taking just the ore. A strip mine can also go down a ladder shaft to the height you pick (or type
   `/workplace strip <height>`), where iron, gold and diamonds are.
3. Hand it to a Miner (a villager at a blast furnace you gave a pickaxe), with **pickaxes** and some torches in a chest
   by the blast furnace.

Sneak-right-click a block to start over.

## How it fits in

- **The miner** digs it out from the top down, leaves a spiral stair in the pit wall so you can climb out, and keeps it
  dry by leaving blocks next to water and lava.
- **Anywhere in the village, or far outside it.** The quarry may be anywhere in the village the blast furnace stands in,
  or outside the village within 128 blocks of it across the ground, however deep it goes (Quarry Distance in the
  [Settings](settings-reach-and-range.md)), so
  you needn't dig a hole among the houses. The miner walks out to it; while nobody is near enough to keep its land
  loaded, the miner waits there.
- **Never their own workstation.** A quarry laid over the miner's own block is refused: mark it somewhere else.
- **Feeds** the builders with stone and ore; with coal in the chests the blast furnace smelts the ore too.
