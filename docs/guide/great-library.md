A domed library on a cross plan: a rotunda of bookshelf galleries under a copper dome that weathers green, four wings
of reading halls, gardens in the corners of its terrace, and a portico of six quartz columns. At its heart an
enchanting table ringed with bookshelves.

## What it needs

A **Town** whose scholars have finished **20 research levels**, the **Old Sage** (on a server without Legends, a Master
Scholar stands in) and the Master Architect. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

The village's **scholars open a second tier of research**: a row of its own at the top of the scholar's screen
(sneak-right-click a Scholar), under the heading "The Great Library's research". Until the Library stands, each of its
topics reads "Needs the Great Library". Each level costs **24 paper, 6 books and 8 emeralds times its level** and takes
twice as long as a first-tier level.

| Topic | Levels | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Swift Hands IV–V | 2 (after Swift Hands III) | every job in the village 5% faster a level |
| Scholarship | 2 | scholars 25% faster a level; the second level makes research cost a quarter less |
| Engineering | 2 | builders' bags hold 9 more stacks a level (27 to 45); a Wonder's crew takes 2 more builders a level |
| Cartography | 1 | explorers range twice as far, and their maps can lead to trial chambers and ancient cities |
| Trade Law | 2 | caravans carry 2 more stacks a level; the treasury takes 10% more a level |
| Pharmacy | 1 | the ill get well in a day instead of three; each Nurse cures one villager a day without a remedy |

The speed bonuses add up with everything else under the usual limit: work is never more than **twice as fast**
(the **Fastest Work Pace** setting). If the Library is **damaged**, the village keeps its levels, but their bonuses stop (Swift Hands counts
to III) and the scholars wait, until its builder repairs it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your [scholars](jobs-services.md) (20 levels of research first), rare things found by exploring
  (a rare worker in the village finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: the village's research, and every bonus it brings.
