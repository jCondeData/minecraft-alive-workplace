Three storage builds for a village with a lot of goods. Each one is a [Storehouse](storehouse.md): a porter moves in
at its Storehouse block, and the village counts it as its storehouse. With **Tom's Simple Storage** on the server they
are built of its blocks, so the whole build is one big store you can search from a terminal. Without it they are built
of plain chests and barrels, and work like any Storehouse.

## The three builds

- **Storage Hall**: a long stone-and-birch hall with a porch. Inside, an island of chests and barrels stands round an
  Inventory Connector, a counter by the door holds a Storage Terminal and a Crafting Terminal either side of the
  Storehouse, and two Filing Cabinets for paper and maps stand against the back wall. A cable under the floor links
  them all.
- **Terminal Nook**: a small room that fits beside a house: six chests round a connector along the back wall and a
  Storage Terminal in front of it.
- **Warehouse**: a big two-story store with four racks of chests and barrels below and two more in the loft, a
  Crafting Terminal at the packing counter inside the loading doors, and a loading bay with a handcart out front. The
  racks stand on Inventory Trim in the floor, which links them to the connector.

## With Tom's Simple Storage

Every chest, barrel and cabinet in the build is on the connector, so the terminals list everything in it and you can
take what you need from one screen. The Storehouse reads the whole network as one store: porters fill it, builders and
workers take from it, and each item is counted once on the Storehouse board ("Linked through an inventory
connector").

## Without it

The same buildings in plain blocks: a chest where the connector stands, a crafting table for the Crafting Terminal, a
counter block for the Storage Terminal (the Nook just leaves that spot open), wooden boards where the cables run and a
chest for each filing cabinet. Every chest and barrel is one of the Storehouse's own stores.

## Building it

Take any of the three from the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md), place it and stock the chests by the builder's
table with what its tooltip lists: with Tom's installed that includes the connector, terminals and cables, which you
craft from Tom's recipes. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Settings

- **Storage Mod Networks** (on): off, every chest is its own store again, even on Tom's network.
- **Modded Build Library** (on): off, the builds are always the plain ones.
