Why wait for the raiders to come to you? A war party takes the fight to their camp.

## Mustering

On the Village Hall's [Defense page](defense-page.md), click **Muster a war party** (the white banner). It tells you
first how many guards would march and how many stay to keep the walls. A click takes three in four of the village's
guards, nearest the hall first, twelve at most, enlists them on the [Rally Banner](rally-banner.md) in your inventory
and raises it. They gather by the hall and follow you, as a raised Rally Banner always makes them. No Rally Banner? Craft
one first: the war party gathers under it.

## Hiring the Siege-breakers

Beside it, **Hire the Siege-breakers** (the crossbow) brings five hired swords in iron: two with crossbows, two knights
with shields in the village's colors, and a medic with healing potions. They cost **30 emeralds, 3 less for each level
of Commerce** research (or CobbleDollars, with that mod). They follow whoever hired them, join your Rally Banner while
it has room, and stay until the second dawn, or until the dawn after the lair falls, whichever comes first. One band of
hired swords at a time.

## At the lair

Use a [Scouting Report](scouting-report.md) to find the way. The raiders turn out to meet you, six at a time from the
tents, more as they fall, until the camp's whole strength is spent. At a City's lair the captain leaves his fire and
fights at the front. Every kill there earns a guard double experience: Masters are made at the lair.

## When the lair breaks

Kill the captain with your war party there and the loot chest is yours, as always. The chronicle names who led the war
party and every guard who killed there, the village gets **10 days' peace** from camps instead of the usual 5, and
every villager is 10 happier for two days: "the warband is broken".

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** [guards](guard.md), a Rally Banner, and emeralds for the Siege-breakers.
- **What it affects:** the lair's strength, and the raids and [sieges](sieges.md) it would have sent. See
  [Lairs and sieges](lairs-and-sieges.md).
- **The cost:** the guards who march aren't on the walls. The Defense page tells you how many stay behind.
