The hall's Quests page grows into a **journal** with four tabs: everything the village and its people want of you, in
one place.

## The four tabs

- **Village**: the quests on the hall's board today, the same [daily quests](quests.md) as always.
- **Personal**: requests from villagers who are your friends (see [Personal requests](personal-requests.md)). Others
  with enough hearts can join one with a click.
- **Story**: the tale this village is living through, chapter by chapter: done chapters ticked, the one happening now
  with its quest, and when the next begins (see [Story arcs](story-arcs.md)).
- **Bounties**: wanted outlaws and their rewards; click one to take it (see [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md)).

Each quest shows who asked, a line per objective with its progress, the [reward](quest-rewards.md) and the days left.
**Click** it to hand in what it asks for from your inventory; **shift-click** to track it.

## Tracking a quest

A tracked quest shows as a bar at the top of your screen, for you only: "Follow the tracks (2/5)", filling as you go.
You track one quest at a time, and it stays tracked through a restart or a trip to the Nether. Shift-click it again, or
type `/workplace quests untrack`, to stop.

![A tracked quest's bar at the top](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/quest_journal/still-2.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** the hall's daily quests, your [friends](friendship.md)' requests, the village's
  [stories](story-arcs.md) and its bounties.
- **What it affects:** finishing quests pays emeralds and earns [standing](reputation.md) and friendship.
