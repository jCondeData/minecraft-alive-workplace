The Spring Infirmary: a white Dispensary in a dark oak frame, its gable to the street under a steep red roof with a
vent on the ridge and a little gabled wall dormer over each long side, lamp posts either side of the step (rebuilt from
scratch; a Healing Center already standing keeps its old look until refreshed). Inside the front door is the waiting
room, its benches facing each other under the windows, a clock and a bookcase by the door; behind it, across the room,
the reception desk and counters, and behind those the **brewing stand** and its chest under shelves of herbs.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). An Expert **builder** sells it for 16 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Without Cobblemon the Healing Machine's spot just stays empty.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Healing Center, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Healing Center II** adds the **Spring Hall**, a taller cross range across the back: a warm pool rimmed in mossy stone with benches round it, a tea table, towel cupboards and a couch, under lamps hung from the ridge.
- **Healing Center III** adds the **Consulting Room** beside the front (the doctor's desk, a couch for patients, book cupboards) and a walled **herb garden** behind the hall, with raised beds of healing herbs, a flowering tree and a bench, entered by the hall's back door.
- **Healing Center IV** digs the **Cistern** under the Spring Hall, down a stone stair from its west end: the spring's source pool, the still room's chests and herb shelves, treatment benches and a table for two. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was.

## What it does for the village

Stand a villager by the brewing stand and sneak-right-click them with a **honey bottle** and they become the village's **Nurse** (without the honey bottle, a jobless villager takes the stand as a cleric). Right-click the nurse with an empty hand to get your health back and bad effects cleared; nurses look after hurt villagers and golems and cure the village's sick with a honey bottle, milk or a healing potion from their chest. With Cobblemon, the Healing Machine heals your team. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a pink banner (healing and graves).
