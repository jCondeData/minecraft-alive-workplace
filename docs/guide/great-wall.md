A curtain of stone 97 long and 12 high on a battered plinth, with a crenellated walk lit by braziers, four towers under
spruce roofs, and the **Great Gate** at its center, its portcullis over a gate of fence gates.

## What it needs

A **Town** with a **finished Gatehouse**, the **Golem Smith** (on a server without Legends, 4 iron golems and a Master
Tinkerer stand in) and the Master Architect. Place it like the other walls: stand inside the village and face out, so
its stairs are on your side. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## Its stages and goods

| Stage | Goods from the village |
|---|---|
| The Great Gate | 8 iron swords (Weaponsmith), 128 arrows (Fletcher), 128 bread (Chef) |
| The West Run | 8 iron shovels (Toolsmith), 12 potions of strength (Cleric), 64 baked potatoes (Chef) |
| The East Run | 8 crossbows (Fletcher), 8 iron chestplates (Armorer), 8 shields (Carpenter) |
| The Walk and the Towers' Crowns | 16 iron blocks (Armorer), 4 pumpkins (Farmer), 16 campfires (Carpenter) |

## While it stands

- **No raid gathers behind it.**
- **Four iron golem sentries** keep its walk.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a [Gatehouse](defence.md#walls-and-gates) first, iron golems or a Master Tinkerer, and goods from
  eight jobs.
- **What it affects**: [raids](defence.md#raids) on the village, and where they can come from.
