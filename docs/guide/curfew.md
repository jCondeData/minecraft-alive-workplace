Everyone home by dark.

## What it does

While the Curfew is in force, from dusk to dawn **everyone but the guards and mercenaries stays in bed**, where no
monster can hurt them, and **raids are half as likely**.

The price is the evening: **nobody trades at night** ("Curfew: come back in the morning."), **festivals end at dusk
without their fireworks**, **market traders leave at dusk**, and **nobody sets out after midday**: explorers and
netherworkers stay home rather than start a trip late. It can't be in force together with [Open Gates](open-gates.md).

## Reforming it

While it's in force, a Porter asks for lamps instead: 192 lanterns, then 96 glowstone, then 32 monsters slain
([The Lamplighters](the-lamplighters.md)). After that raids stay half as likely and the nights safe, but trading,
festivals, markets and night work go on, and you get two Street Lamp blueprints.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it protects**: your villagers at night, and the village from [raids](defence.md#raids).
- **What it costs**: night trading, the [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) fireworks, the evening market, and the
  trips of your [explorers and netherworkers](jobs-land.md).
- **Good with**: a village short of guards.
