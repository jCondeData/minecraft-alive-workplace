The Gate Watch: two stone piers with a road arch between them, and the guard's timber barrack room bridging over both on a jetty, under one steep slate roof with a watch gable over the gate. The west pier is the guardroom, with the worker's **grindstone**, an anvil, a gear chest and the ladder up; the east pier holds the armory chest and a barred lock-up cell. Upstairs are two bunks, a mess table and a watch desk at the window over the gate: everything a guard needs to live and work in the same place.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). Newly explored villages often have a guard house already (a smaller village house of its own look), with its worker inside.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the grindstone with a **sword** and they become a **Guard** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a grindstone as a weaponsmith). Guards fight any monster within 24 blocks of their post, at any hour, and wear the best weapons and armor in the chest. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

**A home.** Its bunks are the worker's own. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a purple banner (other buildings).
