A finished map of your village, drawn at its Village Hall: the land round the hall as it is today, with a colored banner
on every building the builders put up.

## Getting one

Carry an **empty map**, right-click the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and click **Draw a map of the village**. The
map is called after the village ("Map of Thornholm") and says which day it was drawn.

## Reading it

The map is centered on the hall and locked, like a map copied on a cartography table. Each building has a banner
colored by what it is; the workplaces and the hall carry their names:

| Color | What |
|---|---|
| Red | the Village Hall |
| White | homes |
| Blue | school, library, research |
| Pink | healing and graves |
| Yellow | stores, shops and markets |
| Lime | farms, ranches and gardens |
| Black | towers, barracks and gates |
| Orange | workshops |
| Light blue | wells, fountains and the like |
| Purple | other buildings |

The legend is on the map's tooltip, with how many of each there are.

## How it fits in

- **Only what builders finished** shows up: a building your builders put up from a blueprint gets its banner.
- **Draw it again** as the village grows: the map shows the village on the day it was drawn.
