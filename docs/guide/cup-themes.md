Every [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md) has a theme: which Pokémon may come, the level they all battle at, how many each
trainer brings, what the fair sells, the feast's dish, the bard's music and the colors of the fireworks and the
[Cup banner](cup-champions.md). They come in this order; the host's owner may click the theme on the Cup page to pick
the next one instead, until sign-up closes.

The bouts run from noon to midnight, except the Lantern Cup's, from dusk until dawn (its delegates stay until then
too). Every Pokémon battles at the theme's level, whatever its own.

A Pokémon with two types may come if either is one of the Cup's: a Charizard (Fire and Flying) goes to the Sun Cup, a
Zubat (Poison and Flying) stays home from the Blossom Cup. In the Little Cup a Pokémon that has already evolved once
stays home, even from a baby (a Pikachu, after Pichu), and so does one that never evolves (Ditto). Each Pokémon that
stays home is named with the reason, such as "Zubat: not one of the Cup's types".

## The Workers' Cup

Only Pokémon that have **helped a villager at work on 3 days or more** may come. Each day one of your pastured Pokémon
helps a worker at their job ([Pokémon partners at work](partners-at-work.md)), it gets one more day on its count, never
two in a day; the count stays with the Pokémon in the pasture, the PC or your party. The village trainers bring Pokémon
of the types that help at work.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Your team**: the first Pokémon in your party the theme allows, up to its number; the others are named with the
  reason they stay home ([Cup bouts](cup-bouts.md)).
- **The village's team**: delegates and host trainers field teams that follow the theme.
- **The fair and the feast**: the traders at the fair sell the theme's wares, and the feast serves its dish first if
  the store has it ([Cup day](cup-day.md)). The Camp Cook makes several of the dishes on stock orders
  ([Camp Cook](camp-cook.md)).
