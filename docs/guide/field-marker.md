The Field Marker marks out a plot of land and hands it to a worker to look after.

## Getting one

Craft a **stick**, **wheat seeds** and **paper** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

Right-click a corner block of the plot, then the opposite corner; the tooltip shows the size. Sneak-right-click a
block to start over. Then hand it to the worker:

- **A Farmer** (the vanilla one at a composter) harvests and replants the field, sows, tills, and cuts sugar cane,
  cactus, bamboo and kelp. Put seeds (and a hoe for bare dirt) in their chest. Give a farmer a blank marker and they
  take on the farm by their composter.
- **A Lumberjack** makes it a tree farm of their own.
- **An Orchard Keeper** plants a whole orchard on it.

## How it fits in

- **Feeds** the village's food and wood: a porter carries the harvest to the [Storehouse](storehouse.md).
- **A village's own farmers** look after the farm by their composter by themselves once there's a chest by it.
