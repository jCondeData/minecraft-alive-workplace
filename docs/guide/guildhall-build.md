A two-story guild hall: stone below, a timber-framed plaster story above, under a slate roof, with a guild banner
either side of the door. Inside, a long table with a high seat at each end, the guild's charter framed over the hearth
and an archive upstairs.

## The tiers

- **Guildhall**: the hall itself, with its long table, hearth and archive.
- **Guildhall II**: adds a stone tower wing: a meeting room with a round table, a chart room, and an open belfry with
  the guild bell.

## What it does

A chartered [guild](guilds.md) gives no perks until a builder finishes a Guildhall in the village. Each guild needs its
own: when a Guildhall is finished, the oldest chartered guild without one claims it and is **founded**, and its perks
are in force. The hall's "What next?" reminds you when a guild is still waiting ("Give a builder the Guildhall
blueprint").

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint. Hand it to a
[builder](builder.md) as usual; the table's styles swap its woods and stones.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your builders, and stone, spruce and slate in their chests.
- **What it affects**: whether a guild's perks are on.
- **What breaks it**: if the Guildhall is damaged and no longer finished, its guild's perks pause until it's repaired.
