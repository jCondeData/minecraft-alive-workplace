Break up enough [bandit camps](defence.md#bandit-camps) and somebody notices. In a Town that has broken up two of
them, the bandits may come back with a king.

## Smoke on Three Hills

"Smoke rises from three hills round the village: three bandit camps went up in a night." The three chiefs call
themselves **Captains of the Bandit King**: vindicators in an iron helmet and chestplate with an iron axe, tougher
than a plain chief, each with a few bandits (axes and crossbows) who stay close to their camp. Every camp flies his
**war banner**, black with a red skull. Take all three banners: chat counts them ("A war banner of the Bandit King is
taken (2 of 3)").

## The King's Fort

With his banners lost, the King comes himself. The next dawn his men start a fort 110 to 150 blocks from the village,
a stage each dawn: first a palisade of sharpened logs round a yard with a fire and tents, then two watchtowers and a
stable, then his **keep** with his black war banner over the door.

![Day 3: the keep, its postern open](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/bandit_king/still-2.jpg)

While it rises, win allies. Any of them helps:

- **A neighbor's pledge**: finish a quest at a neighboring village and 3 of its guards come for the storm.
- **Swords for hire**: bring 24 emeralds to the hall and 6 mercenaries are paid to fight.
- **The deserter**: **Pell** waits by the ashes of the first camp. Bring him 16 bread and he tells you of the
  **postern**, a small door in the palisade behind the keep, and sees that it's left open.

## The Storm

When the keep stands, raise your **Rally Banner** near the fort: your guards and your allies follow you in. Until the
King falls his raiders come every second night.

The King himself is a vindicator with a golden crown, iron armor and a diamond axe, much tougher than a chief, and a
boss bar shows his health when you're within about 30 blocks. At half health he blows a goat horn and four more
bandits rush out of the keep, but only once. His men stay inside the palisade, and the ones on the towers stay up there.

![The Bandit King falls](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/bandit_king/still-4.jpg)

His **treasure chest** sits beside his throne: emeralds, gold, diamonds, an enchanted sword or crossbow, and always
**the King's Crown**, a golden helmet with Protection III and Unbreaking III.

## How it ends

Kill him within 10 days and the village holds a festival, everyone who helped in a chapter gains 150 standing and the
honor **Kingslayer**, no bandit camp comes for 10 days, and Pell stays on in the village.

Wait too long and he takes his tribute, a third of the village's treasury, rides off with his band and leaves his fort
a ruin. The villagers are 10 less happy for 3 days.

## How it fits with the rest

- It grows out of [bandit camps](defence.md#bandit-camps), so the Bandit Camps setting and the Threat Level both
  matter: with no camps, no King.
- Your [guards](defence.md#guards), a [Rally Banner](defence.md#leading-your-guards) and mercenaries from the
  [Village Hall](village-hall.md) are the army; the treasury pays the mercenaries and loses the tribute.
- Taking the banners and storming the fort count as helping, so friends who join in share the standing and the honor.
