**Tonics** are drinks your own villagers make that put a spring in a worker's step: one sip and they work **25% faster
for 20 minutes**, a whole Minecraft day.

## Every tonic

| Tonic | For | Made by | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Miner's Brew](miners-brew.md) | Miners, Sifters, Netherworkers | Cleric | a glass bottle, glowstone dust, coal, sugar |
| [Smith's Draft](smiths-draught.md) | Armorers, Toolsmiths, Weaponsmiths, Tinkerers, Ball Smiths | Cleric | a glass bottle, blaze powder, 2 iron nuggets |
| [Scholar's Infusion](scholars-infusion.md) | Scholars, Teachers, Librarians, Cartographers, Fossil Scientists | Cleric | a glass bottle, an amethyst shard, glow berries |
| [Builder's Tea](builders-tea.md) | Builders, Carpenters, Masons, Dyers | Chef | a glass bottle, 2 sweet berries, sugar |
| [Harvest Cordial](harvest-cordial.md) | Farmers, Orchard Keepers, Florists, Beekeepers, Composters, Shepherds, Butchers, Ranchers, Chefs | Chef | a glass bottle, an apple, wheat, sugar |
| [Woodsman's Broth](woodsmans-broth.md) | Lumberjacks, Fletchers, Fishermen, Porters, Postmen | Chef | a bowl, cooked salmon, a carrot, a brown mushroom |

## How they're made

You can't craft a tonic. Your [Cleric](cleric.md) brews theirs at the brewing stand once the guards' potions are done,
fetching the makings from the chests by the stand and from the store; your [Chef](chef.md) cooks theirs once the day's
menu is made. Each keeps **4 of every tonic** in the chests by their workstation. Keep the makings in those chests and
the tonics keep coming.

## Giving one

Right-click (not sneak) a grown villager whose job the tonic suits: "Dara drinks the Miner's Brew: 25% faster for 20
minutes." Their status line shows the tonic and the minutes left. A villager it doesn't suit refuses it, and you keep
it. Tonics never stack: a second one just starts the time again.

![A Toolsmith, a Scholar, an Orchard Keeper and a Lumberjack drink theirs](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/tonics/still-4.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: the Cleric and the Chef, and the makings in their chests.
- **What they affect**: one worker's speed for a day. It shares the **Fastest Work Pace** cap with every other speed-up
  ([work pace](work-pace.md)).
- **What stops them**: the **Tonics** setting off: none are made, villagers refuse them, and a tonic drunk earlier does
  nothing.
