The Armorer is vanilla's armorer, put to work as the village's smelter, and the one who makes sure no guard goes out
without armor.

## Getting an armorer

A villager without a job takes a free **blast furnace** as an Armorer, as in vanilla. If a villager there has another
job (a [Miner](miner.md) shares the block), sneak-right-click them with **coal** or **charcoal**. Every armorer with a
chest by their blast furnace smelts for their village.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click an armorer with coal or charcoal to hire them: they then fetch ore from your own miners and
storehouse too.

## What they do

- **Smelting.** They keep the blast furnace (and any furnaces) by their workstation going with the ore in the chests
  there (raw metal and ore blocks), fed with coal or charcoal, and take the ingots out into the chests. When the chests
  run out of ore or fuel, they fetch more: from the storehouse, and from miners and other workers who don't smelt their
  own. A Novice carries three stacks of ore a trip, one more for each level.
- **Armor for guards.** A guard of the village with an empty armor slot (and nothing for it in their chest) gets a
  piece: one from the armorer's chests, or one made from iron, brought to the chests by the guard's post.

Over their head: how many ingots they've smelted, and whether they're smelting, fetching ore or fuel, or making a piece
for a guard.

## How the armorer fits in

- **Fed by:** the [Miner](miner.md)'s ore and the storehouse's coal.
- **Feeds:** iron for the [Toolsmith](toolsmith.md), the [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md), the
  [Fletcher](fletcher.md)'s crossbows and the [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md). They keep 24 iron ingots (a set of armor);
  the [Porter](porter.md) carries the rest to the storehouse. Armor for every [Guard](guard.md).
- **Stops when:** there's no chest by the blast furnace, or no ore or fuel anywhere they can reach.
