When the final of a [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md) ends, the champion's village **holds the Cup**. A player who wins
stands for a village, and that village holds it, banner and pride included; the player gets the purse and no
trainer experience.

## The title

The village holds that host's Cup until a new champion is crowned there; a Cup that isn't held (too few entrants,
called off) leaves the holder in place. The champion goes on the **roll of champions** on the Cup page, into every
circuit village's chronicle, and the holder is named:

- on its hall's name ("Holders of the Thornholm Cup, won on day 12");
- in other halls' trade-route lists;
- on its [Village Map](village-map.md) ("Holders of the Thornholm Cup").

![The roll of champions](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_champions/still-3.jpg)

## The Cup banner

A banner with a trophy on it, in the theme's colors (gold on red for the Grand Cup), flies over the holder: on both
faces of its [Arena III](arena-build.md)'s champion's pole if it has one, otherwise on top of its
[Village Hall](village-hall.md). It comes down when the title passes. A far village that wins gets its banner the next
time it's loaded. You can't make one on a loom: the banner belongs to the village. Break it, and it goes back up at the
hall's next round. A block on top of the hall is never replaced: the banner stands on the roof over it instead. A
village that holds two hosts' Cups flies one banner, and keeps it (and its pride) until it holds none.

![The banner over the new holder's hall](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_champions/still-2.jpg)

## Pride

While they hold the Cup, every villager of the holding village is happier: the mood reason "our village holds the Cup"
adds 5 ([Moods](moods.md)). Visiting delegates don't get it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Trainers**: the winning Leader (or host trainer) gets two extra win bonuses of trainer experience; one who isn't
  there gets them when their village next loads ([Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md)).
- **Next time**: the defending champion is seeded first at the host's next Cup.
- **Switched off**: with the **Festival Cup** setting off nobody holds a Cup, the banners come down at each holder's
  next round and the pride goes; they come back when it's on again.
