Pokémon pastured near a workstation already make the work go faster ([Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md)). With this
switched on, you **see** them doing it: at moments of the work one of the worker's pastured helpers walks over, does
its bit, and walks back.

## What they do

- **Builder**: a Fighting type shoulders the planks or logs from the chest and punches each block home; a Rock type
  carries the stone; a Steel type the iron parts.
- **Farmer**: a Water type waters a 3 by 3 patch of the field (the farmland really gets wet); a Grass type sparkles
  over the crops; a Ground type walks the furrow ahead of the farmer.
- **Lumberjack**: a Fighting type strikes the trunk with each chop; a Grass or Bug type plants the sapling.
- **Porter**: carries a barrel on its back on the round. **Carpenter, Mason**: holds the board or stone.
- **Postman**: a Flying type takes the air mail up and out of sight, and lands back empty-handed.
- **Armorer, Miner, Fisherman**: a Fire type breathes fire into the furnace or smoker.
- **Chef**: a Fire type fans the flames, a Normal type carries the dish to the chest.
- **Nurse**: a pink pulse over the villager being cured or the player being healed.
- **And more**: the miner's partner digs alongside, the fisherman's swims round the bobber, the scholar's and teacher's
  float a book, the beekeeper's circles the hive, the florist's sprinkles the garden, the tinkerer's sparks over the
  work, the rancher's walks beside the wild horse.
- **The Pokémon jobs**: a Bug type flits between the [Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md)'s plants, a Fire type lights the
  [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md)'s campfire, a Flying type circles the [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md)'s sighting, a
  Normal type keeps the [Daycare Keeper](daycare-keeper.md)'s pair company, a Rock type taps the
  [Gem Grower](gem-grower.md)'s cluster loose.

![The patch watered](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/partners_land/still-3.jpg)

## Careful with your Pokémon

A pastured Pokémon is never untethered: it only walks as far as its pasture lets it, and work farther away gets no show
(the speed bonus still counts). A show never touches a Pokémon in battle, ridden or on your shoulder, nor its held item,
friendship, moves or stats. What it carries is just for show, and gone when the show ends.

## How it fits with the rest

- **The speed is the same either way**: shows only change what you see. The **Partners at Work** setting off keeps the
  bonus and hides the shows.
- **The Workers' Cup**: each day a Pokémon helps a worker counts toward the Workers' Cup ([themes](cup-themes.md)).
- **Quiet when nobody's there**: no shows with no player within 48 blocks, and only a few at once.
