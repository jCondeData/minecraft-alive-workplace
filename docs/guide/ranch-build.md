A barn with a hayloft gable over two stable aisles, water troughs, a **smoker** and a chest, and a fenced paddock beside it.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Ranch, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Ranch II** doubles the paddock and adds a stable wing and a brick silo.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the smoker with a **saddle** or a **golden carrot** and they become the **Rancher**: they break in the wild horses, donkeys and llamas nearby, put on the saddles and armor you leave in the chest, and breed them. Left alone, a jobless villager takes a smoker as a **Butcher**, who looks after cows, pigs, chickens and rabbits; raw meat makes them a **Chef** instead. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a lime banner (farms, ranches and gardens).
