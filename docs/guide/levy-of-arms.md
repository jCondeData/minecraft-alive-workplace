# Levy of Arms

```infobox
type: Edict
expansion: M30, M33
Kind: a realm edict, proclaimed by the capital
Boost: every member sends relief to a raided member, wherever it is, in bands twice as big
Cost: each member pays 2 emeralds a day into the realm treasury
Reform: Arm the levy: 12 iron swords, and 3 raids beaten with relief
Reformed: the bands still ride, and no member pays dues
categories: Edicts, Defense
related: edicts.md, defence.md, crowns-peace.md
```

When one village is raided, the whole realm rides.

## What it does

A realm edict (see [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md) for how realm edicts work). While the Levy of Arms is in force,
when a member village is raided **every member sends relief, however far away it is**, and the **bands are twice as
big** (12 riders at most). Each member pays **2 emeralds a day** into the realm treasury.

## Reforming it

**Arm the levy**: bring 12 iron swords to the capital, and beat 3 raids with relief while it's in force. After that the
bands still ride and no member pays dues.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: the [guards](defence.md#guards) of every member village, and their treasuries.
- **What it affects**: [raids](defence.md#raids) on any member.
- **Good with**: [Crown's Peace](crowns-peace.md), for a realm that wants to be left alone.
