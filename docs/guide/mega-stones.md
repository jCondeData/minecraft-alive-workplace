With **Mega Showdown** on the server, your village makes its own Mega Stones and Key Stones, and a realm rewards the
player who beats all its Trainer Leaders with a Mega Bracelet.

## What the Jeweler cuts

Once your [Jeweler](jeweler.md) is a Journeyman, they cut these at their stonecutter, from what is in their chest, the
village store and the other workers' chests:

| Put in | Out comes |
|---|---|
| Key Stone Ore, 2 gold ingots, 4 emeralds | a Key Stone |
| a Mega Crystal (or the Mega Stone one drops), 2 gold ingots, 4 emeralds | a Mega Stone |
| 4 Mega Meteoroid ores of any kind, 2 gold ingots, 4 emeralds (a Master Jeweler) | a Mega Stone |

The Mega Stone is always one for a Pokémon your village's own trainers use: the Jeweler looks at the teams of the
[trainers](trainer.md) near the hall, nearest first, and cuts the stone of the first Pokémon whose stone the store
doesn't have yet. So a village with a Master who fields a Gengar gets a Gengarite, then the next one. They keep one of
each stone and two Key Stones in the store. Anything missing goes on the requests board.

## What the Miner keeps

A [Miner](miner.md) who digs into Key Stone Ore, Mega Crystals or Mega Meteoroid ores takes the Key Stones, the Mega
Stones and the ores (dug with Silk Touch) to the **village store** for the Jeweler, instead of leaving them in the chest
by the Miner's Bench.

## A Mega Bracelet of your own

Beat the [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md) of every village of a [realm](realms.md) and the capital's hall gives
you a **Mega Bracelet**, straight into your inventory. Each new Leader you beat tells you how many are left:
"Trainer Leaders of the Barony of Oakmere beaten: 1 of 3." You get one bracelet per realm.

## Settings

**Village Megas** on the settings screen turns all of this on or off, with the [Mega trainers](mega-trainers.md).
Off, the Jeweler cuts no stones, the Miner keeps everything and no bracelet is given. With Jewelers off, nothing is
cut either. Without Mega Showdown nothing changes at all.
