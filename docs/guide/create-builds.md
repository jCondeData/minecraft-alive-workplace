With the Create mod installed, the **Blueprint Table** offers nine buildings with a working Create machine inside. Each
one turns the moment your builder is done: a water wheel on the side is driven by water falling from a little tank,
and the windmill's sails start as soon as the builder gives its bearing a push.

![The first three Create builds, their machines turning](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/create_builds/still-3.jpg)

## The builds

- **Watermill**: a stone-and-timber mill. A pond behind it feeds a timber channel over the top of the wheel, which
  turns the millstone inside; a sack hoist's hood juts over the front steps, and the farmer's chests are in the
  granary beside it, his composter in the yard.
- **Windmill** (the Stage Mill, rebuilt from scratch): a dark timber tower rising out of a round brick base, a walkway
  stage round its middle and four long white sails on the cap. The millstone, the farmer's composter and his chest share
  the round room on the ground.
- **Workshop**: a timber shop between a water tower and a brick furnace stack. The tower's head tank pours onto a wheel
  that drives a belt to a depot under a mechanical press, which you can watch through the glazed gable on the street;
  the armorer's blast furnace sits at the foot of the stack.
- **Brass Foundry**: a tall brick hall with a glazed gear lantern on its copper roof and a cookhouse on its side. Its wheel is geared up fast enough for a mechanical mixer over a
  basin and a blaze burner, and a belt carries things out to a depot by the door. The chef's smoker is inside.
- **Sawmill**: a water tank on log legs pours down a chute onto the wheel. In the long saw shed a belt carries logs
  through two mechanical saws to a depot, and the Lumberjack's fletching table is in the lodge beside it.
- **Crushing Mill** (the Fall House, rebuilt from scratch): a stone hall with a glazed bay in front, so you can see the
  two crushing wheels from the road. Water falls down a tall tower behind it onto the wheel that turns them, towards each
  other, fed by a belt over the gap. What they crush drops into a pit with an item drain in front. The blast furnace and
  a chest stand under the brick chimney.
- **Greenhouse** (the Leat House, rebuilt from scratch): a ridge-and-furrow glasshouse, three glass gables to the
  street with the tall nave in the middle and a brick boiler and stack behind. The village leat runs past its west
  side in an open stone trough, turning an undershot water wheel; its shaft drives a belt along the inside of the west
  wall, under a mechanical harvester and a mechanical plough hung there like tools on a rack, to a depot in a hatch in
  the front wall. Beds of wheat, carrots and potatoes lie either side of the walk, and the farmer's composter and chest
  are at the walk's head.
- **Clock Tower**: a slender stone tower with a timber clock room at the top, a cuckoo clock on each of its four
  sides and a copper spire. Water pours from a stone spout down the tower's front corner and turns the wheel by the
  door; through a slit in the wall you can watch the drive climb to the clocks. A map room with the cartography table
  is at its foot. It's there to look at, and the clocks keep the time.
- **Pump House**: a small brick house where the wheel turns a mechanical pump. It draws from a little pool in the floor
  that fills itself up again, and pumps the water into a tank. A cauldron makes it a leatherworker's workplace.

![The six newer Create builds](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/create_builds_more/anim.gif)

## Building one

Pick it at the Blueprint Table, place the blueprint where its front should go, fill the chests near your builder's
table with what it lists, and hand it to the builder, as for any build. The moving parts go in last and in order, so
they turn as soon as they're down (see [Contraptions from a blueprint](create-contraptions.md)).

## Good to know

- Without Create, or with the **Create builds** setting off, these buildings aren't offered at the table.
- Each has a job block inside, so a villager without a job can take it and work there.
