The Nurse Station is the Nurse's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Nurse works at a **brewing stand**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a honey bottle.
The Nurse Station is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:nurse_station`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Nurse Station takes it by themselves and becomes a **Nurse**: no item needed.
Right-click the nurse with an empty hand to get your health back and bad effects cleared. Nurses look after hurt villagers and golems, and cure the village's sick.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Nurse Station to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** honey bottles, milk or healing potions in the chests, which they use on the sick.
- **With Cobblemon** they heal your team along with you.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Nurse Station or a brewing stand); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
