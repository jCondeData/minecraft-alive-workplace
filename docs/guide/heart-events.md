The better a villager knows you, the more they tell you. At **2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 hearts** of
[friendship](friendship.md), a villager has something to say. The next time you're within 8 blocks while they're off
work, they walk up, face you and tell it: three to five lines over their head, one every 3 seconds, each also in your
chat in gray, so you can read it again.

Walk away halfway and they stop; next time they start again from the first line. Told to the end, it adds 20
friendship, the village's chronicle gets a line ("Dara told Jesse about growing up in Thornholm") and it becomes part
of their **life story**.

## The five stories

- **2 hearts, Where I come from.** Born in the village, they name both parents; hired at an inn, they tell of the road;
  here since before the hall, the village before it had a name; brought back from a grave, what they remember of it.
- **4 hearts, My work.** One for each kind of work: the first wall a builder raised, the day a miner's lamp went out,
  the year the harvest failed, a guard's first raid. Someone without a trade asks what you think they should do, and
  names the free workstation nearest the hall.
- **6 hearts, What keeps me up at night.** From their life now: hunger, no bed of their own, raids, sickness in the
  house, loneliness, or, if all is well, worry about you and your travels.
- **8 hearts, The people I love.** Their partner and wedding day, the one they're courting, their children, the
  partner they lost, or "the village is my family".
- **10 hearts, What I dream of.** To be a Master, to see the Nether or the sea, a finer house, the village a City.
  Told to the end, they give you their [keepsake](keepsakes.md).

![Shift-click her in the hall: her life story](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/heart_event/still-3.jpg)

## The life story page

**Shift-click** a named villager on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s list for their life story: your hearts with
them (and whether they have something to tell you now, or at how many hearts they will), their name day, their family,
their partner, any request they have open, and the story so far, one book for each event anyone was told.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** [friendship](friendship.md): trades, quests, [gifts](gift.md).
- **What it reflects:** the village as it is. The 6-heart story changes with [food](food-and-diet.md), beds,
  [raids](raids.md) and [sickness](sickness.md); the 8-heart one with [couples and weddings](couples-and-weddings.md).
- **What stops it:** a villager busy at work, or one with a request to ask, waits. With **Heart events** off in the
  settings, nobody walks up.
