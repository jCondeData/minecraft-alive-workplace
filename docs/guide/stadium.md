An oval arena of stone round a grass battle field ruled in white, with stands of red and white seats, a trainer's box
at each end and a professor's box, a covered stand, a gateway arch, a scoreboard and four floodlight towers that light
up at night. Outside are a nurse's booth with a Healing Machine and pitches for market stalls.

Under the stands you can walk right round: a lit concourse behind the arched entrances, stairs up from it into the
front rows, two food kiosks, a first-aid room, a trophy room, and on the south side the players' tunnel out to the pitch
with the home and away dressing rooms off it. In a village with its
own colors, the seats are built in them.

## What it needs

**Cobblemon**, a **Town** and the **Pokémon Professor** (on a server without Legends, 25 pastured Pokémon of 10
different types stand in), and the Master Architect. Without Cobblemon it isn't on the Wonders page at all. See
[Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

- **Stadium Days**: every second afternoon, from noon until dusk, two to four visiting trainers stand in the two
  trainer boxes. They come from the villages of its realm (without a realm, the villages it trades with by caravan,
  either way): each sends the trainer it would send to a Cup. When those have none to send, travelling trainers of
  Journeyman to Master rank come instead. You'll read "Stadium Day at (the village): 3 visiting trainers stand in the
  boxes until dusk" in chat.
- **Challenge a visitor** as you would the village's own trainers: right-click them. A win pays CobbleDollars
  (emeralds without CobbleDollars), once a day for each visitor. There are no badges and no gym leaders: battles pay
  money only.
- **The village's trainers take them on**: from an hour after noon, one bout an hour, the village's best trainer
  first. The server can't play a battle out with no player in it, so a bout is settled at once by rank and by the two
  teams (a Master's team beats a Novice's). Both gain battle experience (the visitor's trainer at home too), and the
  result is in chat ("At the Stadium: Expert Trainer Rhea beats Journeyman Trainer the Traveler") and the chronicle.
- **The stands fill**: the villagers off work walk in and sit down to watch, and those who watched are 5 happier
  ("watched a Stadium Day") until the next one. At dusk the visitors leave by the gate.
- **The Festival Cup is held here**: among the villages taking part in a Cup, the nearest one with a Stadium hosts
  it, and the Stadium serves as its Arena (its field the ring, its boxes, its seats), so its village gets the Cup's
  fair by the gate and the crowds. A village with an Arena whose Cup circuit has a Stadium holds no Cup of its own.
- If the Stadium is **damaged** (less of it standing than `wonderStanding`), there are no Stadium Days and it hosts
  no Cup until its builder repairs it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your [Pokémon](pokemon.md) in pastures, a [Ball Smith](pokemon.md), rare things found by
  exploring (a rare worker in the village finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: battles for you and the village's [trainers](pokemon.md), and the village's
  [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md).
