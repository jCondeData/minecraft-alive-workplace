Every household starts here, and nobody ever falls below it. A **Peasant** household needs nothing and wants nothing:
it works, eats and pays the usual tax, exactly what every worker paid before classes came.

## What a Peasant can do

Peasants take any job of plain Minecraft villagers (farmer, fisherman, smith, librarian and the rest), the Builder,
Miner, Lumberjack, Porter, Guard and the other jobs no class asks for. The higher crafts need a higher class: a Peasant
offered the Scholar's job is refused ("Dara is Peasant class; Scholar needs Burgher or better"). Nobody is ever fired, though: a Peasant who
already had such a job keeps it.

Peasants wear just their job's clothes.

## Rising to Artisan

A Peasant household rises to [Artisan](artisan.md) once it has had everything an Artisan needs for two dawns running:

- a home: a building one of your builders put up, with their bed in it;
- fed from the store 3 days running, on a varied diet;
- a chapel, a school or a clinic near home;
- **Work Clothes**, from a Tailor.

The hall's Classes page lists the Peasant households closest to rising and what they still lack.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds a rise**: your [builder](builder.md) (homes, a Chapel, a School or Clinic), your
  [farmers and chefs](jobs-land.md) (a varied diet), and a Tailor.
- **What it affects**: a village of only Peasants pays the plain tax and can't fill its higher jobs.
