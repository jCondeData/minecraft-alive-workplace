The Trade Board is the Pokémon Trader's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Pokémon Trader works at a **Shop Counter**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a Poké Ball.
The Trade Board is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:trade_board`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Trade Board takes it by themselves and becomes a **Pokémon Trader**: no item needed.
Right-click the trader to see today's offers, pick one and one of your Pokémon that fits, and swap. Offers change every day; each player gets one trade per trader a day.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Trade Board to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

## How it fits in

- **Needs Cobblemon**: without it there's nothing to trade.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Trade Board or a Shop Counter); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
