An **edict** is a law for your village. Each one gives the village a boost and makes it pay for it, and each one can be
**reformed**: do a few big jobs for the village and the cost goes away for good, while the boost stays.

## The Book of Edicts

Click the lectern button on the Village Hall's screen (or sneak-right-click with a Village Ledger) to open the **Book of
Edicts**. At the top are the village's slots: one for a Hamlet, two for a Village, three for a Town and four for a City,
each showing the edict in force and its days, a free slot, or the rank that opens it. Below is every edict, its boost
in green and its cost in red.

**Click an edict twice** to proclaim it; **click one in force** to lift it. An edict stays in force at least 3 days
before it can be lifted. Everyone in the village hears of it, and the chronicle writes it down. Only the hall's owner
and their friends may proclaim or lift; anyone else can read the book. A village that drops a rank loses its newest
edict. Curfew and Open Gates can't be in force together.

## Reforms

While an edict is in force, its reform shows up, one step a day: a villager of the fitting trade asks you for it, and
the steps follow as a short [story](story-arcs.md); with nobody of the trade to ask, they go up on the hall's quest
page. Bring a few hundred of something, slay monsters round the village, or win a Pokémon battle. Finish the last step
and fireworks go up over the hall: the edict is reformed for that village, and stays reformed even if you lift it and
proclaim it again.

![Reformed: fireworks over the hall](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/reform/still-2.jpg)

## Every village edict

| Edict | Boost | Cost | Reform |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Long Shifts](long-shifts.md) | everyone works 20% faster | every grown villager is 10 less happy | [The Shift Bell](the-shift-bell.md) |
| [Free Bread](free-bread.md) | everyone fed in the last day is 10 happier | the village eats 30% more | [The Common Granary](the-common-granary.md) |
| [Large Families](large-families.md) | up to two babies a day | babies need more food; illness 50% more often | [The Midwives](the-midwives.md) |
| [Open Gates](open-gates.md) | more guests, travelers and market traders | bandits camp nearby twice as often | [The Watchful Gate](the-watchful-gate.md) |
| [Festival Season](festival-season.md) | a festival every 4 days instead of 8 | each one costs the treasury | [The Festival Fund](the-festival-fund.md) |
| [Tithe](tithe.md) | a tenth of what players pay in trades goes to the treasury | prices 10% higher | [The Fair Ledger](the-fair-ledger.md) |
| [Curfew](curfew.md) | safe nights, raids half as likely | nobody out, trading or celebrating after dusk | [The Lamplighters](the-lamplighters.md) |
| [Conscription](conscription.md) | every grown villager fights in a raid | all work stops until noon the next day | [The Militia Drill](the-militia-drill.md) |

## How it fits with the rest

Edicts touch almost everything: how fast every [job](jobs.md) works, how much food the village eats, the
[treasury](village-hall.md), [festivals and markets](festivals-and-markets.md), raids and [bandit camps](defence.md).
"What next?" on the hall says when a slot is free or a reform step waits. Whoever runs the server can switch edicts off
in the [settings](settings.md) and change how long one must stay in force.
