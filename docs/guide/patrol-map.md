A Patrol Map gives a guard a route to walk by day, instead of wandering round their post.

## Getting one

Craft a **map** and **red dye** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

1. Right-click the ground at up to eight places, in the order you want them walked. The tooltip counts the points.
   Sneak-right-click the ground to start over.
2. Sneak-right-click a guard with it. By day they walk the route, point to point; at night they stay by their post as
   always.

The map keeps its route, so you can hand the same route to the next guard. To take a guard's route away,
sneak-right-click them with a Patrol Map with no points marked.

## How it fits in

- **Close to home.** A guard walks a route within 64 blocks of their post.
