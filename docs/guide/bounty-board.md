A notice board on two posts, with a poster pinned up for every outlaw the village wants caught.

## Getting one

Craft **planks** with a **paper** in the top middle, a full row of planks under it and a **stick** in each bottom
corner. Put it by your [Village Hall](village-hall.md).

## Bounties

A village of [Village rank](ranks.md) or bigger posts a bounty now and then: at most one every 3 days, two open at
once, each up for 7 days. Players near the village are told in gold ("Thornholm posts a bounty: Grimwald the Red, last
seen 160 blocks north-east. Reward: 15 emeralds."), and villagers gossip about where he was seen.

Right-click the board (or open the hall's [quest journal](quest-journal.md)) for the **Bounties** tab, and click a
bounty to take it: you get a [Wanted Poster](wanted-poster.md) and a [map to his hideout](hideout-map.md). Every player
can take each bounty once.

![Grimwald the Red, Ironhide, and his band](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/bounty/still-3.jpg)

## The captains

Each captain has a name and a **quirk**: Ironhide (full iron armor, 20 more health), Fleetfoot (Speed II), Crossbow
Ace, Beast Rider (on a ravager), Night Stalker (invisible at night until he's hit), Warlord (a band of 5) or Hexer (an
evoker). He waits at his hideout, a bandit camp 120 to 200 blocks from the hall, with a band of 2 or 3, once you come
near.

## Claiming it

He drops a [Captain's Insignia](captains-insignia.md) when he falls. Right-click the board or the Village Hall with it,
or use the tab's hand-in button: the reward (12 emeralds, more in a bigger village) goes once, to whoever hands it in,
with +40 [standing](reputation.md). An unclaimed bounty comes down after 7 days and its band leaves in a puff of smoke.

With Cobblemon, half the posters are on a [rogue Pokémon](rogue-pokemon.md) instead.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** your village's [rank](ranks.md).
- **What it affects:** emeralds, [standing](reputation.md), and fewer bandits round your village.
- **What stops it:** a village below Village rank posts nothing. A village set [At peace](defense-page.md), or every
  village when the [Threat Level](threat-level.md) is 0 or the world is on Peaceful, posts no new bounty; ones already up
  stay until they are paid or run out. With **Bounty Board** off in the settings nothing new
  is posted; open bounties can still be taken and paid.
