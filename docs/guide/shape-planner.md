The Shape Planner draws big simple shapes as blueprints: walls, towers, domes and arches for your builders.

## Getting one

Craft a [Blank Blueprint](blank-blueprint.md) and a **compass** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

Right-click to open it. Pick a **box**, **cylinder**, **dome**, **sphere**, **cone**, **pyramid** or **arch**, its
size, solid or hollow, and a block you're carrying to build it in. Then draw it: you get a [Blueprint](blueprint.md),
and it takes a Blank Blueprint. The tooltip reminds you of the last shape you set up.

## How it fits in

- **Builders build it** like any other blueprint: stock the block you picked in the chests round their table.
- **Big jobs.** A crew of builders makes short work of a long wall.

More in [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).
