Besides buildings, the [Steward](steward.md) keeps the village's people busy: nobody stays jobless for long, and the
scholars never sit idle.

## Giving out jobs

Each morning every grown jobless villager (not a child, not a nitwit) gets a free workstation, the village's biggest
gap first:

1. a [Builder](builder.md) while the village has none;
2. a [Farmer](farmer.md) while food is short;
3. [Guards](guard.md) while guards are short;
4. a [Porter](porter.md) at a free Storehouse;
5. a [Scholar](scholar.md) while nothing is being researched;
6. then whatever free workstation is nearest.

He can pick any job a shared block offers, which the hall can't: an Orchard Keeper at the composter of a Berry Farm
that has none, say. A job with no free block left makes him propose its workplace instead
([his rules](steward-rules.md)).

In **Ask me first** the morning's jobs are one proposal ("Give 3 villagers jobs: Dara, Farmer at the composter 12
blocks east; ..."), and approving sends them off. In **Run the village** they go straight away.

![Who goes where](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/steward_jobs/still-2.jpg)

## Picking the research

When a scholar is at work and nothing is being researched, he picks the next topic by what the village lacks:

- **Fortification** after a raid in the last 7 days;
- **Medicine** with 2 or more villagers ill;
- **Green Thumb** while food is short;
- **Logistics** with the store 80% full;
- otherwise **Swift Hands**, **Hearth** and **Kinship**, in that order.

He only picks a topic that's open to the village. In Ask me first it's a proposal ("Research Medicine: 3 villagers are
ill"); either way the hall's chronicle notes it. See [Research](research.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Fed by**: free workstations near the hall, and the village's numbers (food, guards, the sick, raids).
- **Affects**: [work pace](work-pace.md) and everything the new workers make; research bonuses for the whole village.
- **Stopped by**: no free workstations (place some), **Rest** on his desk, or the **Stewards** setting off.
- Jobs he gives aren't held back by the **Workers per Village** cap, since they're given by order.
