The Weaponsmith keeps the village's gear in one piece: worn tools and armor get mended, and no guard goes without a
sword.

## Getting a weaponsmith

A villager without a job takes a free **grindstone** as a Weaponsmith, as in vanilla. If a villager there has another job
(a [Guard](guard.md) shares the block), sneak-right-click them with an **iron ingot**.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a weaponsmith with an iron ingot to hire them for your own workers and guards.

## What they do

- **Mending.** A tool, weapon or piece of armor with a quarter or more of its durability used up counts as worn. The
  weaponsmith finds worn gear in the chests by the grindstone, by the guards' posts and at the other workers, mends it
  the way an anvil does (each ingot, plank, diamond or bit of leather puts back a quarter) and takes it back where it
  was. **Drop your own worn gear in their chest** and it comes back mended.
- **Swords.** A guard of the village with no weapon and none in their chest gets a sword, iron from the storehouse or
  the smelters, else stone, brought to the chest by their post.

Over their head: how much they've mended, and what they're fetching, mending or bringing back.

## How the weaponsmith fits in

- **Fed by:** an [Armorer](armorer.md)'s ingots, the storehouse's planks and leather.
- **Feeds:** every [Guard](guard.md), and every worker whose tool is wearing thin, like the [Miner](miner.md).
- **Works beside:** the [Toolsmith](toolsmith.md), who makes new tools when there's nothing left to mend.
