The [Curfew](curfew.md) empties the streets at dusk. A Porter asks if lamps could light them instead: "The curfew keeps
us in because the streets are dark. Light them, and we'll walk home safe without one."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Talk to the porter about the curfew.
2. **The First Step.** Lanterns for every street corner: bring **192 lanterns**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **96 glowstone** for the brightest lamps.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: clear the dark of what prowls in it: **slay 32 monsters**.

Each step pays emeralds ("Lanterns! The corners won't be dark.").

## How it ends

The Curfew is **reformed** for good: raids stay half as likely and the nights safe, but trading, festivals, markets and
night work go on as before. Whoever finished it gets **two Street Lamp blueprints** to light more of the village, and
the chronicle notes the first lamplit night. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while the [Curfew](curfew.md) is in force.
- The lanterns come from your [tinkerers and smiths](jobs-crafts.md), the glowstone from a
  [Netherworker](jobs-land.md).
- Hand the Street Lamp blueprints to a [builder](builder.md) like any other.
