The paper every blueprint is drawn on.

## Getting it

Craft **paper** and **blue dye** together, anywhere in the grid: you get two.

## What it's for

- **At the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md):** **Get Blueprint** turns one into a copy of the build you picked.
- **The [Scan Tool](scan-tool.md):** crafted from one and a spyglass, and every build you save with it takes another.
- **The [Shape Planner](shape-planner.md):** crafted from one and a compass, and every shape it draws takes another.

Keep a few in your pocket when you're planning a village.
