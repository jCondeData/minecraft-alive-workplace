The Tinker's Bench is the Tinkerer's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Tinkerer works at a **smithing table**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding redstone.
The Tinker's Bench is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:tinkers_bench`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Tinker's Bench takes it by themselves and becomes a **Tinkerer**: no item needed.
They make the redstone and iron parts a builder needs (pistons, rails, hoppers, lanterns, iron doors, chains) and between jobs patch up the village's iron golems.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Tinker's Bench to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** redstone, iron and ore in the chests nearby; the bench has a little forge for ore.
- **Feeds** the builders, and keeps the stock orders on a [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s board.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Tinker's Bench or a smithing table); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
