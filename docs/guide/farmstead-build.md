A farmhouse with one bed beside a 9 by 5 field of farmland round a water channel, a scarecrow, and a **composter** with a chest for the harvest.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Farmstead, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Farmstead II** adds a barn behind the house and a second field behind the first, with a second composter.

## What it does for the village

A jobless villager takes the composter by themselves as a **Farmer**, and a village's own farmers look after the farm by their composter by themselves as soon as there's a chest by it: they harvest, replant and sow. The harvest feeds the village and the chef. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

**A home.** Its one bed is a home too. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a lime banner (farms, ranches and gardens).
