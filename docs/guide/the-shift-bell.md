[Long Shifts](long-shifts.md) makes everyone work faster, and everyone a little less happy. Before long the smithy
feels it: "We hammer from dawn to dark and nobody knows when the day ends. Give us a bell to ring the work in and out,
and we'll keep the long shifts gladly."

![The Toolsmith asks for the Shift Bell](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/reform_story/still-1.jpg)

## The story

1. **The Ask.** The Toolsmith wipes their brow and waves you over (an Armorer or Weaponsmith if there's no Toolsmith).
   Talk to them.
2. **The First Step.** Clocks for every workshop: bring **24 clocks** to the hall.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **128 gold ingots** for the bell's voice.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: **300 bread** for the workers' supper at the bell-raising.

Each step pays emeralds when you hand it in, and the villagers talk about how it's going ("Two dozen clocks! Now we'll
know the hour.").

## How it ends

Long Shifts is **reformed** for good: the village keeps working 20% faster, and nobody is unhappy about it any more.
The **Shift Bell** goes up beside the hall, a bell on two posts, and rings the work day in every morning and out every
afternoon. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

![The bell rings the work day in](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/reform_story/still-4.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Long Shifts](long-shifts.md) is in force, and only if the village has a smith to ask. Without
  one the same three steps go up on the hall's quest page instead, and whatever you hand in counts either way.
- The clocks and gold come from your [miners and smiths](jobs-crafts.md), the bread from your farmers and chefs.
- It runs beside any bigger [story](story-arcs.md) the village is living through.
