In a realm, nobody fights alone. When a member is raided, the others send guards on horseback.

## Who comes

The [capital](capital.md) always sends a band. Every other member in the same world within 1,024 blocks, with at least
two guards, sends one too. A band is one rider for every 3 guards the village has, at least 1 and at most 3, plus one per
level of the realm's **Levies** research; 6 riders at most come in all.

After a ride as long as the road (half a minute at least), they come in on saddled horses at the village's edge on
their home's side, or muster before the finished [Royal Hall](royal-hall-build.md) when it's the capital that's raided.
Each is named for their home ("Ashford's guard"), wears iron and carries a shield painted with the realm's arms. Players
in the village read "2 guards from Ashford ride in to help Brookfield!"

## In the fight

They fight as [guards](guard.md) anywhere in the raided village. When the raid is over they stay a minute more, until
dawn at the latest, then ride off. While they're away, the sending hall's guards icon says "2 guards away helping
Brookfield", and both chronicles note it.

## How it fits in

- **What sets it off:** a raid or [siege](sieges.md) on a member, or a vanilla pillager raid. Never a war in a
  [feud](feuds.md).
- **What it costs:** an emerald a rider from the [realm treasury](realm-treasury.md), paid as they set out. A member's
  band the treasury can't pay stays home; the capital's comes anyway.
- **Bigger with:** the **Levies** research and the [Levy of Arms](levy-of-arms.md) edict, which sends relief from every
  member, wherever it is, in bands twice as big.
- **From friends abroad:** [alliances](alliances.md) send riders the same way, at their own cost.
- **What stops it:** **Relief in a Raid** off in the settings screen, or **Realms** off.
