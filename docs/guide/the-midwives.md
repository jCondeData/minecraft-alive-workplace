[Large Families](large-families.md) fills the cradles faster than anyone can mind them. The Nurse looks tired and asks
for a word: "Big families are a blessing, but I can't be at every cradle. Help me set up a proper lying-in, and send word
for a midwife."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Talk to the Nurse about the families.
2. **The First Step.** Mothers need strength: bring **48 honey bottles**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: babies need warm blankets, so **192 white wool**.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: **64 golden carrots** to keep the little ones healthy.

Each step pays emeralds, and the village talks of nothing but babies ("Soft wool for the cradles.").

## How it ends

Large Families is **reformed** for good: the village still has up to two babies a day, but big families need no more
food than usual and fall ill no more often. A **midwife** comes to stay: a new villager who becomes a Nurse at
Journeyman. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Large Families](large-families.md) is in force.
- The honey comes from your [beekeepers](jobs-land.md), the wool from shepherds, the golden carrots from farmers and
  gold.
- The midwife is one more [Nurse](jobs-services.md) to look after the sick.
