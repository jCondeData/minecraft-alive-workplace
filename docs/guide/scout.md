A Scout is a [Guard](guard.md) with a spyglass: the one who goes out to find where the raiders camp, and comes back to
tell you.

## Making a scout

Sneak-right-click a guard with a **spyglass**. It goes into their other hand, as a bow makes an archer and a shield a
knight, and whatever they held there comes back to you. "Scout" shows over their head. Hand them a bow, a shield or a
healing potion and they're that kind of guard again, and you get the spyglass back.

## Scouting the lair

On the Village Hall's [Defense page](defense-page.md), click **Scout the lair** (the spyglass) while a lair stands. The
nearest free scout sets off, on foot or on their horse. Scouts only go out by day.

They walk until they're about 24 blocks from the camp, raise the spyglass, look it over for a few seconds and come
home. The raiders let them be as long as they keep more than 8 blocks off. Back home, the Defense page shows the lair's
exact place, its captain and its strength, and a [Scouting Report](scouting-report.md) waits in the chest by the
scout's grindstone.

A scout who isn't home within a day is lost, and the chronicle says so: "went to scout the lair and never came back".

## Other work

- **Trailing.** When raiders flee at dawn, a scout of the village follows them home, and the lair's place is known at
  once.
- **Lookout.** A Scout at a [Guard Post](guard-post.md) within 24 blocks of a finished
  [Lookout Tower](lookout-tower-build.md) or [Wall Tower](wall-tower-build.md) warns of a raid the evening before, as a
  guard there does, and also counts the raiders: "about 9 strong". See [Raids](raids.md).

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** a spyglass (copper and amethyst), and a guard to give it to.
- **What it leads to:** a [war party](war-party.md) marching on the lair with the Scouting Report in hand.
- **What breaks it:** sending a scout too close; the band goes for anyone within 8 blocks.
