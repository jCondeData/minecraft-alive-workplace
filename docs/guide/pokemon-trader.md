The Pokémon Trader swaps Pokémon with you: theirs for one of yours that fits what they want. They need **Cobblemon**
installed.

## Getting a Pokémon trader

Stand a villager by a **Shop Counter** (an emerald over planks and a chest) and sneak-right-click them with a **Poké
Ball**. (A bed picks the [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md), an emerald the [Shopkeeper](shopkeeper.md).) Some villages already
have a **Trade Hall**.

## Trading Pokémon

Right-click the trader to see today's offers, one card each: "my Tinkaton, level 62, for any Fighting type, level 55 or
higher", or now and then a special request for one kind of Pokémon (or its evolutions). Pick an offer and you see which
of your Pokémon would do; click one, then click again to swap. A held item comes back to you.

Offers change every day and are the same for everyone, and each player gets one trade per trader a day. Every trade
earns the trader experience: better traders have more offers and stronger Pokémon, sometimes a shiny. Stay within 8
blocks or the screen closes.

![Click again to trade](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/trader/still-3.jpg)

## How the Pokémon trader fits in

- **Shares the counter with:** the [Shopkeeper](shopkeeper.md) and the [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md): each needs a Shop
  Counter of their own.
- **Good for:** filling gaps in your team before you take on the village's [Trainers](trainer.md).

## Trading

They also trade like any villager: they sell Poké Balls, Great Balls, Ultra Balls, Exp. Candy, Rare Candy and an Ability
Capsule, and buy red apricorns.

See also [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).
