# /workplace standing

```infobox
type: Command
expansion: M31
Syntax: /workplace standing
Who: everyone
Shows: your standing, title and honors in every village
categories: Commands, Quests
related: reputation.md, command-quests.md, friendship.md
```

How every village on the server thinks of you, wherever you are.

## What it says

"Your standing:", then a line per village that knows you: "Thornholm: Hero, 340 standing", with your honors under it
("Honors: Kingslayer, Wayfinder"). If no village knows you yet, it tells you how to start: finish a village's quests,
come to its festivals and fight for it.

## How it fits with the rest

It reads the same standing as the name tag on a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen (see
[Reputation and titles](reputation.md)). With **Reputation and titles** off in the settings it says "Standing and
titles are switched off on this server."
