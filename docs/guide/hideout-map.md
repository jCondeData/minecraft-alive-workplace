A map to where the outlaw hides, handed to you with the [Wanted Poster](wanted-poster.md) when you take a bounty.

## Using it

Hold it to see the land round his hideout and a **red X** on the spot: a bandit camp 120 to 200 blocks from the
[Village Hall](village-hall.md). The camp, his own banner on a spruce pole by the fire, and his band of 2 or 3 (more for
a Warlord) are there once you come within about 96 blocks.

## How it fits with the rest

Beat him and bring his [Captain's Insignia](captains-insignia.md) back to a [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md) or the hall
for the reward. If nobody claims the bounty in 7 days, it comes down and his band leaves in a puff of smoke.
