The Undertaker's Table is the Undertaker's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Undertaker works at a **brewing stand**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a golden apple or a totem.
The Undertaker's Table is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:undertakers_table`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Undertaker's Table takes it by themselves and becomes a **Undertaker**: no item needed.
With a golden apple, a healing potion or a totem of undying in the chests, the undertaker goes to the nearest [Grave](grave.md) within 32 blocks and brings back whoever lies there.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Undertaker's Table to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** golden apples, healing potions or totems. Without one they ask for a golden apple, and the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s board shows it.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Undertaker's Table or a brewing stand); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
