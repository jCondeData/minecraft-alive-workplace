  bare tower rooms and narthex.
- Detail. OLD: clear glass, one stone color, the stray wedge. A: mossy band, louvred belfries, colored rose and apse
  glass; the checker floor pokes out under the chapel walls (back, zoomed). B: colored windows, niches, finials; one-wide
  windows read as dotted strips; the same floor leak (right, back).
