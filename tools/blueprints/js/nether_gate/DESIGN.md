# Nether Gate (`nether_gate`, `nether_gate_2`): design notes

Blind design for ROADMAP 52 (tools/blueprints/REBUILD.md). The only input was the brief,
`tools/blueprints/briefs/nether_gate.md`. Fixed by the brief: one obsidian frame, its inside's bottom-left cell at
(4, 2, 3), axis X, walked into from the front (z = 0); a netherworker workstation (the Nether Brazier) and a chest or
barrel within 8 across and 4 up of it; Level I at most 13 x 16 x 13, Level II at most 15 x 16 x 14 and >= Level I on
every axis, keeping >= 60 % of Level I's blocks.

## Three rough concepts

**A. The Blackstone Gatehouse.** Massing: a closed two-tower gatehouse (two square blackstone towers, a vaulted
passage between them), the portal at the passage's far end. Roof: hipped caps on the towers, a low gable over the
passage. Plan: passage -> portal; brazier and chests in the tower bases. Story: the village fortified the one door
the Nether can come through. *Weak:* the portal hides in a tunnel, so it doesn't read from the street; the towers
eat the plot; it is a castle gate that could be anywhere.

**B. The Ash Gate (built).** Massing: a monumental roofed gate, two blackstone piers carrying a steep crimson roof
with flared eaves (a ceremonial gate, a paifang/torii read in Nether stone), the obsidian frame recessed a block
between the piers on a two-step dais; a low timbered Provisioning Lodge against the gate's east side, its smaller
roof tucked under the gate's eave; Level II adds a tall Haul House behind the lodge and a walled nether-wart terrace
behind the gate. Roof: one steep flared gable along x over the gate (the hero, ridge y 12, horned), a 45-degree
gable over the lodge (ridge y 8, under the gate's eave), and at II a taller gable over the Haul House, its front
gable rising behind the lodge. Plan: street -> steps -> portal; the lodge opens sideways off the same dais; the
terrace is reached down steps behind the portal. Story: the village treats its way to the Nether as a threshold to
be honoured and watched: a gate you climb up to, with soul fire either side, and the netherworker's provisions
packed in the lodge beside it.

**C. The Ruined Portal Shrine.** Massing: a found, half-collapsed ring of blackstone round the frame, netherrack and
magma bleeding into the ground, a lean-to of salvaged crimson boards for the worker. Roof: none over the gate, a
lean-to over the hut. Plan: an open ruin with the hut to one side. Story: the villagers restored a ruined portal.
*Weak:* reads like the game's own ruined portals, "messy on purpose" fights the checks (blocks held up by nothing),
and there is no roofline to recognise.

**B is strongest**: the roof over a portal is a silhouette no other workshop has (a gate standing alone with a
heavy flared roof, readable from 20 blocks from every side), the frame is the build's centre instead of being
tucked inside it, and the steps make the portal a place you approach. A is the safest (a box with a door); C the
most "Nether" but the least designed.

## Named parts (feedback can point at these)

- **Dais**: the raised platform (floor y 1), a broad flight of two steps across the front, two steps down at the back,
  a step along the west edge, soul-fire pedestals at the front corners.
- **Piers**: two 2 x 3 blackstone piers (x 1-2 and 7-8, z 2-4), basalt-cornered, a gilded band, chiseled caps.
- **Frame**: the obsidian frame (x 3-6, y 1-5, z 3, gilded corners), recessed a block between the piers.
- **Frieze**: the solid head over the frame between the piers, a gilded plaque in it.
- **Eave beams**: crimson-stem beams a block proud of the piers, front and back, under the roof, soul lanterns
  hanging from their ends.
- **Gate Roof**: steep crimson gable along x, nether-brick kick at the eaves, warped barge boards, crimson-planked
  gables, horned ridge ends with soul lanterns on posts.
- **Lodge**: the netherworker's Provisioning Lodge (x 9-12, z 2-6), crimson frame and quartz infill on a basalt
  plinth, open to the dais at the front; the Nether Brazier at its back wall, a chest and a barrel beside it.
- **Haul House** (II): the store behind the lodge (x 9-13, z 6-12), taller, gable to the front over the lodge roof,
  chests and barrels inside, a door down to the terrace.
- **Wart Terrace** (II): behind the gate (x 0-8, z 7-13): two raised soul-sand beds of ripe nether wart, a basalt path
  with shroomlights set in it, a blackstone wall round it, a soul-lantern pillar at the path's end.

## Palette (60/30/10)

60 blackstone (polished blackstone bricks with cracked, blackstone in the plinth), 30 crimson (roof, stems, planks)
with quartz-brick infill on the lodge, 10 warped trim (barge boards, shutters) and gilded blackstone. Soul fire and
soul lanterns are the gate's light, shroomlights the terrace's. Darkest at the foot (basalt and blackstone), the warm
red roof on top, the pale lodge beside the black gate so each mass reads apart.
