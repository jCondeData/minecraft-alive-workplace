The Kitchen Stove is the Chef's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Chef works at a **smoker**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding raw meat, raw fish or a potato.
The Kitchen Stove is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:kitchen_stove`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Kitchen Stove takes it by themselves and becomes a **Chef**: no item needed.
With the makings in the chests and the storehouse the chef cooks bread, cookies, pies, cake, stews and everything a smoker cooks, a batch at a time.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Kitchen Stove to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).

## How it fits in

- **Feeds** the village: the chef's food is what the villagers eat.
- **A warm glow.** The stove gives off a little light.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with a pickaxe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Kitchen Stove or a smoker); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
