A stone lab under a copper roof, made for Cobblemon: inside stands Cobblemon's **Fossil Analyzer**, where a fossil scientist brings fossils back to life.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). A Journeyman **fossil scientist** sells it for 12 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Without Cobblemon the analyzer's spot stays empty.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Research Lab, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Research Lab II** adds a museum hall beside the lab, with a big skeleton of bone blocks on show.

## What it does for the village

Stand a villager by the Fossil Analyzer and sneak-right-click them with a **fossil** and they become its **Fossil Scientist**. Right-click them holding a fossil and pay 8 emeralds: they revive it while they work, and the Pokémon joins your party (or your PC). See [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a blue banner (school, library, research).
