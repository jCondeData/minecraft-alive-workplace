# M35 design note: Wonders (2.0)

Written for ROADMAP 35.1 (2026-10-11). The roadmap items 35.2 to 35.24 hold the full specs and their tests; this note
is the overview the owner reviews and the map the lanes build from. Lanes don't wait for the owner's reply; anything
he changes goes back into the items.

**In one line:** a Town or City can raise one of eight **Wonders**, a huge build in four stages that every builder in
the village works on at once, fed by goods from every job, drawn up by the Master Architect and needing a second
Legend; once finished it changes what its village (or its whole realm) can do, for as long as most of it still stands.

## What you'll see (the short version)

1. **The Wonders page.** Once the village is a Town, the hall's **What next?** names the Wonder closest to ready, and a
   new **Wonders** tab on the Village Hall opens a page listing every Wonder on the server's list: its icon, name and
   power in one line, then each requirement ticked or crossed with what's missing ("2 of 3 Chapels", "the Seer: not in
   the village"), and the cap ("Wonders: 0 of 1 for a Town"). A Wonder already going up shows its stage and how far
   along it is.
2. **Drawing up the plans.** With every line ticked, the owner (or a friend) clicks **Draw up the plans**: 32 emeralds
   are paid (from the realm's treasury if the village is in a realm, else the hall's treasury, else the player's own
   emeralds) and the player gets a **Wonder Plan**, a rolled blueprint with a gold seal. Held, it shows the whole Wonder
   as see-through blocks; sneak-right-click turns it, the style screen mirrors it. It can only go down with its centre
   inside the village and all of it within 96 blocks of the hall, never over a finished building or another Wonder;
   otherwise it says why.
3. **The Foundation Stone.** Hand the placed plan to any builder of the village: a **Foundation Stone** (carved stone
   with a brass plaque) goes down by the Wonder's front corner. The chests within 8 blocks of it are the Wonder's
   stockpile. Every player on the server is told ("Thornholm has begun the Great Cathedral") and the chronicle notes
   it. Right-click the stone for the stage, how far along it is, the stage's material list with what the chests are
   short of, and its goods.
4. **The crew.** Every builder of the village with no build of their own joins (up to 12); **Call every builder** on
   the stone brings in the rest as they finish. The lead is the Master Architect, or the crew's best builder, named on
   the stone and the hall. They work side by side in 8 × 8 columns, never building over thin air. More builders means
   more hands, not faster hands.
5. **Scaffolding.** Where a builder can't reach (a raised arm, a spire's tip, a dome's crown), they put up a column of
   vanilla scaffolding, climb it, and take it down when the stage is done. This works on every build, not only Wonders.
6. **The goods.** Each stage also asks for goods from the village's jobs (bread from the Chef, candles from the Dyer,
   books from the Librarian…). They go on the requests board, porters carry them to the stockpile first, crafters
   make what's short as stock orders, and the daily quest asks for the shortest line. Anyone can hand them in. The
   stone's screen shows "64 of 128 bread · Chef", or "no Chef in the village".
7. **The dedication.** When every good of a stage is in, the next morning (or straight away by day) the villagers off
   work gather at the stone, the Bard plays, the guards line up, the bell rings, the goods are taken and the stage
   begins. The chronicle notes each one.
8. **The finished Wonder.** Every player on the server is told; that evening the village holds a festival with a
   bigger firework show; the chronicles of the village and of every linked village record it; the Village Map gets a
   gold banner on it. Its **powers** switch on (the table below).
9. **Keeping it up.** Once a day it counts how much of it still stands. Below 90% its powers stop, the hall and the
   stone say "damaged", and the village's builders repair it before anything else. Back at 90%, the powers return.
   Copper turning green doesn't count as damage.
10. **Cancelling.** The owner or an op sneak-breaks the stone (or uses `/workplace cancel`): what's built stays, the
    plan comes back, and the goods of a stage not yet dedicated are handed back.

Each Wonder has a time-lapse scene (a crew of 10 raising it on a sped-up server, filmed in a slow orbit), which are
the 2.0 trailers, and a section in the README's **Wonders** part.

Every Wonder also needs the **Master Architect** to draw up its plans (stand-in: a Master builder). A Legend's
stand-in is used while Legends are off or `wonderLegends` is false. "Realm" means the villages linked to its village
(M33's realm; until that's on, its caravan partners), always including its own.

the Observatory: a Legend requirement is a presence check, nothing is used up.

## Technical detail

### Where it lives

A new core package `wonder/` (names are proposals; the items may rename them):

- `wonder/Wonders`: the reload listener (registered through `Platform`, like `legend/Legends`) loading
  `data/<namespace>/wonders/*.json`, the caps check, `Wonders.power(level, hall, type)` and its cache.
- `wonder/Wonder` (one loaded file), `wonder/WonderManager` (saved data, one per dimension, like `BuildSiteManager`),
  `wonder/WonderSite` (one Wonder in progress or finished), `wonder/WondersPage` (the hall page, a `ChoiceMenu`),
  `wonder/WonderPlanItem`, `wonder/FoundationStoneBlock` (and its block entity and screen), `wonder/Crew` (35.5),
  `wonder/WonderGoods` (35.7), `wonder/Dedication`, `wonder/WonderUpkeep` (35.8).
- `wonder/powers/`: one class per power type, registered by id (like `legend/Powers` for Legends).
- `build/Scaffolds` (35.6), used by `BuilderWork` when `findStandingSpot` finds nothing.
- Two new condition types in `rules/`: `Coastal` and `ModLoaded`; `LegendSettled` gains its `stand_in`.
- Drawing: `tools/blueprints/kit.py` gains `wonder_stages()`; the eight designs live in `tools/blueprints/wonders.py`;
  the test folly in the gametest resources.

Everything is core (Minecraft and our own packages). Cobblemon's part (the Stadium's data, `pastured_pokemon`'s count)
goes through the existing `compat/` extension point that `rules/PasturedPokemon` already reads
(`legend/PokemonCensus`), and the Stadium's file is loaded with `fabric:load_conditions`, as
`luxury_recipes/berry_wine_cobblemon.json` and `guilds/trainers.json` are today.

How it plugs into the code that exists:

- **Builds** are ordinary `BuildSite`s in `BuildSiteManager`, one per stage part, so `BuilderWork` raises them as it
  raises anything (CLEAR top-down, FOUNDATION, STRUCTURE bottom-up, DECORATION, LANDSCAPE). The Foundation Stone is
  their bench (`BuildSite.bench`), so `SupplyContainers` finds the stockpile with no new code. Only stage I fills a
  foundation; only the last stage levels the ground (the existing `no_level_ground` flag on the others).
- **Whole blueprint:** `BlueprintLibrary` builds `aliveworkplace:wonder/<id>` by merging the stage parts (cached) and
  hides `wonder/` from the Blueprint Table, as it hides `research/` today.
- **Crews** replace, for Wonder sites only, today's helper window (`BuilderWork.helperStep`, `Builders.MAX_HELPERS` = 3);
  other builds keep helpers exactly as now.
- **Repairs** use `build/Upkeep` and `BuildPlan.repair` per part, with a Wonder's parts first in the queue.
- **Goods** use `work/Requests` (a new request posted for a block, not a villager), `store/PorterWork`,
  `store/StockOrders` and `hall/VillageQuests`.
- **The celebration** uses `hall/Festivals`, `hall/Chronicle`, `hall/VillageMaps` and `hall/Decorations` (beauty).
- **The page**: `hall/HallPages` keeps one spare tab today, taken by the Classes page; 35.3 raises
  `HallPages.SPARES` from 1 to 2 and registers the Wonders page with `registerSpare`, so the six tabs kept for add-ons
  stay free.
- **The pace**: every speed bonus (the Library's topics) is a `work/Pace` source, so the shared 2× cap (30.2,
  `maxWorkPace`) already holds.

Rules for the format:

- `name`, `description` and stage `name`s are lang keys in `en_us.json`. `icon` is any item.
- `parts`: a structure id (`structure/wonder/<id>/stage_<n>_<part>.nbt`, each at most 48 a side) and its offset from
  the Wonder's corner, before the plan's turn and mirror. The part counts above are illustrative; 35.9 sets them.
- Goods lines: `item` or `tag`, `count`, `job` (a profession id, only to name who makes it and to post stock orders).
  `potion` (optional) narrows a potion item to one kind; added here because the roadmap's goods need potions of
  healing and strength. The Dyer is `minecraft:leatherworker` in the code (`craft/DyerWork`), and the explorers'
  maps come from `minecraft:cartographer`.
- Found lines (35.7a/b, the owner's change): `"found": true` (job optional, default the Cartographer), count 1, and
  `"helper": {"days": n, "condition": {...}}`, the rare worker who finds it in play time. The shipped lists and their
  pacing live in `tools/blueprints/wonder_goods.py` (see the wiki's Wonders page, "Found goods and rare workers").
- `crew` caps the crew below `wonderCrew` (the lower wins). `enabled: false` hides it.
- The Master Architect requirement is not written in the file: the loader adds it to every Wonder (a `legend`
  `aliveworkplace:master_architect` with stand-in a level-5 `aliveworkplace:builder`), so a server owner's Wonder
  needs it too.
- A file with a mistake (an unknown condition or power type, a missing part, a bad count) is logged with its path and
  the reason and skipped; the rest load.

### Condition types (the `rules/` toolbox)

Already in `rules/Conditions` (built for M29 and M31), used as they are: `rank_at_least` (`rank`), `research_levels`
(`count`, optional `tree`), `finished` (`blueprint` and `count`, counting any style; or `styles`), `villagers`
(`count`), `job_level` (`job`, `level` 1 to 5, `count`), `caravan_routes` (`count`), `iron_golems` (`count`),
`meal_kinds` (`count`), `pastured_pokemon` (`count`, `types`; false without Cobblemon), `not`.

Changed or new in 35.2:

| Type | Fields | Holds when |
|---|---|---|
| `legend` (changed) | `id`, new optional `stand_in` (a list of conditions) | the Legend has settled in the village. With `stand_in`, while Legends are off (`legends`, or M29's gate) or `wonderLegends` is false, the stand-in list holds instead (all of it). Today's `legend` files read exactly as before. |
| `coastal` (new) | none | an ocean or beach biome within 48 blocks of the hall. It reuses M32's `threat.Conditions.coastToday` (a 16-block grid, looked at once a day per hall), so the Lighthouse and drowned pirates agree on what a coast is. |
| `mod_loaded` (new) | `mod` | that mod is installed (asked through `Platform`, since core can't call the loader). The existing `cobblemon` type stays. |

Each new type gets a true and a false GameTest and a lang line `rule.aliveworkplace.<type>` for the page.

### Power types (`wonder/powers/`)

The rest of the mod asks `Wonders.power(level, hall, type)`, which returns the power's numbers when a finished,
undamaged Wonder of that village (or, for a realm power, of a linked village) has it, else nothing. The cache is
rebuilt when a Wonder is finished, damaged or repaired, and on load.

| Type | Numbers | Read by | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mood` | `scope` (`village` or `realm`), `amount`, optional `home_amount` | `people/Moods` (shown among the reasons) | Colossus; generic, for server owners' Wonders |
| `beauty` | `points` | `hall/Decorations.beauty` | Cathedral, Colossus; generic |
| `undertakers` | `search`, `revive_by` | `grave/UndertakerWork` | Cathedral |
| `weddings` | `mood`, `days`, `gifted_factor` | `people/Couples` | Cathedral |
| `caravan_range` | `factor` (2) | `hall/Caravans.RANGE` | Lighthouse |
| `caravan_speed` | `factor` (0.5) | `Caravans.travelTicks` | Lighthouse |
| `ferry_fares` | `factor` (0.5) | `travel/TravelNetwork.fare` | Lighthouse |
| `early_warning` | none | `guard/VillageRaids`, M32's pirates | Lighthouse |
| `research_tier` | `tier` (2) | `research/Research` | Great Library |
| `raid_barrier` | `margin_degrees` (10) | `guard/VillageRaids.gatheringPoint`, `guard/BanditCamps`, `threat/Ladders` | Great Wall |
| `sentries` | `count` (4), `range` (16) | `wonder/powers/Sentries` | Great Wall |
| `sky_chart` | `size` (6,144; 35.18a) | `hall/VillageMaps`, `explore/Explorers` | Observatory |
| `forecast` | none | a new hall page | Observatory |
| `harvest` | `scope` (`realm`), `extra_every` (4) | farm and orchard work | Hanging Gardens |
| `births` | `meals` (12), `per_day` (2) | `hall/VillageGrowth` | Hanging Gardens |
| `travellers` | `factor` (2), `expert_factor` (2) | `inn/Innkeepers` | Colossus |
| `stadium_days` | `every_days` (2), `visitors` (2 to 4) | `trainer/Trainers` (compat) | Stadium |
| `tournament_host` | none | `cup/` (the Festival Cup) | Stadium |

A server owner's own Wonder can use any type; `mood` and `beauty` are the two made for that.

### Config switches (`config/aliveworkplace.json`, in the Mod Menu screen unless noted)

**The expansion gate.** M35 gets its own flag in `Expansions`, `public static final boolean M35 = false;`, with a
comment listing its switches, and `Expansions.milestoneOf` gains a case mapping all eight switches below to `M35`.
The boolean switches default to `Expansions.on(Expansions.M35)` in `WorkplaceConfig`, and `WorkplaceConfig.apply()`
ANDs each with the flag (`Wonders.ENABLED = wonders && Expansions.on(Expansions.M35)`, and the same for
`wonderLegends` and `builderScaffolding`), exactly as M29 to M34 do. Until the release that finishes 35.24 flips the
flag, every switch is off and hidden whatever the file says: no Wonders tab, no plans, no scaffolding; GameTests and the
showcase client open the gate (`Expansions.openForTests`). A missing value takes its default; no `configVersion` bump.

| Switch | Default | Range | Off means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wonders` | true (behind the gate) | | no Wonders tab, no plans, no new Wonders; finished ones keep their blocks, their powers stop, and everything stays saved for when it's on again |
| `wonderCaps` | [0, 0, 1, 2] (Hamlet, Village, Town, City) | each 0 to 8 | in the file only, like `mythicLegendCap`; a short list is filled from the defaults |
| `wonderLegends` | true (behind the gate) | | each Wonder's Legends are replaced by their stand-ins |
| `wonderServerLimit` | 0 (no limit) | 0 to 64 | how many villages on the server may each build the same kind of Wonder |
| `disabledWonders` | [] | Wonder ids | in the file only, like `disabledArcs`: those Wonders are off the page and can't be started; one already finished keeps its blocks and powers |
| `wonderCrew` | 12 | 1 to 32 | most builders on one Wonder |
| `builderScaffolding` | true (behind the gate) | | builders skip a step with nowhere to stand, as today |
| `wonderStanding` | 90 | 50 to 100 | the share (%) of a Wonder that must stand for its powers |

`/workplace wonders` (every Wonder on the server with its village, owner and stage) answers "Wonders are off" while
`wonders` is.

Not saved: the crew's 8 × 8 columns (worked out again from the part each time its `BuildPlan` is made, which is the
same every time), `Wonders.power`'s cache (rebuilt on load), which crew member works which column (each takes the
nearest free one again after a reload). A Wonder's parts are only touched through their own `BuildSite`s, so
`BuilderWork` stays restartable at any tick: a stage starts only when every part of the one before is `DONE` and its
goods are dedicated, which is read from the saved data, not remembered.

A world saved before M35 has no `aliveworkplace_wonders` file and no `wonder` or `scaffolds` fields; it loads and saves
unchanged (35.4's test).

### Performance budget

- The Wonders page and What next? read each condition at most once a hall round (`VillageNeeds.CHECK_EVERY`, 600 ticks)
  and cache the lines; `coastal` is once a day per hall.
- Upkeep counts at most 4,096 blocks a tick, only while the Wonder's chunks are loaded; a Cathedral is counted in about
  4 ticks, once a day.
- A 12-builder crew must stay within 25.2's benchmark targets (`PERF=true`, 35.5's check). Each member asks for a column
  only when theirs is empty, nearest-first over at most a few hundred columns.
- The Sky Chart searches one structure kind an in-game hour, and only while the server's tick has room. At 6,144
  blocks (35.18a) a kind is 144 regions of 32 chunks: one region every 5 ticks while the average tick is under 40 ms
  (720 ticks a kind), never a chunk loaded; the chart's 16,384 biome samples are the same at any size.

### As built in 35.2 (differences from the above)

- **`WonderManager` and `WonderSite` exist from 35.2**, small: `id`, `wonder`, `hall`, `owner`/`ownerName`, `stage`,
  `begun`, `finished`, since the caps and `/workplace wonders` need them. 35.4 adds the placement, stone, parts and the
  rest of the table above to the same record (each new field optional).
- **A "missing part" isn't checked at load**: the parts' structures arrive with 35.3 and the Wonders' own items, so the
  loader checks that each part is a valid id with a 3-number offset; whether the structure exists is 35.3's check.
- **`enabled: false` skips the file quietly** (as tonics do), so a pack can switch one of ours off with just
  `{"enabled": false}`; `fabric:load_conditions` are read with `Guilds.conditionsMet`.
- **A `legend` also stands in when the Legend's file isn't loaded** (a pack removed it), not only while Legends or
  `wonderLegends` are off.
- **Only 35.2's five switches exist** (`wonders`, `wonderCaps`, `wonderLegends`, `wonderServerLimit`,
  `disabledWonders`); `Expansions.milestoneOf` already maps all eight to M35, so the later three only need their field.
- **A bare name in `disabledWonders` is ours** (`great_cathedral` means `aliveworkplace:great_cathedral`).
- **The caps are checked in this order**: switched off, unknown or disabled, the same kind, one going up, the rank cap,
  the server limit; then the requirements (`Wonders.startRefusal`). The rank cap counts finished Wonders and the one
  going up.
