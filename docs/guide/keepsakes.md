When a villager tells you their last story, what they dream of, they give you something of theirs: a **keepsake**,
named after them, with a line of what it was to them and "A keepsake from Dara of Thornholm". With a full inventory it
lands at your feet. Each villager gives each player theirs once.

## What they give

It goes by their kind of work:

| Their work | Keepsake | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| building and crafting | Trowel | an iron shovel, Efficiency II |
| mining and smithing | Lucky Pick | an iron pickaxe, Fortune I |
| the land | Grandmother's Seeds | 4 torchflower seeds |
| animals and water | Old Rod | a fishing rod, Luck of the Sea II |
| the kitchen | Secret Recipe | a signed book with their pumpkin pie recipe, and 2 pumpkin pies |
| learning | Annotated Atlas | an enchanted book of Mending |
| healing | Remedy | a golden apple |
| arms | Old Shield | a shield, Unbreaking II |
| trade and travel | Spyglass | a spyglass |
| music | Favorite Record | the music disc Otherside |
| Pokémon (with Cobblemon) | First Poké Ball | a Premier Ball |
| no trade yet | Pressed Flower | a cornflower |

## How it fits with the rest

A keepsake is the end of a long [friendship](friendship.md): ten hearts, built from trades, quests and
[gifts](gift.md). They're ordinary items underneath, so the pick digs and the shield blocks.
