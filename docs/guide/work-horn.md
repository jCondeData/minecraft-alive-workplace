When the work has to be done **now**, blow the **Work Horn**. The village answers with a rush.

## Calling a rush

Hold right-click with the horn anywhere in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md); after a moment it sounds,
like a goat horn but lower. "Thornholm answers the horn: 12 villagers rush to work!" For **5 minutes** every grown
villager of the village works **50% faster**, with sparks flying over the busy workers now and then.

![The rush: 50% faster, sparks over the builders](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/work_horn/still-3.jpg)

## The cost

When the rush is over, everyone who answered is **worn out**: 10 less happy until the next dawn. A village answers the
horn **once a day**; blow it again and you're told "The village has already answered the horn today."

In a village someone owns, only the owner, their friends and operators can call a rush. Anyone can in a village nobody
owns. The [Book of Edicts](edicts.md)' last row shows the horn: ready, rushing (minutes left), or used today.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a goat horn (goats ram them off rocks), gold and an emerald.
- **What it affects**: every job's speed for 5 minutes, then the village's [moods](moods.md). The rush shares the
  **Fastest Work Pace** cap with every other speed-up ([work pace](work-pace.md)), such as [Long Shifts](long-shifts.md).
- **What stops it**: away from a Village Hall nothing answers ("No village hears the horn here"); with the **Work Horns**
  setting off the horn only sounds.
