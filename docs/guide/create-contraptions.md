With the Create mod installed, a builder can build a working **contraption**: a blueprint with Create's moving parts in
it, such as a water wheel, shafts, gearboxes, cogwheels and belts. It can be one of the village's own Create builds or a
machine you built yourself and saved as a blueprint.

## How it goes

You hand over the blueprint as for any build. The builder puts up everything that holds the machine up first: the
floor, the walls, the frames, the wheel. Then come the shafts and gearboxes, then the cogwheels, and the belts last, so
nothing goes in before what it sits on or what drives it.

![The builder at work on the contraption](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/create_contraption/still-2.jpg)

Every part keeps the way it faces and turns, as in the blueprint, turned with the building if you placed it turned.
A belt keeps its length and finds its pulleys in the new place, so it runs as soon as it's down.

**Broke a part while they were building?** If a shaft or cogwheel the builder already put in goes missing, they come
back and put it in again before they call the build done (put a spare in the chest).

## Does it turn?

A few seconds after the build is finished, the builder checks every moving part, and you get a line in chat:

- "Bob checked the Drive Line: all 8 moving parts turn." Everything works.
- "Bob checked the Drive Line: 1 part doesn't turn: Shaft at 12 64 -30." Something isn't joined up, or has nothing to
  drive it; the line names up to four parts and where they are.

If the village has a Mechanic near the build, the line comes from the Mechanic.

## Good to know

- Without Create, or with the **Create builds** setting off, buildings are built the usual way, layer by layer, and
  nothing is checked.
- The water for a water wheel is poured last, as for every build, so the wheel starts turning once it's done.
