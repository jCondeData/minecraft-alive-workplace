A man-made mountain of four mud-brick terraces, each a vaulted hall under a garden. They step back toward the rear, so
from the front the gardens fall in green tiers hung with leaves and vines, while the back rises as a sheer brick cliff.
A grand stair climbs from the forecourt between two reflecting pools, and a stair up each terrace's face leads past a
farm on every level to the crown, where the Water Pavilion stands over the cistern that a shaft of water fills from the
vaults below. Inside are the Vaults (a nursery of young trees and a seed store), the Gallery's orangery and the Upper
Hall. Hanging Gardens your village began before this design keep building, and are repaired, as they were first drawn.

## What it needs

A **Town** and the **Grand Chef** (on a server without Legends, a Master Chef and 8 kinds of meal in the store stand
in), and the Master Architect. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

- **Farmers and orchard keepers harvest more**, a quarter more, in the village and across its realm (with realms
  off, in the villages whose caravans trade with it): one more of each crop for every four a worker picks, so 40 wheat
  cut are 50 in the chest. The crops don't grow any faster.
- **The village grows faster**: a baby needs 12 meals in the store instead of 16, and up to two are born a day when
  there are free beds.
- **Its terraces are village farms**: a farmer who takes a terrace's composter looks after its fields by themselves.

A damaged Garden (more than a tenth of it missing) does none of this until its builders repair it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a Master [Chef](jobs-crafts.md) with a full larder, rare things found by exploring (a rare worker
  in the village finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: every [farm and orchard](jobs-land.md), the village store, and how fast the village grows (see
  [Village life](village-life.md)).
- Its terraces hold four village farms of their own, with a composter, a chest and a field on each level.
