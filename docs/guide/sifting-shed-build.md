Rebuilt from scratch as "the Tipple": a timber hopper loft standing on dark oak legs over a stone-walled screen
room, under a steep slate gable with a hoist beam and its lantern at the top. Gravel is tipped from the loft through a
hatch and a funnel into the **cauldron** below, where the Sifter works; the gravel and sand heaps lie in board bays under
a lean-to on the east side, and sieves hang drying on a rail on the west. Buildings already standing keep their old
drawing until you refresh them.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Sifting Shed, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Sifting Shed II** adds a covered incline gallery climbing from the back yard up into the loft, a second cauldron and chest in its shelter (through a back door from the screen room), and heaps of red sand and soul sand in board bays either side of its foot.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the cauldron with **gravel**, **sand**, **red sand** or **soul sand** and they become the **Sifter** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a cauldron as a leatherworker, the village's dyer). Put those in the chest and they sift out flint, nuggets, clay, seeds, quartz and now and then an emerald or a diamond. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a lime banner (farms, ranches and gardens).
