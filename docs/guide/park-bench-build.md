A spruce bench between two little hedges of oak leaves, with a lantern behind it. Tuck one along a path or by the
square and the village feels like a place people live.

## Building it

Take a Park Bench blueprint from the Blueprint Table and place it facing the path. It needs only a dozen blocks. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Beauty.** A finished Park Bench within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach adds 1 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md).
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **Light.** Its lantern counts as light by any bed close to it, which helps the village's [needs](village-needs.md).
- **The village map.** Benches are too small for a mark on the [village map](village-map.md).
