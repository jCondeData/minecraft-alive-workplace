The Steward is the village's planner. He reads its [City Plan](city-plan.md), works out what the village is short of,
finds a plot for it in the right zone and has the builders build it. Leave him to it and a village grows by itself:
homes when beds run short, a farm when food does, a school when children come, roads between it all and a wall once
raiders show up.

## Appointing him

A Steward has to be a builder who has been at it a while: a **Builder of Journeyman level or higher** (about 350 blocks
of steady work). Bring him beside the village's [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and sneak-right-click him with the
hall's **City Plan**. He's told he is "now the Steward of" the village and changes into a clerk's long coat.

- One Steward per hall. Another villager can't take a hall that already has one.
- Only a grown villager will do, and only at his own hall: the plan of one village appoints nobody at another.
- A younger builder is turned down with a note saying the level he still needs.
- Breaking the hall ends the job.

## His day

Each morning he walks his **rounds** with the plan in his hands: up to six stops (his builds going up, the empty zones,
the storehouse), a few seconds at each, then back to the hall. There he plans until evening. The line over his head
says what he wants and why: "Planning a Stone House: 3 villagers have no bed".

He levels up like any worker: from every build of his that's finished, every villager he gives a job, and his trades.
His level sets how many of his builds may be open at once (1, 2, 2, 3, 4 from Novice to Master), never more than the
village's rank allows (a Hamlet 1, a Village 2, a Town 3, a City 4) nor the server's **Steward's open builds** setting.

## What he plans

- **Buildings**: homes, workshops for jobs that have none, farms, a storehouse, a clinic, a school, decorations. Each
  idea comes from one of [his rules](steward-rules.md) and goes on [his desk](stewards-desk.md) as a proposal.
- **Jobs and research**: every morning the jobless get work, and idle scholars a topic
  ([Jobs and research](steward-jobs-and-research.md)).
- **[Roads](roads.md)** with lamps, [bridges](bridges.md) and steps, and [roads to the villages you trade
  with](roads-between-villages.md).
- **[A wall](walls.md)** once the village has been raided or bandits camp nearby.
- **[Old houses renewed](old-house-renewal.md)** one at a time, in zones where you allow it.

## Safe by design

Everything he builds stays inside his own village's zones, never in a Keep Clear zone, never in another owner's
protected village, and never through a place where a player placed or broke blocks unless the hall's owner approved
that build by hand. His builders only clear natural blocks: if you put a block in the way after a build started, it
stays and the desk tells you "A player's block is in the way". See [Village protection](village-protection.md).

## Trading

Right-click him to trade, like any villager. A Novice buys 24 paper for an emerald and sells two
[Blank Blueprints](blank-blueprint.md) for one; an Apprentice buys 4 books for an emerald and sells a
[Village Ledger](village-ledger.md) for 4; a Journeyman sells a City Plan for 6; an Expert pays 2 emeralds for a book
and quill; a Master sells eight Blank Blueprints for 3.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Feeds him**: the village's numbers, the same ones the hall's **What next?** tips use, so the two always agree.
  [Builders](builder.md) do the actual building, so with no builder nothing goes up.
- **What he changes**: what gets built, where, and in which style; who works which job; what the scholars
  [research](research.md).
- **What stops him**: the **Stewards** setting off (he stands idle), **Rest** on his desk, no Blueprint Table within
  reach of a plot, or two of his builds waiting a whole day for materials (he proposes nothing new until they're
  supplied, and posts a shopping list).
- Settings: [Steward settings](settings-steward.md). The rules he follows: `/workplace steward explain`
  ([the command](command-steward-explain.md)).
