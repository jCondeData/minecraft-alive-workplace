A Price Tag puts a price in CobbleDollars on something you sell at your shop.

## Getting it

Craft **paper**, a **gold nugget** and **string** together, anywhere in the grid: you get four.

## Using it

Right-click it to set a price in CobbleDollars, or rename it in an anvil to a number (like 250). Then put it in a
[Shop Counter](shop-counter.md)'s price row, under the item it's the price of. The tooltip shows the price it holds.

## How it fits in

- **Made for CobbleDollars.** Without CobbleDollars installed, the price is paid in emeralds.
- **Your takings** go where all the shop's takings go: the chests round the counter, or with CobbleDollars straight to
  you.
