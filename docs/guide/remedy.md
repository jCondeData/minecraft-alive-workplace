The Remedy is the one cure that works on everything: ordinary illness, a witch's hex and the plague.

## Making one

Craft it from a **glass bottle**, a **glistering melon slice**, a **honey bottle** and a **fermented spider eye**, laid
anywhere in the crafting grid. A [Physician](physician.md) brews them the same way from those four things left in the
chest by their brewing stand, and sells them too.

## Using it

- **On a villager:** right-click them with it. Any illness goes, whatever it was, and they stay well for **ten days**:
  no illness, hex or plague can touch them in that time. It works on a villager who is well, too, so you can keep your
  best workers safe before a plague reaches them.
- **On yourself:** drink it. It takes away poison, wither, hunger and nausea, and the glass bottle comes back to you.

## How it fits in

- **What it doesn't:** the Gray Cough of [the Sickness](the-sickness.md). A Remedy only eases it for a day; only the
  Three-Herb Tonic ends it.
- **What it cures:** the [plague](plague.md), which only a Remedy cures; a [witch coven](witch-coven.md)'s hex, which
  otherwise lasts until the coven is broken; and ordinary [sickness](sickness.md).
- **Who uses it:** the [Physician](physician.md) carries one from house to house in a plague. Keep the chest by their
  stand stocked and they cure people instead of only sending them home.
- **What feeds it:** honey from the [Beekeeper](beekeeper.md), glistering melon from a melon farm and a little gold,
  and spider eyes.
