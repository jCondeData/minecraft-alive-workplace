Neighbors don't always get along. A feud is a village's way of saying so: no more trade, worse prices, and on a server
that allows it, war.

## Declaring a feud

On the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s Pacts tab, **Declare a feud on X** (an iron sword) stands next to every other
player's village within 2,048 blocks. If that village is in a [realm](realms.md), the feud is with the whole realm; if
yours is, your whole realm is on your side. The other owner gets a letter.

## What a feud does

- Every [trade pact](trade-pacts.md), [alliance](alliances.md) and offer between the two sides ends, and so do the
  caravan routes. No new route or pact can be made while it stands.
- Each side's [price board](price-board.md) pays the rivals 25% less for their goods and charges them 25% more. The
  Prices tab tells them why.
- Villagers grumble: "I won't buy a thing from Thornholm's people."

## Peace

Either side can **Offer peace** (a white banner). The other owner gets a letter and accepts on their tab, and the feud
ends. A feud without a war fades by itself after 7 days.

## War

On a server that allows PvP, the side that declared may **Raise the feud to war** once it has stood a day. The war starts
at the next dawn and lasts 3 days, or until peace is accepted. During it:

- each side's guards treat the other side's owners and their friends as foes inside their own village, within 64 blocks
  of its hall, and go for them;
- those players may fight the enemy village's guards, even in a protected village;
- nothing else changes: blocks, chests, villagers, golems, animals and the treasury stay protected.

When it ends, both chronicles record the guards each side lost. No treasury changes hands.

![In the war, Thornholm's guards fight Ashford's owner](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/feud/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits in

- **Who fights:** your [guards](guard.md). [Relief riders](relief-riders.md) and allies answer raids only, never a war.
- **What stops it:** the [Crown's Peace](crowns-peace.md) edict, under which no feud can be declared on a realm's
  member. **Feuds** off in the settings screen: none, and existing feuds end quietly. **Feud Wars** off, or PvP off
  on the server: no wars, and the war button is grayed out and says why.
