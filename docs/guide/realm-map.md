The Realm Map is your whole realm on one sheet: every member, every road between them, your friends and your rivals.

## Drawing it

On the Realm tab of any member's [Village Hall](village-hall.md) (behind the minecart), click **Draw the Realm Map** (a
filled map) with an empty map in your inventory. It becomes **Map of the County of Thornholm**, named for your
[realm](realms.md). Without an empty map nothing is drawn, and a village that isn't in a realm has no button.

## What's on it

- a **banner for every member** in the realm's color (the base color of its arms; white without arms), the capital's
  marked "(capital)";
- [colonies](colonies.md) a member founded that aren't in the realm, in **lime**;
- [allies](alliances.md) in **light blue** and rivals in a [feud](feuds.md) in **red**, each named;
- every member's caravan routes as **dotted lines** between the halls;
- a colony whose settlers are on the road as a **red cross** where they're heading;
- land the server has loaded drawn as on a vanilla map, the rest as plain parchment.

The tooltip has the legend, as the [village map](village-map.md) does.

![The Realm Map in an item frame](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/realm_map/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits in

- **What changes it:** draw a fresh one as the realm grows; a map once drawn stays as it was.
- **What stops it:** **Realm Map** off in the settings screen takes the button away; maps already drawn stay.
  **Realms** off hides the whole Realm tab.
