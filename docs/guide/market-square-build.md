The heart of a busy village, rebuilt from scratch as **the Market House**: a paved square with a rosette in its
middle and flowering azaleas in its front corners, and across the back a timber-framed Market Court standing on an open
stone undercroft, jettied out on corbels under a steep brick-tile roof with a cross gable and a lantern cupola. A little
balcony looks out over the middle of the square, where the market is opened; an outside stair climbs the east side to
the court's door, and a striped stall with produce on its counter stands on the west. Under the court the undercroft is
the covered trading floor, with benches and hanging lanterns; upstairs the Market Court holds the reeve's tables, the
cupboards of standard weights and a desk. It's the biggest decoration and the one that brings traders to town.

Market Squares already standing keep their old look (the fountain, kiosks and bell) until you refresh them.

## Building it

Take a Market Square blueprint from the Blueprint Table and place it in the middle of the village. It needs about 750
blocks, mostly stone, bricks, dark oak and white concrete for the plaster, plus glass panes, spruce, lanterns, a little
red and yellow wool and a few flowers. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Market days.** A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and a finished Market Square holds a
  [market](market-days.md) once a week: traveling traders come to the square in the morning and stay until nightfall.
  No square, no market.
- **Beauty.** It adds 5 to the village's [beauty](decorations.md), the most of any decoration.
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **No bell of its own.** The rebuilt square has no bell block (the market's bell is only a sound now), so festivals,
  and [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md) in a village with no Chapel, gather at the village's own bell. An old square
  keeps its bell until it's refreshed.
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) with the stores and shops.
- **Turned off.** With [Market Days](settings-festivals-and-market-days.md) switched off, the square is still a
  decoration but no traders come.
