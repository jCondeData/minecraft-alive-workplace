A village that has never been attacked doesn't want a wall. Once raiders come, the [Steward](steward.md) proposes one.

## When he asks

After a [raid](raids.md) in the last 7 days, or with a [bandit camp](bandit-camps.md) preying on the village, the desk
gets a proposal: "A Palisade wall: The village was raided in the last 7 days, or bandits camp nearby", with how many
segments, towers and gates it takes and how far round it goes.

It follows the **wall line** you drew on the [City Plan](city-plan.md) (open or closed). With no line drawn he draws
his own, 4 blocks outside the zones, and shows it on the plan first, so you see what he means before you approve it.

## What gets built

- **A Hamlet or a Village** gets a palisade: [Palisade](palisade-build.md) segments,
  [Palisade Towers](palisade-tower-build.md) and [Palisade Gates](palisade-gate-build.md).
- **A Town or a City** gets stone: [Stone Wall](stone-wall-build.md) segments, [Wall Towers](wall-tower-build.md)
  and a [Gatehouse](gatehouse-build.md). A Town replaces its palisade with stone, a piece at a time.

There's a tower at every corner and at least every 28 blocks, whole segments between them, and a gate wherever a road
on the plan crosses the line. Each piece sits at its own ground height. The builders put up 3 pieces at a time, and
the chronicle notes the wall begun and finished.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Keeps raiders out**: gates shut at night, as every gate does; the [guards](guard.md) still patrol inside. See
  [Defense](defence.md).
- **Counts once**: the whole wall is one building for the village's [rank](ranks.md), not one per piece.
- **Stopped by**: the **Stewards Build Walls** setting off (he never proposes one), a village never raided, or
  declining the proposal (back in 3 days if raids continue).
- Leaves players' builds alone, like everything he builds.
