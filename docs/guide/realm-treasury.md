A [realm](realms.md) keeps its own purse, apart from each village's [treasury](treasury.md). It's what the realm spends
on the things no single village could.

## Seeing it

On the Realm tab, the emerald block beside the arms shows what the treasury holds of its cap (to the cent, out of 256
emeralds for a Barony), the levy, what it pays for, and the last 20 movements, newest first, each with its day and
reason: a levy from Ashford in green, realm research paid in red.

## Filling it

- **The levy** (the hopper) takes a share of each member's daily takings before the rest goes into the village's own
  treasury. At the capital, its ruler clicks it to step through 0, 5, 10 and 20%, and every member's owner online is
  told. Ten workers in a well-kept Hamlet take in 3 emeralds a day, so at 10% the realm gets a
  tenth of that, and the village keeps the rest.
- **Gifts:** anyone may click the treasury with emeralds to give one, or shift-click to give the stack. With
  CobbleDollars, a click with an empty hand gives 100 CobbleDollars (shift-click: 6,400).

## Taking from it

Only the capital's owner, their friends and operators may take: right-click for an emerald, shift-right-click for a
stack (in CobbleDollars with that mod).

## The cap

| Rank | Holds at most |
|---|---|
| Barony | 256 emeralds |
| County | 512 |
| Duchy | 1,024 |
| Kingdom | 2,048 |

A finished [Royal Hall](royal-hall-build.md) within 32 blocks of the capital's hall doubles it; a Royal Hall II triples
it. A levy that doesn't fit stays with the village.

## How it fits in

- **What it pays for:** [realm research](realm-research.md) (8 emeralds a level), [relief riders](relief-riders.md) (an
  emerald a rider), and the upkeep of realm edicts such as the [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md).
- **What runs it dry:** a treasury short of an edict's upkeep lets the edict lapse; a member's relief band the treasury
  can't pay stays home (the capital's comes anyway).
- **Raising the levy:** the [Common Market](common-market.md) edict puts the levy 5 points higher.
