The Firewarden is the villager who runs toward the smoke. When a roof catches, they're there with a bucket before the
flames reach the next house.

## Getting a Firewarden

Stand a villager by a **grindstone** and sneak-right-click them with a **water bucket**. The grindstone is shared: a
sword there still makes a [Guard](guard.md) and an iron ingot a [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md). The
[Fire Station](fire-station-build.md) is built around a Firewarden's grindstone, so it's the natural place for one.

## On duty

A Firewarden works at any hour. When a [fire](fire.md) starts in the village, they run to the nearest flames within 48
blocks of their grindstone and throw a bucket over them: everything burning within two blocks goes out in a hiss of
steam. They carry **two buckets**, and when both are empty they fill them again at the nearest water source or water
cauldron within 16 blocks of the grindstone. Dig them a little pond beside their post, and they're never long away.
Over their head you can read how many buckets are full and whether they're putting out a fire.

Every bucket earns them experience, a little more for each flame it put out. From **Journeyman** a Firewarden rings the
village bell when a fire starts, and two villagers who aren't at work run over with buckets of their own: a **bucket
brigade**. Only those two answer the bell, so the rest of the village doesn't run and hide.

When the last flame is out, the chronicle names who put it out: "Rowan the Firewarden put it out", or the bucket
brigade.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** water near the grindstone. A Firewarden with no pond or water cauldron within reach fights one
  fire with two buckets and then walks home dry.
- **What it answers:** the [village fire](fire.md), lit by lightning, a chimney spark in a long dry spell or a
  [drought](drought.md), and the burning arrows of [drowned pirates](drowned-pirates.md).
- **Helped by:** Water-type Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the grindstone (with Cobblemon). They douse fires in reach
  themselves, a splash every two seconds.
- **After the fire:** your [builders](builder.md) put back what burned on their next round of the village.
- **The hall knows:** a wooden village without a Firewarden gets a tip in **What next?** to build the Fire Station.
- **What stops it:** **Firewardens** off in the settings screen. Firewardens already hired then stand idle, and the
  grindstone no longer offers the job.

## Trading

Firewardens buy empty buckets and sell a water bucket. Higher up they trade in clay, a campfire, kelp and bricks, and at
the top a potion of fire resistance and a lightning rod.
