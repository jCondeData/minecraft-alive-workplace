The Habitat Keeper looks after the wild Pokémon round the village: she sets out snacks to draw them in, keeps the
Saccharine trees sweet, and tells everyone when something special turns up.

## Getting a keeper

Stand a villager by a **Pasture Block** and sneak-right-click them with a **honey bottle**. A jobless villager never
takes your pasture by themselves. Put a chest by the pasture for snacks, honey and saplings. A
[Habitat Garden](habitat-garden-build.md) is made for her.

## Lures

She keeps up to **3 Poké Snacks** set out within 32 blocks of her pasture, taking them from her chests or the
[Camp Cook](camp-cook.md)'s, and sets out another when one is eaten up. Mark a spot with a [Field Marker](field-marker.md)
(mark it, then right-click her with it); unmarked, she picks grass 16 to 32 blocks out.

Sneak-right-click her with an empty hand to pick her **lure**: any snack, a type, an egg group or, with Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer,
Alphas. She then asks the Camp Cook for snacks seasoned with the berries that draw those Pokémon.

## Honey

**Saccharine logs** within 32 blocks are slathered with honey bottles from the chests (Cobblemon's own way of drawing
Pokémon with hidden abilities) and slathered again when it wears off. Saccharine saplings from the chests are planted
round the lure spots.

## Sightings

Every minute she looks over the wild Pokémon within 48 blocks of the pasture. A shiny one, one Cobblemon rarely spawns,
or with Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer an Alpha, is told to the players in the village and written in the chronicle ("Bramble spotted a
shiny Eevee east of her pasture"), once. The hall lists her last five sightings.

## Trading

Saccharine saplings and honey (Novice), glass bottles bought and honeycomb (Apprentice), Poké Snacks and the **Habitat
Garden** blueprint for 12 emeralds (Journeyman), Saccharine logs (Expert), a spyglass (Master).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Fed by**: the Camp Cook's snacks, berries from the [Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md), honey bottles (a
  [Beekeeper](beekeeper.md)'s), and Flying or Grass Pokémon pastured nearby, who scout and sweeten the snacks
  ([Pokémon partners at work](partners-at-work.md)).
- **Affects**: which wild Pokémon come near the village, and news of rare ones.
- **Grows into**: an Expert keeper with a finished Habitat Garden founds the village's own habitat on Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer
  ([The village's habitat](village-habitat.md)).
- **Stops her**: her Pasture Block gone, no chest, no lure spot, or the **Habitat Keepers** setting off. The **Habitat
  sightings** setting off keeps her sightings to herself.
