In a [realm](realms.md), learning travels. What the capital knows, every member knows, and the capital's
[scholars](scholar.md) study things only a realm can use.

## Sharing

A member's research screen (sneak-right-click a Scholar) shows each topic at the higher of its own level and the
capital's. Where the capital knows more, the tooltip says "From the capital: Drill Journeyman", and every bonus that
topic gives works in the member too: its guards hit harder with the capital's Drill, its market gets the capital's
Commerce. A topic the capital knows fully can't be taken up again. The member's own levels are unchanged, and only they
count toward its rank. A village that leaves the realm keeps only its own levels.

A member's scholars with nothing chosen at home help the capital at half pace: on the capital's own topic, or on the
realm's topic while the capital has none.

## The realm topics

The capital's research screen has a **Realm** tab (a gold helmet). Its ruler picks a topic there, and the capital's
scholars take it up when they have nothing of their own. Every level costs 16 paper and 8 emeralds from the
[realm treasury](realm-treasury.md), and every level after a topic's first takes 2 books as well. While the treasury is
short, they wait. A topic can't be chosen before the ones in its Needs column. Its effect reaches the realm's villages
only: a village outside the realm gets none of it, even one with the same owner.

| Topic | Levels | Each level | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Royal Roads | 2 | caravans between the realm's villages arrive 25% sooner | |
| Common Coin | 2 | the board sells at 107%, then 104% of what it pays, instead of 110% | Royal Roads I |
| Royal Post | 1 | parcels between the realm's mailboxes go out at noon as well as at dawn | Royal Roads I |
| Ferry Charter | 1 | Travel Tickets between the realm's posts cost half (at least an emerald) for members' owners and friends | Royal Post I |
| Levies | 2 | relief bands one rider bigger per village | |
| Charters | 2 | a Colony Charter costs a quarter less, and settlers bring 16 more of each supply | Royal Roads II |
| Concord | 2 | wellbeing 5% higher in every member | Levies I |
| Granaries | 1 | at dawn a member with under 16 meals in store gets up to 32 sent from the fullest | Concord I, Royal Roads II |

A finished level is told to every member's owner online and written in the capital's chronicle.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** paper and books from the [Librarian](librarian.md), emeralds from the levy, and busy scholars.
- **What it affects:** [caravans](caravan-sales.md), the [price board](price-board.md), the post
  ([Mailbox](mailbox.md)), [Travel Tickets](travel-ticket.md), [relief riders](relief-riders.md),
  [colonies](colonies.md) and every member's wellbeing.
- **What stops it:** **Realms** off in the settings screen: nothing is shared, and the realm research waits, saved.
