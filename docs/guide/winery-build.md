A stone press house over a half-sunk cellar, earth banked up its sides. A stone stair climbs the front to the press
room, where the cauldron stands by the door; the cellar door is at the ground, under the stair's landing, and inside are
racks of casks and two big chests.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**; a [Vintner](vintner.md) who reaches Journeyman also sells it, for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Place the blueprint, stock the chests by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists (hold Shift over it), and
hand it to the builder. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

**Winery II** adds a back door onto a tasting porch (a table, stools, a cask) under a lean-to roof, then two terraces of
sweet berry rows stepping down to a pergola hung with glow berries. The builder only builds what's new.

**Winery III** digs a **barrel vault** under the barrel cellar, down a ladder under a hatch in its corner: casks two high under a vault of stone, a tasting table and a chest of bottles. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was; the workers' chests stay where they were.

## What it does for the village

Its cauldron is a **Vintner's** workstation: sneak-right-click a villager by it with sweet berries, glow berries or an
apple. The chests in the cellar are in reach for their fruit and bottles. Villages sometimes grow a small Winery of
their own, with a Vintner already living in it.

## How it fits with the rest

The Vintner presses the [Cider](cider.md), [Berry Wine](berry-wine.md) and [Vintage Wine](vintage-wine.md) the classes
need. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
