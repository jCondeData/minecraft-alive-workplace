The Founder's statue: a robed villager in stone, drawn from the game's own villager so it reads as one from far off,
nose and all. A copper cloak hangs from its shoulders and weathers green; one hand rests on its chest, the other holds
up a lantern lit day and night. It stands on a stepped plinth ringed with braziers, over an arch the main road and its
caravans pass through.

## What it needs

A **City** and the **Founder** (on a server without Legends, City rank alone is enough), and the Master Architect. It is
the only Wonder that needs a City. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

- **The whole realm is happier**: every villager in its realm by 8, and its own village by 12.
- **More travelers** come to the inn, twice as many, and twice as many of them are Experts.
- The village is the more **beautiful** for it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a City, rare things found by exploring (a rare worker in the village finds each faster), and the
  best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: the mood of every village in the realm, the travelers at the [inn](jobs-services.md) you can
  hire, and the village's decorations.
