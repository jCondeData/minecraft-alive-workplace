Put a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) down and every villager within its reach gets a **first name** of their own:
Bram, Clover, Dara, Elowen, Fenn, Garrick and seventy-odd more. Within a village, nobody shares a name while there are
names left to give.

## Where you see them

- over their head when you look at them;
- on the hall's list of everyone who lives there, with their job, mood and traits;
- in the village's chronicle (weddings, babies, who built what);
- on their [grave](graves.md), if they die.

A name you give with a **Name Tag** is kept, and so is any name a villager already had.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** a Village Hall. Villagers outside every hall's reach stay nameless, as in vanilla.
- **Affects:** [chatter](chatter.md) (villagers greet you by name: "Good day, Steve!" and you know who said it), the
  [graves](graves.md) of named villagers, and [couples](couples-and-weddings.md) in the chronicle.
- **Turned off:** the [Villager Names](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch.
