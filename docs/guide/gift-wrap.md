Colored paper and string, for wrapping a present for a villager.

## Getting it

Craft **paper**, **string** and **any dye** together, anywhere in the grid: you get four.

## Using it

Put one Gift Wrap and **any one item** on the crafting grid and take out a [Gift](gift.md): a red box with a gold bow.
One item is taken from the stack on the grid, with everything on it (its name, enchantments, wear). The wrapping is used
up when the gift is given.

## How it fits with the rest

Gifts are the quickest way to make a [friend](friendship.md). The recipe works even with friendship switched off, but
a villager then hands the Gift back unopened.
