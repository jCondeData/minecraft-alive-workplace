Three settings for what a village gives back to the players who look after it. See [Settings](settings.md) for where
to change them.

## Village Quests

Normally **on**: Village Halls post [quests](quests.md) for players each morning. **Off:** no new quests go up; open
ones can still be finished and paid.

## Village Treasury

Normally **on**: villages with a Village Hall put by takings every morning for players to collect at the hall (see
[Treasury](treasury.md)). **Off:** nothing is put by.

## Daily Takings

Normally **20**, a slider from 0 to 500: what each worker brings the village treasury a day, in hundredths of an
emerald, before wellbeing and rank. At 20 that's a fifth of an emerald a worker; 100 would be a whole emerald.

## How they interact

- The treasury grows with the number of workers, the village's [wellbeing](village-needs.md) (half as much when it's
  badly kept, half again when it's well kept) and its [rank](ranks.md) (a quarter more a rank).
- Quests also pay a quarter more a rank, so a growing village is worth more to you both ways.
- With CobbleDollars installed, both pay in CobbleDollars at the
  [CobbleDollar Rate](settings-cobbledollar-rate.md).
