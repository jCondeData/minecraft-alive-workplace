A round tower banded in white and red on a stepped stone mole, its lantern lit by night, with the keeper's cottage, a
boathouse, a quay and a timber jetty out to a ferry stop at its foot.

## What it needs

A **Town on the coast**, the **Merchant Prince** (on a server without Legends, a village with 3 caravan routes stands
in) and the Master Architect. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

- **Caravans** from the village reach **twice as far** (villages up to 4,096 blocks away show on the Trade Routes
  page) and take **half the time** on the road.
- **Ferry fares** to and from the village's Travel Posts are **halved** (never below an emerald), and the Travel Post
  at the end of the jetty joins the ferry network under the village's name, such as "Thornholm Lighthouse".
- The keeper watches the sea: **the day before drowned pirates raid**, everyone in the village is told ("Sails off the
  west shore: Drowned Pirates will strike Thornholm tomorrow night."), the chronicle notes it, and at dusk on the day
  the guards gather on the shore. Raids by land are left to the village's own lookouts.
- If the Lighthouse is **damaged** (less of it standing than the **Wonder Standing** setting), all four are off until its builder
  repairs it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: [caravan routes](trade-and-caravans.md), the coast, rare things found by exploring (a rare worker
  in the village finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: trade with far villages, the [Ferryman](jobs-services.md)'s prices, and the village's
  [defense](defence.md).
