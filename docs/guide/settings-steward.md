The switches for villages that build themselves, as the settings screen names them. See [Settings](settings.md) for
how to open the screen.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Stewards** | on | A Journeyman Builder appointed with the City Plan plans the village. Off: no new Stewards, and those appointed stand idle (the plan still paints). |
| **Steward's open builds** | 4 (1 to 8) | The most of a Steward's builds going up at once, whatever his level and the village's rank. |
| **Stewards Run Villages** | on | A Steward set to Run the village starts his builds himself. Off: every village asks first. |
| **Stewards Build Roads** | on | The roads on the plan get built, and new buildings get a lane to the nearest road. Off: roads stay drawn but unbuilt. |
| **Roads Between Villages** | on | Villages with a trade route each build their half of a road to the other. |
| **Road to Another Village** | 256 (32 to 512) | The longest half of such a road, in blocks. |
| **Stewards Build Walls** | on | A raided village's Steward proposes a wall. Off: he never does. |
| **Steward Renewals** | on | Old houses in renew zones get rebuilt. Off: they're listed, never renewed. |

Two older settings matter too: **Build Distance** (how far a plot may be from a builder's Blueprint Table) and
**Village Hall Reach** (how far the plan reaches; a cell of the plan is 4 by 4 blocks at 64).

## How it fits with the rest

- [The Steward](steward.md), [Roads](roads.md), [Roads between villages](roads-between-villages.md), [Walls](walls.md)
  and [Old houses renewed](old-house-renewal.md) each say what their switch changes.
- Turning **Stewards** off leaves everything already built standing.
