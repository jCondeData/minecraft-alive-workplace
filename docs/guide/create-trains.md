With the Create mod installed, two villages that trade with each other can be joined by rail. Give each village a
**Train Station**, and once the road between them is built, their builders lay Create track along it. From then on the
villages' caravans take the train instead of walking.

## Setting it up

1. **A trade route.** Set one up on the Village Hall's Trade Routes page, as for any caravan. The villages build the
   road to each other as they always do.
2. **A Train Station in each village.** It's at the Blueprint Table: a short run of Create track with a Create
   station beside it, a platform with benches and a timber shelter. Any Create station near the Village Hall counts, so
   one you placed yourself works too.
3. **Track along the road.** When both villages have a station and a village's half of the road is finished, one of
   its builders walks out and lays Create track down the middle of the road. Keep Create track in the builder's
   chests: they take it from there like any other material. The chronicle says when the track is laid, and when
   both halves are done: "The railway to Ashford is open: caravans go by train".

![The Train Station and the track between the villages](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/village_train/still-1.jpg)

## Riding the train

A caravan between two villages joined by rail goes by train:

- **It's quicker.** The trip takes a third of the time it takes on foot (never less than about twenty seconds).
- **It's safe.** A caravan on the train never goes missing on the way.
- **You see it go.** Steam rises over the station and its bell rings when the train leaves and when it comes in; the
  chronicle reads "A train left for ... with ..." and "A train from ... came in with ...". The goods go into the
  storehouse as a walking caravan's would, and anything for sale is sold as usual.

![The caravan's goods arrived by train](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/village_train/still-2.jpg)

## Good to know

- **A station broken while the train is on its way:** the caravan gets off where the track ends and walks the rest.
  It still arrives with everything it carried, a little later, and the chronicle says so. Until the station is
  rebuilt, caravans walk.
- **Only one village with a station,** or a road that isn't finished, or a road that ends at a milestone: caravans walk.
- The train is the villagers' own trip: you don't need to assemble a Create train for it.
- With the **Village trains** setting off, the Train Station isn't at the table, no track is laid and caravans walk;
  a train already on its way still comes in.
