# Free Bread

```infobox
type: Edict
expansion: M30
Boost: everyone fed in the last day is 10 happier
Cost: the village eats 30% more
Reform: [The Common Granary](the-common-granary.md): 512 wheat, 128 hay bales, 48 barrels
Reformed: the good mood stays, the extra eating goes
categories: Edicts
related: edicts.md, the-common-granary.md, village-life.md
```

Bread for everyone, from the village's own store. People love it; the store feels it.

## What it does

While Free Bread is in force, **every villager who ate in the last day is 10 happier**, with "free bread" as the
reason. In return the **village eats 30% more** from its store.

## Reforming it

While it's in force, a Farmer asks for a **Common Granary**: 512 wheat, then 128 hay bales, then 48 barrels
([The Common Granary](the-common-granary.md)). Once it's full the free bread no longer eats into the store, and the
village sits down to a harvest supper at the hall.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a full store. Your [farmers, fishermen and chefs](jobs-land.md) have to keep up with 30% more
  eating; a hungry villager gets no good mood from it.
- **What it affects**: the village's mood (see [Village life](village-life.md)).
- **Good with**: [Long Shifts](long-shifts.md), whose unhappiness it offsets.
