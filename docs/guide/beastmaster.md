"Every guard of theirs walks with a war dog at heel, and the horses they breed outrun the wind."

## How they come

Keep **10 animals** within 16 blocks of your Ranchers', Butchers' and Shepherds' workstations. Then, at a pillager
outpost within 1500 blocks, a prisoner may be waiting in an iron-bar cage at the foot of the tower: break a bar to free
them, and they come to your hall the next morning. A child of two schooled Master Ranchers may also grow up as the
Beastmaster.

![A bar broken: the prisoner is free](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_sites/still-2.jpg)

## What they do

- **War dogs.** With 3 bones in the chests by their workstation they tame one of the village's wolves for each
  [guard](guard.md) without one (or breed a pair when only two are left), and fit it with wolf armor when the chests
  hold 6 armadillo scutes. The dog follows its guard on patrol and in a raid and attacks what the guard fights. A lost
  dog is replaced 2 days after it fell.
- **Horses.** Once a day, with golden carrots (or apples) in the chests, they breed the ranch's two best horses of a
  kind, up to 8. Each foal gets the best speed, jump and health of its parents and a little more. Grown foals are
  saddled from the chests, and guards ride them first.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your herders' animals, wolves near the village, bones, scutes and golden carrots.
- **What they affect**: your guards and your [defense](defence.md).
- **What stops them**: a strike, or empty chests.
