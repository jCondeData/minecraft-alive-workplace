The guild of trade and carrying.

## Perks

- Shopkeepers, Innkeepers, Ferrymen, Postmen and Porters work **15% faster**.
- Travelers at the [inn](innkeeper.md) cost **a quarter less** to hire: a 16-emerald traveler costs 12.
- [Porters](porter.md) carry **3 more stacks**.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** of any of those trades with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master merchants, and a busy inn and store.
- **What it affects**: hiring new workers, the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s deliveries and the mail.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
