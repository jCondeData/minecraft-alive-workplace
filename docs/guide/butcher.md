The Butcher is the village's herder: they look after the cows, pigs, chickens and rabbits round their smoker.

## Getting a butcher

A villager without a job takes a free **smoker** as a Butcher, as in vanilla. If a villager at a smoker has another job,
sneak-right-click them with a **lead** to make them a Butcher again. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the smoker with food
for breeding and some empty buckets.

## What they do

Every animal within 16 blocks of the smoker is theirs to look after:

- They breed the cows, pigs, chickens and rabbits with food from the chests, until there are 8 of each.
- They pick up eggs, feathers and whatever else the animals drop.
- They milk the cows into the buckets in the chests, keeping 4 milk buckets there.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a butcher with a **lead** to hire them. A hired butcher also keeps your herd in check: once
there are more than 10 grown animals of a kind, the extra ones go for meat and leather. Babies, named animals and animals
on a lead are never taken.

**With Cobblemon** they do the chores you can do to the Pokémon in a Pasture Block near their smoker: milk a Miltank,
brush a feathered Pokémon for feathers, and the rest of Cobblemon's own list.

Over their head: how many buckets of milk, and whether they're milking, picking up eggs, feeding a pair or tending a
Pokémon.

## How the butcher fits in

- **Feeds:** the [Chef](chef.md) takes milk and eggs from the butcher's chests for cakes and pies. The
  [Porter](porter.md) carries the rest to the storehouse (leaving the breeding food).
- **Stops when:** there's no chest by the smoker ("needs a chest by the smoker").

See also [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md), the [Shepherd](shepherd.md) for sheep and the [Rancher](rancher.md) for
horses.
