With Cobblemon, a village with an [Arena](arena-build.md) holds its festivals as a **Festival Cup**: a Pokémon
tournament for the Trainer Leaders of the villages it trades with, and for any player who signs up. The stands fill, a
fair opens, the bouts go on all afternoon, and the winning village holds the Cup until someone takes it.

## Who hosts

A village hosts the Cup when it has a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), a finished Arena and at least **Village**
rank ([Ranks](ranks.md)). Every one of its [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) is a Cup (the server can make it
every second, third... festival). The hall's **Festival Cup** button opens the Cup page; when a village can't host, it
says why: no Arena, too small, no hall, or the Cup switched off.

## The circuit and the entrants

The host's **circuit** is the host and every village with a hall it has a trade route with, either way, seven at
most, nearest first ([Trade and caravans](trade-and-caravans.md)). Each sends one entrant:

- its [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md), or else its best [Trainer](trainer.md), or else nobody;
- the host sends its Leader and up to two of its Trainers to fill the bracket;
- players who **sign up** on the Cup page, each standing for a village they own, one whose owner made them a friend, or
  one nobody owns; two players a village at most.

The bracket holds 4 (8 at a Town host, 16 at a City), with byes for the top seeds (Leaders first, by tier, then
players, then the host's trainers; the defending champion is seeded first). With fewer than 4 entrants there's no Cup,
just a plain festival, and the page says why.

## The Cup page

![The Cup's card and rules](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_page/still-1.jpg)

The page shows the next Cup (its [theme](cup-themes.md), day and rules in plain words), the circuit's villages with how
far each is and whom it sends, the players signed up, **Sign up** and **Withdraw**, and the **roll of champions**.
Sign-up opens when the festival before ends and closes at dawn on Cup day, when the bracket is drawn. Players in
circuit villages are told when sign-up opens and on the evening before. The host's owner may pick another theme on the
page until sign-up closes.

## The day, the bouts, the champion

- [Cup day](cup-day.md): the delegates walk in, a fair, the stands, the feast and the fireworks.
- [Cup bouts](cup-bouts.md): villagers battle at the ring; a player's bout is a real battle, with a purse.
- [Champions](cup-champions.md): the title, the Cup banner and the village's pride.
- [The eight themes](cup-themes.md): who may fight, at what level, and what the fair sells.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Feeds it**: trade routes (the circuit), Trainer Leaders and Trainers, the Arena, the village's rank.
- **It affects**: the festival (it becomes the Cup), trainer XP for the villagers who fight, the village's mood while
  it holds the Cup, and the chronicles of every circuit village.
- **Stops it**: the **Festival Cup** setting off (festivals stay plain), no Cobblemon, the Arena taken down (a plain
  festival), or the host not loaded on the morning (put off a day at a time, called off after 8 days).
