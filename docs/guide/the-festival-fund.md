[Festival Season](festival-season.md) means a festival every few days, and a thin purse. The Bard asks if you'd help
start a fund: "The festivals are the best thing in this village, but the treasury pays for every one. Help me fill a
festival fund, and the next one will be grand."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Talk to the Bard about the festival fund.
2. **The First Step.** Every festival needs cake: bring **24 cakes**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **256 firework rockets**, set by for the next one.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: **48 note blocks** for a band worth hearing.

Each step pays emeralds, and the village can't stop talking about it ("Fireworks enough for a grand night.").

## How it ends

Festival Season is **reformed** for good: the villagers put by for their own festivals, one every 4 days, and the
treasury pays nothing. The next festival is **grand**: twice the fireworks, and its good mood lasts a day longer.
Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Festival Season](festival-season.md) is in force.
- Cakes come from your [chefs](jobs-crafts.md) and farmers; fireworks need paper and gunpowder.
- A reformed Festival Season never empties the [treasury](village-hall.md) again.
