A [Nurse](nurse.md) can work at Cobblemon's own **Healing Machine**, just like the counter of a Pokémon Center in the
games.

## Getting her there

Stand a villager by a Healing Machine and sneak-right-click them with a **honey bottle**. A jobless villager never takes
your machine by themselves. The [Pokémon Center](pokemon-center-build.md) has one on its counter, and Arena III has one
in each trainers' room ([Arena](arena-build.md)).

## Healing

Right-click her: she puts your Poké Balls in the machine ("Back in a moment!"), it plays its own animation and heal
time, and your team comes back full. It's free. If the machine is busy with another team, she asks you to wait a
moment. While she's on shift the machine stays charged.

A nurse at a brewing stand heals your team by hand, as before.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Before a battle**: heal up before challenging a [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md) or a Cup bout.
- **Stopped by**: the **Nurse Healing Machines** setting off (she heals by hand instead, as at any station).
