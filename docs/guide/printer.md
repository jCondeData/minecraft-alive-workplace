The Printer runs the village press: the weekly news the Burghers read, books for the Scholars, and a gilded copy of the
village's whole chronicle for the Nobles.

## Getting a Printer

Stand a villager by a **cartography table** and sneak-right-click them with an **ink sac**. (A compass brings the
[Cartographer](cartographer.md) back; netherrack the [Netherworker](netherworker.md).) It's an
[Artisan](artisan.md)'s job: the villager's household must be Artisan or better to take it up. Put a chest within 8
blocks of the table.

## What they print

| Level | Prints | From |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | 2 books | 3 paper and a leather (only while the village has a [Scholar](scholar.md)) |
| Novice | 2 copies of the [Village Gazette](village-gazette.md) | 3 paper and an ink sac |
| Journeyman | an [Illuminated Book](illuminated-book.md) | a book, 2 gold nuggets, a lapis lazuli and a glow ink sac |

They keep the store in **8 of each**, from their own chests, the store and the other workers' chests (never a
builder's); a missing making shows on the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s requests board. The papers are written the day
they're printed, from the nearest [Village Hall](village-hall.md).

## Trading

At every level they buy paper, ink sacs, leather, glow ink sacs or gold nuggets, and sell today's Gazette for an
emerald, books (2 for an emerald, 5 for 2 from Expert) and from **Journeyman** the Illuminated Book for 8. A Journeyman
also sells the [Print Shop](print-shop-build.md)'s blueprint for 12 emeralds.

## Family trees

On anyone's Family page at the Village Hall (see [Family names](family-names.md)), the book button has the village's
Printer print that family's tree for **2 emeralds**: a written book, "The Ashby Family", every member generation by
generation. With no Printer in the village, the button tells you so and nothing is printed.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** paper from sugar cane, ink sacs from squid, leather from cows (a [Butcher](butcher.md) keeps them breeding), gold from a
  [Miner](miner.md), lapis and glow ink for the Illuminated Book. An [Artisan](artisan.md) household makes every
  Printer 10% faster.
- **What it affects:** [Burghers](burgher.md) need the Gazette every 7 days; [Nobles](noble.md) want the Illuminated
  Book every 16. The village's Scholars get their books. With a caravan route, the hall posts the **Spread the news**
  quest: carry this week's Gazette to the hall at the other end of the route for 6 emeralds. See
  [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
- **What stops it:** no chest, no paper or ink, a household below Artisan (no new Printer; one already working keeps
  the job), or the **Printers** switch off in the settings (no Printer job, Printers already hired stand idle, and no
  Spread the news quest).
