The Netherworker goes where your villagers usually can't: through the portal, and back a few minutes later with a bag
full of the Nether.

## Getting a netherworker

Build a **Nether portal**, put a **cartography table** within 32 blocks of it, stand a villager by the table and
sneak-right-click them with **netherrack**. (A compass at a cartography table picks the [Cartographer](cartographer.md).)
Put a chest within 8 blocks of the table.

## A trip

With **three rations** in the chests (bread, cooked meat and the like) they pack whatever gear is there, walk to the
portal and step through. While they're away they're out of sight and safe; over their head the line says when they'll
be back. About five minutes later they step out, put their finds and their gear in the chests, and rest a minute before
the next trip.

What they bring back depends on what they took:

| Taken along | Brings back |
|---|---|
| Nothing but food | netherrack, soul sand, basalt, blackstone, gravel, gold nuggets, glowstone dust, nether wart, ghast tears, magma cream |
| A pickaxe | quartz, glowstone, obsidian, magma blocks, gilded blackstone |
| A diamond pickaxe | ancient debris, now and then |
| An axe | crimson and warped stems and fungi, wart blocks, shroomlights, vines |
| A sword and armor | the fortress: blaze rods, nether bricks, gold, magma cream, the odd wither skeleton skull |
| A fire resistance potion | a second haul from the wastes, and half the risk |

**Armor matters.** Without it a netherworker comes back hurt half the time; with it, much less often. Tools lose some
durability on every trip, and a fire resistance potion is drunk (the bottle comes back). With Cobblemon installed they
sometimes find a Fire Stone, a Dusk Stone or a Magmarizer.

## How the netherworker fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Chef](chef.md)'s bread and roasts make good rations. The gear is yours to put
  in the chest.
- **Feeds:** nether wart and blaze rods are what a [Cleric](cleric.md) brews the guards' potions with; quartz and
  glowstone go to the storehouse with the [Porter](porter.md).
- **Faster with:** the **Expeditions** research (back 20% sooner a level; see [Scholar](scholar.md)).
- **Stops when:** there's no portal within 32 blocks, or fewer than three rations in the chests.

## Trading

Netherworkers buy bread and cooked porkchops, and sell netherrack, quartz, nether wart, glowstone, blaze rods and, at
Master, netherite scrap.
