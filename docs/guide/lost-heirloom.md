# The lost heirloom

```infobox
type: Quest
expansion: M31
From: a villager's [personal request](personal-requests.md)
The heirloom: Grandmother's Compass, Grandfather's Pocket Watch, Mother's Spyglass, Father's Horn, the Lucky Crystal or Great-aunt's Diary
Lost at: a ruined portal, a shipwreck, trail ruins or an abandoned mineshaft, within 1500 blocks
You get: "Map to where it was lost"
Time: 10 days
Pays: 8 emeralds, and 150 friendship
categories: Quests, Items
related: personal-requests.md, map-to-the.md, friendship.md, explorer-s-map.md
```

Every family has something it lost long ago. A villager who counts you a friend may ask you to find it.

## Finding it

1. Say **[I'll help]** to the request and you get a **map to where it was lost**, drawn like an explorer's map with the
   place marked: a ruined portal, a shipwreck, trail ruins or an abandoned mineshaft, up to 1500 blocks away.
2. Travel there. When you come within 64 blocks, the heirloom is put in place: in the nearest chest or barrel, in a
   suspicious gravel block to brush at trail ruins, perhaps in a chest minecart down a mineshaft, or in a new barrel on
   the ground if the chests are gone.
3. Bring it home and right-click the villager with it.

The heirloom is one of six: **Grandmother's Compass** (a compass), **Grandfather's Pocket Watch** (a clock), **Mother's
Spyglass**, **Father's Horn** (a goat horn), **the Lucky Crystal** (an amethyst shard) or **Great-aunt's Diary**, a
written book with three pages of her story.

## How it ends

The request is done: 8 emeralds, 150 friendship, and the chronicle tells the heirloom's story ("Grandmother's Compass is
home with Dara's family again, found at the ruin by Jesse...").

## How it fits with the rest

It's a reason to go exploring with a [map](map-to-the.md) in hand, and one of the biggest favors a
[friend](friendship.md) can ask. Miss the 10 days and it's a missed request like any other.
