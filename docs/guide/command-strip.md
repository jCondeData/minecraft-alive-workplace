# /workplace strip

```infobox
type: Command
image: https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/quarry/still-1.jpg
caption: A miner at a quarry
Syntax: /workplace strip <height>
Who: everyone, holding a Quarry Marker
Does: turns the marker's quarry into a strip mine at that height
Example: /workplace strip -54
categories: Commands, Jobs
related: jobs-land.md, command-sites.md, command-cancel.md, jobs.md
```

Hold a **Quarry Marker** and type `/workplace strip <height>`: the marker now plans a **strip mine** at that Y level
instead of an open pit. The miner digs a ladder shaft down to it and then tunnels at that height. The answer tells you
which ores are found at that height, and reminds you that the miner digs a ladder shaft down to it.

## Using it

1. Hold the Quarry Marker (in either hand).
2. Pick a height: deep for diamonds, higher up for iron and coal. The height must be inside the world; you're told the
   limits if it isn't.
3. Mark the corners on the ground as usual, and the miner gets to work.

No marker in hand? "Hold a Quarry Marker to set the height of its strip mine."

## How it interacts

- The miner and the Quarry Marker are in [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).
- Follow it with [/workplace sites](command-sites.md) and stop it with [/workplace cancel](command-cancel.md).
