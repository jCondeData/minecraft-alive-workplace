"Show them your partners and they'll tell you what each one was born good at."

## How they come

Keep **25 Pokémon of 10 types** in your village's Pasture Blocks, and the **Pokémon Professor** may visit the inn some
morning.

## What they do

- **Hints.** Right-click them with an empty hand: for each Pokémon in your party, every stat's IV in words ("Fantastic",
  "Best"), the stats its nature raises and lowers, whether it has its hidden ability, and its EVs.
- **The village Pokédex.** Every species kept in your pastures is logged in the hall. The more species, the more of the
  Professor's own research tree, the **Pokédex**, opens on the research screen: Field Notes (partners help 5% more a
  level) at 15 species, Kinship Studies (one more partner per worker) at 25, Berry Science (orchard keepers pick one
  more berry from each plant) at 45, Evolution Studies (the Professor sells one evolution stone a day for 8 emeralds) at
  60 and Regional Survey (exact IVs and EVs) at 80.

![Hints: IVs in words, nature, hidden ability, EVs](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_professor/still-2.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your Pokémon, and Pasture Blocks in the village.
- **What they affect**: your [Pokémon partners](pokemon.md), your [Orchard Keepers](orchard-keeper.md) and your own
  team building.
- **What stops them**: a strike ("won't look at anyone's Pokémon while they're on strike").
