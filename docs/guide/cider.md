Cider is the Artisans' little treat: apple cider pressed at a cauldron, the first thing a new
[Vintner](vintner.md) learns to make.

## Getting it

A Novice Vintner presses one from **3 apples and a glass bottle** and keeps 8 in the village store. You can buy 2 for
an emerald from any Vintner, and the chest in a village's Winery now and then holds a bottle.

## What it's for

- **A want of the Artisans.** An [Artisan](artisan.md) household takes one from the store every 4 days. It isn't a
  need, so nobody falls a class without it, but every want a household has makes it pay 10% more tax.
- **A drink for you.** Drink it like a potion: one drumstick of hunger back, and the empty bottle stays in your hand.

## How it fits with the rest

Apples come from an [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md) or oak leaves; bottles from glass. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md)
for every luxury and who needs it.
