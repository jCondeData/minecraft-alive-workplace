The Fletcher arms the guards for fighting at range.

## Getting a fletcher

A villager without a job takes a free **fletching table** as a Fletcher, as in vanilla. If a villager there has another
job (a [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md) shares the block), sneak-right-click them with **flint**.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a fletcher with flint to hire them: they then make bows and arrows for your own guards.

## What they do

- **A bow for every guard.** A guard with no bow (and none in their chest) gets a **crossbow** if there's iron for one,
  else a **bow**.
- **Arrows.** A guard with a bow and few special arrows gets **spectral arrows**, eight at a time, made from glowstone
  and arrows; the arrows themselves from flint, sticks and feathers if need be.

Everything is made with the game's recipes from the fletcher's chests, the storehouse and the smelters, and brought to
the chest by the guard's post, where the guard picks it up.

Over their head: how many they've made; "the guards have their bows" when all's well.

## How the fletcher fits in

- **Fed by:** an [Armorer](armorer.md)'s iron, a [Sifter](sifter.md)'s flint, the storehouse's sticks and feathers.
- **Feeds:** every [Guard](guard.md). A guard with a bow or crossbow becomes an Archer and shoots creepers from a safe
  distance.
