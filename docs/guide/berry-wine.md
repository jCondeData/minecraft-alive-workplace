Berry Wine is what a Burgher drinks with the evening news. The village gets through a lot of it: every Burgher household
takes a bottle every other day.

## Getting it

An Apprentice [Vintner](vintner.md) presses it from **6 sweet berries and a glass bottle**, or from 4 glow berries, or
(with Cobblemon) from 4 of Cobblemon's berries. You can buy it from a Vintner for 2 emeralds.

## What it's for

- **A need of the Burghers.** A [Burgher](burgher.md) household takes one from the village store every 2 days. Three
  dawns without and the household comes down to Artisan.
- **The start of Vintage Wine.** A Journeyman Vintner lays bottles down; once one is 3 days old it becomes
  [Vintage Wine](vintage-wine.md).
- **A drink for you**: a drumstick and a half of hunger, and the bottle comes back.

## How it fits with the rest

Berries come from a [Berry Farm](berry-farm-build.md), a [Farmer](farmer.md) or the wild. Keep the Vintner in bottles:
running out of Berry Wine is the quickest way to lose your Burghers. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
