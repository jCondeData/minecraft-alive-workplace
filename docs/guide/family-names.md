Every villager in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) belongs to a family, and the village keeps their
tree: who was born to whom, who married whom, and who has passed on.

## Family names

A villager whose parents nobody knows **founds a family**, and their children take its name. The hall's list says
"Dara (Ashby)", while the name over Dara's head (or one a Name Tag gave her) never changes. Babies take a parent's
family name when they're born.

When a baby is born and the village knows the grandparents, the chronicle says so: "Mira was born to Dara and Tomas,
grandchild of Bram Ashby and Ilse Corwen". A family's first great-grandchild gets a line of its own: "The Ashby family
has its third generation: Mira".

## The Family page

Shift-click a named villager on the hall's list for their life story, then press the **Family** button (a flowering
azalea). It shows their grandparents, parents, partner, brothers and sisters, children and grandchildren, each with
their trade and their days. The living are name tags, babies eggs, and the dead skulls with a cross ("Bram (Ashby) †",
"Died on day 40"). Click anyone to open their own family.

![Mira's grandparents, Bram marked dead](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/family_tree/still-3.jpg)

## A printed family tree

With a [Printer](printer.md) in the village, the page's book button prints the family's tree for **2 emeralds**: a
written book, "The Ashby Family", with a cover (the village, the day, how many people in how many generations) and
everyone of the family, generation by generation. No Printer, or not enough emeralds, and nothing is printed: the
button tells you why.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md) and [babies](babies-and-growth.md). The hall's book keeps up
  to 400 people; past that, the longest dead are forgotten first.
- **What it affects:** the chronicle's birth lines, the Printer's work, and [households](households.md) (children take
  their parents' class).
- **Setting:** with **Family Names** off, the hall and the chronicle go by first names ("Bram's Family"); the names are
  still kept, so they come back when it's on again.
