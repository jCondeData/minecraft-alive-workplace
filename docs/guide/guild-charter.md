A sealed charter that makes a Master the head of their trade's [guild](guilds.md).

## Using it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** villager with it. They become the **Guild Master** of their trade's guild, the village is
told, and the chronicle notes it. The guild's perks start once a builder finishes a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md) for
it.

If the charter can't be used, you're told why:

- the villager isn't a Master yet;
- they don't live in a village with a Village Hall;
- their trade has no guild;
- they already lead a guild;
- the village is only a Hamlet ("a village needs Village rank for a guild");
- the village already has that guild, and who leads it;
- the village has all the guilds its rank allows.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: paper from the Librarian, an emerald, gold and red dye; and a Master to hand it to.
- **What it affects**: which [guild](guilds.md) your village gets, and so which jobs work better.
- **What stops it**: the **Guilds** setting off: charters are refused.
