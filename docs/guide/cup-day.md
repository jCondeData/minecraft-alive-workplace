The [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md) is the host village's festival, from morning to the final.

## Morning: the delegates arrive

From early morning the entrants of the far villages arrive as **delegates**: visiting villagers with their Leader's
name, a Trainer Leader's outfit and "of" their village ("Mira of Thornholm"), walking in from the edge of the village
on the side their home lies, to the [Arena](arena-build.md). The host's own entrants walk there too. Delegates take no
job and no bed, and leave at dawn out of sight. A real Trainer Leader whose village is loaded is "away at the Cup" that
day and takes no challenges.

![A delegate walks in](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_day/still-2.jpg)

## The fair

The market's traveling traders come to the Arena's **fair lane**, two more of them than on a market day, and each
also sells the theme's wares ([themes](cup-themes.md)). See [Market days](market-days.md).

![The fair](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_day/still-1.jpg)

## The afternoon off

From noon the villagers who are off work gather in the stands and sit on the benches; for the final, every villager
comes. They cheer when their own village's entrant wins a bout, and eat the festival's feast (the theme's dish first,
if the store has it). The bard plays the theme's music disc at the ring.

The [bouts](cup-bouts.md) start at the theme's hour (noon, or dusk for the Lantern Cup) and run until midnight (dawn
for the Lantern Cup). Any bout left at the end is settled as an exhibition; a player who isn't at the ring loses by
walkover.

## After the final

Fireworks in the theme's colors, **Hero of the Village** for players at the Arena, and the festival's mood lift for
everyone who came. The chronicle of every circuit village gets the Cup: host, theme, champion and the final ("The
Grand Cup at Thornholm: Mira of Thornholm is champion, beating Dara of Ashford in the final"). Then the
[champion](cup-champions.md) takes the Cup home.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Replaces** the host's ordinary [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) that day.
- **Needs** the host loaded on its Cup morning: if not, the Cup is put off a day at a time, and called off after 8
  days. A host whose Arena is gone holds a plain festival.
- **Feeds** the village's mood (the festival lift) and the circuit's chronicles.
