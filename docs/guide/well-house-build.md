The Well House is a roofed well where the [Well Keeper](well-keeper.md) works, with water close enough to carry to the
fields all day.

## What it looks like

A stone platform three blocks high with steps up the front. In its middle a kerbed well shaft goes three deep down to
real water, under a spruce frame with a lantern on a chain. The Well Keeper's cauldron sits in a back corner, a wooden
trough of water runs along the back, and benches stand either side, all under a dark oak hip roof on four spruce posts.

## Building it

Take the Well House from the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md), place it and stock the chests by the builder's table
with what its tooltip lists. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md). It's a single build with no upgrade.

## Putting it to work

Stand a villager by its cauldron and sneak-right-click them with **a shovel**: the village has its Well Keeper, with the
well right beside them. The house has the cauldron and no other job block.

## How it fits in

- **What it answers:** the [drought](drought.md). A drought can come to any village, so when yours has no Well Keeper,
  **What next?** at the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) suggests the Well House.
- **Where to put it:** close to your fields. The Well Keeper fetches water and waters farmland within 24 blocks of
  the cauldron.
- **The village map:** it's marked under care on the [village map](village-map.md).
- **Not the same as the Well:** the decoration [Well](well-build.md) (or a Fountain) is what stops villagers worrying
  about the wells in a drought; the Well House is the Well Keeper's workplace.
