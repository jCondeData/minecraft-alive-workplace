A timber hall under a dark slate roof, built round the most important block in your village: the **Village Hall**. The
village bell hangs under its porch, notices are pinned up by the door, and inside four benches face the hall block at
the head of a red runner, between two blue banners.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** from the start: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two).

## Building it

Pick a spot in the middle of your village, hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the
gold edge is the front). Stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint
lists when you hold Shift over it, including one Village Hall block, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in
another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**,
**Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

When the builder sets the Village Hall block in its place, it works exactly like one you put down yourself: everyone
within 64 blocks becomes its village, with a name, a rank, quests, caravans and everything else on
[The Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s page. It's yours, as if you had placed it, and the village's chronicle starts
with its founding. Right-click the hall block inside to open the hall's screen.

The bell under the porch is a real bell, the village's meeting point, and you can ring it like any other.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the hall, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click
the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new, and the
Village Hall block stays where it is the whole time, so your village never loses its hall.

- **Village Hall II** adds a meeting room on the east, through a doorway from the hall, with a council table for eight
  and bookshelves.
- **Village Hall III** adds a stone tower at the end of the meeting room, with a clock on two sides and a great bell in
  its belfry.

On a Village Map drawn at the hall, it's marked as the hall.
