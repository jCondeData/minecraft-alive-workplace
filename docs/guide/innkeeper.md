The Innkeeper takes in travelers, and travelers are skilled workers you can hire on the spot.

## Getting an innkeeper

Stand a villager by a **Shop Counter** and sneak-right-click them with a **bed**. Put free beds nearby for the guests, and one more for the innkeeper: they may
sleep in one of those beds themselves.

## What they do

Each morning, while there's a free bed nearby and fewer than two guests, a **traveler** comes to stay. Travelers already
know a trade: most are Apprentices or Journeymen, now and then an Expert. While they travel they won't take a job.

**Hire one.** Right-click a traveler to see what they'd be and what they cost: 8 emeralds for an Apprentice, 16 for a
Journeyman, 32 for an Expert (or the same in CobbleDollars). Hired, they stay, take the first free workstation and start
at their level. Nobody hires them? After two days they move on (while you're standing close by, they wait until you step away).

![Hiring a traveler](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/innkeeper/still-4.jpg)

Over their head: how many guests they've hosted, and "waiting for travelers", "the inn is full" or "needs a free bed for
guests".

## How the innkeeper fits in

- **Feeds:** a skilled worker at once, where a child (even one a [Teacher](teacher.md) taught) has to grow up first. A hired traveler
  works like any villager with a job (see [Giving villagers jobs](jobs.md)), so give them a free workstation.
- **Stops when:** there's no free bed nearby ("needs a free bed for guests").

## Trading

Innkeepers buy wheat and sugar, and sell bread, mushroom stew, a red bed, pumpkin pies, cake, lanterns and, at Master, a
jukebox.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
