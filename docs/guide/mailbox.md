A Mailbox is your address on the server: mail for you arrives here, and you post parcels to other players from it. It's
also the Postman's workstation.

## Getting one

Craft it from **three iron nuggets** on top, an **iron nugget** on each side of a **chest**, and a **fence** under the
chest. It's in the mod's creative tab too.

## Sending and getting mail

Place it and it's yours. When mail arrives, the red flag goes up. To send something, open your mailbox, put the items
in the top row, type a player's name and press **Send**; write in the **Letter** line to send a letter with it.
`/workplace mail` tells you where your parcels are. Only you, your friends (`/workplace friend add <name>`) and
operators can open your mailbox.

## The Postman

A mailbox never takes a villager by itself: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of it and **sneak-right-click them
with paper**. The postman walks a round of 64 blocks; mail going further goes with the night mail and arrives at dawn.
At a mailbox a postman works at, any player can collect the parcels waiting for them, the same as at a
[Postal Desk](postal-desk.md).

## How it fits in

- **Courier routes.** When there's no mail, a postman hauls between your chests with a
  [Delivery Note](delivery-note.md).
- **With Cobblemon**, a postman with a Flying partner sends air mail.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with a pickaxe; it drops itself and whatever is inside, and it's no longer your address
  until you put a mailbox down again.
