An Explorer's Map leads you to a place a village's explorer found: named for it, with the spot marked like a treasure
map's.

## Getting one

The village's **Cartographers** (vanilla, at a cartography table) go on expeditions with food from their chest. Put
**empty maps** in that chest and every third trip they draw you an Explorer's Map to somewhere nearby, and leave it in
the chest.

## Where they lead

A village, a ruined portal, a pillager outpost, a desert or jungle temple, an igloo, a swamp hut, trail ruins, a
shipwreck or ocean ruins: whatever lies within a treasure map's reach of the village.

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** the cartographer's chest: food for the trip, empty maps to draw on, and a sword if you'd like them to hunt
  on the way.
- **Their other finds** (flint, feathers, clay, saplings, the odd emerald or diamond) go in the same chest.
