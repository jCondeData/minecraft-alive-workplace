Villagers fall in love. In a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), now and then two grown villagers who
aren't family start **courting**, and the chronicle notes it: "Bram and Clover are courting".

## The wedding

Two days later they **marry**. The wedding is held at the village's finished **Chapel**, or at its **bell** when there
isn't one, with fireworks overhead, and everyone there is in a better mood afterward, as after a festival. The chronicle
remembers it.

## Married life

- A couple is happier when the other is near: +5 to their [mood](moods.md) while courting, +10 once married.
- A married couple is first in line for a [baby](babies-and-growth.md) when the village has a free bed.
- The hall's list says who's with whom.
- When one of them dies, the other **mourns** for five days (-15 to their mood).

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** grown, unmarried villagers who aren't family. The bigger the village, the more matches.
- **Affects:** [moods](moods.md) (love, weddings and mourning), [babies](babies-and-growth.md) and the chronicle.
- **The Chapel.** Build one from the Blueprint Table for weddings worth the name; it's a decoration too
  ([Decorations](decorations.md)).
- **Graves.** A spouse who dies leaves a [grave](graves.md); an Undertaker can bring them back.
- **Turned off:** the [Weddings](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch.
