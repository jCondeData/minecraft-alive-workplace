Your own villages, and your friends', can trade freely. Another player's village is a foreign country: to send it
caravans you need a trade pact.

## Offering a pact

Click the minecart on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen and pick the **Pacts** tab (a book and quill). Every
other player's village within 2,048 blocks with no pact yet has **Offer X a trade pact**. The other owner gets a letter
(in their mailbox, their hands, or waiting at the post office) and an **Accept** or **Decline** line on their own Pacts
tab. An offer not answered within 3 days lapses, and you hear of a decline or a lapse.

## What a pact does

- **Routes:** a caravan route to another player's village needs a pact; without one, its line on the Routes tab says so
  and a click does nothing. Routes from before pacts existed became trade pacts by themselves.
- **Paid caravans:** a pact caravan's cargo of what the other village waits for is paid for at the receiving village's
  [board price](price-board.md), as far as its treasury goes.
- **Gifts:** click a pact to turn gifts on for your side, and your caravans ask nothing.

## Ending it

Either side ends a trade pact at once with **End**: its routes both ways stop. The other owner gets a letter and both
chronicles note it.

## How it fits in

- **What it leads to:** caravans that earn ([Caravan sales](caravan-sales.md)), and maybe an [alliance](alliances.md).
- **What breaks it:** a [feud](feuds.md) ends every pact between the two sides and blocks new ones while it stands.
- **What stops it:** **Trade Pacts** off in the settings screen: routes need no pact and there's no Pacts tab; pacts
  stay saved.
