The Sifter shakes loose blocks through a sieve and keeps what falls out. It's slow treasure, but it never runs out
while there's gravel.

## Getting a sifter

Stand a villager by a **cauldron** and sneak-right-click them with **gravel**, **sand**, **red sand** or **soul sand**.
(Leather at a cauldron picks the [Leatherworker](leatherworker-dyer.md) instead.) Put a chest within 8 blocks.

## What they do

One block at a time from the chests, shaken through the sieve, and what comes out goes into the chests:

| Block | What can come out |
|---|---|
| Gravel | flint, iron and gold nuggets, coal, raw copper, lapis, amethyst shards, now and then an emerald or a diamond |
| Sand | cactus, dead bushes, nuggets, clay, a glass bottle, prismarine shards, a nautilus shell |
| Dirt | seeds, potatoes, carrots, bone meal, saplings |
| Soul sand | quartz, nether wart, gold nuggets, bones, glowstone dust, a ghast tear |

With Cobblemon installed, now and then an evolution stone turns up too. Each block takes a moment, quicker as they level
up.

## How the sifter fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Mason](mason.md) crushes cobblestone into gravel and gravel into sand; a [Miner](miner.md) digs up
  gravel and dirt.
- **Feeds:** flint for a [Fletcher](fletcher.md)'s arrows, clay for bricks, seeds for the [Farmer](farmer.md). The
  [Porter](porter.md) takes the finds to the storehouse.
- **Stops when:** there's nothing to sift ("needs gravel, sand, dirt or soul sand to sift"). They then ask for gravel on
  the Storehouse's requests board.

## Trading

Sifters buy gravel and sand, and sell flint, iron and gold nuggets, amethyst shards, lapis and, at Master, a diamond.
