# M38 design note: Cobblemon depth (2.2)

Written for ROADMAP 38.1 (2026-10-17). The roadmap items 38.2 to 38.10 hold the full specs and their tests; this note
is the overview the owner reviews and the map the lanes build from. Lanes don't wait for the owner's reply; anything
he changes goes back into the items.

**In one line:** three of the pack's Pokémon mods come into village life: the village knows the **raid dens** around
it, and you can hire its trainers to help you raid them; more village trainers **Mega Evolve**, wear a Mega
Bracelet so you can see who, and the village can cut Mega Stones and Key Stones and raise a Mega Shrine; and the Chef,
the Camp Cook, the Innkeeper and the farmers cook, serve and grow **Cobblecuisine**'s dishes.

Everything here needs its mod. Without Cobblemon Raid Dens there are no dens; without Mega Showdown no Megas, bracelets
or shrine; without Cobblecuisine the kitchens cook what they cook today. Nothing breaks and nothing is logged as an
error when a mod is missing. It ships as **2.2**, behind `Expansions.M38` (off on `main` until the release that
finishes the milestone).

## What you'll see (the short version)

### Raid dens (Cobblemon Raid Dens)

1. **The village knows its dens (38.2).** A glowing raid den crystal within 160 blocks of a Village Hall shows on the
   village map as a small crystal mark and on the hall's **Defence** page as a line: "Raid den, tier 3, Fire, active"
   (or "resting" once it has been beaten and hasn't come back yet). A den that's broken or gone drops off the list.
2. **Hire raid helpers (38.1a, the owner's change of 2026-10-19).** The village never raids a den by itself any more:
   the raid is yours, and its trainers help. On the Defence page, next to each glowing den, **Hire a raid helper**
   costs 5 emeralds (or CobbleDollars) and hires the village's best free trainer; click again for another, up to 3.
   They follow you. When you join the raid at that den, each one hands you a cheer (an Attack, Defense or Heal Cheer,
   the raid mod's own items), once a raid. Then they stand round the crystal and their Pokémon chip away at the boss
   every few seconds (a Master more than a Novice), but never below a quarter of its health: the finishing blows are
   yours. Win, and the chronicle says so: "Jesse and Master Trainer Mira's band beat the tier 3 Fire raid den". Won,
   lost, or a day later, they walk home.
3. **Gone: Den Wardens (38.3) and the village's solo challenges (38.4).** A guard standing watch at a den, and a
   trainer beating a den alone with the loot coming home, were our ideas; the owner didn't want the village raiding
   alone and didn't see the point of the warden, so both were taken out when the helpers came in.

### Megas (Mega Showdown)

4. **More trainers Mega Evolve (38.5).** Today only Masters carry a Mega Stone. Now also: the Trainer Leaders of a
   City, the trainers in the Festival Cup's final, and the Stadium Day's visiting champions. Every village trainer who
   can Mega Evolve wears a **Mega Bracelet** on their outfit (a gold band with the rainbow Key Stone, drawn the way
   vanilla draws its villager outfit layers), so you can tell who before you battle them.
5. **Mega Stones and Key Stones from the village (38.6).** The Jeweller cuts them: a Mega Stone Crystal (or Key Stone
   ore) with gold and emeralds becomes a Key Stone, or the Mega Stone of a Pokémon the village's trainers use. Miners
   keep the Key Stone ore and Mega meteorite ores they dig for the Jeweller instead of dumping them. A player who beats
   every Trainer Leader in a realm once gets a **Mega Bracelet** from the capital's hall.
6. **The Mega Shrine (38.7).** A new decoration at the Blueprint Table (listed only when Mega Showdown is installed):
   Mega Showdown's pedestal on a dais of Mega meteorite bricks. On festival days a village trainer Mega Evolves their
   ace by the pedestal; villagers off work gather to watch and go home happier (+3 mood).

### Cobblecuisine

7. **On the Chef's menu (38.8).** The Chef cooks Cobblecuisine's dishes from their own crafting recipes, with the
   makings from the store: salads and sandwiches from level 1, curries from level 3, cakes from level 4 and up, and keeps
   16 of each. Each dish counts as its own kind of food, so a village eating them has a more varied diet (better moods).
   The Camp Cook adds the curries to the camp menu, and the Innkeeper serves the drinks (Bouncy Brionne Soda, Teddiursa
   Iced Coffee, Fruit Punch).
8. **Bean farms (38.9).** When a dish on the menu wants a colour of bean and the store holds fewer than 16, farmers plant
   Cobblecuisine's beans on their fields and harvest them when ripe, into the store like any crop.
9. **Dishes for partners and luxuries (38.10).** Partner Pokémon get a Poképuff or a shake from the store once a day
   (their treat), and two dishes become luxuries: the Deluxe shakes for Burghers every 4 days, the Clodsire Eclair for
   Nobles every 8.

### Data files, one example each

**`data/aliveworkplace/raid_den_rules/default.json`** (38.2 to 38.4): what a village does with the dens. One file;
a datapack can replace it.

```json
{
  "block": "cobblemonraiddens:raid_crystal_block",
  "reach": 160,
  "properties": {"active": "is_active", "tier": "raid_tier", "type": "raid_type"},
  "tiers": ["tier_one", "tier_two", "tier_three", "tier_four", "tier_five", "tier_six", "tier_seven"],
  "challenge": {"leader_max_tier": 3, "master_max_tier": 4, "player_nearby": 8},
  "loot": "cobblemonraiddens:raid/tier/{tier}",
  "warden_cheers": ["cobblemonraiddens:cheer_attack", "cobblemonraiddens:cheer_defense", "cobblemonraiddens:cheer_heal"],
  "raid_advancements": ["cobblemonraiddens:join_raid", "cobblemonraiddens:tier_one", "cobblemonraiddens:tier_seven"]
}
```

(`raid_advancements` lists all seven tiers in the real file; shortened here.)

**`data/aliveworkplace/luxury_recipes/mega_keystone.json`** (38.6): the Jeweller's Key Stone, in the existing luxury
recipe format plus `requires`.

```json
{
  "job": "aliveworkplace:jeweller",
  "level": 3,
  "requires": "mega_showdown",
  "inputs": [{"item": "mega_showdown:keystone_ore", "count": 1}, {"item": "minecraft:gold_ingot", "count": 2}, {"item": "minecraft:emerald", "count": 4}],
  "output": {"id": "mega_showdown:keystone", "count": 1},
  "ticks": 200,
  "enabled": true
}
```

A Mega Stone recipe is the same with `mega_showdown:mega_stone_crystal` in, and `"output": {"tag":
"mega_showdown:mega_stone", "for": "village_trainers"}`: the Jeweller picks the stone of a Pokémon on one of the
village's trainers' teams that the store doesn't hold yet.

**`data/aliveworkplace/cuisine_dishes/salads.json`** (38.8): the dish list, by job level. Files are merged; an id
that doesn't exist (Cobblecuisine missing or renamed) is skipped quietly.

```json
{
  "requires": "cobblecuisine",
  "dishes": [
    {"id": "cobblecuisine:mixed_vegetable_salad", "level": 1, "group": "salad"},
    {"id": "cobblecuisine:sweet_jam_sandwich", "level": 1, "group": "sandwich"},
    {"id": "cobblecuisine:bean_medley_curry", "level": 3, "group": "curry", "camp": true},
    {"id": "cobblecuisine:fruit_honey_cake", "level": 4, "group": "cake"},
    {"id": "cobblecuisine:lemon_soda", "level": 2, "group": "drink", "inn": true}
  ]
}
```

`group` is what `Diet` counts as a kind of food (38.8); `camp` puts it on the Camp Cook's menu, `inn` on the
Innkeeper's. The `[LEGACY]` dishes Cobblecuisine still ships are left off the lists.
A dish is cooked from the recipe whose **result** it is, found through the recipe manager: recipe ids don't always
match the dish (`cobblecuisine:cake_honey_fruit` makes `fruit_honey_cake`, and each deluxe shake has six recipes).
**As built (38.8):** five files (`salads`, `sandwiches`, `drinks`, `curries`, `cakes`, 27 dishes); the drinks at level 2
and a Master's flan (group `dessert`) at 5. The Camp Cook's curries are `camp_menu` files rather than a `camp` flag
here. Bean Medley Curry is left off: its recipe asks for `#cobblemon:mint_leaf`, which Cobblemon 1.7.3 doesn't have,
so it can't be cooked (`CobblemonDepthCompatTests.cuisineDishListIdsExist` checks every listed dish can be).

**As built (38.9):** `farm/BeanFarms` (core) reads `cobblecuisine:bean_seeds`, `cobblecuisine:bean_crop` and its `age`
by name; the beans wanted are the `#cobblecuisine:beans` items of the listed dishes' own recipes (any level). Its own
switch `beanFarms`; with `cuisineDishes` off nothing wants beans. The crop's loot picks the colour.

**As built (38.10):** `work/PartnerTreats` (core) feeds, from `LuxuryWork.store`, one `#aliveworkplace:partner_treats`
item (the five Poké Puffs and the 18 shakes, optional tag entries) to each Pokémon `Partners.countDay` counts that day,
for the Rancher's 6 friendship; its day is `aliveworkplace_treat_day` in the Pokémon's persistent data (there was no
existing treat field). The luxuries `deluxe_shake` (`#aliveworkplace:deluxe_shakes`, every 4) and `clodsire_eclair`
(every 8) carry `fabric:load_conditions` and the new optional luxury field `"switch": "cuisineDishes"`; they are Burgher
and Noble wants, and the hall's Classes page leaves out a want whose luxury isn't loaded. Switch `cuisineDishes`, as
the table says.

### Fallbacks

- **A mod missing:** its features don't exist. No dens on the map or the Defence page, no raid helper button;
  no bracelet layer, extra Mega holders, stone recipes or shrine at the table; the menus, diet, camp, inn, farms and
  luxuries are today's. Data files say `requires` and are skipped quietly.
- **M28 off (trainers, partners, the Camp Cook):** no raid helpers (no trainers), no partner treats, no camp curries.
- **M34 off (the Jeweller, luxuries):** the stone recipes and the dish luxuries simply don't run.
- **M35 off (the Stadium):** no visiting champions to hold stones.
- **Raid Dens' raids out of reach (a newer version renamed what 38.1a reads):** the helpers still follow and cheer,
  but don't hurt the boss, and a lost raid is only noticed when the day is up.
- **Cobblemon 1.8 runs (`-Pcobblemon18`):** Raid Dens swaps to 0.13.2 (its build for 1.8). Cobblecuisine has no build
  for 1.8; 2.0.1 asks only for Cobblemon 1.7.0 or newer, so the 1.8 runs keep it, and the id test says at once if it
  doesn't load there.

### Other decisions taken (not the owner's)

- **Dens are found when their chunk loads**, never by scanning: a den nobody has been near isn't known, which matches
  what a villager could know.
- **A raid helper's cheer is one item per helper per raid**, never a stack, so it can't be farmed (38.1a).
- **Mega Stones are cut only for Pokémon the village's trainers use**, so the Jeweller never floods the store with all
  of them.
- **Seven bean colours**, as the mod has them.
- **Local compat runs** on the 7 GB dev box need `-PcompatHeap=2G` (and `flock /home/claude/mc.lock` when lanes
  share the box) now that the runtime holds two more mods; CI keeps the default heap.

### As built in 38.2

- The known dens are saved per dimension (`aliveworkplace_raid_dens`), not on the hall: a den is listed for every hall
  within 160 blocks of it, so two villages near one den both see it.
- The Defense page row is slots 28 to 34 (up to seven dens, nearest first); row 37 to 43 stays free for 38.3's
  warden buttons. The map's banner is magenta (`Kind.RAID_DEN`), only for dens on the map's 128-block square.
- `Platform.onChunkLoad` is new (Fabric's `ServerChunkEvents.CHUNK_LOAD`); the dens found there wait for the level's
  next tick before the hall search.

### As built in 38.3 (removed in 38.1a)

- Wardens are saved per dimension (`aliveworkplace_den_wardens`: guard id, den, the players cheered this raid), not as
  a guard attachment; `glowAnnounced` wasn't needed, since the line is written on the change from resting to glowing.
- The raid is noticed through a mixin on `PlayerAdvancements.award` (`join_raid` or a tier advancement completed
  within 16 blocks of a watched den).
- Wild Pokémon are found through the `WildPokemon` extension (Cobblemon fills it); without it the warden only stands
  watch.

### As built in 38.4 (removed in 38.1a)

- `lastDenChallenge` and `tiredUntil` are saved per dimension (`aliveworkplace_den_challenges`: per hall, per
  trainer), not in the hall's data or a trainer attachment.
- The odds: 45% at one rank over the den's tier, 15% a rank more or less, kept between 10% and 90%; a Master (rank 5)
  against a tier 4 den has 45%, a Leader (rank 4) against tier 3 the same.
- Raid Dens' tier tables need its own loot context (the Raid Pouch being opened), so `compat/raiddens` reads
  `RaidLootContexts`' three public fields by name and fills the `RaidDenLoot` extension; Raid Dens isn't a compile
  dependency. Without them a won den still goes dark, with no loot.
- "A player nearby can come along and watch": the players in the village or within 32 blocks of the den are told when
  the trainer sets off and how it ended; the challenge itself is settled at once.

### As built in 38.5

- Who can Mega Evolve is decided in core (`trainer/MegaBracelets`), not by a saved field: a Master (as before, not
  switched), and with `villageMegas` a Leader whose nearest hall is a City, a Stadium visitor, or a trainer (or the
  delegate standing in) in a Cup final still to be fought today. The bracelet is a client layer told by a small packet
  every 40 ticks when it changes, and when a player starts seeing the trainer.
- In a Cup final the Mega must fit the theme; with no Mega Evolution that does (the Little Cup) the team stays as it
  is. The simulated villager-against-villager bouts have no Mega Evolution, only real battles against a player do.
- A Mega Stone holder's AI Mega Evolves at any tier (a Novice can reach a Cup final).

### As built in 38.6

- The recipes are `mega_keystone`, `mega_stone` (the Crystal block), `mega_stone_cut` (the plain `mega_showdown:mega_stone`
  a Crystal drops without Silk Touch) and `mega_stone_meteorite` (4 Mega meteorite ores, level 4), loaded with Fabric's
  `fabric:all_mods_loaded` condition (Cobblemon and Mega Showdown) rather than a `requires` field, and switched by
  `"when": "village_megas"` (`villageMegas` with Jewellers on). The luxury format gained `keep` (2 Key Stones, 1 of
  each Mega Stone) and `when`.
- "The Pokémon the village's trainers use": the home teams of the trainers round the nearest hall (48 blocks without
  one), nearest first, from `CobblemonMegas.teamStones` (the `MegaStones.TeamStones` extension), cached per trainer.
- The Miner's finds are the item tag `aliveworkplace:jeweller_finds` (Key Stone Ore, Key Stone, Mega Crystal, Mega
  Stone, `#aliveworkplace:mega_meteorite_ores`), taken to the store at every drop-off and when the quarry ends.
- `megaBracelet` lives in `aliveworkplace_leader_bracelets` on the overworld (per player: the villages beaten, the
  realms given), with the villages known to have a Leader of their own (`leaderHalls`, from each hall's round).

### As built in 38.7

- The shrine (`aliveworkplace:mega_shrine`, 7x6x7, beauty 2) is `StarterBlueprints.MEGA_ONLY`, listed by
  `BlueprintLibrary.megaOk` while `mega_showdown:pedestal` is a block and `villageMegas` is on (no `blueprint_unlocks`
  `requires_blocks` field was needed), and mapped to `villageMegas` in the expansion switches.
- Mega Showdown has no meteorite stairs or slabs, so the dais is edged with deepslate brick steps.
- The show (`trainer/MegaShrines`, called first by `Festivals.tick`): the festival's first 1,500 ticks; the crowd 4
  blocks in front of the dais; the trainer able to Mega Evolve first; once a day per village; watchers within 12 blocks
  keep `mega_show_day` (+3 mood for `Festivals.MOOD_DAYS`). The Mega Evolution is light, sound and a chat line.
### As built in 38.1a (with 38.3a and 38.4a)

The owner (2026-10-19): "raid dens must not be done by villagers alone; the player can hire villagers to assist in the
raid", "the raid is done with villagers as assistance; if not possible to code, scratch it entirely", and of the Den
Warden, "not sure what this is for, may be useless". The decision: the village's solo challenge (38.4) and the warden
(38.3: posting, standing watch, shooing, the "glows again" line that only watched dens had) are gone; the dens list
(38.2) stays; the player hires the village's trainers as raid helpers instead.

- **Who:** the village's trainers (any rank, Trainer Leaders included), the best free one first; a trainer hired by
  someone, or walking home from a raid, isn't free. Up to 3 per player, for one den at a time; a resting den takes none.
- **Price:** `denHelperPrice` emeralds each (5; 100 CobbleDollars an emerald with CobbleDollars), a config number
  rather than a data file: one number, and the server owner sets it on the settings screen.
- **Following:** a walk target on the player every 5 ticks, a hop to them from 24 blocks (as the freed carter does),
  and the Training Post walk held back while hired. They're the village's own trainers (no new villager is spawned),
  so no profession changes and no `refreshBrain` is needed (B117 is about spawned guards).
- **The raid is noticed through Raid Dens' statistics, not its advancements:** `raids_joined` / `raids_hosted` on
  joining and `tier_<n>_completed` on a win are awarded every raid (a mixin on `ServerPlayer.awardStat`), while the
  `join_raid` and tier advancements 38.3 used come only once in a player's life. A raid counts as the den's when the
  player is within 16 blocks of it, or was within the last minute (raids are fought in the mod's own dimension).
- **Cheers:** one per helper per raid, cycling Attack, Defense, Heal; a hiring covers one raid, so a second join in
  the same raid (or a host and a join) gives nothing more.
- **Boss damage is built.** Raid Dens 0.11.3 keeps its running raids in `RaidHelper.ACTIVE_RAIDS` (public, raid id
  to `RaidInstance`); `RaidInstance` has public `isPlayerIn(ServerPlayer)`, `getCurrentHealth()`, `getMaxHealth()`,
  `getRaidState()` and `healBoss(float share)`, which adds that share of the full health (clamped to the maximum) and
  sends every battle the new health. `compat/raiddens/RaidDensRaids` reads them by name (no compile dependency) and
  fills the `RaidAssist` extension. Every 5 seconds each helper takes `0.006 x rank / den tier x (1 + team's average
  level / 100)` of the boss's full health (a Master with no team, at a tier 3 den: 1% a strike, so three Masters about
  36% a minute). The win is only decided inside a player's battle when the health reaches 0, so the helpers never take
  it under a quarter (`FLOOR`): the last blows are the player's. A raid that ends shows as `SUCCESS` (won) or `FAILED`
  / `CANCELLED` (lost) and the helpers go home.
- **Tier limit (decided):** helpers join any tier, but a helper more than one rank under the den's tier (a Novice at
  tier 3 and up, an Apprentice at 4 and up, ... a Master at 7) only cheers: their Pokémon can't scratch that boss.
- **Their Pokémon aren't sent out on the ground:** our trainers' Pokémon exist only in battle, so the helpers stand
  round the crystal and their blows show as the boss bar dropping and sparks at them.
- **Ending:** a won raid writes the chronicle ("<player> and <best helper>'s band beat the tier 3 Fire raid den") and
  gives each helper a battle's experience with the win bonus; won, lost, the raid gone, or a day after the hiring, the
  helpers walk home (moved there after 2 minutes if stuck). Switched off, anyone hired walks home.
- **Old saves:** the 38.3 and 38.4 files stay on disk unread (a posted warden is a guard again); the old config keys
  are dropped as the file loads (Gson ignores unknown keys). 38.4's loot-table reader went with it.

1. **Tier 4 and up are for players only.** Village trainers challenge dens up to tier 3, Leaders and Masters alike
   (38.4: change "up to tier 4 for a Master" to tier 3).
2. **The Mega Bracelet is given once per player per realm** (as written).
