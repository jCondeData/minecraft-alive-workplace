# /workplace quests

```infobox
type: Command
expansion: M31
Syntax: /workplace quests, /workplace quests untrack
Who: everyone
Shows: your open quests in the village nearest you
categories: Commands, Quests
related: quest-journal.md, quests.md, command-standing.md
```

The [quest journal](quest-journal.md) in chat, for when you're far from the hall.

## /workplace quests

Lists your quests in the village nearest you, one line each: the quest, who asked, the objective and how far along it
is, and a clickable **[Track]** (or **[Untrack]** on the one you track). Click [Track] and that quest's bar appears at
the top of your screen. With nothing open: "No open quests in the village nearest you."

## /workplace quests untrack

Stops tracking: "You're no longer tracking a quest." The bar goes away.

## How it fits with the rest

It's the same list as the journal's tabs. A quest that closed since you last looked says "That quest isn't open any
more."
