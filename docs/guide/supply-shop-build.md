A smart white shop with a blue-green roof, a **Shop Counter** inside and barrels of stock along the walls. It's the Master builder's own blueprint, and the grandest of the starter builds.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). A Master **builder** sells it for 20 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Supply Shop, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Supply Shop II** adds a storeroom behind and the shopkeeper's bedroom upstairs.
- **Supply Shop III** adds a post office annex with a **Mailbox**.

## What it does for the village

A villager without a job who comes by takes the Shop Counter and becomes its **Shopkeeper**: open the counter to set your prices and keep the goods in the chests beside it, and other players buy by trading with the shopkeeper. At tier III, sneak-right-click a villager by the Mailbox with **paper** to make them the **Postman**, who carries the mail and, when there's none, hauls between your chests. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a yellow banner (stores, shops and markets).
