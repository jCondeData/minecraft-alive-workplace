The Florist grows the flowers a village needs: for dyes, for the builders' flower beds and for every empty flower pot in
town.

## Getting a florist

Stand a villager by a **composter** and sneak-right-click them with **any flower**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the
composter, and some **bone meal** in it.

## What they do

The garden is the grass within 5 blocks of the composter. The florist spreads bone meal from the chest on it, and the
flowers of that biome come up (any grass that comes with them is weeded out). Bone meal on a tall flower (a sunflower,
lilac, rose bush or peony) gives another one. The small flowers are picked and put in the chests, until there are 64
there; then the florist stops growing more.

Between times they walk round the village and put a flower in every **empty flower pot** within 24 blocks of their
composter.

Over their head: how many flowers they've grown, and whether they're growing, picking, filling a pot or putting the
flowers away.

## How the florist fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Composter](composter.md) makes the bone meal.
- **Feeds:** a [Leatherworker](leatherworker-dyer.md) makes dyes from the flowers when a builder needs something
  colored, and a [Builder](builder.md) takes the flowers a blueprint's beds and pots want. A
  [Beekeeper](beekeeper.md) plants flowers round the hives from their own chests, so a few of yours there help.
- **Stops when:** the chests have no bone meal ("needs bone meal to grow flowers"), or there's no chest by the
  composter.
