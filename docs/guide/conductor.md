The Conductor runs the village's railway. With the Create mod installed, a villager without a job standing by a
**Train Station** takes it as their workstation and becomes the village's Conductor: you'll know them by the maroon
railwayman's cap with its gold band and badge, the maroon tunic with two rows of brass buttons, a gold watch chain and
a ticket punch at the belt.

## Getting a Conductor

Build the village a [Train Station](create-trains.md) (it's at the Blueprint Table), or place a Create station near the
Village Hall yourself, and have a villager without a job nearby. They walk over to it and take the job by themselves,
the way a farmer takes a composter. One station, one Conductor.

## Driving the train

When the village puts its own train together at the station, the Conductor takes the seat. Without a Conductor a
villager without a job drives it, as before; a villager with another job is never taken off it. The chronicle says who
took the seat.

As the train pulls in at a station, the Conductor calls the stop, once, over their head and in the chat of anyone
standing near the station:

> Ada: Now arriving at Ashford! All change, mind the gap.

While there's no train yet, or after you break it up, the Conductor waits at the station like any villager at their
workstation, and takes the seat of the next train the village builds.

## Trading

The Conductor sells what a railway needs, for emeralds: rails and powered rails, Create track, and as they grow in
rank train schedules, track signals, railway casing and, as a Master, train controls. Novices also buy coal.

## Good to know

- **Without Create** there is no Conductor, and a world that had one loads them as a villager without a job.
- With the **Village trains** setting off, a villager without a job no longer takes a station; a Conductor already
  hired keeps the job.
