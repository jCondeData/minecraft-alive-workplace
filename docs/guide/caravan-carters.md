Caravans used to travel unseen. Now you can watch them go: a carter leading two pack llamas out of the village, or
another village's caravan coming in.

## Leaving

By the [Storehouse](storehouse.md) stands a carter, dressed like a [Porter](porter.md), whose name reads "Thornholm's
caravan". They hold two llamas on leads, each carrying a chest and wearing a carpet in the village's color. They walk off
toward the village the caravan is bound for, and at the edge of your village they're gone.

## Arriving

The other village's carter, in its own color, comes in at the edge of your village from the side that village lies on
and walks to the Storehouse. The llamas stand still for six seconds while the goods are unloaded (you hear the chests
open and close), then they head back out the way they came.

## The colors

A village with a Village Banner wears its banner's base color. One without gets one of the sixteen colors, picked from
where its hall stands, so it always wears the same.

## How it fits in

- **What it shows:** the caravans of your [trade routes](trade-and-caravans.md) and their
  [sales](caravan-sales.md). They're only a sight: right-clicking the carter or a llama does nothing, nothing can hurt
  them, and they leave nothing behind.
- **What stops it:** **Caravans You Can See** off in the settings screen: no carters, and any out walking are taken
  away. The goods travel exactly the same.
