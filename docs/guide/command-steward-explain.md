Wondering why the [Steward](steward.md) wants a Stone House and not a well? Stand near the
[Village Hall](village-hall.md) and type:

`/workplace steward explain`

## What it shows

A header ("The Steward's rules for Thornholm: 12 of 77 hold"), then every rule, each marked **[held]** or **[not held]**
with its priority and every condition's number, such as:

- "Villagers without a bed: 3 (at least 2)"
- "Food in store: 14 of the 40 wanted (2 for each grown-up)"
- "Beauty: 1 (under 3)"

A rule that held but won't be acted on says why: "Not before the village is a Town", "Needs the mod cobblemon",
"Done 1 of the 1 times it may be" or "Resting after it was last done (days left: 2)". Each rule ends with its reason.

Last come **today's wishes**, numbered in order, or "No wishes ranked today: the Steward ranks them each morning after
his rounds".

![Explain: the last rules and today's wishes](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/steward_rules/still-2.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- It reads the same numbers as the hall's **What next?** page and [the Steward's rules](steward-rules.md), so it
  explains both.
- With no hall near you: "There's no Village Hall near you".
- Changes nothing: to act on a wish, use [his desk](stewards-desk.md).
