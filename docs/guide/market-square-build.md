The heart of a busy village: a paved square with a fountain in the middle, kiosks under red, white and blue awnings,
benches, flower beds, crates of melons and pumpkins, and the village **bell**. It's the biggest decoration and the one
that brings traders to town.

## Building it

Take a Market Square blueprint from the Blueprint Table and place it in the middle of the village. It needs about 300
blocks, mostly stone and spruce, plus wool and carpet in three colors and a few flowers. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Market days.** A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and a finished Market Square holds a
  [market](market-days.md) once a week: traveling traders come to the square in the morning and stay until nightfall.
  No square, no market.
- **Beauty.** It adds 5 to the village's [beauty](decorations.md), the most of any decoration.
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **The bell.** Its bell is a real village bell: villagers gather round it, and [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md) are
  held at the bell when the village has no Chapel.
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) with the stores and shops.
- **Turned off.** With [Market Days](settings-festivals-and-market-days.md) switched off, the square is still a
  decoration but no traders come.
