The [Village Hall](village-hall.md) can draw you a map of the village. Open the hall, go to the map page, and it draws
the village on an **empty map** from your inventory: "Map of Oakvale".

## What's on it

- the land round the hall as it is today, centered on the hall, a block a pixel;
- a **banner** for the hall and for every building the builders finished there, **colored by what it is**, with the
  workplaces named;
- the legend on the map's tooltip, and the day it was drawn.

The kinds are homes; school, library and research; healing and graves; stores, shops and markets; farms, ranches and
gardens; towers, barracks and gates; workshops; wells, fountains and the like; and other buildings. Lamps, benches and
plain wall sections are left off.

The map is finished and locked, like one copied at a cartography table: draw a new one when the village has grown.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** your builders. Only buildings a builder finished get a banner (see [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md)).
- Hang it in an item frame by the hall for a village notice board, or take it along to find your way back.
