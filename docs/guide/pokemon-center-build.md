A bright hall with a glass front under a red roof: Cobblemon's **Healing Machine** on the counter, a **PC** beside it, potions and benches. It comes in three looks: the classic Center, a timber **Mountain Lodge** under a steep red gable with a porch, and a flat-roofed sandstone **Sunny Plaza** with a striped awning.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). It's listed only when Cobblemon is installed. A Journeyman **nurse** sells it too, for 14 emeralds, in one of its three looks. In a village of Village rank or higher without one, the hall's **What next?** page suggests building it.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Pokémon Center, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Pokémon Center II** adds a lodge behind it (a trade corner with a **Shop Counter**, four beds upstairs) and a garden with two **Pasture Blocks**. The Lodge and Plaza looks have their own II: the Lodge grows a two-story wing and a pen, the Plaza a wing and a courtyard.

## What it does for the village

The Healing Machine heals your team, and the PCs are there when you need a box. Sneak-right-click a villager by the Shop Counter of tier II with a **Poké Ball** to make them a **Pokémon Trader**, or with an emerald a shopkeeper. The beds upstairs are free beds the village can grow into. See [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a pink banner (healing and graves).
