In a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), every grown villager has a **mood**, a score from 0 to 100. The
hall's list shows it ("Happy (82)", "Content (55)", "Unhappy (24)") with the reasons behind it, so you can see at a
glance who needs a bed or a better meal.

## What makes a mood

Everyone starts at 50. Then:

| Good | Points | Bad | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| fed today | +15 | hungry | -20 |
| a [varied diet](food-and-diet.md) | +10 | the same food every day | -10 |
| a bed of their own | +15 | no bed | -15 |
| a fine home (a tier II building) | +5 more | | |
| a grand home (tier III) | +10 more | | |
| a job | +10 | no job | -5 |
| [Cheerful](traits.md) by nature | +10 | [ill](sickness.md) | -20 |
| [decorations](decorations.md) near home | +5 a point of beauty, up to +15 | mourning a partner | -15 |
| in love (+5) or married (+10), with the partner nearby | | | |
| enjoyed a [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) | +15 for two days | | |
| glad of a [quest](quests.md) done for the village | varies | | |

**Happy** villagers (75 and up) work 7% faster. **Unhappy** ones (under 30) work 15% slower. In between they're
content and work at the usual pace.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** the store ([Food and diet](food-and-diet.md)), beds and homes, jobs, [decorations](decorations.md) within
  16 blocks of their bed, [couples](couples-and-weddings.md), [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) and
  [quests](quests.md).
- **Affects:** the [work pace](work-pace.md) of each worker. Most of what makes a mood (food, beds) is also what the
  village [needs](village-needs.md) to be well kept and to [grow](babies-and-growth.md).
- **What spoils it:** an empty store, too few beds, sickness and loss. [Chatter](chatter.md) gives you hints: "I wish
  I had a bed of my own."
- **Turned off:** the [Moods](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch. With moods off, nobody is sped up or slowed
  down by how they feel.
