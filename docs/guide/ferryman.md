The Ferryman sells tickets between your villages and rows you there.

## Getting a ferryman

Craft a **Travel Post** (a sign over planks and a boat). Name it in an anvil first, say "Riverside", then place it. A
villager without a job takes it by themselves and becomes its Ferryman. Many villages already have a **Ferry House**.

## Traveling

Right-click travel posts to add them to the ones you know. A ferryman sells a **Travel Ticket** to every other post you
know, an emerald for every 256 blocks (16 at most; 8 to another dimension). With CobbleDollars installed, right-click the ferryman with an empty hand for
tickets paid in CobbleDollars (sneak for the usual trades).

Use a ticket within 16 blocks of any travel post: you sit in a boat, rowed off by the post's ferryman if they're close
by, the view fades, and you come to on the far side next to the ticket's post. Get out early (sneak, or take a hit) and
you land straight away. The ticket is used up on the way.

![The ferry ride](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/extras/still-3.jpg)

## How the ferryman fits in

- **Links up:** your villages, so you can visit each one's hall, shop and storehouse quickly.
- **Works beside:** the [Postman](postman.md), whose night mail reaches the villages you ferry to.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
