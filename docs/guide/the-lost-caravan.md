"The caravan from Thornholm to Ashford is a day overdue." Somewhere on the long road the wagon, its goods and its carter
went missing. Only caravans on roads longer than 300 blocks can be lost, about one in twenty, and only when neither
village already has a story running.

## Overdue

Both villages hear of it, both chronicles note what the caravan carried, and the hall that sent it hands out a **map**
with the wreck marked. The wreck lies by the road, about a third of the way along: the settlers' covered wagon on its
side, its canvas torn, crates and barrels spilled in the grass. Come within a few blocks and the chapter is done: "The
caravan's wreck: tracks lead away from the road."

## The tracks

Raiders took the wagon. Five marks of a trail lead off the road: trampled dirt and a dropped lantern, a smashed
barrel, a torn banner on a fence post, cart ruts, a crate stuck with arrows. Find them in order: at each the action bar
says which way the tracks go next. They end at a **raiders' stockade** 120 to 180 blocks on, where a Captain, two bandits
with axes and two with crossbows wait.

![A mark of the trail, the tracks pointing on](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/caravan_trail/still-2.jpg)

## The captive

The carter is alive, locked in the stockade's cage. Flip the lever by the cage and the gate opens: "You came for me!
Take me home, I'll keep up." He follows you like a rallied guard. Walk him to within 24 blocks of either village's hall.

The stockade's chest holds the lost goods. Carry every stack of them to the village they were going to and they go into
its store, for 8 emeralds and a little standing there. You don't need to: the story goes on without them.

![Tobin follows the player home](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/lost_caravan/still-4.jpg)

## How it ends

The carter settles in the village he reached, ready to take a free workstation like any newcomer. Everyone who helped
gains 150 standing in both villages and the honor **Wayfinder**, and the carter counts you as a friend.

Nobody comes for him within 7 days and he slips away and finds his own way home with nothing: the goods are lost and
the story ends badly.

## How it fits with the rest

- It grows out of [caravans](trade-and-caravans.md): the longer your trade roads, the likelier it is.
- The goods it carried never arrive until you carry them in, so the receiving village goes without.
- Your guards and a [Rally Banner](defence.md#leading-your-guards) help at the stockade.
