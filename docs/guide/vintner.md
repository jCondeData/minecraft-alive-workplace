The Vintner presses the village's drinks: cider for the Artisans, berry wine for the Burghers and aged vintage wine for
the Nobles. Every bottle goes into the village store, where households take what they need at dawn.

## Getting a Vintner

Stand a villager by a **cauldron** and sneak-right-click them with **sweet berries, glow berries or an apple**. Put a
chest within 8 blocks of the cauldron: that's where they keep their fruit, bottles and what they make. The purple
splashes over the cauldron mean they're at work, and the line over their head counts what they've made ("Vintner · 3
made").

## What they make

| Level | Drink | From |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | [Cider](cider.md) | 3 apples and a glass bottle |
| Apprentice | [Berry Wine](berry-wine.md) | 6 sweet berries (or 4 glow berries, or with Cobblemon 4 berries) and a glass bottle |
| Journeyman | [Vintage Wine](vintage-wine.md) | a Berry Wine that has aged 3 days |

A Vintner fills the store's stock orders first, then keeps the store and their own chests at **8 of each** drink their
level allows. They take their makings from their own chests, the village store and the other workers' chests (never a
builder's), and when something is missing it shows on the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s requests board, where you can
hand it over. Vintage Wine waits for a Berry Wine that is 3 days old, and each Berry Wine's tooltip counts it down:
"Pressed on day 42 · vintage in 2 days".

## Trading

At every level they buy one of what they press with (apples at Novice, sweet berries at Apprentice, glass bottles at
Journeyman, glow berries at Expert, apples again at Master) and sell the drinks of their level: 2 Cider for an emerald,
a Berry Wine for 2 emeralds, a Vintage Wine for 5 (an Expert sells 2 Berry Wine for 3, a Master 2 Vintage Wine for 8). From **Journeyman** they also sell
the [Winery](winery-build.md)'s blueprint for 12 emeralds.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** apples from an [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md), berries from a [Berry Farm](berry-farm-build.md)
  or a [Farmer](farmer.md), and glass bottles. An [Artisan](artisan.md) household makes every Vintner 10% faster.
- **What it affects:** [Artisans](artisan.md) want Cider, [Burghers](burgher.md) need Berry Wine every 2 days and
  [Nobles](noble.md) need Vintage Wine every 2 days. Without Berry Wine in the store no household can rise past Artisan. See
  [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
- **What stops it:** no chest by the cauldron, no bottles, or the **Vintners** switch off in the settings (the job
  can't be picked, and Vintners already hired stand idle). A broken cauldron leaves them jobless, as with any job.
