"The Master who stood up on the day the village became a City and wrote down how it all began."

## How they come

The day your village first rises to **City** ([ranks](ranks.md)), its most experienced Master is seized by the
**Founder's mood**: they ask for a block of gold, a block of emeralds and a diamond in the chest by their workstation.
Bring them within three days and they write the **Charter** of your village, a book of its story drawn from the
chronicle, and become the Founder, a Mythic Legend announced to the whole server. If the mood passes with nothing made,
it goes after a week to the next most experienced Master.

## What they do

- **The statue.** Your builders raise the Founder's statue on open ground near the hall, with materials from their
  chests: a stepped plinth, a copper plaque and the Founder in stone with a hand raised. While it stands it's worth 5
  beauty and everyone in the village is 5 happier.
- **New villages.** Every 7 days the hall's owner is given a [Founder's Wagon](founders-wagon.md) to found a new village
  of their own.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: everything that makes a City, and your [builders](builder.md) for the statue.
- **What they affect**: [moods](moods.md), [decorations](decorations.md) (the statue's beauty) and how many villages
  you have.
- **What stops them**: a strike stops the wagons; the statue keeps its beauty while it stands.
