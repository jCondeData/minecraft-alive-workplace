The Tailor dresses the village. Every class above Peasant needs a new set of clothes every 8 days, and the Tailor is
the one who sews them.

## Getting a Tailor

Stand a villager by a **loom** and sneak-right-click them with **string**. (Shears bring the [Shepherd](shepherd.md)
back.) Put a chest within 8 blocks of the loom for their wool, leather and what they make.

## What they make

| Level | Clothes | From |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | [Work Clothes](work-clothes.md) | 3 wool of any colors, 2 leather and a string |
| Apprentice | [Fine Clothes](fine-clothes.md) | 4 dyed wool (any colors but white), a string and 2 gold nuggets |
| Journeyman | [Noble Robes](noble-robes.md) | 5 wool of one rich color (purple, blue, red or black), a rabbit hide and a gold ingot |

They keep the village store in **8 of each** set they know, taking wool from their own chests, the store and the
other workers' chests (never a builder's). With dye to hand they dye white wool themselves. A missing making shows on
the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s requests board, where you can hand it over.

## Trading

At every level they buy wool, string or leather, and sell their clothes: Work Clothes for 3 emeralds, Fine Clothes for
6, Noble Robes for 14 (12 from a Master). From **Journeyman** they also sell the
[Tailor's Shop](tailors-shop-build.md)'s blueprint for 12 emeralds.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** wool from a [Shepherd](shepherd.md), leather and rabbit hide from cows and rabbits (a [Butcher](butcher.md)
  keeps both breeding), dye from the [Florist](florist.md)'s flowers, gold from a [Miner](miner.md). An
  [Artisan](artisan.md) household makes every Tailor 10% faster.
- **What it affects:** [Artisans](artisan.md) need Work Clothes, [Burghers](burgher.md) Fine Clothes and
  [Nobles](noble.md) Noble Robes, every 8 days. No Tailor, no household above Peasant for long. See
  [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
- **What stops it:** no chest by the loom, no wool, or the **Tailors** switch off in the settings (the job can't be
  picked, and Tailors already hired stand idle).
