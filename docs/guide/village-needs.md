A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) has needs, and how well they're met is its **wellbeing**, from 0 to
100%. The hall's top row shows it, along with the villagers, beds, food in store and guards.

## What a village needs

- **Food.** Every grown villager eats once a day from the store ([Food and diet](food-and-diet.md)). The share who are
  fed counts most: 40% of the wellbeing.
- **Beds.** A bed of their own for everyone: 30%.
- **Safety.** Half of it is guards (one for every ten villagers), half is light by the beds (a torch or lantern
  within a few blocks of each). Together: 30%.

On top of that come **[decorations](decorations.md)** (1% a point of beauty, up to 10%), the scholars' **Hearth**
[research](research.md) (10% a level) and **Cheerful** [villagers](traits.md) (1% each, up to 5%). While a
[bandit camp](bandit-camps.md) stands nearby, the village feels less safe.

## What wellbeing does

- **The pace of all the work.** At 50% everyone works at the usual pace. Up to 25% faster when everything's right, and
  down to 20% slower when nobody is fed, housed or safe. See [Work pace](work-pace.md).
- **Growth.** A village needs at least half wellbeing to have [babies](babies-and-growth.md).
- **The treasury.** A well-kept village puts by half again as much, a badly kept one half as much
  ([Treasury](treasury.md)).

## What next?

Click the compass on the hall's screen: **What next?** lists what the village lacks most, from a builder or beds to
guards or decorations, each with how to put it right. It's the quickest way to raise wellbeing.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** farmers, fishermen and chefs (food), builders (houses and decorations), guards and torches.
- **Affects:** [work pace](work-pace.md), [growth](babies-and-growth.md) and the [treasury](treasury.md).
- Villages without a hall have no needs and work at the usual pace.
