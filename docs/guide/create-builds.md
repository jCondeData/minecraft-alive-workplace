With the Create mod installed, the **Blueprint Table** offers nine buildings with a working Create machine inside. Each
one turns the moment your builder is done: a water wheel on the side is driven by water falling from a little tank,
and the windmill's sails start as soon as the builder gives its bearing a push.

![The first three Create builds, their machines turning](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/create_builds/still-3.jpg)

## The builds

- **Watermill**: a stone-and-timber mill house whose wheel turns a millstone, with the farmer's composter.
- **Windmill**: a stone tower with 24 sails on its front and a millstone at the bottom.
- **Workshop**: a brick shop whose wheel drives a belt to a depot under a mechanical press, with the armorer's blast
  furnace.
- **Brass Foundry**: a tall brick hall with a chimney. Its wheel is geared up fast enough for a mechanical mixer over a
  basin and a blaze burner, and a belt carries things out to a depot by the door. The chef's smoker is inside.
- **Sawmill**: a timber mill with a log store on its side. A belt runs through two mechanical saws to a depot, beside
  the fletching table.
- **Crushing Mill**: two crushing wheels turning towards each other, fed by a belt over the gap. What they crush drops
  into a pit with an item drain in front. A blast furnace stands by the door.
- **Greenhouse**: a glass house with beds of wheat, carrots and potatoes, watered by two channels. A belt runs down
  the aisle to a depot, with a little cart on it carrying a mechanical harvester and a mechanical plough. Glue the cart
  to a contraption of your own and it will reap the beds. The farmer's composter is in the back corner.
- **Clock Tower**: a stone tower with a white belfry and a cuckoo clock in its front, turned by a shaft that runs up
  the middle of the tower from the wheel at its foot. It's there to look at, and the clock keeps the time.
- **Pump House**: a small brick house where the wheel turns a mechanical pump. It draws from a little pool in the floor
  that fills itself up again, and pumps the water into a tank. A cauldron makes it a leatherworker's workplace.

![The six newer Create builds](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/create_builds_more/anim.gif)

## Building one

Pick it at the Blueprint Table, place the blueprint where its front should go, fill the chests near your builder's
table with what it lists, and hand it to the builder, as for any build. The moving parts go in last and in order, so
they turn as soon as they're down (see [Contraptions from a blueprint](create-contraptions.md)).

## Good to know

- Without Create, or with the **Create builds** setting off, these buildings aren't offered at the table.
- Each has a job block inside, so a villager without a job can take it and work there.
