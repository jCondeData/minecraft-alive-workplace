A barn of spruce planks between dark oak posts on a cobblestone plinth, under a dark oak gable roof with big doors front
and back. Its floor is straw: the nursery. Behind it, a fenced paddock round Cobblemon's **Pasture Block**, with a
trough and bushes.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** when Cobblemon is installed: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. A Journeyman [Daycare Keeper](daycare-keeper.md) sells it for 14 emeralds.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes it, they sell the upgrade too (the Blueprint Table has it as well):

- **Daycare II** adds a second paddock behind the first with its own Pasture Block, and a hatchery corner on the barn's
  east side: a lean-to with nests of hay, lanterns over them, and a barrel.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click the villager by its Pasture Block with an **egg** and she becomes its
[Daycare Keeper](daycare-keeper.md), finding eggs for the pairs you leave. Like every finished building, it counts
toward the village's [rank](ranks.md).
