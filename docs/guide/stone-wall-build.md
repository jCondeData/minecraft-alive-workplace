A seven-block stretch of stone wall with battlements on top and a ladder up the inside. Place one after another to ring your village.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two).

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Stand inside the village and place each piece facing out. Wall Towers go at the ends and corners, and a Gatehouse where the road comes in.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Stone Wall, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Stone Wall II** puts timber hoardings over the battlements: a roofed walkway with murder holes.

## What it does for the village

A wall keeps the monsters out while your guards fight. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

Wall stretches aren't drawn on the Village Map.
