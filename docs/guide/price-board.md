The price board is the village's market stall: every good it trades, what it pays and asks, and a click to buy or sell.

## Reading it

Click the minecart on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen and pick the **Prices** tab. Every trade good has its
icon. A **gold star** means the village is known for it, a **red mark** that it's short of it, and a little arrow in
the corner shows how the price moved since yesterday: green up, red down, gray steady.

Hover a good to see the bundle ("A bundle of 16"), **We sell** and **We pay**, how the price moved, and which village
on its trade routes pays the most for it and which the least ("Dearer in Ashford", "Cheaper in Ridgeway",
with their prices). It also says how many bundles the Storehouse can spare.

## Trading

- **Selling:** carrying a bundle of the good (16 logs for Timber)? Click it to sell a bundle, shift-click to sell all
  the village will take. You're paid **We pay** from the village's [treasury](treasury.md).
- **Buying:** right-click (or click, if you carry none) to buy a bundle, shift-click for as many as you can pay for.
  You pay **We sell**, 10% over the price, into the treasury.

After a click, the good's tooltip and the bar above your hotbar say what came of it: "You sell 32 × Timber to Thornholm
for 2 emeralds. Its treasury can't pay for more." Each bundle moves that day's price 2%: down when you sell, up when you
buy.

**Whole emeralds.** Paid in emeralds, a trade settles to the whole emerald, and the cents stay with the village; a sale
that wouldn't come to one emerald isn't made, so shift-click to sell several at once. With CobbleDollars, trades are
exact to the cent.

**What stops a trade:** the treasury can't pay for more, the Storehouse has no room, it has no bundle to spare (it
keeps 16 of every item back), or you can't pay.

## The treasury nugget

The gold nugget in the page's top row is the village's treasury, to the cent. The hall's owner and their friends click
it to collect the whole emeralds. Since strangers' trades draw on it, only they may.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** the [Storehouse](storehouse.md) and its chests (no Storehouse with a chest, no market), and the
  workers who fill them. See [Economy](economy.md) for how prices are set.
- **Open to all:** in a [protected](village-protection.md) village a stranger's right-click on the hall opens just this
  page, so anyone can trade anywhere.
- **Cheaper with:** the realm's **Common Coin** research (see [Realm research](realm-research.md)) and the
  [Common Market](common-market.md) edict.
- **Dearer for:** your rivals in a [feud](feuds.md), who pay a quarter more and get a quarter less.
- **What stops it:** **Village Economy** off in the settings screen.
