An apple cordial for everyone in the fields and pens, the Chef included. The [Chef](chef.md) makes it after the day's menu.

## Getting it

Your [Chef](chef.md) makes it when they have nothing more pressing, from a glass bottle, an apple, wheat and sugar in the chests by their workstation,
and keeps 4 in those chests. Take one from there.

## Giving it

Right-click a grown villager it suits. They drink it and work **25% faster for 20 minutes**, a whole day; their status
line shows the minutes left. Anyone else refuses it and you keep it. A second tonic just starts the time again.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: the [Chef](chef.md), and a glass bottle, an apple, wheat and sugar in their chests.
- **What it affects**: the speed of one worker: [Farmers](farmer.md), [Orchard Keepers](orchard-keeper.md), [Florists](florist.md), [Beekeepers](beekeeper.md), [Composters](composter.md), [Shepherds](shepherd.md), [Butchers](butcher.md), [Ranchers](rancher.md) and [Chefs](chef.md). It shares the **Fastest Work Pace** cap with every other
  speed-up ([work pace](work-pace.md)).
- **What stops it**: the **Tonics** setting off. See [Tonics](tonics.md) for all six.
