The Cartographer is the village's explorer. They pack food, set out on an expedition through the land round the village,
and come home with whatever they found.

## Getting a cartographer

A villager without a job takes a free **cartography table** as a Cartographer, as in vanilla. If a villager there has
another job, sneak-right-click them with a **compass**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the table with food in it.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a cartographer with a **compass** to make them your explorer.

## An expedition

With at least two rations in the chests (filling food with no bad effects: no raw chicken, rotten flesh or golden
apples), they set out on a string of stops within 48 blocks of the table, eating one ration a stop and searching each
for what the land has: flint, feathers, clay, saplings, flowers, mushrooms, nuggets, raw ore, honeycomb, the odd
emerald or diamond. With a **sword or axe** from the chests they hunt along the way too (meat, gunpowder, pearls, spider
eyes). Then they come home, put the finds in the chests and rest before the next one.

**Maps.** Leave **empty maps** in the chest, and every third expedition they draw you an **Explorer's Map** to a place
nearby: a village, a temple or a shipwreck.

**With Cobblemon** they also bring back apricorns, berries, Poké Balls, Exp. Candy, evolution stones and the odd fossil.

Over their head: how many expeditions, and which stop they're on.

## How the cartographer fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Chef](chef.md)'s cooking makes good rations.
- **Feeds:** a fossil they find is what a [Fossil Scientist](fossil-scientist.md) revives; apricorns go to a
  [Ball Smith](ball-smith.md). The [Porter](porter.md) takes the rest to the storehouse.
- **Faster with:** the **Expeditions** research (back 20% sooner a level; see [Scholar](scholar.md)).
- **Stops when:** there's no food in the chests, or no chest by the table.

See also [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md) and the [Netherworker](netherworker.md), who shares the cartography table.
