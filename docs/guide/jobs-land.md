# Jobs on the land

The workers who bring things in: stone and ore, wood, fruit, crops, fish, honey, flowers, animals and finds from far
away. Each one stores what they gather in the chests within 8 blocks of their workstation (a porter carries it on to
the storehouse).

## Miner

Put down a **blast furnace**, stand a villager by it and sneak-right-click them with a **pickaxe**.

1. Craft a **Quarry Marker** (a stick, red dye and paper). Right-click one corner block, then the opposite corner (up to
   32 by 32). Sneak-right-click the air to choose the depth, or a **strip mine**.
2. Put **pickaxes** (and some torches) in a chest by the blast furnace.
3. Hand the marker to the miner.

They dig the area out from the top down, leave a spiral stair in the pit wall so you can climb out, and keep it dry by
leaving blocks next to water and lava. A **strip mine** digs tunnels instead and takes just the ore; strip mines down a
ladder shaft go to the heights where iron, gold and diamonds are. With coal in the chests, their blast furnace smelts the
ore too.

![A miner digging out a quarry](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/quarry/still-2.jpg)

## Lumberjack

A **fletching table** near some trees, and an **axe** in your hand. Put spare axes in the chest. The lumberjack fells the
trees within 16 blocks one at a time and plants a sapling where each stood. With bone meal in the chest they grow the
saplings faster; with a furnace nearby they burn logs into charcoal; and when a builder needs stripped logs, they strip
some. Hand them a **Field Marker** for a tree farm of their own.

![Felled and replanted](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/forest/still-3.jpg)

## Orchard Keeper

A **composter**, and **sweet berries**, **glow berries** or an **apple** in your hand. They pick everything ripe within 16
blocks (berries, cocoa, and with Cobblemon apricorns and berry plants) without breaking the plants. Hand them a **Field
Marker** and they plant a whole orchard.

## Farmer

Any **Farmer** (the vanilla one at a composter) can look after a field for you. Craft a **Field Marker** (a stick, wheat
seeds and paper), right-click two opposite corners of your field and hand it to the farmer, with seeds (and a hoe for
bare dirt) in the chest. They harvest and replant, sow, till, cut sugar cane, cactus, bamboo and kelp, and pick pumpkins
and melons. A village's own farmers look after the farm by their composter by themselves as soon as there's a chest by
it.

![A farmer at work on a field](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/farm/still-2.jpg)

## Fisherman

Hand any **Fisherman** (the vanilla one at a barrel) a **fishing rod**. They cast from the shore of the nearest water and
bring the catch back to the barrel. Put spare rods in the barrel, and a smoker with coal beside it to have the fish come
back cooked. Fishermen sometimes go out in a boat, too.

![Fishing from the shore](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/fish/still-2.jpg)

## Beekeeper

A **beehive** or **bee nest**, and a **glass bottle** or **shears**. They harvest full hives within 16 blocks (honey
bottles or honeycomb), plant flowers round hives that have few, and breed the bees. A campfire under a hive keeps the
bees calm.

## Florist

A **composter** and a **small flower**. With bone meal in the chest they grow the biome's flowers on the grass round the
composter, pick them for the dyers and builders, and fill any empty flower pots nearby.

## Composter

A **composter** and **bone meal**. The village's scraps (seeds, saplings, leaves, rotten flesh) go in, and bone meal comes
out for the farmers, florists and lumberjacks: more of it than a vanilla composter gives.

## Sifter

A **cauldron** and **gravel**, **sand**, **red sand** or **soul sand**. Put those in the chest and they sift out flint,
nuggets, clay, seeds, quartz and now and then an emerald or a diamond (with Cobblemon, the odd evolution stone).

## Rancher

A **smoker** and a **saddle** or a **golden carrot**. They break in the wild horses, donkeys and llamas nearby, put on the
saddles and armor you leave in the chest, and breed them. With Cobblemon they groom pastured Pokémon (friendship goes up)
and run a **daycare**: right-click them to leave two Pokémon, who gain experience while you're away.

## Shepherd and Butcher

**Shepherds** (vanilla, at a loom) shear and breed the sheep nearby. **Butchers** (vanilla, at a smoker) look after the
cows, pigs, chickens and rabbits: they breed them, collect eggs and feathers and milk the cows. Hire a butcher with a
**lead** and they keep your herd at ten of each kind. With Cobblemon they also milk a Miltank, brush the feathered
Pokémon and more, just as you can.

## Netherworker

A **cartography table** within 32 blocks of a Nether portal, and **netherrack**. With three rations (bread, cooked meat) in
the chest they step through the portal and come back a few minutes later with the Nether's goods. Leave them a pickaxe,
an axe, a sword or a fire resistance potion and they bring back more.

## Cartographer (explorer)

**Cartographers** (vanilla, at a cartography table) go on expeditions with food from the chest, bringing back what the
land has: flint, feathers, clay, saplings, the odd emerald or diamond. With a sword they hunt too, and with empty maps in
the chest every third trip they draw you an **Explorer's Map** to a village, temple or shipwreck nearby.
