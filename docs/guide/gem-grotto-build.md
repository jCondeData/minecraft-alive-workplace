A stone shed of cobbled deepslate and stone brick under a deepslate tile roof (nothing near the lava burns), over a
**lava pool behind glass** with ledges of polished blackstone round it, where the grower sets her tumblestones over the
lava. A **stonecutter** stands by the door, and the west wall has an **amethyst niche**: amethyst blocks with clusters on
them, waiting for a budding amethyst from a geode nearby (a budding block can't be carried).

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**, with or without Cobblemon: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. A Journeyman [Gem Grower](gem-grower.md) sells it for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes it, they sell the upgrade too (the Blueprint Table has it as well):

- **Gem Grotto II** adds a deeper chamber behind, through an arch in the back wall, with four **Deepslate Crystal
  Cores** on stone plinths for Type Gems (plain deepslate without Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer).

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click the villager by its stonecutter with an **amethyst shard** and she becomes its
[Gem Grower](gem-grower.md), tending the beds round it. Like every finished building, it counts toward the village's
[rank](ranks.md).
