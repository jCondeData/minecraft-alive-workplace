A raid on a village with walls isn't a scramble in the streets any more: it's a siege. The gates shut, the bars come
down and the raiders have to break their way in. This page is the long version; [Lairs and sieges](lairs-and-sieges.md)
has the short one.

## When it begins

When raiders who ram gates or climb walls come to a village with at least one finished wall or gate build, everyone
reads "Oakvale is under siege! The gates are shut and the portcullis is down." The gates shut whatever the hour, and
iron bars drop across the gateway of every finished Gatehouse. You can still open the fence gates by hand. The raiders
gather beyond your outermost wall on the side they come from; none appears inside.

## The gate

A ram goes for the gate nearest the raiders: a ravager, or raiders with axes. Every blow is heard, the block cracks a
little more each time, and at the end it breaks, dropping nothing. The others wait a few blocks behind and walk in
through the gap.

- A [Palisade Gate](palisade-gate-build.md) holds about **10 seconds**.
- A [Gatehouse](gatehouse-build.md) holds about **a minute**: the portcullis goes first, then the gate behind it.
- A **Gatehouse II** has a barbican in front with a second gate and portcullis. The rams must break all of it before
  the inner gate, so it holds several times as long.

Only gates your builders made can break. A gate you placed yourself is never hit, even right beside one.

## Ladders

Raiders who carry ladders set them up the outside of a wall, a rung every half second, and climb over. Chat warns you:
"Ladders at the walls of Oakvale! A guard on the wall can throw them down." A guard on the walkway within 2 blocks of a
ladder's top throws the whole thing down, whoever is on it, and the raiders need 10 seconds to raise it again. Ladders go
on any wall, yours too, but never against a gate. When it's over every ladder is taken away.

## Battle stations

When a siege begins, or at dusk on a day the village was warned, the guards go to their stations instead of the bell:

- **Archers** climb to the tops of finished Wall Towers and Lookout Towers and the walkways of the Gatehouse, Stone Wall
  and Palisade, on the raiders' side, each to the spot with the best view of the ground in front of the gate (in a gap
  of the battlements, not behind a merlon). From a station they shoot 24 blocks (16 from the ground) and hit a quarter harder
  at foes 3 or more blocks below. They never climb down to chase.
- **Knights** stand 3 blocks inside the gate the rams go for, **medics** 3 blocks behind them.
- Guards with only a sword, and anyone who finds no free station, answer the bell as before.

## The defense upgrades

Each wall and gate build has a II, clicked over the finished one so the builder adds only what's new.
[Palisade](palisade-build.md) II gets a fighting step and a ditch of stakes; [Stone Wall](stone-wall-build.md) II roofed
timber hoardings with murder holes; [Wall Tower](wall-tower-build.md) II a roofed archers' gallery; Gatehouse II the
barbican.

## The morning after

At the dawn the gates open, the chronicle has a siege report: "The siege: 9 raiders came and 7 fell, 5 to the guards and
2 to players. The gate held. Hero of the night: Brannoc, with 3 kills." If the siege was fought off, every player who
killed a raider is a Hero of the Village for a day, and every villager is 10 happier for a day. For 2 days the builders
mend walls, gates and towers before any house.

## How it fits in

- **Who besieges:** the [pillager warband](pillager-warband.md) (rams and ladders), the
  [desert raiders](desert-raiders.md) (sand ramps) and the [piglin incursion](piglin-incursion.md) (hoglin rams).
  Monsters, bandits, pirates and witches raid the old way, though witches throw potions over your walls.
- **Stronger with:** the **Ramparts** research (after Fortification I) at the [Scholar](scholar.md)'s desk: gates have
  twice the hit points in a siege, and archers on stations reach 28 blocks.
- **What stops it:** **Sieges** off in the settings screen makes every raid a plain one. **Siege Damage** off keeps the
  siege but no gate ever breaks; the `mobGriefing` game rule off does the same and keeps ladders away too. A
  [Threat Level](threat-level.md) of 1 or less means no sieges at all.
