The guild of everyone who grows, gathers and cooks the village's food.

## Perks

- Farmers, Orchard Keepers, Florists, Beekeepers, Composters and Chefs work **15% faster**.
- The village's own farms and the Orchard Keepers' rounds **reach 8 blocks further**: 24 instead of 16.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** of any of those trades with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master farmers and cooks, and room for bigger fields.
- **What it affects**: the [store](storehouse.md), the village's [diet](food-and-diet.md) and everything that eats.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
