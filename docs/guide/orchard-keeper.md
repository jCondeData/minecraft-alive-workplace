The Orchard Keeper picks fruit without ever cutting the plant, so everything grows back for the next round.

## Getting an orchard keeper

Stand a villager by a **composter** in your orchard and sneak-right-click them with **sweet berries**, **glow berries** or
an **apple**. A composter's other jobs come with other items (wheat for a [Farmer](farmer.md), a flower for a
[Florist](florist.md), bone meal for a [Composter](composter.md)). Put a chest within 8 blocks of the composter.

## What they do

They walk round everything within 16 blocks of their composter and pick whatever is ripe: sweet berry bushes, glow
berries on cave vines, cocoa pods, and with Cobblemon installed its apricorns and berry plants. They carry a picking
pole, so fruit up in a tree is in reach. With about 48 fruit in hand they take the harvest to the chests. Fruit they
can't get to after a while is left alone for a bit.

**An orchard of your own.** Mark ground with a **Field Marker** (a stick, wheat seeds and paper) and hand it to them.
They keep it planted from the chests: sweet berry bushes two blocks apart, and with Cobblemon apricorn trees three apart
and berry plants. They pick what grows there even beyond their usual 16 blocks. Sneak-right-click them with an empty hand
to see the orchard and a **[Stop the orchard]** button: the marker comes back, the plants stay.

Over their head: how much they've picked, and whether they're picking, planting, taking the harvest in or waiting for
fruit to ripen.

## How the orchard keeper fits in

- **Feeds:** the [Porter](porter.md) carries the fruit to the storehouse (leaving 32 seeds of each kind for planting),
  where the village eats it and a [Chef](chef.md) cooks with it. With Cobblemon, apricorns are what a
  [Ball Smith](ball-smith.md) turns into Poké Balls.
- **Helped by:** a Grass, Bug or Flying Pokémon in a Pasture Block nearby (see [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md)).
- **Stops when:** nothing is ripe ("waiting for fruit to ripen"), or the composter is gone.

## Trading

Orchard keepers buy sweet berries, cocoa beans and glow berries, and sell apples, glow berries, the **Berry Farm**
blueprint (at Expert) and, at Master, a golden apple.
