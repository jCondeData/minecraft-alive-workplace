The Shopkeeper runs your own shop: you set the prices, stock the chests, and other players buy from them while you're off
doing something else.

## Getting a shopkeeper

Craft a **Shop Counter** (an emerald over planks and a chest) and place it near a villager without a job: the shop is
yours and they become its Shopkeeper. A villager already at a counter becomes one with an **emerald**. (A bed at a Shop
Counter picks the [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md), a Poké Ball the [Pokémon Trader](pokemon-trader.md).)

## Running the shop

Open the counter to set your prices. It has nine columns: the top slot of each is what one sale hands over (the item and
how many), the slot under it is what that costs. Put the goods in chests by the counter. Other players buy by trading
with the shopkeeper, and the payment lands in your chests. Every buyer pays exactly your price (no Hero of the Village,
Silver Tongue or trade fair discount comes off it). What's for sale is always what's in the chests: the offers are
rebuilt each time someone talks to the shopkeeper. The counter keeps a log of the last 20 sales; a sneak-right-click shows the latest 10.

**With CobbleDollars**, the shop gets its own screen with prices in CobbleDollars, and a **Price Tag** sets a price
directly: right-click the tag to set its price with buttons (or rename it in an anvil to a number) and put it in a price
slot. Without CobbleDollars a tag's price is paid in emeralds at the server's rate, over the trade screen's two slots: a
10,000 tag asks a stack of emeralds and 36 more, and past two stacks it asks emerald blocks and the emeralds left over.
A tag over 584 emeralds (58,400 at 100 per emerald) is too dear for the trade screen: it isn't offered, and the tag
says so. Money a shop takes while you're
offline is paid to you, with a note, the next time you join. If CobbleDollars is gone from the server by then, it's paid
in emeralds at the server's rate (1,234 CobbleDollars: 12 emeralds), and what doesn't make a whole emerald stays owed.
Emeralds that don't fit in your inventory land at your feet, and a fortune is paid at most an inventory's worth
(2,304 emeralds) a join, the rest at your next joins.

![The sales log](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/counter/still-3.jpg)

Over their head: how many sales, and "open for business".

## How the shopkeeper fits in

- **Fed by:** whatever you stock. A [Postman](postman.md)'s courier route can bring goods from your other chests to the
  shop's.
- **Moves in by themselves:** build a **Supply Shop** from the Blueprint Table and a shopkeeper moves in (see
  [Builder](builder.md)).
