With **Cobblecuisine** on the server, your village's cooks make its salads, sandwiches, curries, cakes and drinks, and
your villagers love the variety.

## What the Chef cooks

The [Chef](chef.md) cooks Cobblecuisine's dishes after his usual bread, pies and stews, each from its own recipe, with
the makings from the chests by his stove and the village's spare food. He keeps 16 of each. What he can make grows
with him:

- **Novice:** salads (Mixed Vegetable, Spore Mushroom, Pumpkin Pie) and sandwiches (Sweet Jam, Salty Egg, Salty Veggie).
  He roasts the berries for the sandwiches himself.
- **Apprentice:** three more salads, two more sandwiches, and the drinks: Bouncy Brionne Soda, Teddiursa Iced Coffee
  and Fruit Punch.
- **Journeyman:** six curries, from the Beanburger Curry to the Dry Frozen Curry.
- **Expert:** five cakes, such as the Fruit & Honey Cake and the Twin Mushroom Cake.
- **Master:** the Jigglypuff Fruity Flan and the Miltank Mix au Lait.

Fill a chest by his stove with beans, Salad Mix, mints, berries and the other makings, and the dishes appear beside them.

## Candies and medicines from the Campfire Pot's recipes

With Cobblemon on the server, the Chef also cooks **every Campfire Pot recipe**: the EXP Candies, the IV and EV candies
(Health, Mighty, Tough and the rest), Potions, Revives, status heals, X items, sweets and mochi, and any pot recipe
another mod or data pack adds. He only cooks one when the chests hold all its makings (sculk and honeycomb for an EXP
Candy XS, say) and keeps 16 of each in the chests by his stove, where you can take them. This needs only Cobblemon, and
the **Cobblecuisine Dishes** setting turns it off with the rest.

## A more varied diet

Villagers eat the dishes from the store like any meal, and each counts as its own kind of food: a salad, a sandwich and
a curry are three kinds, so a village eating them has a varied diet and better moods (see
[Food and diet](food-and-diet.md)). Three different salads are still just salad.

## The Camp Cook and the inn

The [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md) adds three curries to her menu: the Beanburger, Mild Honey and Spicy Potato Curries. She
cooks them over her fire and keeps 16 of each by her Campfire Pot.

The [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md) serves each guest staying at her inn a drink: a Bouncy Brionne Soda, a Teddiursa Iced
Coffee or a Fruit Punch, once a stay. She takes it from the chests by her counter, or else from the village store, so
keep some there.

## Beans on the farm

Your [Farmers](farmer.md) grow the beans the dishes need. When the store holds fewer than 16 of a colour of bean that
a dish on the menu uses, a farmer takes **bean seeds** from her chests and sows them on her field's empty farmland. She
cuts the beans only when they're fully grown and takes them to the store with the rest of her harvest. The colour of
each bean is Cobblecuisine's choice, so a field brings a mix. Once the store has 16 of every colour the menu wants,
she plants no more, and the seeds stay in the chest. Put a few bean seeds in her chest to start.

## Treats for partner Pokémon

Pokémon in a Pasture Block that help a villager at work (see [Partners at work](partners-at-work.md)) get a treat once a
day: a **Poké Puff** or a **shake** from the village store, the chests of your hall's kitchens and storehouses. Each
treat makes the Pokémon a little friendlier, with hearts. Keep a stack of Poké Puffs or shakes in a storehouse chest
and your partners will eat one each a day; with none in the store, they go without.

## New luxuries

Two of Cobblecuisine's dishes become luxuries (see [Classes](classes.md)): **Burghers** like a **Deluxe
Shake** (any of the six) every 4 days, and **Nobles** a **Clodsire Eclair** every 8. They're wants, not needs: a
household that has had one lately pays a little more tax. A household takes one from the store at dawn when it's due,
so keep some in a storehouse chest.

## Settings

On the settings screen, **Cobblecuisine Dishes** turns this on or off. Off, the cooks make what they made before and
the dishes count for the diet like any other food; partners get no treats and the two dish luxuries aren't wanted. Without Cobblecuisine nothing changes at all. **Bean Farms** turns the farmers' beans on or off: off, they never sow beans.
