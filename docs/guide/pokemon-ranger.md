"Where an Alpha roams near a village, the Ranger comes to calm it."

## How they come

While a wild Alpha Pokémon (or any wild one of level 50 or more) roams within 96 blocks of your hall, the **Pokémon
Ranger** may turn up beside the hall one morning in three, in a red vest with a capture styler on the wrist.

## What they do

- **Calming.** Each morning they walk to an Alpha within 96 blocks of the hall that isn't calmed yet. Beside it come
  sparkles and a calm chime: it never again attacks a villager or a player in the village.
- **Befriending.** Once a day they walk to a wild Pokémon within 64 blocks (never a legendary, mythical, Ultra Beast or
  paradox one) and lead it to a Pasture Block of the village with room, where it becomes the hall owner's Pokémon. The
  chronicle notes each one.

![A wild Pikachu led into the owner's Pasture Block](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_ranger/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: a dangerous neighbor, and Pasture Blocks with room.
- **What they affect**: the village's safety, and your [Pokémon](pokemon.md) collection (and with it the
  [Pokémon Professor](pokemon-professor.md)'s Pokédex).
- **What stops them**: a strike; full pastures or a hall with no owner mean no befriending.
