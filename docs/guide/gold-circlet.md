A thin gold crown with an emerald and an amethyst: the finest thing the village makes.

## Getting it

A Journeyman [Jeweler](jeweler.md) makes it from **2 gold ingots**, an **emerald** and an **amethyst shard**, and sells
it for 20 emeralds.

## What it's for

A want of the [Nobles](noble.md): each Noble household takes one from the village store every 32 days. It isn't a
need, but every want a household has makes it pay 10% more tax, and a Noble pays four times a Peasant's tax already.

## How it fits with the rest

Gold from a [Miner](miner.md), amethyst from geodes. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
