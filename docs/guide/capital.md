Every [realm](realms.md) has one capital: the village that founded it, until the realm chooses another. Whoever rules
the capital rules the realm.

## What the capital decides

The capital's owner, their friends and operators decide for the whole realm: adding, inviting and letting go of
villages, the realm's name and arms, the [levy](realm-treasury.md), the [realm research](realm-research.md) and the
realm edicts in its Book of Edicts. Its hall's name icon says "Capital of the County of Thornholm"; a member's says "In
the County of Thornholm". Anyone may still take their own village out of the realm at any time.

## Moving the capital

On a member's Realm tab, **Put Ashford forward as capital** (a bell) works for any member that's a Town or better.

- If one player owns every member, the capital moves at once.
- Otherwise a **vote** runs for a day. Every member's tab shows it ("Vote: move the capital to Ashford?") with **Vote for
  Ashford** and **Vote to keep Thornholm**. A player's vote counts once for each member they own, and they may change it
  until the day is up. The move needs more villages for it than against; a village that didn't vote counts for the
  current capital, and a tie keeps it.

When the capital moves, the realm keeps everything: its name, arms, rank, treasury, research and edicts. Fireworks go up
over the new capital and every member's owner hears of it.

## Losing the capital

If the capital's hall is broken, or its ruler clicks **Give up the crown**, the crown passes to the biggest member that's
a Town or better. With no Town left, the realm ends and every member is free.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** a strong village. The capital must be a Town for a County, a City for a Duchy, and for a Kingdom
  it and one more member must be Cities (see [Realms](realms.md)).
- **What it affects:** the [Royal Hall](royal-hall-build.md) is drawn and built by the capital, within 32 blocks of its
  hall, and raises the treasury's cap. [Relief riders](relief-riders.md) always come from the capital, wherever it is.
- **Realm edicts:** the capital proclaims them, such as the [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md); see
  [Edicts](edicts.md).
