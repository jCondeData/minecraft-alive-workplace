A straw figure with a touch of gold, set up by the fields for a good harvest.

## What it does

In **harvest season**, autumn on the village calendar ([seasons](seasons.md)), the crops within **32 blocks** of a
Harvest Idol grow **25% faster**, and golden sparkles rise from it. The rest of the year it's an ornament.

It speeds up wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, melon and pumpkin stems, sweet berry bushes, cocoa, nether wart,
torchflowers and pitcher plants (and with Cobblemon, its berries, apricorns, mints and grains). Two idols in reach of the
same field are no better than one.

![Half-way: the idol's field is ahead](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/harvest_idol/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: the season; it needs nothing else.
- **What it affects**: your [Farmers](farmer.md) and [Orchard Keepers](orchard-keeper.md) bring in more, and the
  [store](storehouse.md) fills faster. In harvest season with no idol by the fields, the hall's "What next?" suggests
  one near the fields round the hall.
- **What stops it**: any other season, or the **Harvest Idols** setting off.
