**The Red Dome**: a white eight-sided hall under a stepped red dome with a glazed eye on top, known by its outline from any side of the village. Stone pilasters stand at its corners and its windows sit back over sills. Inside, waiting benches line both sides of a red aisle up to the desk, where Cobblemon's **Healing Machine** sits right under the eye, with a **PC** beside it and a stocked corner of barrels, chests and shelves behind. It comes in three looks: the Red Dome; a log **Mountain Lodge** under a steep red front gable with a porch deck, a jettied cross gable, a stone chimney and a dormer, window boxes and a back porch; and a flat-roofed sandstone **Sunny Plaza** with a striped awning.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). It's listed only when Cobblemon is installed. A Journeyman **nurse** sells it too, for 14 emeralds, in one of its three looks. In a village of Village rank or higher without one, the hall's **What next?** page suggests building it.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Pokémon Center, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Pokémon Center II** adds a gabled **Trade Hall** on the east side with a **Shop Counter**, and behind it a garden of planted beds in soil, with paths to a roofed, domed pavilion and a **Pasture Block**. The Lodge and Plaza looks have their own II: the Lodge carries its cross gable east into a log Trader's wing (with its own Shop Counter) and gets a paddock behind, with a field shelter, a trough, stepping-stone paths and a Pasture Block on a stone pad; the Plaza gets a wing and a courtyard.

## What it does for the village

The Healing Machine heals your team, and the PC is there when you need a box. Sneak-right-click a villager by the Shop Counter of tier II with a **Poké Ball** to make them a **Pokémon Trader**, or with an emerald a shopkeeper; a honey bottle by the Pasture Block makes a Habitat Keeper. A Pokémon Center built before the new look keeps its old shape, and its Shop Counter and Pasture Block keep working where they stand. See [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a pink banner (healing and graves).
