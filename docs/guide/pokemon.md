With Cobblemon installed, Pokémon help the village work, and villagers become trainers, tutors, smiths and traders.
Without Cobblemon none of this is there, and everything else works the same.

## Pokémon partners

Keep Pokémon in a **Pasture Block** within 16 blocks of a villager's workstation. If their type suits the job, each one
makes the work go faster, up to three helpers (more with the village's Kinship research). The line over the villager's
head says who's helping ("· with Machop"), and sneak-right-clicking the worker shows how much faster they are.

| Job | Helpful types |
|---|---|
| Builder, Carpenter, Mason | Fighting, Rock, Steel |
| Miner | Ground, Rock, Steel |
| Lumberjack | Grass, Bug, Fighting |
| Orchard Keeper | Grass, Bug, Flying |
| Farmer | Grass, Ground, Water |
| Fisherman | Water, Ice |
| Armorer | Fire, Steel (more ore each trip) |
| Toolsmith, Ball Smith | Steel, Fire |
| Weaponsmith | Steel, Fighting |
| Fletcher | Flying, Bug |
| Nurse | Fairy, Normal, Psychic |
| Porter | Fighting, Normal (they carry more) |
| Chef | Fire, Normal |
| Fossil Scientist | Rock, Psychic |
| Guard | Fighting, Dragon (they fight beside the guard) |
| Postman | Flying (air mail: far parcels arrive at once) |

Fire types by a miner's or fisherman's furnace smelt some of what's in it on the spot. It works alongside Cobbleworkers
too: the same Pokémon can work the pasture and help the villager.

## Trainers

*Full article: [Trainer](trainer.md), [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md).*

Craft a **Training Post** (a target block on planks) and place it near a villager without a job: they become a
**Trainer**. Right-click them to battle. Every trainer starts as a Novice with two young Pokémon and ranks up as people
battle them, until a Master fields six fully evolved Pokémon and plays smart. Beating one pays CobbleDollars (emeralds
without CobbleDollars), once a day per trainer. With Radical Cobblemon Trainers, their teams follow your level cap; with
Mega Showdown, a Master Mega Evolves their ace.

![A battle with a Master trainer](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/battle/still-2.jpg)

**Trainer Leader.** Sneak-right-click a villager at a Training Post with a **block of gold**. The village's leader battles
at Expert strength from the start, pays three times the prize, and takes one challenge a day from each player.

## Move Tutor

*Full article: [Move Tutor](move-tutor.md).*

A **Training Post** and a **book**. Right-click the tutor, pick one of your Pokémon and a move it could learn but won't get
from leveling (tutor moves, TM moves, egg moves). Click once to choose, again to pay and teach. A new tutor teaches
weaker moves; a Master teaches everything.

![The Move Tutor's lessons](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/tutor/still-1.jpg)

## Ball Smith

*Full article: [Ball Smith](ball-smith.md).*

A **smithing table** and an **apricorn**. Put apricorns and metal in the chest (copper for Poké Balls, iron for Great
Balls, gold for Ultra Balls, diamonds for the rarest) and the smith makes balls, four at a time, learning harder ones as
they level up. Sneak-right-click them to pick which balls to make.

![Choosing which balls to make](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/smith_orders/still-1.jpg)

## Pokémon Trader

*Full article: [Pokémon Trader](pokemon-trader.md).*

A **Shop Counter** and a **Poké Ball**. Right-click the trader to see today's offers ("my Tinkaton, level 62, for any
Fighting type, level 55 or higher"). Pick an offer, then one of your Pokémon that fits, and click twice to swap. A held
item comes back to you. Offers change every day and are the same for everyone; each player gets one trade per trader a
day. Better traders offer more, sometimes a shiny.

![The Pokémon Trader's offers](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/trader/still-1.jpg)

## Fossil Scientist

*Full article: [Fossil Scientist](fossil-scientist.md).*

Cobblemon's **Fossil Analyzer** and a **fossil**. Right-click the scientist holding a fossil and pay 8 emeralds: they
revive it while they work, and the Pokémon joins your party (or your PC) when it's done.

## Also with Cobblemon

- **Nurses** heal your whole team along with you.
- **Ranchers** groom pastured Pokémon and run a **daycare** where your Pokémon gain experience.
- **Butchers** milk a Miltank, brush feathered Pokémon and more.
- **Chefs** cook Poké Snacks, Poké Bait and Aprijuice.
- **Explorers** bring back apricorns, berries, Poké Balls, Exp. Candy and the odd fossil or evolution stone.
- **Postmen** with a Flying partner send air mail.
- Quests may ask you to beat one of the village's trainers.
