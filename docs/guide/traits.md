Every villager is a little different. Each has a **trait**, and half of them a second one that doesn't clash with the
first. A villager's traits are part of who they are: they never change, so a Diligent builder stays diligent for life.
The [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s list shows them.

## The eight traits

| Trait | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Diligent** | works 10% faster |
| **Lazy** | works 10% slower |
| **Nimble** | walks 15% faster |
| **Clever** | learns a quarter faster: more experience for the same work, so they level up sooner |
| **Strong** | hits 15% harder: a good pick for a guard |
| **Cheerful** | a little happier themselves, and makes the whole village happier: 1% more wellbeing each, up to 5% |
| **Glutton** | eats twice a day |
| **Frugal** | eats every other day |

## How it interacts

- **Work pace.** Diligent and Lazy count in a worker's [work pace](work-pace.md), alongside moods, research and the
  village's upkeep.
- **Food.** Gluttons empty the store twice as fast and the Frugal at half the rate; see [Food and diet](food-and-diet.md).
- **Mood and wellbeing.** A Cheerful villager's own [mood](moods.md) is 10 points higher, and every Cheerful grown-up
  adds to the village's [wellbeing](village-needs.md).
- **Picking jobs.** Put the Strong ones at the grindstone and the Clever ones where levels matter most, like the
  Blueprint Table. See [Giving villagers jobs](jobs.md).
- **Turned off:** the [Villager Traits](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch. Nobody has a trait while it's off.
