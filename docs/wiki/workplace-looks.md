# Workplace looks

The twelve workplaces the Steward asks a builder for each have their own building, so a player can tell the trade
from the street, and so does every job's house that villages grow by themselves, the Weaver's Cottage and the Inn.
Before, all of them were the same timber house with a different room inside (the owner's playtest, 2026-10-16).

Roadmap items: 26.11, 36.8, B170, 36.21, B639, 50.1, 50.2, 50.3, 50.5, 50.8, 50.11, 50.12

## 1. What a player sees

- **Builder's Workshop**: a rubble-stone workshop under a jettied brick-and-timber loft, one long slate roof sweeping
  down over a plank store with cart doors, and a timber jib crane in the fenced materials yard, its hook lifting a block
  of dressed stone off the stacks: the sign of the trade from down the street. Inside, the Blueprint Table with his
  chest beside it by the hearth, three more chests and a lumber rack in the store, and the Builder's bed up the ladder
  in the loft (rebuilt from scratch in 50.1).
- **Carpenter's Workshop**: a cruck-framed shop under a steep roof with a dormer and a stone glue-stove flue; logs, a
  sawhorse and plank stacks on the porch, an L of workbench round the crafting table, the Carpenter's bed in the loft
  (rebuilt from scratch in 50.2). The village houses below keep their own looks.
- **Tinker's Workshop**: the Tinker's Tower, a square tower on the street under a copper cap with a dark oak band,
  the workshop behind it with the smithing table at its forge and a brick stack up its side (rebuilt from scratch in
  50.3). Its II lifts the tower a story: a stores loft of double chests up a ladder and a lamp room on top for fine
  work, a lightning rod on the new cap.
- **Kitchen**: a white plastered cookhouse under a hipped roof, a domed brick bread oven bulging out of its side (the
  smoker is its mouth inside) with a tall smoking flue, and herb beds either side of the path.
- **Post Office**: a narrow two-story house, its timber upper floor jettied over the street with flower boxes, a red
  tiled roof with its gable to the street and a little dovecote on the ridge. A ladder leads upstairs.
- **Guard House**: two stone piers with a road arch between them (the guardroom with the grindstone in one, an armory
  and a barred lock-up in the other) and the guard's timber barrack room bridging both on a jetty under one steep slate
  roof, a watch gable over the gate (rebuilt from scratch in 51.8); a ladder inside leads up.
- **Clinic** (rebuilt from scratch in 50.8, "the pavilion ward"): one tall white ward on a stone plinth, its gable to
  the street, under a steep slate roof with a louvered lantern ventilator on the ridge and a small dormer breaking the
  east eave. Six white beds stand heads to the long walls, a window between each pair; the Nurse's station across the
  front end holds the brewing stand, the remedy chest, a medicine cupboard and her desk. The west slope runs on as a
  catslide over a sun veranda on log posts, its ends closed in boards, with seats under the windows and a door in from
  the ward. Its ridge is closed under a slab cap. 15 x 14 x 12; standing Clinics keep their old drawing until refreshed.
- **Flower Shop** (rebuilt from scratch in 50.11, "the glasshouse florist"): a glazed gable to the street on a red brick
  dwarf wall, the Florist's grass beds either side of the aisle that leads to her composter, a small brick parlor with
  the counter and a shop window, and a fenced cutting yard behind. Level II closes the yard with a second, lower glass
  range and puts a vented lantern on the ridge. 13 x 12 x 13 (II 17 x 12 x 15).
- **Supply Shop** (rebuilt from scratch in 50.11): a timber-framed shop on a stone plinth, two display bays under
  copper awnings either side of the door, a dormer on each roof slope and a loading door in the back gable. The Shop
  Counter stands before a stock wall of chests and casks. Level II adds the shopkeeper's room behind, Level III a feed
  and hardware room on the east side, Level IV a stocked cellar. Level III no longer has a Mailbox, so it no longer
  gives a Postman (the Post Office does). 11 x 11 x 11 at Level I.
- **Market Stall** (rebuilt from scratch in 50.11): a gabled spruce booth whose shop board folds down as a counter
  under a red and white canopy, selling from a second side too. Level II puts the Shop Counter in the booth's counter
  and adds a lock-up storeroom next door, Level III runs it back with more chests. It no longer has a composter, so it
  no longer gives a Farmer. 8 x 8 x 6 at Level I.
- **Ferry House** (rebuilt from scratch in 50.5): a ferry station. A stone waiting room with a stove, a table, a bench
  and the station clock under a white plastered upper floor (the Ferryman's bedroom, up a stair inside) and a slate roof
  with a lookout cupola carrying the ferry light; a boarded landing the whole width of the front under a canopy with a
  teal valance, the Travel Post by the landing steps and benches against the wall. 15 x 17 x 11; standing Ferry Houses
  keep their old drawing until refreshed.
- **Trainer's House** (Cobblemon; rebuilt from scratch in 50.12, "the Battle Court"): a gateway of two stone piers

## 2. How it works

- Drawn by `tools/blueprints/workplaces.py`; `village.py`'s `workplace` returns them, and `StarterBlueprints` lists each
  with its size. The blueprint ids are unchanged, so a workplace standing or being built in a save carries on.
- Each keeps exactly one of its job's block (the one the worker takes), no other vanilla job block, a bed, no trapdoor
  in the row along its front where villagers walk (B69), and a ladder to every upper floor.
- The village houses are drawn by `tools/blueprints/village_looks.py` (one drawing per job, given the village style's
  materials) and furnished by `village.py` as before: the street jigsaw, the door at the same spot, the job's room, the
  bed and the villager (or the spawn jigsaw) where they were, so `VillageHouses` and the village pools are unchanged.
  Their footprints grew (up to 13 x 17 x 13); the four luxury houses stay at most 10 wide.
- The Weaver's Cottage (`countryside.py`) and the Inn (`starter.py`) keep their sizes, rooms and job blocks. (The
  Weaver's Cottage was rebuilt from scratch since, in 49.8: [The Weaver's Cottage](weavers-cottage.md).)

## 6. Decisions

The owner (2026-10-16): "make new completely different exteriors for all different kinds of workshops ... nothing
should look identical". Each building differs in footprint, roof form and materials and has one signature from its
trade. The Ferry House kept its plot size so the shore rule and its tests stay as they are.

## 7. Known limits

- Builds already standing keep their old look until their owner asks: since 36.21 the Village Hall's Repairs page
  shows **Refresh to the latest look** on every finished build whose blueprint was redrawn since, and a Builder brings
  it up to the new drawing (the old drawing's leftovers away, the new pieces in; chests, beds in use, job blocks and a
  player's own blocks stay; nothing doubled, a furnished build's old pieces included; builder.md). Only the older drawings the mod ships are recognized (those changed since
  1.0). A workplace half built when the update arrives is finished from the new drawing over what the old one put up.
- Village houses already grown keep the old look; new chunks grow the new ones. The larger houses fit a village's
  street a little less often than the old small one. Measured (36.8, 50 villages of each type): villages stay as
  full as before (houses per village moved by at most 1% for every type), but vanilla houses take the room, so a
  village grows fewer of ours (2.2 to 2.5 per village, from 3 to 4; desert villages about 0.4, in 14 of 50).
