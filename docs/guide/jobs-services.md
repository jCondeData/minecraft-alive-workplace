# Porters, post and services

The workers who keep a village running: carrying goods, delivering mail, selling, healing, teaching and more.

## Porter and the Storehouse

Craft a **Storehouse** (three planks over a barrel, a chest and a barrel), put chests within 8 blocks of it, and a
villager without a job becomes its **Porter**. The porter walks round the village's workers and carries what they make to
the storehouse: the miner's ore, the lumberjack's logs, the farmer's harvest, the fisherman's catch. Tools, seeds and
whatever each worker still needs stay where they are. The storehouse is part of the village's stock, so a builder short
of stone finds it there.

![A porter carrying goods to the storehouse](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/porter/still-2.jpg)

**The requests board.** Right-click the Storehouse to see what the workers nearby are waiting for (a builder's missing
blocks, a miner's pickaxe, a farmer's seeds) and how much is already in store. Click one to hand over what you have,
straight into that worker's chest.

![The requests board](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/porter/still-4.jpg)

**Stock orders.** Click the book on the board to ask the crafters to keep an item in store (16, 32, 64...).

**Drop Box.** Craft one from a barrel and a hopper and put it within 48 blocks of the storehouse. Whatever you drop in
(the loot from a trip, anything), the porter takes to the storehouse first.

## Postman and mail

Craft a **Mailbox** (iron nuggets round a chest, on a fence) and place it: it's yours, and mail for you arrives there
(the red flag goes up). To send something, open your mailbox, put items in the top row, type a player's name and press
**Send**; write in the **Letter** line to send a letter too. `/workplace mail` shows where your parcels are.

A **Postman** carries it: stand a villager by a Mailbox and sneak-right-click them with **paper**. They walk a round of 64
blocks; mail going further goes with the night mail and arrives at dawn. Only you, your friends and operators can open
your mailbox.

**Courier routes.** When there's no mail, postmen haul between your chests. Craft a **Delivery Note** (paper, a feather
and an ink sac), right-click the chest to take from, then the one to bring to, and hand the note to a postman.

![The mailbox screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/mail/still-1.jpg)

## Shopkeeper

Craft a **Shop Counter** (an emerald over planks and a chest) and place it near a villager without a job: the shop is
yours and they become its **Shopkeeper**. Open the counter to set your prices: the top slot of each column is what one
sale hands over, the slot below it the price. Put the goods in chests by the counter. Other players buy by trading with
the shopkeeper, and the payment lands in your chests. With CobbleDollars, the shop gets its own screen with prices in
CobbleDollars, and **Price Tags** set a price directly.

![Setting prices at the Shop Counter](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/counter/still-1.jpg)

## Ferryman

Craft a **Travel Post** (a sign over planks and a boat; name it in an anvil first, say "Riverside") and place it.
Right-click posts to add them to the ones you know. A villager without a job becomes its **Ferryman** and sells **Travel
Tickets** to every other post you know (an emerald for every 256 blocks). Use a ticket near any travel post and you
arrive at the ticket's post. Many villages already have a ferry house.

![Buying a travel ticket](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/ferry_menu/still-1.jpg)

## Innkeeper

A **Shop Counter** and a **bed**. Each morning, while there's a free bed nearby, a **traveler** comes to stay. Travelers
already know a trade: right-click one to **hire them** (8 emeralds for an Apprentice, 16 for a Journeyman, 32 for an
Expert). They join your village at their level. If nobody hires them, they move on after two days.

![A traveler checks in](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/innkeeper/still-4.jpg)

## Nurse

A **brewing stand** and a **honey bottle**. Right-click the nurse with an empty hand to get your health back and bad
effects cleared (with Cobblemon, your team is healed too). Nurses also look after hurt villagers and golems, and cure the
village's sick with a honey bottle, milk or a healing potion from their chest.

![A nurse at work](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/nurse/still-2.jpg)

## Teacher

A **lectern** and a **book**. By day the teacher gives the village's children lessons. A child who went to school starts
their first job as an Apprentice instead of a Novice.

## Scholar and research

A **lectern** and **paper**, in a village with a Village Hall. Sneak-right-click the scholar with an empty hand for the
village's **research tree**, and click a topic to research it next. The scholar takes the cost (paper, books, emeralds)
from the chest and works it out at the lectern. Every level is a bonus for the whole village:

| Topic | What each level does |
|---|---|
| Swift Hands | every job a little faster |
| Hearth | a happier village |
| Drill | guards hit harder |
| Kinship | one more Pokémon partner per worker |
| Lore | schooled children start as Journeymen |
| Architecture | the Town Hall blueprint |
| Logistics | porters carry more |
| Craftsmanship | crafters work faster |
| Medicine | villagers fall ill less often |
| Fortification | guards turn aside some blows |
| Commerce | one more market trader, cheaper mercenaries |
| Expeditions | explorers and netherworkers back sooner |
| Green Thumb | composters make bone meal faster |
| Warding | explosions no longer break blocks in the village |

![The research screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/scholar/still-4.jpg)

## Undertaker

A **brewing stand** and a **golden apple** (or a totem). A villager with a job or a name who dies leaves a **grave**. With
a golden apple, a healing potion or a totem in the chest, the undertaker brings them back: job, level, trades and name
as they were.

## Bard

A **jukebox** and a **music disc**. Put discs in the chest and the bard plays them every morning and evening. No discs?
They make up a tune on the harp.
