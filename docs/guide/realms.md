One village is a home. Several, bound together under one capital, are a realm: a Barony that can grow into a Kingdom,
with a treasury of its own, shared learning, and riders who come when a member is attacked.

## Founding a realm

Click the minecart on a Town's [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and pick the **Realm** tab (a gold helmet). **Found a
realm** says what it takes ("A Town founds the Barony of Thornholm, with itself as the capital"), or in red why the
village can't yet. A click founds it: fireworks over the hall, a line in the chronicle, and the village is the realm's
[capital](capital.md).

## Members

At the capital, the Realm tab lists every village you may add: your own and your friends' villages within 2,048 blocks
that aren't in a realm. One click, **Add Ashford**, and it's in. Another player's village gets **Invite Ashford**
instead: its owner receives a letter and can **Join** or **Decline** on their own Realm tab within 3 days. A village's
[colonies](colonies.md) join with it.

The rows under the title list the members, the capital first, each with its rank, villagers, wellbeing, where it lies,
its owner and the day it joined. The capital's ruler can click a member to let it go; any member's owner can **Leave
the realm**.

## Ranks

| Rank | Villages | And |
|---|---|---|
| Barony | 1 or more | a realm starts as a Barony |
| County | 3 | the capital a Town or better |
| Duchy | 5 | the capital a City |
| Kingdom | 8 | the capital and one more member a City |

When the realm rises, fireworks go up over the capital and every member's owner online reads "Rejoice! the Barony of
Thornholm has risen to the County of Thornholm."

## Name and arms

Click the realm's title with a Name Tag renamed in an anvil to rename the realm ("the Barony of Eldmark"); the tag is
used up. Click the arms (a white banner at first) with any banner and that becomes the realm's arms; the banner isn't
used up. The arms go on the realm's [relief riders](relief-riders.md)' shields and the [Realm Map](realm-map.md).

## What a realm gives

- **A [treasury](realm-treasury.md)** filled by a levy on each member's takings and by gifts.
- **[Shared research](realm-research.md):** members use the capital's levels, and the capital studies eight realm topics.
- **[Relief riders](relief-riders.md)** from the capital and nearby members when a member is raided.
- **Realm edicts** proclaimed by the capital for every member: the [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md),
  [Common Market](common-market.md), [Levy of Arms](levy-of-arms.md), [Realm Festival](realm-festival.md),
  [Crown's Peace](crowns-peace.md) and [Royal Charter](royal-charter.md).
- **A [Royal Hall](royal-hall-build.md)** by the capital, for a bigger treasury.
- **A place on the [weekly leaderboard](weekly-leaderboard.md)'s** Greatest realm board.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** villages that grow. Ranks come from members' ranks, so a capital becoming a City can raise the
  whole realm.
- **Neighbors:** [trade pacts](trade-pacts.md) and [alliances](alliances.md) with other players' villages, and
  [feuds](feuds.md) when it goes wrong: a feud with one member is a feud with the whole realm.
- **What breaks it:** a member's hall broken takes that village out. If the capital's hall is broken, or its ruler
  gives up the crown, the biggest member that's a Town or better takes the crown; with no Town left, the realm ends.
- **What stops it:** **Realms** off in the settings screen hides the Realm tab; realms stay saved and come back.
  **Villages per Realm** sets the most members (12 unless changed).
