The Composter turns the village's scraps into bone meal, and gets more out of them than a vanilla composter does.

## Getting a composter

Stand a villager by a **composter** and sneak-right-click them with **bone meal**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of it.

## What they do

They take scraps from the chests by the composter (seeds, saplings, leaves, crop waste, spoiled food and rotten flesh)
and put them in one at a time. When their own chests are empty, they fetch scraps from the storehouse. A vanilla
composter needs seven layers for a bone meal; theirs needs only **five**, and nothing is wasted: each scrap adds its
usual share of a layer, and rotten flesh counts half. The bone meal goes in the chests.

Over their head: how much bone meal they've made, and whether they're composting or out of scraps.

## How the composter fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Farmer](farmer.md)'s spare seeds, a [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md)'s spare saplings, and anything else the
  [Porter](porter.md) brings to the storehouse.
- **Feeds:** the bone meal is for the [Florist](florist.md) (flowers), the [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md) (saplings), the
  [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md) and the [Farmer](farmer.md).
- **Faster with:** the village's **Green Thumb** research (see [Scholar](scholar.md)): a layer less for every bone meal,
  a level.
- **Stops when:** there are no scraps anywhere. They then ask for some on the Storehouse's requests board.

## Trading

Composters buy rotten flesh and wheat seeds, and sell bone meal, podzol, moss blocks, mycelium and rooted dirt.
