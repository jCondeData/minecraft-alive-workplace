The Teacher gives the village's children lessons, so they start their working lives a level up.

## Getting a teacher

Stand a villager by a **lectern** and sneak-right-click them with a **book**. (Lapis brings the
[Librarian](librarian.md) back, paper picks the [Scholar](scholar.md).)

## What they do

By day the teacher calls over the children within 32 blocks who haven't been to school yet, and every child within 5
blocks of the lectern has lessons while the teacher stands by it. A couple of minutes of lessons and a child has been to
school. When they grow up and take a job, they start as an **Apprentice** instead of a Novice, with the Novice and
Apprentice trades.

Over their head: how many pupils they've taught, and how many children they're teaching or calling in; "no children to
teach" when there are none.

## How the teacher fits in

- **Fed by:** babies. A village grows when there's a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village (see
  [Village life](village-life.md)).
- **Feeds:** every job in the village, a level ahead.
- **Better with:** the **Lore** research: schooled children start as **Journeymen** (see [Scholar](scholar.md)).

## Trading

Teachers buy paper, ink sacs and feathers, and sell books, a book and quill, a map, a bookshelf, a clock and, at Master,
bottles o' enchanting.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
