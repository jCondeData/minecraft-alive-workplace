[Free Bread](free-bread.md) makes the village happier, and the village eats more. "Free bread is a fine thing, but we
bake it from seed corn. Help us fill a common granary, and nobody will go hungry to feed the rest."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** A Farmer stops you at the hall's door. Hear them out.
2. **The First Step.** A granary needs grain: bring **512 wheat**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: grain keeps best baled, so **128 hay bales** for the lofts.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: **48 barrels** to keep the flour dry.

Each step pays emeralds, and the villagers watch the granary fill ("Hay bales stacked to the rafters. A good sight.").

## How it ends

Free Bread is **reformed** for good: everyone fed still gets their good mood, and the free bread no longer eats into the
store. The next evening the village sits down to a **harvest supper** at the hall: a festival's feast, without the
fireworks. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Free Bread](free-bread.md) is in force and the village has a Farmer. Without one the same steps
  go up on the hall's quest page instead.
- Your [farmers](jobs-land.md) grow the wheat and bale it; a [carpenter](jobs-crafts.md) makes barrels.
- The supper is a [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) feast for the whole village.
