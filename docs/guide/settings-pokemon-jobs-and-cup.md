The switches for Pokémon and villagers together, as the settings screen names them. See [Settings](settings.md) for
how to open the screen. Most need Cobblemon; the Gem Grower doesn't.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Partners at Work** | on | Pastured Pokémon are seen helping at work. Off: they still speed the work, unseen ([Partners at work](partners-at-work.md)). |
| **Nurse Healing Machines** | on | A nurse at a Healing Machine heals your team in it. Off: she heals by hand ([Healing Machine](nurse-healing-machine.md)). |
| **Berry Breeders** | on | The [Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md) job. Off: no new ones, and those hired stand idle. |
| **Camp Cooks** | on | The [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md) job. Off: the same. |
| **Habitat Keepers** | on | The [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md) job. Off: the same. |
| **Daycare Keepers** | on | The [Daycare Keeper](daycare-keeper.md) job. Off: no new ones, and those hired find no eggs. |
| **Gem Growers** | on | The [Gem Grower](gem-grower.md) job. Off: no new ones, and those hired stand idle. |
| **Habitat sightings** | on | Habitat Keepers tell the village of shiny, rare and Alpha Pokémon. Off: they keep them to themselves. |
| **Village habitats** | on | An Expert Habitat Keeper founds the village's own Habitat Block (Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer) ([The village's habitat](village-habitat.md)). |
| **Pokémon village houses** | on | New villages sometimes grow the Pokémon jobs' houses ([Pokémon village houses](pokemon-village-houses.md)). |
| **Festival Cup** | on | Villages with an Arena hold their festivals as the [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md). Off: festivals stay plain. |
| **A Cup every … festivals** | 1 (1 to 8) | 1 makes every festival a Cup, 2 every second one, and so on. |

The Cup's purse is paid in emeralds without CobbleDollars, at the **CobbleDollar Rate**
([the setting](settings-cobbledollar-rate.md)).
