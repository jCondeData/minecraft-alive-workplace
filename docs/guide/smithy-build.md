A stone forge under a timber roof, open to the street: a **blast furnace**, an anvil and a **smithing table**, and a **grindstone** in a lean-to. Three workstations in one building.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

Under the forge the Smithy has a **coal store**, down a ladder by the forge: heaps of coal behind boards, raw iron and chests of ore. Your builder digs it out of the ground himself, so give him room to dig: four blocks under the floor.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Smithy, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Smithy II** adds a coal and ore store behind, with a second blast furnace.

## What it does for the village

Jobless villagers take these blocks by themselves as the vanilla smiths, who now work for the village: the **Armorer** smelts the village's ore and makes armor for guards who have none, the **Toolsmith** makes the tools workers are waiting for, and the **Weaponsmith** mends worn gear and gives an unarmed guard a sword. Or pick another job with an item: a **pickaxe** at the blast furnace makes a **Miner**, **redstone** at the smithing table a **Tinkerer**, a **sword** at the grindstone a **Guard**. See [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with an orange banner (workshops).
