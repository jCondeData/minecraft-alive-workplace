A stone library lined with bookshelves, with a **lectern** for a scholar. It's where a village does its research.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Library, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Library II** adds a study tower with a second lectern.
- **Library III** adds an enchanting room behind the hall: an **Enchanting Table** ringed by fifteen bookshelves, and a third lectern.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by a lectern with **paper** and they become a **Scholar**: in a village with a Village Hall, sneak-right-click them with an empty hand for the village's research tree. Research counts toward the village's rank, too (a Town needs 3 levels, a City 7).

A jobless villager takes a lectern as a **Librarian**. In Library III, a librarian with the Enchanting Table nearby enchants the guards' weapons and armor and the workers' tools with lapis from their chest, and the fifteen bookshelves make the enchantments stronger. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md) and [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a blue banner (school, library, research).
