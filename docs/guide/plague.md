A Town or a City can be struck by a **plague**. It's likelier when the village has more villagers than beds, and a
witch coven's hex or a traveler from a faraway land who stops at the inn can bring one too. It starts with one
villager falling ill, and everyone in the village reads "A plague has broken out in Oakvale: the ill pass it on to
anyone near them."

## What a plague does

While it lasts, a villager who spends time within a few steps of someone ill may catch it. The ill work slowly, sneeze,
and don't get better by themselves; the Nurse's honey, milk and potions can't help them. After two days they go to bed
and stay there. Research Medicine at the Village Hall and fewer villagers catch it. The plague is over once nobody in
the village has been ill for a whole day. The Village Hall's list of villagers shows who has the plague, who the
Physician has seen, and who is in bed.

![The Physician going house to house](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/plague/anim.gif)

## The Physician

Stand a villager by a **brewing stand** and sneak-right-click them with a **glistering melon slice**: they become the
village's **Physician**. (A glass bottle at the same stand still makes a Cleric.) With a Physician in the village you
hear of a plague at its very first case, a day before it spreads. In a plague they go from house to house with a
**Remedy** from the chest beside their brewing stand and cure the ill. With no Remedy to hand they send the ill home to
rest, and someone the Physician has seen only passes it on to the people they live with. Keep the chest stocked: they
can also brew Remedies themselves from a glass bottle, a glistering melon slice, a honey bottle and a fermented spider
eye left in it.

With **Cobblemon**, keep Fairy- or Psychic-type Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the brewing stand: the Physician gets
round the village faster.

## The Physician's House

The **Blueprint Table** has a **Physician's House** for your builder to put up: a white ward with the brewing stand on
a counter of herbs, four beds each between white screens (the quarantine ward) and a herb garden out front. Stand a
villager by its brewing stand and hand them a glistering melon slice, and the village has its Physician. When a Town
has no Physician, **What next?** at the hall suggests it.

## The Remedy

Craft a **Remedy** from a glass bottle, a glistering melon slice, a honey bottle and a fermented spider eye (any
arrangement). Right-click a villager with it to cure any illness, a witch's hex and the plague included; they then stay
well for ten days. Drink one yourself and it takes away poison, wither, hunger and nausea, and you keep the bottle.

## Settings

**Physicians** in the settings screen turns the job off (Physicians already hired stand idle). To turn plagues off
altogether, set `plague` to `false` under `disasters` in the config file.
