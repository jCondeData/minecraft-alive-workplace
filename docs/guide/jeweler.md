The Jeweler makes the village's finest things: rings for the Burghers, brooches and circlets for the Nobles.

## Getting a Jeweler

Stand a villager by a **stonecutter** and sneak-right-click them with a **gold nugget**. (A clay ball brings the
[Mason](mason.md) back.) It's a [Burgher](burgher.md)'s job: the villager's household must be Burgher or better to take
it up. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the stonecutter.

## What they make

| Level | Piece | From |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | [Amethyst Ring](amethyst-ring.md) | 2 amethyst shards and 2 copper ingots |
| Apprentice | [Emerald Brooch](emerald-brooch.md) | an emerald and 3 gold nuggets |
| Journeyman | [Gold Circlet](gold-circlet.md) | 2 gold ingots, an emerald and an amethyst shard |

They keep the store in **8 of each** piece they know, from their own chests, the store and the other workers' chests
(never a builder's); a missing making shows on the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s requests board.

## Trading

At every level they buy amethyst, copper or gold, and sell their pieces: a ring for 4 emeralds, a brooch for 9, a
circlet for 20 (17 from a Master). From **Journeyman** they also sell the
[Jeweler's Workshop](jewelers-workshop-build.md)'s blueprint for 12 emeralds.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** amethyst from a geode, copper and gold from a [Miner](miner.md), emeralds from trading or the
  [treasury](treasury.md). An [Artisan](artisan.md) household makes every Jeweler 10% faster.
- **What it affects:** [Burghers](burgher.md) want an Amethyst Ring every 16 days; [Nobles](noble.md) need an Emerald
  Brooch every 16 days and want a Gold Circlet every 32. No Jeweler, no Nobles. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
- **What stops it:** no chest, nothing to work with, a household below Burgher (no new Jeweler; one already working
  keeps the job), or the **Jewelers** switch off in the settings (the job can't be picked, and Jewelers already hired
  stand idle).
