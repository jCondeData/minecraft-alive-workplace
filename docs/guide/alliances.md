A trade pact is about business. An alliance is a promise: when your village is raided, your ally's guards ride to help.

## Making one

On the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s Pacts tab, **Offer X an alliance** sends the other owner a letter and an
**Accept** or **Decline** line on their Pacts tab, as for a [trade pact](trade-pacts.md). An offer lapses after 3 days.

![Rowan's letter: an alliance offered to Ashford](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/pacts/still-1.jpg)

## What it does

When an allied village, or any village in an ally's realm, is raided, allies within 1,024 blocks with at least two
guards send [relief riders](relief-riders.md), at their own cost. Both owners hear of it.

## Ending it

Either side ends an alliance with **End**, with a day's notice. The other owner gets a letter, and both chronicles note
it.

## How it fits in

- **Works with:** [realms](realms.md), whose members already help each other; an alliance brings a neighbor's guards
  too.
- **What breaks it:** a [feud](feuds.md) between the two sides ends it. Allies answer raids only, never a feud's war.
- **What stops it:** **Alliances** off in the settings screen: none offered and no riders; alliances stay saved.
