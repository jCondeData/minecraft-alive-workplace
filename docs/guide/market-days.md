# Market days

Once a week, a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and a finished [Market Square](market-square-build.md)
holds a **market**. In the morning traveling traders come to the square with their wares, and they stay until about
nightfall. Everyone in the village is told it's market day, and the chronicle notes it.

## What they sell

The traders bring a wandering trader's goods, and each also sells a **blueprint** for 6 emeralds: a starter building,
often in another style, or a decoration such as a [Fountain](fountain-build.md) or a [Gazebo](gazebo-build.md).

## How many come

Two traders, a third from **Town** [rank](ranks.md), and one more for every level of the scholars' **Commerce**
[research](research.md).

## How it interacts

- **Needs:** the [Market Square](market-square-build.md). It's in the Blueprint Table; without it, no market.
- **Grows with:** the village's [rank](ranks.md) and its Commerce research.
- **Pairs with:** [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md), the village's other good day.
- **Turned off:** the [Market Days](settings-festivals-and-market-days.md) switch.
