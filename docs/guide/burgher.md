The village's well-to-do: townhouse folk who read the news over a glass of berry wine.

## What a Burgher needs

- **a tier II home**: a home with its first upgrade done (a Starter Cottage II, say) or a Townhouse;
- **fed from the store 3 days running**, on **a varied diet**;
- **a school**, and **one more service** near home: a chapel, clinic, library or tavern;
- **a Market Square** somewhere in the village;
- **Fine Clothes** every 8 days, **Berry Wine** every 2 days and the **Gazette** every 7 days, from the store.

Three dawns running without one of them and the household comes down to [Artisan](artisan.md).

**Wants**: an **Amethyst Ring** every 16 days, a **library** near home, and decorations near home. Each want had makes
the household pay 10% more tax.

## What Burghers give

- **Two and a half times the tax** into the [treasury](village-hall.md).
- **Scholars research 15% faster.**
- **Once the village has 3 Burgher households**: one more caravan route, and one more trader on market days, who sells
  the Townhouse and Manor blueprints.
- **The Burgher jobs**: only a Burgher (or better) can take up the Scholar, Undertaker and Jeweler, and with Cobblemon
  the Trainer Leader.

## Rising to Noble

Two dawns running with everything a [Noble](noble.md) needs: a tier III home, every service, a Town, decorations, Noble
Robes, Vintage Wine and an Emerald Brooch.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your [builder](builder.md) (upgrades, a Townhouse, a School, a Market Square), a Tailor, a
  Vintner and a Printer.
- **What it affects**: research, [caravans](trade-and-caravans.md), [market days](festivals-and-markets.md) and the
  treasury.
- **What breaks it**: running out of Berry Wine (it goes every 2 days) or missing a week's Gazette.
