The Porter is the village's carrier. They walk round the other workers, pick up what they've made, and take it to the
storehouse, where every worker nearby can find it.

## Getting a porter

Craft a **Storehouse** (three planks over a barrel, a chest and a barrel) and put chests within 8 blocks of it. A
villager without a job takes it by themselves and becomes its Porter. Whoever places a Storehouse owns it: its porter
works for them and their friends. A village's own storehouse works for the village.

## What they do

The porter walks round the village's workers and takes the **goods** piling up in their chests: the miner's ore, the
lumberjack's logs, the farmer's harvest, the fisherman's catch. They never take what a job needs: the miner's torches and
ladders and some stone to patch the stairs, the farmer's seeds, the lumberjack's saplings, coal for a furnace, ore and
fish still waiting to be smelted or cooked. A worker's pile has to add up to 16 or more to be worth a trip. Builders make
nothing to carry away.

Over their head: how many items they've carried, and whose goods they're fetching; "the storehouse chests are full" means
it's time for more chests.

## The requests board

Right-click the Storehouse to see what the workers nearby are waiting for (a builder's missing blocks, a miner's
pickaxe, a farmer's seeds) and how much is already in store. Click one to hand over what you're carrying, straight into
that worker's chest.

![The requests board](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/porter/still-4.jpg)

**Stock orders.** Click the book on the board, hold an item and click it to order it (16, 32, 64...), up to 18 orders.
The [Carpenter](carpenter.md), [Mason](mason.md), [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md) and [Chef](chef.md) keep that many in the
storehouse, making more when it runs low.

**Drop Box.** Craft one from a barrel and a hopper and put it within 48 blocks of the Storehouse. Whatever you drop in,
the porter takes to the storehouse first. Workers never take from it.

![Emptying a Drop Box](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/dropbox/still-2.jpg)

## How the porter fits in

- **Fed by:** every worker who makes something: [Miner](miner.md), [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md),
  [Farmer](farmer.md), [Fisherman](fisherman.md), [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md), [Sifter](sifter.md) and the rest.
- **Feeds:** the storehouse is part of the village's stock, so a [Builder](builder.md) short of stone finds it there, a
  [Chef](chef.md) finds the harvest, and every villager eats from it (see [Village life](village-life.md)).
- **Faster with:** the **Logistics** research (3 more stacks a level; see [Scholar](scholar.md)).
- **Stops when:** there are no chests by the Storehouse, or they're full.

## Trading

Porters sell chests, barrels, leads, a chest minecart, the **Storehouse Shed** blueprint and, at Master, a shulker shell; they
buy leather.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
