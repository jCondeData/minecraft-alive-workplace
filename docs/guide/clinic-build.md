A pavilion ward: one tall white ward with its gable to the street, under a steep slate roof with a little lantern
ventilator on its ridge and a small dormer on its east side. Inside, six white beds stand heads to the long walls with
a window between each pair, and at the front end is the Nurse's station: her **brewing stand** and the remedy chest
beside it, a medicine cupboard and her desk. On the west side the roof runs on over a sun veranda, with seats under the
windows and a door in from the ward (rebuilt from scratch; a Clinic already standing keeps its old look until
refreshed).

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). Newly explored villages often have one of these houses already, with its worker inside.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the brewing stand with a **honey bottle** and they become the **Nurse** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a brewing stand as a cleric). Right-click the nurse with an empty hand to get your health back and bad effects cleared; nurses look after hurt villagers and golems and cure the village's sick. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

**A home.** Its six ward beds are free beds; one can be the Nurse's own. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a purple banner (other buildings).
