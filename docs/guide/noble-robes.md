Robes for the village's first families: five lengths of one rich color, trimmed with fur and gold.

## Getting it

A Journeyman [Tailor](tailor.md) sews a set from **5 wool of the same color**, purple, blue, red or black, with a
**rabbit hide** and a **gold ingot**. Mixed colors won't do. A Tailor sells them for 14 emeralds.

## What it's for

A need of the [Nobles](noble.md): every Noble household takes a set from the village store every 8 days. A Burgher
household can't rise without them, and a Noble one that goes three dawns without comes back down.

## How it fits with the rest

Rabbit hide is the part most often missing: it only comes from rabbits, which a [Butcher](butcher.md) keeps
breeding. See
[Luxuries](luxuries.md).
