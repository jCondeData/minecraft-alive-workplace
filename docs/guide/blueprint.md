A Blueprint is a building on paper. Hand one to a Builder and they build it for you, block by block, from your chests.

## Getting one

- **At a [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md):** pick a build in the library and press **Get Blueprint**; it costs a
  [Blank Blueprint](blank-blueprint.md).
- **From a builder:** builders sell blueprints, more as they level up, and once a builder finishes a building that has
  a bigger version they sell its upgrade.
- **Your own:** a [Scan Tool](scan-tool.md) or a [Shape Planner](shape-planner.md) makes one.

## Using it

1. **Place it.** Hold it and right-click the ground where the front should go. You see the building as see-through
   blocks while you hold it; the gold edge is the front. Sneak-right-click the ground to turn it, and sneak-right-click
   the air to pick it back up.
2. **Pick a style** (before placing): sneak-right-click the air for Stonework, Sandstone, Dark Oak, Cherry or, with
   Cobblemon, Apricorn. The **Mirror** button flips it left to right.
3. **Check the materials.** Hold Shift over it for the full list, several to a line ("64 Oak Planks · 32 Cobblestone").
   A very big build lists as many as fit in about 20 lines and then says how many more there are; the
   [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md) shows all of them. Once it's placed, its tooltip shows only what the builder's
   chests are still missing, the same way. With a Book and Quill in your other hand, right-click to write the list
   into the book.
4. **Hand it over.** Right-click a Builder with it. Sneak while you hand it over and they take the building down
   instead, putting the blocks back in the chests.

Building into a hillside? Right-click the air with a placed blueprint to leave the ground around it as it is.

## How it fits in

- **Needs** a Blueprint Table close enough: the tooltip tells you when there's none within reach.
- **Comes back.** When the builder finishes, the blueprint goes back in the chest so you can build it again.
- **Upgrades.** Right-click a finished building with its upgrade and the blueprint lines up exactly over it; the
  builder only changes what's new.
