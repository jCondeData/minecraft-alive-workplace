The Colony Camp is where a new colony begins: the settlers' wagons drawn up round a timber platform, and the colony's
hall under a striped canvas awning.

## What it looks like

Two covered wagons, a timber platform under a striped canvas awning, a [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md) with a
lamp, supply crates round the chest, a campfire with log seats and a cooking pot, three bedrolls and a flagpole with a
white banner. The colony's [Village Hall](village-hall.md) stands on the platform and its [Travel Post](travel-post.md)
at the front by the lantern.

## How you get one

You don't build it from a blueprint: your [settlers](colonies.md) make it when they arrive, on the nearest open ground
within 48 blocks of the charter's spot, turned to face the village they came from. The chest holds the supplies they
brought.

## What happens next

The colony's builder works at the camp's Blueprint Table and starts at once on a Starter Cottage and a Storehouse, one
on either side of the camp, in the style most of the mother village's buildings use. Where the ground isn't level and
dry, that blueprint waits in the chest for you to place. The Travel Post is already on the travel network under the
colony's name, so you can travel straight there.

![Tomas starts the Sandstone cottage](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/colony/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** a [Colony Charter](colony-charter.md) and the supplies the settlers packed.
- **What it leads to:** a village that grows like any other, tied to its mother by a sister route.
