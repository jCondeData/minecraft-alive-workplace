Now and then a village earns a **Legend**: one named villager with powers no ordinary worker has. A Legend is always
a Master of their trade, wears their own outfit over it, has their name in gold over their head and gives off a soft
sparkle every few seconds. Their coming is announced, and the chronicle keeps it.

![A Legend: her outfit, gold name and sparkle](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legends_hall/still-1.jpg)

## Nothing is secret

Click the nether star on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) for the **Legends page**. Your own Legends come first,
then every other Legend as a card: their rarity, how they come, each thing the village must have with how far it has
got ("Kinds of meal in the store: 5 of 8"), the luxury they like and their powers. When a card says "This village has
all they need: they may come", you only have to wait.

## How rare

- **Rare**: one of each in every village.
- **Legendary**: one of each in the whole world. The first village that earns them keeps them.
- **Mythic**: as many as the village's rank allows: a Town may hold one, a City two. A Mythic Legend is announced to
  the whole server, with how far from spawn.

## Four ways to come

- **A visit.** Once a day a village with no Legend guest may get one, at the place their card names: the
  [inn](inn-build.md) in the morning instead of that day's traveler (it needs an [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md) and a free
  bed), a festival, the Chapel at a full-moon midnight, or beside the hall. By default one day in four.
  [Open Gates](open-gates.md) makes visits twice as likely.
- **Found out in the world.** Walk into a ruined portal, a pillager outpost or a shipwreck within 1500 blocks of a
  village you own (or a friend's) that has earned the Legend found there, and a small camp appears with them in it.
  Each structure is used once.
- **Born.** When the child of two schooled Masters grows up, one time in 20 they are a Rare Legend of a parent's trade
  (otherwise one time in 4 they are [Gifted](gifted.md)).
- **A [strange mood](strange-moods.md).** A Master in a happy village is seized, asks for three rare materials, and
  makes a Masterwork.

## Guests, and settling

A visiting or found Legend arrives as a **guest** and stays up to three days. Right-click them for their terms: what
they would bring and what they want to stay. The moment every need is met they settle: they take the free bed in a
tier III home and their trade as a Master. If not, they leave on the third evening, and the chronicle says what they
missed. Legends without a trade of their own (the Old Sage, the Seer, the Merchant Prince, the two Pokémon Legends)
take the **Legend** job, which needs no workstation.

## What a Legend needs

A settled Legend needs three things:

- **a home of their own**: their bed in a finished building of tier III or better, shared with nobody but a spouse;
- **their luxury once a week**: wine (a honey bottle, or the [Vintner](vintner.md)'s Cider, Berry Wine or Vintage
  Wine), jewels (an amethyst shard, an emerald, or the [Jeweler](jeweler.md)'s Amethyst Ring, Emerald Brooch or Gold
  Circlet), books (a book, or the [Printer](printer.md)'s Village Gazette or Illuminated Book) or fine clothing (leather armor,
  or the [Tailor](tailor.md)'s Work Clothes, Fine Clothes or Noble Robes), taken from a chest in their home or else
  the [Storehouse](storehouse.md). While it lasts they're 10 happier;
- **a happy village**: the grown-ups' average [mood](moods.md) at 60 or more.

A need unmet two days running starts a **strike** (never in their first three days). On strike their powers stop and
they picket by the hall with what they want in red over their head. The day every need is met they go back to work.
Legends never leave.

![A Legend on strike, picketing by the hall](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_strike/still-2.jpg)

## When a Legend falls

A Legend turned into a zombie villager keeps their place: cure them to have them back. One in a
[grave](grave.md) can be brought back by an [Undertaker](undertaker.md). Dead with no grave, their place is kept seven
days, then another village may earn them.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your [builder](builder.md) (tier III homes, a Chapel), your [Scholar](research.md), cooks and
  farmers (meals and a [varied diet](food-and-diet.md)), [caravans](trade-and-caravans.md) and the
  [treasury](treasury.md), and above all a happy village ([moods](moods.md)).
- **What they affect**: almost everything, from build speed to raids and weddings; see each Legend's page.
- **What stops them**: a strike (no home, no luxury, an unhappy village), death, or the **Legends** setting turned off
  (settled Legends live on as ordinary Masters and come back when it's on again). **Legend Needs, Strikes**,
  **Legends at Ruins** and **Strange Moods** switch those parts off one by one.
