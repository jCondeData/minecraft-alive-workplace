Your first builder, your first building and your first village. It takes about ten minutes.

## No village nearby? Bring settlers

Craft a **Settler's Wagon** (three white wool over two hay bales and a block of emerald, over a chest between planks)
and right-click some open ground. Two settlers make camp there: a covered wagon with a chest of supplies (logs, planks,
cobblestone, bread, torches, two blueprints and a Village Hall), a Blueprint Table, a campfire and two bedrolls. The
first settler is already a builder; the other takes whatever job you give them.

![A settlers' camp: the wagon, the campfire and the builder](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/camp/still-1.jpg)

## 1. Hire a builder

Look for a builder's workshop in a village (newly explored villages often have one), or craft a **Blueprint Table**
(blue dye on top, a cartography table in the middle, planks around it) and put it near a villager without a job. They
take it like any job block and become a **Builder**.

## 2. Get a blueprint

Builders sell the starter blueprints, the **Starter Cottage** first and more as they level up. You can also make a copy
of any blueprint at the Blueprint Table for a **Blank Blueprint** (paper and blue dye make two).

## 3. Place it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where the front of the building should go. While you hold it you see the
whole building as see-through blocks, exactly where it will stand; the gold edge is the front. Sneak-right-click the
ground to turn it.

![The see-through preview of a placed blueprint](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/preview/still-1.jpg)

## 4. Stock the chests

Put the materials in chests or barrels within 8 blocks of the builder's Blueprint Table. Hold Shift over the blueprint
to see the list; once it is placed, its tooltip counts what the chests are still short of.

## 5. Hand it over

Right-click the builder with the blueprint. They walk over, clear the ground, lay a foundation if the ground is uneven,
put up the walls and roof, add the doors, beds and torches, and tidy the ground round it. What they're building, how far
along it is and what they're waiting for floats over their head. Sneak-right-click a builder with an empty hand for the
full story.

![Over the builder: progress, what's missing, where it comes from](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/preview/still-3.jpg)

The first player to hand a builder a blueprint hires them: from then on they take orders from you and the friends you
add with `/workplace friend add <name>`.

## 6. Make it a village

Craft a **Village Hall** (gold, a book and gold over planks, an emerald in the middle) and put it in the middle of your
village, by the bell is a good spot. Right-click it to see everyone who lives there, what they're doing and what they
need. From now on villagers get names, moods and a home they care about. See [The Village Hall](village-hall.md).
