A stone gatehouse for the road into a walled village, with fence gates in its arch and ladders up to the top.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two).

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Place it in a line of Stone Walls where the road comes in, facing out.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Gatehouse, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Gatehouse II** adds a barbican in front: a second gate and portcullis, and murder holes over the gateway.

## What it does for the village

In a village with a Village Hall and at least one guard, its gates are **shut at nightfall** and opened in the morning (you can still open them yourself). See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a black banner (towers, barracks and gates).
