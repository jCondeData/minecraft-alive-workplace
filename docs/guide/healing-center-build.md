A bright white clinic under a red roof, with a counter at the front. Behind the counter stands a **brewing stand**, and with Cobblemon installed a **Healing Machine** sits on the counter itself.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). An Expert **builder** sells it for 16 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Without Cobblemon the Healing Machine's spot just stays empty.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Healing Center, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Healing Center II** adds a ward behind the counter with four beds.
- **Healing Center III** adds a berry garden behind the ward, with a composter and a second chest.
- **Healing Center IV** digs a cool **store room** under the front, down a ladder in the back room: shelves of potted herbs and fungi, kelp and moss, chests of remedies. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was.

## What it does for the village

Stand a villager by the brewing stand and sneak-right-click them with a **honey bottle** and they become the village's **Nurse** (without the honey bottle, a jobless villager takes the stand as a cleric). Right-click the nurse with an empty hand to get your health back and bad effects cleared; nurses look after hurt villagers and golems and cure the village's sick with a honey bottle, milk or a healing potion from their chest. With Cobblemon, the Healing Machine heals your team. The ward's beds are free beds the village can grow into. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a pink banner (healing and graves).
