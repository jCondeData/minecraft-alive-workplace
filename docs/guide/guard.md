The Guard keeps the village safe: from monsters at night, from raids, and from bandits.

## Getting a guard

Stand a villager by a **grindstone** and sneak-right-click them with a **sword**. (An iron ingot brings the
[Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md) back.) Put weapons and armor in a chest within 8 blocks of the grindstone: the guard takes
the best of it and wears it, so you can see it.

## On duty

Guards fight any monster within 24 blocks of their post, at any hour, and never run away. They have twice a villager's
health, heal slowly between fights and keep the night watch close to their post. Players, villagers, pets and Pokémon
are always safe from them. Over their head: how many foes they've beaten, and whether they're on patrol, getting better
gear, sparring or fighting.

**Kinds of guard.** What a guard holds in their other hand decides how they fight. Sneak-right-click a guard with one
to choose for them:

- **Archer**: a bow or crossbow. They shoot foes up to 16 blocks away and keep creepers at a safe distance.
- **Knight**: a shield. They block most blows from in front.
- **Medic**: a healing potion. They heal the most hurt villager or golem nearby.

**Training.** A **Training Dummy** within 12 blocks of the grindstone lets guards spar on quiet days and level up, as far
as Expert. Masters are made in real fights.

![Sparring with the Training Dummy](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/guard/still-4.jpg)

**On horseback.** A tame, saddled horse, donkey or mule left free (not on a lead) within 24 blocks of the post is one the
guard rides on patrol.

**Ring the bell** and the guards rush to it and fight anything near it for a minute and a half, while everyone else runs
home to hide.

## Telling them where to go

- **Patrol Map** (a map and red dye): right-click the ground at up to 8 places to mark a route, then sneak-right-click a
  guard with it. By day they walk the route, within 64 blocks of their post; at night they stay by the post. A blank map
  takes the route away.
- **Rally Banner** (any banner and an iron ingot): sneak-right-click guards to enlist them (up to 12), then right-click
  the air to raise it. While it's raised, they follow you and fight whatever goes for you, or whatever you go for. Lower
  it and they go back to their posts.
- **Gates.** In a village with a Village Hall and at least one guard, the gates of finished Gatehouses and Palisade Gates
  shut at nightfall and open again in the morning. You can still open them by hand.

## How the guard fits in

- **Kept in shape by:** the [Armorer](armorer.md) (armor), the [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md) (a sword, and mending), the
  [Fletcher](fletcher.md) (bows and spectral arrows), the [Cleric](cleric.md) (healing, regeneration and strength potions,
  drunk in a fight), the [Librarian](librarian.md) (enchantments) and the [Nurse](nurse.md) (tending their wounds). The
  [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md) keeps the iron golems who fight beside them patched up.
- **Helped by:** Fighting and Dragon Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the post, who follow up every hit with a move of
  their own (with Cobblemon), and a Strong trait (see [Village life](village-life.md)).
- **Stronger with:** the **Drill** research (10% harder hits a level) and **Fortification** (one blow in ten turned aside
  a level); see [Scholar](scholar.md).
- **Fights:** night monsters, raids and bandit camps (see [Threats and defense](defence.md)). Short of guards? Hire three
  mercenaries for 12 emeralds at the Village Hall; they stay until dawn.

## Trading

Guards buy rotten flesh and bones, and sell an iron sword, a shield, an iron chestplate, a crossbow and, at Master, a
diamond sword.
