Eight rare plants that grow only in far-off lands. You won't stumble on them in an ordinary world: a story sends you to
find them.

## The eight

| Herb | Looks like | Grows in | On |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frostbloom | pale blue bells | snowy plains and slopes, groves, ice spikes, snowy taiga | snow block, grass |
| Sunthistle | a spiky yellow thistle | desert, badlands | sand, red sand, terracotta |
| Mirewort | reeds with purple buds | swamp, mangrove swamp | mud, grass |
| Peakmoss | a silver-green tuft | windswept hills, stony and jagged peaks, meadow | stone, andesite, diorite, granite, grass |
| Jungle Orchid | a magenta orchid | every jungle | grass |
| Sea Lavender | small lilac sprays | beaches, stony shore | sand, gravel |
| Glowcap | a pale mushroom that glows | dark forest, in shade | grass, podzol |
| Petal Balm | a herb with pink leaves | cherry grove | grass |

## Finding and keeping them

A story arc plants a patch of 5 to 9 of one herb somewhere in its lands, on its own ground, once you come near. Like a
flower, a herb can be picked (it drops itself) and planted again on its ground, but it never spreads, never grows into
anything and takes no bone meal. It pops off if the block under it goes. The **Glowcap** gives a soft light and only
stands where something is over it, such as a dark forest's leaves.

## The Three-Herb Tonic

A round bottle of green tonic with leaves tied round its neck. In the story of [The Sickness](the-sickness.md), the
village's healer brews it from three far herbs and takes it round to cure the Gray Cough. You can't brew or drink it
yourself.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** [story arcs](story-arcs.md), above all [The Sickness](the-sickness.md), which plant a patch in
  the herbs' lands and send you to fetch them.
- **What it cures:** the Gray Cough of that story. Everyday [sickness](sickness.md) is the Nurse's, and the plague and
  hexes take a [Remedy](remedy.md).
- **What stops it:** story arcs switched off: then no herb is ever planted.
