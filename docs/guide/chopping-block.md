The Chopping Block is the Lumberjack's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Lumberjack works at a **fletching table**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding an axe.
The Chopping Block is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:chopping_block`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Chopping Block takes it by themselves and becomes a **Lumberjack**: no item needed.
They fell the trees within 16 blocks one at a time, replant a sapling where each stood and put the logs in the chests nearby.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Chopping Block to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** spare axes in the chests; bone meal there makes the saplings grow faster.
- **Feeds** the builders and the [Carpenter](jobs-crafts.md) with logs, and strips logs when a builder needs stripped ones.
- **A tree farm.** Hand them a [Field Marker](field-marker.md) for a plot of their own.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Chopping Block or a fletching table); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
