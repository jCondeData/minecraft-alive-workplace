The Village Hall turns a group of villagers into a village that lives: everyone gets a name, the village has needs, a
rank, a treasury and a chronicle, and you can see all of it in one screen.

## Putting one down

Craft a **Village Hall** (gold, a book and gold over planks, with an emerald in the middle) and put it in the middle of
your village; by the bell is a good spot. Everyone within 64 blocks belongs to it. The village gets a made-up name
(*Thornholm*, *Ashford*...); use a **Name Tag** on the hall to call it what you like (the tag isn't used up).

**Moving it.** Mine the hall with an axe and it drops itself, keeping a name you gave it. While it's gone, the village
leaves the caravans' trade routes and its open quests are dropped, so put it back soon.

## The hall's screen

Right-click the hall. Along the top are the village's numbers: its name and rank, villagers, beds, food in store,
guards, wellbeing, what the workers are waiting for and the buildings going up. Below are the page buttons, and below
those everyone who lives there.

Each worker is shown as their workstation, stacked as high as their level, with what they're doing ("Market Stall ·
45%") and what they're waiting for. **Click a villager to make them glow** for ten seconds so you can find them. Click a
villager without a job to see the free workstations, and click one to give them that job.

![The Village Hall's screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall/still-4.jpg)

The **bell** in the middle calls everyone home: villagers who wandered off are brought back beside the hall.

## The hall's pages

- **Quests** (the map icon): the village's quests for you. See [Quests](quests.md).
- **The chronicle** (the book): what happened in the village, newest first: babies born, buildings finished and who
  built them, raids, weddings, quests done.
- **Trade routes** (the minecart): caravans to other villages. See [Trade and caravans](trade-and-caravans.md).
- **What next?** (the compass): what the village lacks most, from a builder or beds to guards or decorations, each with
  how to put it right.
- **[The village map](map-of.md)**: draws the village on an empty map from your inventory, every building marked and colored by what
  it is.
- **The calendar**: the season, the day, and when the next festival is.
- **Mercenaries** (the iron sword): hire three fighters for a night. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

![The chronicle](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall_pages/still-1.jpg)

## Ranks

A village grows from a **Hamlet** to a **Village** (10 villagers and 5 finished buildings), a **Town** (20 villagers,
12 buildings and 3 levels of research) and a **City** (35, 25 and 7). Every rank pays: quests pay a quarter more,
caravans can go to one more village, more traders come on market day, and the village can grow ten villagers bigger. A
rank up is celebrated with fireworks over the hall. Hover over the hall's name icon to see what the next rank still
needs.

## The treasury

Every morning the village puts by its takings: about a fifth of an emerald a worker, more in a well-kept village and a
quarter more for each rank. The hall's name icon (top left) shows what's there; click it to collect. It holds up to a
stack of emeralds per rank, so don't leave it too long.

![The treasury and protection](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall_treasury/still-1.jpg)

## Protecting your village

Whoever places the hall owns it. **Shift-click the hall's name icon** to protect the village: inside its area only you,
your friends (`/workplace friend add <name>`) and operators can break or place blocks, open chests, or hurt the
villagers and animals. Anyone can still walk in, open doors, ring the bell and trade. Shift-click again to open it up.
It's off until you turn it on.

## The Village Ledger

Craft a **[Village Ledger](village-ledger.md)** (a book and an emerald) and right-click your hall with it. From then on, right-click the air
anywhere nearby to open the hall's screen without walking back.

## Needs

A village with a hall has needs: food in store, a bed for everyone, a guard for every ten villagers, light by the beds,
and decorations. How well they're met is the village's **wellbeing**, and it sets the pace of all the work: up to a
quarter faster when everyone's fed, housed and safe, and slower when they're hungry and sleeping rough. More in
[Village life](village-life.md).
