# Alive Workplace: the player guide

Welcome! Alive Workplace gives Minecraft's villagers real jobs. Hand a **Builder** a blueprint and they build it for
you, block by block, from your chests. Miners dig quarries, lumberjacks fell and replant trees, farmers look after your
fields, porters carry everything to the storehouse, guards keep the monsters out, and with Cobblemon your Pokémon lend
a hand and the village's trainers battle you. Put a **Village Hall** in the middle and the village comes alive: names,
moods, weddings, festivals, quests, raids and a treasury.

This guide is everything you need to play. No code, no jargon: just what you do and what you'll see.

## Installing

Alive Workplace is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.1. It goes on the **server and on every player's game**: download the
mod's file from the [releases page](https://github.com/jCondeData/minecraft-alive-workplace/releases), put it in your
`mods` folder (and take out the old one when you update). It works on its own, and it is made for
the Cobbleverse pack: with Cobblemon installed the Pokémon jobs and partners join in. Updating never breaks a world:
your builds, your villagers' jobs and your settings are kept.

Every player gets a **Guide Book** the first time they join, with a page and a picture for each of the basics. Lost
it? Craft another from a book and wheat.
