A folded paper with a shine, its lore saying who it's from and for: "From Dara of Oakvale", "For Mira in Elmford".
Villagers send them to family in other villages, and ask a friend to carry them.

![Three hearts: Dara asks for a letter carried to Mira in Elmford](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/letter/still-1.jpg)

## Carrying it

1. A villager with family in a village within caravan range asks you to carry a letter. Say **[I'll help]** and you
   get the **Sealed Letter to Mira**.
2. Find Mira in her village and **right-click her** with it. She reads it and writes back: you get the **Reply from
   Mira**. She counts it a favor (150 friendship), and her village's chronicle says "Jesse brought Mira a letter from
   Dara of Oakvale".
3. Take the reply home and right-click Dara with it. The request is done: 4 emeralds, her thanks and 150 friendship.

![Home again: the reply to Dara, and her thanks](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/letter/still-4.jpg)

Lost the letter? Right-click Dara empty-handed and she writes another ("Lost it? Never mind, I'll write it out
again"). Lost the reply, and Mira writes again.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** [friendship](friendship.md) (the asking takes 3 hearts) and a second village nearby, linked by
  [caravans](trade-and-caravans.md).
- **What it affects:** your friendship in two villages at once, and both villages' chronicles.
- It isn't the [letter](letter.md) you post from a Mailbox: a Sealed Letter goes by your own hand.
