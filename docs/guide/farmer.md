The Farmer is vanilla's farmer, grown up: give them a field and they run it, from tilling to harvest.

## Getting a farmer

A villager without a job takes a free **composter** by themselves, as in vanilla. If a villager already works at a
composter as something else, sneak-right-click them with **wheat** or **seeds** to make them a Farmer again.

## Giving them a field

1. Craft a **Field Marker** (a stick, wheat seeds and paper), or buy one from a farmer for an emerald.
2. Right-click one corner block of your field, then the opposite one (up to 32 by 32). Sneak-right-click a block to start
   over.
3. Hand it to the farmer. Put seeds (and a hoe, if there's bare dirt to till) in a chest within 8 blocks of their
   composter. The field must be within 48 blocks of it.

**No marker needed in a village.** A village's own farmers look after the farm next to their composter by themselves, as
soon as there's a chest by it.

## What they do

They harvest what's ripe and plant the same crop straight back, sow empty farmland with seeds from the chests, till bare
dirt with a hoe, cut sugar cane, cactus, bamboo and kelp down to the last block (and plant more beside them), pick
pumpkins and melons, and carry the harvest to the chests. With nothing else to do, they put spare seeds in the composter
for bone meal once the chests hold 64. When the field is all tended, they go back to a vanilla farmer's day until
something ripens.

Over their head: the field's size and how much they've harvested. Sneak-right-click them with an empty hand for the full
status and how to stop them.

## How the farmer fits in

- **Feeds:** the [Porter](porter.md) carries the harvest to the storehouse (leaving 64 seeds of each kind), where the
  village eats it and the [Chef](chef.md) cooks with it. Spare seeds go to the [Composter](composter.md).
- **Fed by:** the [Composter](composter.md)'s bone meal.
- **Stops when:** there are no seeds in the chests ("needs seeds"), or the composter is gone.
