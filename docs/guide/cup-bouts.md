Each round of the [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md) starts when the last round's bouts are done. The winner of each bout
goes on; the last one standing is champion.

## Between two villagers

![Both Pokémon out](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cup_bout/still-1.jpg)

A bout between two village entrants is an exhibition at the [Arena](arena-build.md)'s ring. Each trainer stands in
their box; their Pokémon come out one at a time beside the ring, face each other and take turns, the faster first,
each using one of its moves with Cobblemon's own animation and effect. Chat says what happens ("Lucario used Aura
Sphere!", and whether it's super effective, not very effective or missed). Type matchups count, the same chart Pokémon
players know. A fainted Pokémon is called
back and the next one comes out. A bout lasts 90 seconds at most; then the side with more health left wins.

Their teams follow the Cup's [theme](cup-themes.md), and a delegate fields the same team their Leader uses at home.
Everyone at the Arena reads the result ("Mira of Thornholm beat Dara of Ashford: Lucario stands"), and both trainers
get trainer experience, the winner more. A delegate's experience goes home to the real Leader.

## A player's bout

When your bout is next, chat calls you with a bell and a clickable **[I'm ready]**. Come inside the Arena and click it
within 2 minutes, or you lose by walkover (so does a player who's offline). You can also type
`/workplace cup ready` ([the command](command-cup.md)).

- **Against a villager**: a real Cobblemon battle against the delegate or host trainer, in the theme's format (singles
  or doubles), at its level and with its rules.
- **Against another player**: Cobblemon's own player battle.

Your team is the first Pokémon in your party that the theme allows, up to its number; before the battle you're told
which were left out and why ("it has helped a villager at work on 2 days, and this Cup asks for 3"). Everyone fights
with healed copies, so nobody gains experience or keeps damage. Fleeing loses the bout. With no Pokémon that may fight,
you lose the bout.

**[Watch]** on the Cup page, or on the Arena's notice board, puts you in the stands' view of a player bout being
fought.

## The purse

Money only: **100,000 PokéDollars for each bout you win**, **500,000 more for winning the final**, and half as much
again at a City host. Without CobbleDollars it's paid in emeralds, at the server's **CobbleDollar Rate**
([the setting](settings-cobbledollar-rate.md)). The Cup itself goes to the village you stood for
([Champions](cup-champions.md)).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Feeds**: your party, and the village trainers' teams (stronger with experience: see [Trainer](trainer.md)).
- **Affects**: trainer experience for the villagers who fight, your purse, who holds the Cup.
- **No daily limit**: Cup bouts don't use up your ordinary challenges of a Leader or Trainer.
