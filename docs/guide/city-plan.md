The City Plan is your village's master plan: where the homes go, where the workshops, the farms and the market, where
nothing may ever be built, and where the streets and the wall run. It's also what makes a builder the village's
[Steward](steward.md).

## Getting one

Craft a **map**, a [Blank Blueprint](blank-blueprint.md) and a **Heart of the Sea** together, anywhere in the grid. The
Heart of the Sea is on purpose: a planned city is something you work toward. A Journeyman Steward sells another for 6
emeralds.

## Binding it

Right-click a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) with it: "The plan is now" that village's. Its tooltip names the
village. An unbound plan only tells you to right-click a hall first.

## Painting the plan

Right-click the air to open it. You see the village map as it is today (the hall and every finished building's banner
on it) under a grid of 32 by 32 cells. A cell is 4 by 4 blocks with the default **Village Hall Reach** of 64, and the
grid is centered on the hall. Cells nearer another village's hall are left out.

- **+ New** makes a zone: give it a name (1 to 32 letters), a **kind** ([the zones](plan-zones.md)), a building **style** (or As drawn)
  and the **Renew** switch ([old houses](old-house-renewal.md)).
- **Brush** paints cells one by one (drag to paint more), **Area** paints a rectangle, **Erase** takes cells out of
  every zone (right-click erases with any tool), and **Undo** steps back up to 10 times.
- **Road** draws a lane (1 block wide), a street (3) or an avenue (5): click points along it and double-click (or press
  Enter) to end it. Right-click a road to take it off. See [Roads](roads.md).
- **Wall** draws the wall line round the village, **Open** or **Closed**. See [Walls](walls.md).

Only the hall's owner, their friends and operators may change it; anyone else sees it read-only.

## The plan on the ground

While you hold the plan, the zone edges, the roads and the wall line show on the ground around you (within 24 blocks)
as colored dust only you can see. Nothing is placed in the world. Once the village has a plan, the map you get from the
hall's map button shows the zones' tints and the roads, so you can hang the plan in an item frame by the hall
([Village map](village-map.md)).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Appoints the Steward**: sneak-right-click a Journeyman (or better) Builder beside the hall with it
  ([The Steward](steward.md)).
- **Steers everything he builds**: which zone each building goes in, which style it's built in, which roads he lays and
  where the wall runs. Nothing is ever built in a Keep Clear zone.
- **Stays with the hall**: break the hall and the plan is kept on the hall item; put it down again and the plan is
  centered on the new spot.
