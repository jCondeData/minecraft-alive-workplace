The shop board: a steep little timber booth that turns its gable to the street and opens like a box. Its lower board folds down as the counter, with cloth laid out on it; its upper board is propped up as the canopy under a red-and-white valance, with the trader's sign on the gable, a pennant pole at the corner and a side hatch selling from the west. It's the cheerful little shop front of a young village. Rebuilt from scratch in 2026; stalls already standing keep their old look until you refresh them at the Village Hall.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). An Apprentice **builder** sells it for 8 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the stall, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Market Stall II** builds a lock-up shop beside the booth under a lower gable, and the **Shop Counter** takes the middle of the booth's counter board, on the street. A villager without a job who comes by takes the counter and becomes its **Shopkeeper**; open the counter to set prices and put the goods in the chests next to it (see [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md)).
- **Market Stall III** runs the lock-up back into a storeroom with two more double chests and stock on shelves, and a back door for deliveries.

## What it does for the village

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a yellow banner (stores, shops and markets).
