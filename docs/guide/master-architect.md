"Halls for kings, bridges for cities, a lighthouse no storm has moved: all drawn by one hand." The **Master Architect**
comes to a Town that builds in many styles, and makes it grand.

## How they come

Once your village is a **Town** and its builders have finished buildings in **three different styles**, the Master
Architect may visit the inn some morning (it needs an [Innkeeper](innkeeper.md) and a free bed). Give them a tier III
home and their jewels within three days and they settle as a Master [Builder](builder.md).

## What they do

- **Twice as fast.** Every builder within 32 blocks of the Architect works twice as fast. It shares the server's
  **Fastest Work Pace** cap with every other speed-up ([work pace](work-pace.md)).
- **Grander buildings.** Every 3 days the Architect picks a building your builders finished (homes first; never a
  decoration, a defense or anything you built by hand) and hands the least busy builder its next upgrade. A building with no
  upgrade left is **redrawn in the Grand style** on the same spot: only the blocks that differ are taken down and
  placed, with materials from the builder's chests as usual. One at a time, and each one goes in the chronicle.

Sneak-right-click the Architect to pause the rebuilding ("will leave the village's buildings as they are"), and again to
start it.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your builders and their chests (every grand rebuild needs real materials), and a happy village.
- **What they affect**: build speed and your village's looks; higher tiers mean more homes good enough for other
  Legends.
- **What stops them**: a strike stops the rebuild under way and the speed-up until every need is met again.
