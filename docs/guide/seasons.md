The world keeps a **village calendar**: four seasons, each sixteen days long, the same for every village on the server.
Each season has a festival day in the middle.

## The calendar

Open a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and click the **calendar**. It shows the four seasons, which one it is, the day
("Summer, day 3 of 16") and when the village's next festival is.

## How it interacts

- **Festivals.** Each season's middle day is its festival day. Villages also hold their own regular
  [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) every eight days, and you can call one early with a cake.
- **Length.** The **Days per Season** setting changes how long each season lasts, from 1 to 120 days
  ([Seasons and pace settings](settings-seasons-and-pace.md)).
- **Shared.** The calendar counts the world's days, so every village is in the same season on the same day.
