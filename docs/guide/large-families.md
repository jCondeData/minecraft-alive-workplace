# Large Families

Reformed: two babies a day, with no extra food and no extra illness

For a village in a hurry to grow.

## What it does

While Large Families is in force, the village can have **up to two babies a day**. Each baby is dearer, though: there
must be **24 meals in the store** for one to be born, and the family eats 12 of them. And with so many little ones
about, **villagers fall ill 50% more often**.

## Reforming it

While it's in force, the Nurse asks for **midwives**: 48 honey bottles, then 192 white wool, then 64 golden carrots
([The Midwives](the-midwives.md)). After that, big families need no more food than usual and fall ill no more often,
and a midwife comes to stay as a second Nurse.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: food in the store, from your [farmers and chefs](jobs-land.md), and homes for the new families
  from your [builder](builder.md).
- **What it affects**: how fast the village grows, and so its rank (and its edict slots). More people means more
  workers in time.
- **What breaks it**: an empty store (no babies) and illness: keep a [Nurse](jobs-services.md) and research Medicine.
  See [Village life](village-life.md) for babies and sickness.
