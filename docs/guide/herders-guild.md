The guild of the pens and pastures.

## Perks

- Shepherds, Butchers and Ranchers work **15% faster**.
- **Every herd may be 4 bigger**: 12 of a kind bred instead of 8, and 14 kept by a hired butcher instead of 10.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Shepherd, Butcher or Rancher with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master herders, feed for the animals and room in the pens.
- **What it affects**: wool, leather and meat for the village.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
