A purple stone on a copper band: the first piece a new Jeweler learns, and a Burgher's little vanity.

## Getting it

A Novice [Jeweler](jeweler.md) makes one from **2 amethyst shards** and **2 copper ingots**, and keeps 8 in the village
store. A Jeweler sells them for 4 emeralds, and the chest in a village's Jeweler's Workshop now and then holds one.

## What it's for

A want of the [Burghers](burgher.md): each Burgher household takes one from the store every 16 days. It isn't a need,
but every want a household has makes it pay 10% more tax.

## How it fits with the rest

Amethyst comes from geodes, copper from a [Miner](miner.md). See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
