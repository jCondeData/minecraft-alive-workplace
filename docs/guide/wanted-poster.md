"Wanted: Grimwald the Red." The poster you're handed when you take a bounty, so you know who you're after.

## Getting it

Click a bounty on the **Bounties** tab of the [quest journal](quest-journal.md), at the hall or from a
[Bounty Board](bounty-board.md). You get the poster and a [map](hideout-map.md), once per bounty.

## What it tells you

Hold Shift over it: his **name**, his **quirk** (Ironhide, Fleetfoot, Crossbow Ace, Beast Rider, Night Stalker, Warlord
or Hexer, so you know what to bring), where he was **last seen**, and the **reward** in emeralds. A rogue Pokémon's
poster says "Wanted: Rogue Ursaring", its level, and to beat it in battle or catch it.

## How it fits with the rest

The poster is your reminder; what pays is his [Captain's Insignia](captains-insignia.md), handed in at the board or the
hall. See [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md).
