Click the guards icon (the iron sword) on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen and you get the village's war
room: who's camped outside, how strong they are, how your gates stand and what you can do about it.

## What it shows

- **The lair.** Whose camp it is, the captain by name, its strength (how many are at the camp, out raiding and lost in
  the last raid, and how fast it grows), roughly where it is ("south-east, about 90 blocks") and how many days it has
  stood. After a [Scout](scout.md) has been, its exact place, who found it and when. With no camp: "No camp near", and
  how many days none will come after one was broken.
- **The gates.** Open or shut, how many stand, the portcullis up or down, "Under siege" while one is on, and how many
  gate blocks the rams broke that wait for a builder.
- **The last three attacks**, newest first: the day, who came, how many came and fell, and whether they were fought off
  or got away.
- **A warning.** On a day the village was warned of a raid, the attack that's coming and from which side. Without a
  lookout it says "No lookout".

## What you can do here

- **Scout the lair** (a spyglass): sends the nearest free [Scout](scout.md) by day. See
  [Scouting Report](scouting-report.md).
- **Muster a war party** (a white banner) and **Hire the Siege-breakers** (a crossbow): see [War party](war-party.md).
- **At peace** (a white tulip, top right): the hall's owner sets the village at peace. No raids, raiders' camps or
  disasters come there, and a camp already standing packs up and leaves at the next dawn. The guards icon says so.
  Its [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md) posts no new captains; bounties already up stay until paid or run out.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** your [guards](guard.md), a Lookout Tower or Wall Tower for warnings, and the walls and gates your
  builder puts up (see [Sieges](sieges.md)).
- **What it reads:** the raiders' camps of every kind: [bandits](bandit-camps.md), the
  [pillager warband](pillager-warband.md), [desert raiders](desert-raiders.md),
  [drowned pirates](drowned-pirates.md), the [piglin incursion](piglin-incursion.md) and the
  [witch coven](witch-coven.md).
- **What overrides it:** the [Threat Level](threat-level.md) at 0, or Peaceful difficulty: then the page says threats
  are off on the server.
