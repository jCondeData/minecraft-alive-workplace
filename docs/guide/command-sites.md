# /workplace sites

```infobox
type: Command
Syntax: /workplace sites
Who: everyone (operators see everyone's)
Shows: your builds and quarries in progress, each with its status
categories: Commands, Builder
related: command-cancel.md, builder.md, jobs-land.md, command-friend.md
```

Lists your work in progress in chat: every build your builders are putting up and every quarry your miners are digging,
in every dimension. Each line has the work's status, and a button to cancel it. Nothing going on? "No builds in progress."

## Who sees what

You see your own builds and quarries. Operators see everyone's.

## How it interacts

- The cancel button runs [/workplace cancel](command-cancel.md) for that build.
- A village's builds going up also show on its [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen. See
  [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) and [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).
