The Cleric is vanilla's cleric, now brewing for the guards.

## Getting a cleric

A villager without a job takes a free **brewing stand** as a Cleric, as in vanilla. A brewing stand has other jobs too (a
honey bottle picks the [Nurse](nurse.md), a golden apple the [Undertaker](undertaker.md)); a **glass bottle** brings the
Cleric back.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a cleric with a glass bottle to hire them: they then brew for your own guards.

## What they do

At the stand they brew the potions the guards need: **healing**, **regeneration** and **strength**, from what's in the
chests by the stand (nether wart, glistering melon, ghast tears, blaze powder for fuel, and water bottles, or glass bottles
they fill at water within 8 blocks). They keep three of each in the chests. A guard of the village with fewer than two
potions gets one brought to the chest by their post, and drinks it in a fight.

Over their head: how many potions they've brewed, and whether they're brewing, filling bottles or taking one to a guard.

## How the cleric fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Netherworker](netherworker.md)'s nether wart and ghast tears, or your own.
- **Feeds:** every [Guard](guard.md). A guard holding a healing potion in their other hand is a Medic, who heals the most
  hurt villager or golem nearby.
- **Stops when:** there's no chest by the brewing stand, or nothing to brew with.
