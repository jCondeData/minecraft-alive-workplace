A tall town house with its slate gable to the street: a brick shop with a window either side of the door, and two
stories of dark oak and white plaster above, jettied out over the street. It's the home a Burgher household needs.

## Getting the blueprint

It isn't in the Blueprint Table: **builders sell it**. Once the village round the nearest hall has a household of
[Artisans](artisan.md) or better, every builder there offers the Townhouse for **14 emeralds**. Once the village has 3
[Burgher](burgher.md) households, the extra trader they bring on market days sells it too.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and
right-click the builder with it. Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick another style. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

The builder who finishes a tier sells the next one. Right-click the finished house with the upgrade and it lines up
exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new.

- **Townhouse II** adds a walled courtyard garden behind (brick walls, a gate, flower beds, an azalea, a bench and a
  lamp) and fits out the loft as a third bedroom.
- **Townhouse III** adds a stone corner tower, four stories up to a study under a slate cap: shelves of books, a desk,
  a reading chair and windows every way.

![The Manor beside it, the grandest home of all](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/grand_houses/still-2.jpg)
- **Townhouse IV** digs a brick-vaulted **cellar** under the house, down a stone stair from the hall: racks of casks, chests of the household's stock and a corner of coal. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was.

## What it does for the village

- **A grade II home from the first tier**, and grade III once upgraded: exactly what a [Burgher](burgher.md) household
  needs, and what a [Noble](noble.md) household needs after an upgrade. See [Households](households.md).
- **A better mood** for whoever sleeps there: +5 for grade II, +10 for grade III.
- Like every finished building near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), it counts toward the village's rank.

## How it fits with the rest

The Townhouse is the step between the [Artisan's House](artisans-house-build.md) and the
[Noble's Manor](manor-build.md). The hall's **What next?** page names the households that want a grade II home, so
you know when to build one.
