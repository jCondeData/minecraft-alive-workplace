In a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), villagers are people: they have names, quirks and moods, they eat,
fall ill, fall in love, have babies and grow up.

## Names and traits

Every villager gets a [**name**](names.md) of their own (look at them, or see the hall's list; a Name Tag's name stays). Each has a [**trait**](traits.md) or two that never changes:

| Trait | What it does |
|---|---|
| Diligent | works a little faster |
| Lazy | works a little slower |
| Nimble | walks faster |
| Clever | learns their job faster |
| Strong | hits harder (good in a guard) |
| Cheerful | makes the whole village happier |
| Glutton | eats twice a day |
| Frugal | eats every other day |

## Food

More in [Food and diet](food-and-diet.md). Every grown villager eats once a day from the village's store: the chests by the smokers first (the chef's cooking),
then the storehouse's. Anything plain to eat will do. Villagers like a **varied diet**: a store with bread, baked
potatoes, cooked fish and pies keeps them happier than one full of bread. The hall's food icon says how many kinds of
meal the store has.

## Moods

Every grown villager has a [**mood**](moods.md), which the hall's list shows with its reasons: fed or hungry, a bed of their own,
a job, ill or well, decorations near home, company and their diet. Happy villagers work faster, unhappy ones slower.

## Sickness

Now and then a villager [falls ill](sickness.md), more often when they're hungry or sleep rough. The ill sneeze, walk slowly and work at
half pace. They get better after three days, or straight away when a **Nurse** gives them a honey bottle, milk or a
healing potion from her chest.

## Couples, weddings and families

Now and then two grown villagers who aren't family start [**courting**](couples-and-weddings.md), and two days later they **marry**: a wedding at
the village's Chapel (or its bell) with fireworks, and the whole village in a good mood. Married couples are first in
line for a baby. When one dies, the other mourns for a few days.

A baby remembers its parents, and when they grow up and there's a free workstation, they take up a parent's trade.

## The village grows

More in [Babies and growth](babies-and-growth.md). At most once a day, with a **free bed**, plenty of food in the store and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby, up to a limit that rises with the village's [rank](ranks.md) (the hall's villager count says what it still needs). So build houses: the **Terrace**, the **Inn** and the three-tier **Stone
House** are in the Blueprint Table.

## Decorations

The [Well](well-build.md), [Street Lamp](street-lamp-build.md), [Park Bench](park-bench-build.md), [Fountain](fountain-build.md), [Gazebo](gazebo-build.md), [Bandstand](bandstand-build.md), [Market Square](market-square-build.md) and Chapel make a village prettier: each adds to its

## Chatter

More in [Chatter](chatter.md). Walk through a village with a hall and now and then someone off work turns to you and says something over their head:
about their mood, the village's news (a festival tonight, bandits camped to the north) or just hello, by name.

## Graves

A villager with a job or a name who dies leaves a [**grave**](graves.md) where they fell: right-click it to read who lies there. An
[Undertaker](jobs-services.md) can bring them back.
