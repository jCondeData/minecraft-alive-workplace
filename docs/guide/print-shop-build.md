A brick shop in a spruce frame, its plastered gable to the street under a dark oak roof. The cartography table is the
press: it stands in the middle of the room under a skylight. By the door a counter for buyers, and behind it shelves of
paper and ink.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**; a [Printer](printer.md) who reaches Journeyman also sells it, for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Place the blueprint, stock the chests by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists (hold Shift over it), and
hand it to the builder. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

**Print Shop II** adds a lower wing behind: the bindery (a bench with sheets laid out, a chest of paper) and a reading
room lined with bookshelves three high, with a chair on a red carpet under a lantern.

## What it does for the village

Its cartography table is a **Printer's** workstation: sneak-right-click a villager by it with an ink sac (their
household must be Artisan or better). Villages sometimes grow a small Print Shop of their own, with a Printer already
living in it.

## How it fits with the rest

The Printer prints the [Village Gazette](village-gazette.md), the [Illuminated Book](illuminated-book.md), books for the
Scholars and family trees. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
