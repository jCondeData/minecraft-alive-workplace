The Ball Workbench is the Ball Smith's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Ball Smith works at a **smithing table**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding an apricorn.
The Ball Workbench is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:ball_workbench`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Ball Workbench takes it by themselves and becomes a **Ball Smith**: no item needed.
Put apricorns and metal in the chests (copper for Poké Balls, iron for Great Balls, gold for Ultra Balls, diamonds for the rarest) and the smith makes balls four at a time.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Ball Workbench to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

## How it fits in

- **Needs Cobblemon**: the balls and apricorns are Cobblemon's.
- **Fed by** apricorns, which an [Orchard Keeper](fruit-basket.md) picks.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Ball Workbench or a smithing table); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
