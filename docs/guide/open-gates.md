Throw the gates open and the world comes to visit, the good and the bad.

## What it does

While Open Gates is in force, the **inn takes 4 guests** and **up to two travelers arrive each morning**, and **one more
traveling trader** comes on market days. In return, **bandits make camp near the village twice as often**. It can't be
in force together with the [Curfew](curfew.md).

## Reforming it

While it's in force, a Guard asks you to arm the watch: 192 iron ingots, then 256 arrows, then 40 monsters slain
([The Watchful Gate](the-watchful-gate.md)). After that, travelers keep coming and bandits are no likelier to make
camp than usual, and an old soldier comes to guard the gate.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: an inn and an [Innkeeper](jobs-services.md) to take the guests, and
  [market days](festivals-and-markets.md) for the extra trader. Travelers are villagers you can hire.
- **What it affects**: how fast you find new workers, and what's for sale on market days.
- **What breaks it**: [bandit camps](defence.md#bandit-camps). Keep guards ready, or reform it.
