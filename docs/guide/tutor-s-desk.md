The Tutor's Desk is the Move Tutor's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Move Tutor works at a **Training Post**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a book.
The Tutor's Desk is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:tutors_desk`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Tutor's Desk takes it by themselves and becomes a **Move Tutor**: no item needed.
Right-click the tutor, pick one of your Pokémon and a move it could learn but won't get from leveling. A new tutor teaches weaker moves; a Master teaches everything.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Tutor's Desk to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

## How it fits in

- **Needs Cobblemon**: without it there's nothing to teach.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Tutor's Desk or a Training Post); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
