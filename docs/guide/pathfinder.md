"There is no road they haven't walked, and few they didn't walk first." The **Pathfinder** knows where the
strongholds sleep, and will take you there.

## How they come

Once one of your [Cartographers](cartographer.md) reaches **Expert**, keep an eye out at ruined portals within 1500 blocks
of your village: a traveler's camp may be pitched beside one. Talk to the traveler and they're free; they come to your
hall the next morning as a guest. A child of two schooled Master Cartographers may also grow up as the Pathfinder.

## What they do

- **Far expeditions.** Their own expeditions range twice as far and bring back more maps, and now and then a trial key
  or an echo shard.
- **They lead you.** Put 8 food in the chests by their cartography table and sneak-right-click them. Pick a
  **Stronghold**, an **Ancient City** or **Trial Chambers**; they hand you a map to the nearest one within 3000 blocks
  and set off ahead of you, waiting when you fall behind and fighting whatever attacks either of you. At the place they
  plant a banner. Right-click them there and stand still: a few seconds later you're both home beside the Village Hall.
  Once a day; logging out or dying calls it off.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: a Cartographer who has done a lot of exploring, and food in their chests.
- **What they affect**: your [Explorer's Maps](explorer-s-map.md) and how far your village knows the world, and every
  trip you take to the end-game structures.
- **What stops them**: a strike ("won't lead anyone anywhere while they're on strike"), or no food in the chests.
