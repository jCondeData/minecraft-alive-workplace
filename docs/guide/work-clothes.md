Sturdy clothes for a working household. Being properly dressed for work is part of what makes a household Artisan.

## Getting it

A Novice [Tailor](tailor.md) sews a set from **3 wool** (any colors, mixed if you like), **2 leather** and a
**string**, and keeps 8 in the village store. A Tailor sells them for 3 emeralds, and the chest in a village's Tailor's
Shop now and then holds a set.

## What it's for

A need of the [Artisans](artisan.md): every Artisan household takes a set from the store every 8 days. A Peasant
household can't rise to Artisan without one, and an Artisan household that goes three dawns without comes back down.

## How it fits with the rest

Wool from a [Shepherd](shepherd.md), leather from cows (a [Butcher](butcher.md) keeps them breeding). See
[Luxuries](luxuries.md).
