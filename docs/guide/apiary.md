The Apiary is the Beekeeper's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Beekeeper works at a **beehive or bee nest**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a glass bottle or shears.
The Apiary is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:apiary`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Apiary takes it by themselves and becomes a **Beekeeper**: no item needed.
They harvest the full beehives within 16 blocks (honey bottles or honeycomb), plant flowers round hives that have few, and breed the bees.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Apiary to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** glass bottles and shears in the chests.
- **Keep them calm.** A campfire under a hive keeps the bees calm.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Apiary or a beehive or bee nest); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
