The Guard Post is the Guard's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Guard works at a **grindstone**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a sword.
The Guard Post is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:guard_post`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Guard Post takes it by themselves and becomes a **Guard**: no item needed.
Guards fight any monster within 24 blocks of their post, at any hour, and take their weapons and armor from the chests near it.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Guard Post to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Threats and defense](defence.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** the weapons and armor in the chests; the village's smiths, fletchers and clerics keep them in gear.
- **Training.** A [Training Dummy](training-dummy.md) within 12 blocks lets them spar on quiet days.
- **Orders.** A [Patrol Map](patrol-map.md) gives them a route to walk by day, and a [Rally Banner](rally-banner.md) makes them follow you.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Guard Post or a grindstone); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
