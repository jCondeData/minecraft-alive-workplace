Two commands for players in the [Festival Cup](festival-cup.md). They do what the clickable buttons in chat do, so you
rarely need to type them.

## /workplace cup ready

When your bout is called ("Festival Cup, round 1: your bout against Dara of Ashford is next!"), stand inside the
host's [Arena](arena-build.md) and type it (or click **[I'm ready]**) within 2 minutes. If your opponent is ready too
the battle begins; otherwise you wait for them. Outside the Arena you're told to come inside first; with no bout of
yours being called, it says so.

## /workplace cup watch

While a player's bout is being fought, this puts you in the stands' view of the battle, like **[Watch]** on the Cup
page or the Arena's notice board. With nothing being fought: "No player bout is being fought right now."

## How it fits with the rest

- Miss the 2 minutes and you lose by walkover ([Cup bouts](cup-bouts.md)).
- Being in another battle stops the bout from starting: finish it and use `ready` again.
