The Shepherd is vanilla's shepherd, now with a flock to keep and a loom to weave at: the village's weaver.

## Getting a shepherd

A villager without a job takes a free **loom** as a Shepherd, as in vanilla. If a villager at a loom has another job,
sneak-right-click them with **shears** to make them a Shepherd again. Put shears and wheat in a chest within 8 blocks of
the loom.

**Hiring.** A shepherd works for their village. Sneak-right-click one with **shears** to hire them: from then on they
look after your sheep and work with your own workers.

## What they do

They shear the sheep within 16 blocks of the loom with shears from the chests, pick up the wool, and breed the sheep with
wheat until there are 8. With Cobblemon installed they also shear the woolly Pokémon kept in a Pasture Block there
(Wooloo and Dubwool). Everything goes in the chests by the loom.

**Weaving for builders.** When a builder nearby is waiting for wool, dyed wool, carpet, a bed, a banner, a banner
pattern or a painting, the Shepherd makes it at the loom from what the builder can get at, with the game's own recipes,
one step after another: two blue beds from white wool, blue dye and planks are the wool dyed first, then the beds; a
banner from string is the wool spun from the string first, then the banner. What's made goes into the builder's chest.
The flock waits while they weave, and the weaving waits while they shear.

If something is short, the builder's line says what: "Can't be made yet: short of 6 Blue Dye to make 2 Blue Beds". A dye
is the [Florist](florist.md)'s to make, so the line names the dye itself, not the lapis or cornflowers it comes from
(with lapis or a cornflower in the chests, the Shepherd still makes the dye himself). A builder with no Shepherd
nearby reads "Hire a Shepherd to make Blue Beds".

Over their head: how many they've sheared, and whether they're shearing, picking up wool or feeding a pair to breed;
while weaving, "Shepherd · 3 woven" and what they're making ("making 2 Blue Beds (step 1 of 2)").

## How the shepherd fits in

- **Feeds:** the wool goes to the storehouse with the [Porter](porter.md) (who leaves them some wheat for breeding).
  The [Builder](builder.md) gets the wool, carpet, beds and banners they wove, straight into their chests. (The
  [Leatherworker](leatherworker-dyer.md) used to dye wool for builders; that's the Shepherd's work now.)
- **Fed by:** string or wool, and the dyes (the [Florist](florist.md)'s), planks and sticks (the
  [Carpenter](carpenter.md)'s).
- **Stops when:** there's no chest by the loom ("needs a chest by the loom").

See also [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md) and the [Butcher](butcher.md), who keeps the other animals.
