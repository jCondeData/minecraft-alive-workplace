The Rancher breaks in the wild horses round the ranch, saddles them, breeds them and, with Cobblemon, grooms your
Pokémon and runs a daycare.

## Getting a rancher

Stand a villager by a **smoker** and sneak-right-click them with a **saddle** or a **golden carrot**. (Raw meat, fish or a
potato picks the [Chef](chef.md), a lead the [Butcher](butcher.md).) Put a chest within 8 blocks of the smoker.

## What they do

- **Breaking in.** Wild horses, donkeys and llamas near the smoker are broken in a few tries each, the horse rearing
  until it gives in.
- **Saddles and armor.** Tame ones get the saddles from the chests, and horse armor or llama carpets if you leave some.
- **Breeding.** Pairs are bred until there are 8 of a kind: horses and donkeys with golden carrots or golden apples,
  llamas with hay bales, camels with cactus.
- **Grooming (with Cobblemon).** The Pokémon in Pasture Blocks nearby are groomed once a day, and their friendship goes
  up (a little more when there's a berry for them in the chests). A pasture by the ranch is where a Pokémon that
  evolves by friendship grows fond of you.

Over their head: how many animals they've tamed, and whether they're breaking in, saddling, grooming or feeding the
herd.

## The daycare (with Cobblemon)

Right-click the rancher with an empty hand to leave up to **two** Pokémon from your party (a rancher looks after eight
at most). While they're there they gain experience, twice as fast with a Master rancher. Collecting them costs one
emerald plus one for every level they gained (or the same in CobbleDollars). If the rancher dies, your Pokémon go to
your PC.

## How the rancher fits in

- **Feeds:** a tame, saddled horse left free near a [Guard](guard.md)'s post is one the guard rides on patrol.
- **Shares the smoker with:** the [Chef](chef.md) and the [Butcher](butcher.md), who need their own smokers.
- **Stops when:** there's no chest by the smoker.

## Trading

Ranchers buy wheat, apples and hay bales, and sell leads, a saddle, golden carrots, leather, iron and golden horse armor
and, at Master, a name tag.
