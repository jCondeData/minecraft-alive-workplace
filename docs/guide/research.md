A village's **Scholar** works out new ideas at the lectern, and every level of research is a bonus for the whole
village. Research is kept in the [Village Hall](village-hall.md), so it stays with the village even if the scholar
changes.

## Choosing a topic

Sneak-right-click the scholar with an empty hand for the village's **research tree**, and click a topic to research it
next. The scholar takes the cost from the chest (paper, books and emeralds) and works it out at the lectern. Some topics
open only after others.

## The topics

| Topic | Levels | Needs first | What each level does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swift Hands | 3 | | every job in the village 5% faster |
| Hearth | 2 | | the village's wellbeing 10% higher |
| Drill | 3 | | guards hit 10% harder |
| Kinship | 2 | Swift Hands I | one more Pokémon partner per worker |
| Lore | 1 | Hearth I | children who went to school start as Journeymen |
| Architecture | 1 | Swift Hands II, Hearth I | the scholars draw up the Town Hall blueprint |
| Logistics | 2 | Swift Hands I | porters carry 3 more stacks |
| Craftsmanship | 2 | Swift Hands I | carpenters, masons, tinkerers, chefs and the other crafters 15% faster |
| Medicine | 2 | Hearth I | villagers a third less likely to fall ill |
| Fortification | 2 | Drill I | guards turn aside one blow in ten |
| Commerce | 2 | Hearth I | one more trader on market days, and mercenaries 3 emeralds cheaper |
| Expeditions | 2 | Logistics I | explorers and netherworkers back 20% sooner |
| Green Thumb | 2 | Hearth I | composters need a layer less compost for a bone meal |
| Warding | 1 | Fortification I | explosions (creepers, TNT, fireballs) no longer break blocks in the village |

A level costs 16 paper and 4 emeralds for each level number (level II: 32 and 8), plus books from level II on. Lore and
Architecture need 4 books even at level I.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** a scholar with paper, books and emeralds in the chest. The [treasury](treasury.md) is a good source of
  emeralds.
- **Ranks.** Levels researched count towards the village's [rank](ranks.md): a Town needs 3, a City 7.
- **Work pace.** Swift Hands and Craftsmanship add to the [work pace](work-pace.md), up to the Fastest Work Pace cap.
- **Wellbeing.** Hearth raises the village's [wellbeing](village-needs.md); Medicine keeps [sickness](sickness.md) down.
- **Defense.** Drill, Fortification and Warding help against [raids](raids.md); Commerce makes mercenaries cheaper and
  brings more traders on [market days](market-days.md).
