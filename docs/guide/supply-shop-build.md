The hoist house: a chandler's shop that hauls its goods up from the street. A tall timber gable faces the street, jettied out over two glass shop bays, with a copper sign band across it and a hoist beam at the top with the shop's lantern hanging from it. Inside, the **Shop Counter** stands across the room between two counters, with the stock wall of chests and barrels behind and display tables by the door. Rebuilt from scratch in 2026; shops already standing keep their old look until you refresh them at the Village Hall. It's the Master builder's own blueprint, and the grandest of the starter builds.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). A Master **builder** sells it for 20 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Supply Shop, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Supply Shop II** adds a lower house range behind the shop: the shopkeeper's back room with his bed, a desk and his own chest, and a back door to the yard.
- **Supply Shop III** adds a seed and hardware wing beside the shop under its own little street gable: sacks of seed, a side counter, more stock and a shop window.
- **Supply Shop IV** digs a cool **cellar** under the wing, down a ladder: chests, a rack of shelves, a packing table and casks of logs. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was.

## What it does for the village

A villager without a job who comes by takes the Shop Counter and becomes its **Shopkeeper**: open the counter to set your prices and keep the goods in the chests beside it, and other players buy by trading with the shopkeeper. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md). (Before the rebuild, tier III had a Mailbox for a Postman; a [Post Office](post-office-build.md) gives you one now.)

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a yellow banner (stores, shops and markets).
