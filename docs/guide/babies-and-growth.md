A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) grows by itself, as long as you give it room. At most once a day, a
couple has a **baby**.

## What a village needs to grow

All of these at once:

- a **free bed** that nobody has claimed, inside the village (the settlers' wagon bedrolls don't count). The baby
  takes that bed at once, so a village never has more newcomers than free beds; with every bed taken the hall's Beds
  button says **the village is full**;
- at least **16 meals** in the store (the family eats 8 of them for the baby);
- [wellbeing](village-needs.md) of at least **half**: fed, housed and safe enough;
- at least two grown villagers;
- fewer villagers than the cap: **40** normally, ten more for every [rank](ranks.md) above Hamlet.

The hall's villager count tells you which of these is holding the village back. The two grown villagers nearest the
free bed become the parents; a [married couple](couples-and-weddings.md) close by goes first.

## Growing up

Babies grow up as villagers do. A child who goes to the **Teacher**'s lessons starts their first job as an Apprentice
instead of a Novice, and with the scholars' **Lore** [research](research.md), as a Journeyman. A grown child remembers
their parents and, when there's a free workstation, takes up a parent's trade. See
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** beds (build houses: the Terrace, the Inn and the Stone House are in the Blueprint Table), a full store
  ([Food and diet](food-and-diet.md)) and a well-kept village ([Village needs](village-needs.md)).
- **Affects:** the village's size, and so its [rank](ranks.md), its [raids](raids.md) and its treasury.
- **Limited by:** the **Population Cap** setting ([Village life switches](settings-village-life-switches.md)); at 0,
  villages don't grow at all.
