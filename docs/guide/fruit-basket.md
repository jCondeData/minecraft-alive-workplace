The Fruit Basket is the Orchard Keeper's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Orchard Keeper works at a **composter**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding sweet berries, glow berries or an apple.
The Fruit Basket is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:fruit_basket`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Fruit Basket takes it by themselves and becomes a **Orchard Keeper**: no item needed.
They pick everything ripe within 16 blocks (berries, cocoa, and with Cobblemon apricorns and berry plants) without breaking the plants, and store it in the chests nearby.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Fruit Basket to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **An orchard.** Hand them a [Field Marker](field-marker.md) and they plant a whole orchard.
- **Feeds** a [Ball Smith](ball-workbench.md) with the apricorns they pick, with Cobblemon.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Fruit Basket or a composter); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
