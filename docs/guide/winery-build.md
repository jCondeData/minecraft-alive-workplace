A press house where the fruit goes up and the wine comes down. A double stone stair climbs the front to the white,
timber-framed press floor, where the cauldron vat stands on the door's axis; under it is the stone Cask Hall, its door
under the stair's landing, with racks of casks and two big chests. A small stone Tasting Room leans on its east side.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**; a [Vintner](vintner.md) who reaches Journeyman also sells it, for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Place the blueprint, stock the chests by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists (hold Shift over it), and
hand it to the builder. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

**Winery II** plants a vineyard behind it, through the back door: rows of sweet berry bushes in a raised bed beside an arbor
hung with glow berries. The builder only builds what's new.

**Winery III** digs a **vintage vault** under the Cask Hall, down a ladder under a hatch in its floor: racks of old
casks and a tasting table. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it
was; the workers' chests stay where they were.

## What it does for the village

Its cauldron is a **Vintner's** workstation: sneak-right-click a villager by it with sweet berries, glow berries or an
apple. The chests in the Cask Hall and by the vat are in reach for their fruit and bottles. Villages sometimes grow a small Winery of
their own, with a Vintner already living in it.

## How it fits with the rest

The Vintner presses the [Cider](cider.md), [Berry Wine](berry-wine.md) and [Vintage Wine](vintage-wine.md) the classes
need. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
