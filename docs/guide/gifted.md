About one villager in thirty is **Gifted**: as well as their [traits](traits.md) they have a rare gift, shown in gold
on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s list.

## The gifts

| Gift | What it does |
|---|---|
| Prodigy | learns three times as fast |
| Iron Will | never panics, and keeps working through raids and the bell |
| Silver Tongue | every trade with them is 20% cheaper (5 emeralds become 4, 20 become 16) |
| Night Owl | works from dusk to dawn, sleeps from mid-morning to mid-afternoon (builders and miners too; a child or a villager without a trade keeps everyone's hours) |
| Lucky | luck +3 on every find their work makes |
| Hardy | never falls ill, and has twice a villager's health |
| Beloved | everyone whose bed is within 16 blocks of theirs is happier |
| Born Leader | workers of their own trade within 16 blocks work 10% faster |

## Who is Gifted

A villager's gift comes with who they are, so it never changes. When the child of two schooled Masters grows up, one
time in 20 they are a [Legend](legends.md), and otherwise one time in 4 they are Gifted, and the chronicle says so. The
Old Sage's Old Tongues research makes the inn's travelers Gifted twice as often.

![Wren grows up Gifted, with her parents](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/gifted_born/still-1.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: [schooled](teacher.md) Master parents.
- **What it affects**: work speed, trading, [sickness](sickness.md), [moods](moods.md) and [raids](raids.md), one gift
  at a time. A Gifted villager who becomes a Legend keeps their gift.
- **Setting**: **Gifted Villagers, 1 in** sets how rare gifts are; 0 means nobody is Gifted (nothing is erased).
