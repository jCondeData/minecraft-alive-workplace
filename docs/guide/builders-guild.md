The guild of everyone who puts a village up and makes it pretty.

## Perks

- Builders, Carpenters, Masons and Dyers work **15% faster**.
- Up to **5 idle builders** help at one build, instead of 3: a big blueprint goes up with a crowd round it.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Builder, Carpenter, Mason or Dyer with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a
builder finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md). Its founding shows on the [Book of Edicts](edicts.md)' last row.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master builders and crafters, and a Village rank or better.
- **What it affects**: every [build](builder.md) in the village, and the blocks the Carpenter and Mason make for it.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
