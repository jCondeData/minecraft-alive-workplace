A Scouting Report is what a [Scout](scout.md) brings home: a map drawn from the village to the raiders' camp, with a
red X right on the lair. It's your way there.

## Getting one

Make a Scout, then click **Scout the lair** on the Village Hall's [Defense page](defense-page.md). The Scout walks out by
day, looks the camp over through their spyglass from about 24 blocks away and comes home. The report is waiting in the
chest by their grindstone (or at their feet, if the chest is full). Its name says whose camp it is, such as "Scouting
Report: Bandit camp".

## Reading it

The map is drawn at the smallest scale that shows both the village and the lair, with a dark red border. Hover over it
and the tooltip lists the raiders, their captain by name, the camp's strength, and who scouted it on which day. The
Defense page now shows the lair's exact place too.

## How it fits in

- **What it's for:** finding the lair to break it up. Take the map, raise a [war party](war-party.md) and kill the
  captain: the camp scatters and its chest is yours. See [Lairs and sieges](lairs-and-sieges.md).
- **What stops it:** a Scout not home within a day is lost, and brings no report. If the camp was broken up while they
  were out, they come home empty-handed.
- **No report needed:** when raiders flee at dawn, a Scout trails them home and the lair's place is known at once,
  though no map is drawn.
