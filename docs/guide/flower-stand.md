The Flower Stand is the Florist's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Florist works at a **composter**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a small flower.
The Flower Stand is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:flower_stand`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Flower Stand takes it by themselves and becomes a **Florist**: no item needed.
With bone meal in the chests they grow the biome's flowers on the grass round the stand, pick them, and fill any empty flower pots nearby.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Flower Stand to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** bone meal, which a [Composter](compost-bin.md) makes.
- **Feeds** the dyers and builders with flowers.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Flower Stand or a composter); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
