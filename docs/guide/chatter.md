# Chatter

```infobox
type: System
Needs: a Village Hall within reach
Who talks: villagers off work, near a player
How often: at most one line every 20 seconds near each player
Shown: over the villager's head for a few seconds
Setting: Villager Chatter (on)
categories: Village life
related: village-life.md, moods.md, names.md, festivals-and-markets.md
```

Walk through a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and now and then a villager who isn't busy turns to you
and says something, in a line over their head. It's the easiest way to read a village.

## What they talk about

Village news comes first, and it's twice as likely as anything else:

- a festival tonight ("Festival tonight! Don't be late."), on, or just past;
- bandits camped nearby ("Somebody ought to deal with those bandits to the north.");
- a raid ("Get inside, quick!").

Then their own [mood](moods.md) and what it's made of: hungry or fed, a bed of their own or none, a job or none, a
varied diet or the same food every day, or being ill ("Is there a nurse about?"). Children want to play tag. Or just
hello, by your name.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** [moods](moods.md), [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md), [bandit camps](bandit-camps.md),
  [raids](raids.md) and [sickness](sickness.md). If several villagers grumble about beds, build houses.
- **Workers** at work don't chatter: the line over a worker shows what they're doing instead.
- **Turned off:** the [Villager Chatter](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch.
