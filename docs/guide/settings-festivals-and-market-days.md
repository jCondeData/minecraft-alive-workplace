Two switches for the village's good days. See [Settings](settings.md) for where to change them.

## Festivals

Normally **on**: villages with a Village Hall and at least six villagers hold a [festival](festivals-and-markets.md)
every eight days, with a feast, music, dancing and fireworks, and everyone who came is happier for two days.

**Off:** the regular festivals stop. Players can still call one at the hall with a **cake**, at most every three days.

## Market Days

Normally **on**: a village with a Village Hall and a finished [Market Square](market-square-build.md) holds a
[market](market-days.md) once a week, when traveling traders come to the square.

**Off:** no traders come on market day; the Market Square is still a decoration.

## How they interact

- Festivals lift [moods](moods.md), and happy villagers work faster ([work pace](work-pace.md)).
- The festival feast is eaten from the village's store, so keep it stocked ([Food and diet](food-and-diet.md)).
- The number of market traders grows with the village's [rank](ranks.md) and its Commerce [research](research.md).
- The calendar's length is set by **Days per Season** ([Seasons and pace settings](settings-seasons-and-pace.md)).
