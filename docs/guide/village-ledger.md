The Village Ledger keeps a village's accounts in your pocket: the hall's screen, wherever you are nearby.

## Getting one

Craft a **book** and an **emerald** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

Right-click your [Village Hall](village-hall.md) with it to bind it to that hall; the tooltip then names the village.
From then on, right-click with it to open the hall's screen without walking back: the village's numbers, its people,
the treasury and the hall's pages.
