The Drying Loft: from the street it reads like a Poké Ball, red over a black band over white. The white plaster
workshop below holds the **smithing table**, an anvil, a workbench and the forge, whose brick stack climbs the east side
past the ridge. Over it a red loft is jettied out on brackets, its front slatted with louvers where the apricorns dry
on trays; up there, by the stairs, are a sorting table, the Ball Smith's order desk at the window under the hoist, and
his bed. The black beam band between the two halves carries a white boss with a button over the door, and a low store
lean-to behind keeps the chests of apricorns and finished balls.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). It's listed only when Cobblemon is installed. Newly explored villages often have one of these houses already, with its worker inside.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the smithing table with an **apricorn** and they become the **Ball Smith** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a smithing table as a toolsmith). Put apricorns and metal in the chest and they make Poké Balls. See [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

**A home.** Its one bed is the worker's own. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a purple banner (other buildings).
