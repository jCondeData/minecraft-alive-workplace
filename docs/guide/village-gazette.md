The village's own newspaper, printed fresh from what the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) knows that day. A Burgher
household reads one a week.

## What's in it

Right-click to read it. "The Thornholm Gazette", printed on the day it was made:

- the **front page**: the chronicle's newest news;
- **Wanted at the hall**: the open [quests](quests.md) and what they pay;
- **The calendar**: the next festival and the next market day;
- **This week**: the week's births, weddings and households on the rise.

A copy you buy, or one from the creative tab, is printed where you stand the first time you read it: today's news of
the village you're in. With no Village Hall near, it says there's no news to bring.

## What it's for

- **A need of the Burghers.** Every [Burgher](burgher.md) household takes a Gazette from the village store every 7
  days. "This week's" means printed in the last 7 days.
- **Spread the news.** In a village with a Printer and a caravan route, the hall may post a quest to carry this week's
  Gazette to the hall at the other end of the route, for 6 emeralds. An old copy is turned down ("That Gazette is old
  news").

## How it fits with the rest

It needs a [Printer](printer.md) and a hall to print from; paper comes from sugar cane, ink from squid. See
[Luxuries](luxuries.md).
