The village's craftsfolk: settled in a proper home, well fed, and dressed for work.

## What an Artisan needs

A household stays Artisan as long as it has, every dawn:

- **a home** a builder put up, with their bed in it;
- **fed from the store 3 days running**, on **a varied diet**;
- **a chapel, a school or a clinic** within 48 blocks of home (a finished Chapel, School, Schoolhouse, Clinic or
  Healing Center, or a Teacher or Nurse at work);
- **Work Clothes**, a set every 8 days, from the store.

Three dawns running without one of them and the household comes down to [Peasant](peasant.md).

**Wants**: **Cider** every 4 days, and a **tavern** near home (an Inn, or an Innkeeper). Each want had makes the
household pay 10% more tax.

## What Artisans give

- **One and a half times the tax** into the [treasury](village-hall.md).
- **Crafters work 10% faster**: carpenters, masons, dyers, tinkerers, chefs, toolsmiths and the luxury workers.
- **The Artisan jobs**: only an Artisan (or better) can take up the Tinkerer, Chef, Netherworker, Nurse, Teacher,
  Shopkeeper, Innkeeper and Printer, and with Cobblemon the Ball Smith, Move Tutor, Pokémon Trader and Fossil Scientist.

## Rising to Burgher

Two dawns running with everything a [Burgher](burgher.md) needs: a tier II home, a school and one more service, a
Market Square, Fine Clothes, Berry Wine and the Gazette.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your [builder](builder.md) (homes and a Chapel or School), your farmers and
  [chefs](jobs-crafts.md) (a varied diet), a Tailor (Work Clothes) and a Vintner (Cider).
- **What it affects**: the treasury, the speed of every crafter, and which [jobs](jobs.md) the village can fill.
- **What breaks it**: an empty store or running out of Work Clothes.
