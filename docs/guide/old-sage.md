"They remember the old tongues, the star charts and the herb lore, and they will teach a village that earns it, once it
has answered their riddles."

## How they come

When your village finishes its fifth [research](research.md) level, a hermit's hut appears 150 to 250 blocks away, and
you hear a rumor with its distance and direction. Right-click the Sage inside for **three riddles**, one at a time. You
answer by handing over the right item: a wrong one gets a shake of the head, and after two misses a hint. Three right
answers and the Sage comes to your hall the next morning as a guest. A child of two schooled Master Scholars may also
grow up as the Old Sage.

![A riddle, a shake of the head, a hint](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_sage/still-2.jpg)

## Ancient Lore

While the Sage lives in your village, the research screen gets an **Ancient Lore** tab. The Sage works it at a lectern
near their home, paid in paper, books, emeralds and amethyst shards; your [Scholars](scholar.md) with nothing of their own
to research help at half speed.

| Topic | What it does |
|---|---|
| Old Tongues | inn travelers are Journeymen at least, and Gifted twice as often |
| Star Charts | explorers range 25% farther a level, and at II draw a map every second expedition |
| Herb Lore | illness lasts a day less a level, and nurses also cure with any small flower |
| Deep Memory | every villager learns 15% faster a level |
| Runes of Warding | night raids are 20% less likely a level |
| Old Harvests | farmers, orchard keepers and beekeepers work 10% faster a level |
| The Undying Flame | undertakers bring the dead back with nothing from the chest |
| The Golden Age | wellbeing never below 60%, and festivals lift moods for twice as long |
| The Iron Pact | an iron golem joins the village every 5 days (one per 8 villagers), and golems and guards take 25% less damage |

The last three are one last pick each: you choose one of them, for good.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your Scholar's research, and paper, books, emeralds and amethyst in the chests.
- **What they affect**: [sickness](sickness.md), [raids](raids.md), learning, harvests, the undertaker and the
  festivals, through the tree.
- **What stops them**: a strike stops the tree's work; levels already done stay.
