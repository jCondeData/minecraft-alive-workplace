Losing a Master builder to a creeper hurts. That's what **graves** are for. A grown villager with a job or a
[name](names.md) who dies leaves a **grave** where they fell: a mound of earth with a headstone. Right-click it to read
who lies there.

## Bringing them back

The grave holds everything the villager was: job, level, trades and name. An **Undertaker** (a brewing stand and a
golden apple) brings them back as they were, with a **golden apple**, a **healing potion** or a **totem of undying**
from the chest. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

A villager a zombie turns into a zombie villager leaves no grave: cure them the vanilla way instead.

## Laid to rest

Graves don't stay in the street. The Undertaker moves each grave lying about the village to the village's
[Graveyard](graveyard-build.md), in rows, keeping everything in it; a village with no Graveyard gets a small plot near
its [Village Hall](village-hall.md). They can still be brought back from there.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** deaths in [raids](raids.md) and accidents. Guards and walls mean fewer graves.
- **Names.** Named villagers are remembered by name on their headstone ([Names](names.md)).
- **Couples.** When one of a couple dies, the other mourns for five days
  ([Couples and weddings](couples-and-weddings.md)).
