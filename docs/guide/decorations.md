A pretty village is a happier one. Every **decoration** a builder finishes within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s
reach adds to the village's **beauty**.

## The small ones

Besides the big pieces there are fourteen small decorations, quick to build and easy to stock, each a different kind
of thing:

- **Market Cross**: a stepped stone plinth with a tall shaft, lanterns hanging from its arms.
- **Stag Statue**: a stone stag with spreading antlers on a plinth.
- **Flower Planters**: a two-tier brick planter full of flowers, a lantern at each end. Every village has this one
  from the start.
- **Signpost**: a post with four fingerboards and a lantern on top. When it's finished, each board names what lies
  that way: the nearest other village and how far, or the nearest building, or your village's name and "Welcome". The
  boards keep up by themselves when a village is renamed or a new one appears.
- **Stone Archway**: a mossy stone arch over a path, ivy up its sides and a lantern in the opening.
- **Lily Pond**: a little pond with lily pads in a grassy bank of ferns, with a stone seat.
- **Hay Cart**: a cart loaded with hay bales, left by the road.
- **Notice Board**: a roofed board with notices pinned to it.
- **Lantern Pergola**: a walk under slats grown over with azalea, lanterns hanging over the path, benches either side.
- **Fire Pit**: a campfire in a ring of stones with log seats round it.
- **Sundial**: a round stone plaza with hour stones and a sundial in the middle.
- **Topiary Garden**: clipped hedges round four topiary shapes and an urn of azalea.
- **Market Corner**: crates, sacks, hay and produce under a striped awning.
- **Footbridge**: a little plank bridge over a channel of water with lily pads.

The rest start locked at the Blueprint Table: a traveling trader on [market day](market-days.md) may sell any of them,
and each time one of your builders levels up, the village unlocks a few more builds.

## What beauty does

- **Wellbeing.** Each point of beauty adds 1% to the village's [wellbeing](village-needs.md), up to 10%. Wellbeing sets
  the pace of all the work there.
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of decorations are happier: +5 a point of beauty nearby, up to
  +15 ([Moods](moods.md)). So spread them among the houses rather than piling them in one square.
- **What next?** The hall's advice page suggests decorations when the village lacks them.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** builders and blueprints. A decoration you build by hand doesn't count; only a builder's finished build
  does. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).
- **Bought on market day:** traveling traders sometimes sell decoration blueprints ([Market days](market-days.md)).
- **Special ones:** the [Market Square](market-square-build.md) brings market days, the Chapel hosts
  [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md), and the [Bandstand](bandstand-build.md) is the Bard's stage.
