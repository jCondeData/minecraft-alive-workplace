A little wooden cradle on rockers. Put one by a bed and your village becomes a **nursery village**.

## What it does

With a Cradle within **4 blocks of a bed** in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md):

- every child in the village **grows up twice as fast**;
- **one more baby a day** may be born;
- at night a child of the house (the nearest within 8 blocks) **sleeps in the cradle**.

More cradles add nothing to the speed: one by a bed is enough, though each cradle by a bed gets its own sleeper. The
hall's beds icon says whether the village has one.

![Grown up in half the time](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/cradle/still-4.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: beds, homes and food: babies still need room and meals ([babies and growth](babies-and-growth.md)).
- **What it affects**: how fast the village grows. It goes well with [Large Families](large-families.md); the hall's
  "What next?" suggests a Cradle when that edict is in force without one.
- **What stops it**: a cradle more than 4 blocks from a bed, or the **Cradles** setting off (then it's furniture).
