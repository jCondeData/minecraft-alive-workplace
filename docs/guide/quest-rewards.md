Every quest says what it pays before you start: look under it in the [quest journal](quest-journal.md). Stories,
requests and bounties pay in more than emeralds.

## What a quest can pay

- **Emeralds**, to whoever finishes it, more in a bigger village (a quarter more for each rank), or in CobbleDollars
  where that mod keeps the money. A [personal request](personal-requests.md) pays every helper their own.
- **Items** or a roll of loot, to whoever finishes it.
- **A map** to a place worth seeing (see [Map to the place](map-to-the.md)).
- **Friendship** with the villager who asked (see [Friendship](friendship.md)): 150 for a request done.
- **Standing** in the village, and with enough, a title (see [Reputation and titles](reputation.md)): +10 for a daily
  quest, +25 for a request, +40 for a bounty, +50 for each story chapter you helped with, and what a story's ending
  says.
- **Honors**: names a story gives everyone who saw it through, kept for good.
- **The village's mood**: a story can leave the whole village happier, or sadder, for a few days.
- **The treasury**: emeralds into the village's [treasury](treasury.md).
- **A line in the chronicle**, naming who helped.

## How it fits with the rest

Rewards are fixed when a quest goes up, so a village that grows a rank meanwhile doesn't change what an open quest
pays. Items that don't fit in your inventory drop at your feet; with nobody to hand them to, they drop by the hall.
