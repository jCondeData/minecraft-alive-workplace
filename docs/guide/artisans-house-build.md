A narrow timber house on a stone workroom: a crafting table and an anvil downstairs, two beds above. It's a home for the village's craftsfolk, and the one blueprint here you can only buy from a villager.

## Getting the blueprint

Builders sell it: from **Apprentice** level on, a builder offers the Artisan's House for 10 emeralds. It isn't in the Blueprint Table. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Artisan's House, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Artisan's House II** adds a stone workshop wing at the back, with a loom, a third bed and another chest.

## What it does for the village

**A home.** It sleeps two villagers, three at tier II. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md). The crafting table is a **Carpenter**'s workstation (sneak-right-click a villager by it with **planks**), and tier II's loom a **Shepherd**'s.

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a white banner (homes).
