A log watch platform for the corner of a [Palisade](palisade-build.md): four stout spruce posts, a ladder up the inside,
a railed platform near the top and a little dark oak roof over it. It's the same footprint as the stone Wall Tower, so
a village can swap one for the other later.

## Building it

Place it where two palisade lines meet, inside the village, facing out. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Part of the wall.** It closes the corners of a palisade ring, so [raiders](raids.md) can't slip round the ends.
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) with the towers, barracks and gates.
- **Not a lookout.** A day's warning of a raid comes from a guard posted by a Lookout Tower or a Wall Tower; the
  Palisade Tower doesn't give one. See [Raids](raids.md).
