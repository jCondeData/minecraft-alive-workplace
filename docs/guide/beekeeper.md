The Beekeeper looks after every hive round their own: harvesting, planting flowers and keeping the bees breeding.

## Getting a beekeeper

A beehive or bee nest never takes a villager by itself. Stand a villager by one and sneak-right-click them with a **glass
bottle** or **shears**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the hive.

## What they do

- **Harvest.** A full hive within 16 blocks is harvested: a honey bottle with a glass bottle from the chest, or three
  honeycomb with shears. Once the chests hold 16 honey bottles they take honeycomb instead. A **campfire under the hive**
  keeps the bees calm; without one they fly out.
- **Flowers.** A hive with fewer than four flowers near it gets some planted from the chests.
- **Bees.** While a hive has fewer than three bees, they feed two bees flowers so they breed.
- **Candles for builders.** When a builder nearby is waiting for candles, colored candles or honeycomb blocks, the
  Beekeeper fetches the makings from the builder's chests (and her own), makes them at her hive with the game's own
  recipes, and takes them to the builder: a candle from a honeycomb and a string, a red candle from a candle and a red
  dye (the candle first, then the color), a honeycomb block from four honeycomb. She does this in between the hives:
  the two take turns. She fills the Storehouse's stock orders for candles the same way. Short of something, she takes nothing and
  the builder's line names it ("Can't be made yet: short of 1 String to make 1 Candle", or "short of 1 Red Dye to make 1
  Red Candle"); with no Beekeeper nearby it reads "Hire a Beekeeper to make Candles". No other job makes them.

Over their head: how many harvests (or, while making something, "Beekeeper · 3 made for builders"), and whether they're harvesting, planting flowers, feeding bees or putting the honey
away. "No beehives within 16 blocks" or "needs a chest by the beehive" tells you what's missing.

## How the beekeeper fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Florist](florist.md)'s flowers, if you put some in the beekeeper's chest.
- **Feeds:** candles and honeycomb blocks go to the [Builder](builder.md). Honey bottles are what a [Nurse](nurse.md) cures the sick with. The [Porter](porter.md) carries the honey
  and honeycomb to the storehouse.
- **Stops when:** there's no chest, no hive within 16 blocks, or nothing to harvest with.

## Trading

Beekeepers buy dandelions, glass bottles and poppies, and sell honey bottles, honeycomb, candles, a beehive, a honey
block and honeycomb blocks.
