The Colony Charter is the paper that founds a new village: where it goes, what it's called, and the order for the
settlers to go.

## Getting one

Open a City's [Village Hall](village-hall.md), click the minecart and pick the **Colonies** tab. **Buy a Colony
Charter** costs 32 emeralds; the tooltip says who pays what ("The treasury pays 20 emeralds, you pay 12 emeralds"), or
in red why the village can't found a colony now. The charter belongs to that village: its tooltip says "Of Thornholm:
founds its colony".

![A click: the red cross, 612 blocks north-east](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/colony_charter/still-3.jpg)

## Choosing the spot

- **On the map:** right-click the air. The map shows the 2,048 blocks round the hall, north up, with a banner on every
  village (yours red, the others white). Two dashed circles mark the ring a colony may go in, 256 to 1,024 blocks out,
  and a small one round every other hall marks the 128 blocks a colony must keep from it. Point anywhere to see how far
  and which way it is and whether a colony can go there; click to choose it, and a red cross marks it.
- **On the ground:** walk there and right-click the ground with the charter.

The tooltip then says where: "The spot: 612 blocks north-east of Thornholm".

## Naming the colony

Rename the charter in an anvil: "The colony will be called Newbrook". Without a name the settlers make one up.

## Sending the settlers

Right-click the charter's own village hall with it. The charter is used up and the [settlers](colonies.md) get ready.
Call the colony off before they leave and the charter, spot and name and all, comes back to you.

## How it fits in

- **What it costs:** 32 emeralds, from the village's [treasury](treasury.md) first. The realm's **Charters** research
  takes a quarter off a level ([Realm research](realm-research.md)); the [Royal Charter](royal-charter.md) edict adds 32
  emeralds from the realm treasury.
- **What stops it:** a village that isn't a City (or the rank the server sets), one with a colony already on the road,
  still waiting out the days between colonies, or at its limit. **Colonies** off in the settings screen sells none.
