The grandest house in the Blueprint Table: a two-story stone manor built round a hall with a grand stair, under a slate roof with dormers. It's a big job for a builder, over 1,200 blocks at the first tier.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Manor, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Noble's Manor II** adds a walled formal garden behind: clipped hedges, a fountain and gravel walks.
- **Noble's Manor III** adds a gatehouse on the street and a ballroom wing, with a jukebox.

## What it does for the village

The Manor counts as **a grand home** from its very first tier: whoever sleeps in its three beds gets the biggest mood lift a house can give, on top of having a bed of their own. Free beds also let the village grow (see [Village life](village-life.md)). At tier III, sneak-right-click a villager by the jukebox with a **music disc** to make them the village's **Bard**.

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a white banner (homes).
