Some blocks are out of reach: the tip of a tall pillar, a raised arm on a statue, the lantern on top of a dome. A
builder who comes back to one and still finds nowhere to stand puts up **scaffolding**, the vanilla kind, and climbs
it.

## How it goes

The builder walks to a spot beside the block, puts a block of scaffolding under their feet, steps up, and does it
again until the block is in reach. Then they work from the top of the column, at that height. The column is one
unbroken stack from the ground, and it grows as the pillar rises, so the builder never stands on the unfinished work.

![Taking the scaffolding down](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/scaffolding/still-3.jpg)

When that stage of the build is done, the builder climbs back up and takes the scaffolding down from the top, a block
at a time, and it all goes back into the chests. Cancel the build with scaffolding up and it comes down at once, into
the chests too.

## Keeping some in the chests

Put scaffolding in the builder's chests before a tall build. A builder who has enough for the whole column takes it in
one trip and uses it from the start; with too little, they make do as they always did.

With none at all, the builder asks for it on the Storehouse's requests board, where a [Carpenter](carpenter.md) can
make it from bamboo and string. The build never waits for it: that one block is left out, and the rest goes on.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: scaffolding from your chests or from a [Carpenter](carpenter.md).
- **What it affects**: tall builds, and above all the [Wonders](wonders.md), whose domes, towers and statues need it.
- **What stops it**: the **Builder Scaffolding** setting off: blocks with nowhere to stand near them are left
  out. Scaffolding is never used while the builder levels the ground round a finished build, and only the builder in
  charge of a build puts it up (helpers leave those blocks to them).
