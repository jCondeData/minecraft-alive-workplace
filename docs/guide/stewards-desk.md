Once a village has a [Steward](steward.md), the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s **What next?** page becomes his
desk: his name and level, his mode, the builds he has going, his proposals and the hall's usual tips. It works from a
[Village Ledger](village-ledger.md) too, so you can answer him from anywhere.

## The three modes

- **Ask me first**: every build waits for your **Approve**. A new Steward starts here.
- **Run the village**: he starts the builds himself and tells the hall's owner in one chat line each morning ("This
  morning in Thornholm, Mira started: ..."). A server can turn this mode off with the **Stewards Run Villages**
  setting; then every village asks first.
- **Rest**: he plans nothing until you pick another mode.

## Proposals

Each morning, back from his rounds, he puts up to 9 proposals on the desk, and the hall's owner (if online) gets a line
saying new ones are waiting. Each one shows:

- the building, its name and style, and **why** ("3 villagers have no bed");
- **where** ("22 blocks north-east, in Homes 2"), or that it's an upgrade on the building's own spot;
- the five materials it **needs most**, with how many are in store;
- **who builds it**, and after what.

Click one for its page:

- **Approve**: its builder starts it, or takes it next. It's noted in the hall's chronicle.
- **Decline**: not proposed again for 3 days.
- **Show me**: its outline glows in the world for 30 seconds.
- **Another spot**: he looks for the next spot that fits.
- **Another style**: the same building in the next style that suits the spot.

**Approve all** starts every proposal a builder can take. A proposal nobody answers lapses after 3 days.

![A proposal's page](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/steward_desk/still-2.jpg)

## His builds and the shopping list

His open builds are listed with how far along each is and who's on it ("40% built by Tomas"); shift-click one to
**cancel** it (placed blocks stay, the builder's materials go back to the store). When his builds wait for materials,
the desk and the [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s board show **one shopping list** of the 8 things they need most, and the
owner is told once a day. While two of his builds have waited a whole day he proposes nothing new ("No new builds until
his waiting builds are supplied").

![The shopping list](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/steward_safety/still-1.jpg)

The desk also lists the village's [old houses](old-house-renewal.md) and, when a road stops at a gap too wide to
bridge, says so ([Bridges](bridges.md)).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Fed by** [his rules](steward-rules.md), the [zones](plan-zones.md) and the village's builders.
- **Starts** ordinary builds: each is a build site for the nearest [builder](builder.md), owned by the hall's owner,
  exactly as if you had placed the blueprint yourself.
- **Can't start one** when he already has as many builds open as his level allows, no builder can reach the spot,
  another build is in the way, or it would go through a player's build, a Keep Clear zone or another village.
