The smallest build in the Table: a tall dark oak lamp post on a stone brick base, with two lanterns hanging from its
arm. It takes a builder no time at all and lights up a street corner.

## Building it

Grab a Street Lamp blueprint from the Blueprint Table and place it where you want the light. It needs only fourteen
blocks, so line a whole street with them. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Beauty.** Each finished Street Lamp within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach adds 1 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md).
- **Light.** Its lanterns count as light. A villager's bed with a light close by is a lit bed, and lit beds make the
  village safer in its [needs](village-needs.md).
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **The village map.** Lamps are too small to get a mark on the [village map](village-map.md).
