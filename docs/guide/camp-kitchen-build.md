An open timber shelter round a **Campfire Pot**: spruce posts and beams on a cobblestone plinth under a dark oak gable
roof, benches on three sides of the pot, lanterns hanging from the tie beams, and at the back a grain store of Hearty
Grain bales in a log crib, barrels and the cook's chest.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** when Cobblemon is installed: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. A Journeyman [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md) sells it for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. Sneak-right-click the air
first to pick another style. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes it, they sell the upgrade too (the Blueprint Table has it as well):

- **Camp Kitchen II** adds a fenced Hearty Grain plot behind (farmland either side of a water channel) and a cobblestone
  smokehouse on the east with a brick chimney and a smoker by the door.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click the villager by its pot with **Hearty Grains** and she becomes its [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md), cooking
Cobblemon's dishes from the chest. Her meals feed the village. Like every finished building, it counts toward the
village's [rank](ranks.md).
