The Berry Breeder grows the berries nobody finds in the wild. She plants two kinds side by side so they cross, and one
step at a time works her way to the berry you asked for.

## Getting a breeder

Stand a villager by a **composter** and sneak-right-click them with **any Cobblemon berry**. Put a chest within 8 blocks
of the composter and some farmland round it. A [Berry Nursery](berry-nursery-build.md) is laid out for her.

## The berry book

Sneak-right-click her with an empty hand: her **berry book** lists every berry Cobblemon has (a data pack's too). The
ones the village has found are lit; each of the others shows the pair that makes it, grayed while a parent is still
missing, or "Grows wild: no pair makes it". Click one to make it **her goal**. She works out the chain from the berries
the village has (in the chests and the plots): "Mira will breed Sitrus. Steps to go: 2."

## Her work

In her plot (farmland within 8 blocks of the composter, or the plot of a [Field Marker](field-marker.md) you hand her),
she plants the step's two parents in alternating rows, so each plant touches the other kind and Cobblemon can grow the
cross. She puts **Surprise Mulch** and **Growth Mulch** from the chests on them, picks the fruit (the plant stays),
and takes the harvest to the chests, where it's a parent for the next step. A new berry is told to the village ("Mira
bred a berry the village never had: Lum Berry!") and lit in the book. Spare berries go to the chests, 16 of each kept for
the [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md) and the [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md).

The line over her head says what she's at, or what she needs: a goal, a chest, farmland, two beds of farmland side by
side, the parents in her chests.

## Trading

A Novice sells common berries (3 for an emerald); an Apprentice Growth Mulch and Surprise Mulch; a Journeyman the
**Berry Nursery** blueprint for 10 emeralds, and from Journeyman on she sells the berries the village has found.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Feeds her**: berries in the chests, mulch, farmland, and Grass or Bug Pokémon pastured nearby, who make the berries
  grow quicker ([Pokémon partners at work](partners-at-work.md)).
- **She feeds**: the [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md)'s seasonings, the [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md)'s lures, and your
  own berry supply.
- **Stops her**: no chest, no farmland, a goal the village's berries can't reach, or the **Berry Breeders** setting off
  (she stands idle).
