Travel Posts are the stops of the server's ferry network. Find a few, and a ferryman takes you from one to any other
for a fare.

## Getting one

Craft it from **any sign** on top and **planks**, a **boat** and **planks** in the row below. Name it in an anvil
first ("Riverside"): that's the stop's name. Unnamed, it's called after where it stands ("Post at 120, -48"). Many
villages already have a ferry house with one.

## Using it

**Right-click a Travel Post** to add it to the posts you know; you're told how many others you know. A villager
without a job nearby takes the post by themselves and becomes its **Ferryman**. Right-click the ferryman, pick any
other post you know and pay the fare (an emerald for every 256 blocks, 16 at most; 8 to another dimension) for a [Travel Ticket](travel-ticket.md). Use the ticket within
16 blocks of any Travel Post and you arrive at the ticket's post.

## How it fits in

- **Every post you know** is somewhere a ferryman can send you, so right-click the posts you pass.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, and the stop leaves the network.
