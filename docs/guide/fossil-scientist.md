The Fossil Scientist brings fossils back to life as Pokémon, without you building Cobblemon's whole revival machine.
They need **Cobblemon** installed.

## Getting a fossil scientist

A Fossil Analyzer never takes a villager by itself. Place Cobblemon's **Fossil Analyzer**, stand a villager by it and
sneak-right-click them holding a **fossil**.

## Reviving a fossil

Right-click the scientist holding a fossil (for a Galar fossil, one half in each hand) and pay **8 emeralds**. They tell
you how many minutes of their work it will take. They revive it while they work, at most three fossils at a time, and
the Pokémon joins your party (or your PC) when it's done, or the next time you're online. It's Cobblemon's own revival:
the same Pokémon its machine would give.

Over their head: whose fossil they're reviving, or "hand me a fossil to revive".

## How the fossil scientist fits in

- **Fed by:** fossils you dig up, and the odd one a [Cartographer](cartographer.md) brings back from an expedition.
- **Helped by:** Rock and Psychic Pokémon in a Pasture Block nearby (see [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md)).

## Trading

Fossil scientists buy bones and amethyst shards, and sell a brush, suspicious sand and gravel, the **Research Lab**
blueprint and, at Master, a sniffer egg.

See also [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).
