The crown wants more villages.

## What it does

A realm edict (see [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md) for how realm edicts work). While the Royal Charter is in force,
member villages may **found colonies from Town rank**, and **wait half as long** between two colonies. Each colony
costs the realm treasury **32 emeralds more**.

## Reforming it

**Draft the charter**: bring 16 paper and then 4 books to the capital, and see 2 colonies grow to Village rank while it's
in force. After that colonies still come early and quickly, and cost the realm nothing more.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a rich realm treasury and Towns ready to send settlers.
- **What it affects**: how fast your realm spreads, and so how many villages share its edicts.
