The Lumberjack keeps the village in wood. They fell whole trees, leaves and all, plant a sapling where each one stood,
and bring the logs home.

## Getting a lumberjack

Place a **fletching table** near some trees, stand a villager by it and sneak-right-click them with any **axe**. Put spare
axes and a few saplings in a chest within 8 blocks of the table.

## A day in the woods

The lumberjack walks to the nearest grown tree within 16 blocks of their table, chops it down in one go, plants a
sapling where the trunk stood and carries the logs back to the chests. They only cut real trees: a tree has to stand on
dirt with leaves that grew there, so your log cabin, a log wall or a pile of logs is never touched. Huge crimson and
warped fungi count as trees too. They never cut inside a build site or a quarry.

Over their head you see how many trees they've felled and what they're doing: looking for grown trees, chopping,
dropping off logs, planting or giving the saplings bone meal.

## Extras that come with the right chests

- **Bone meal** in the chest: they feed the saplings so they grow faster (they give up on a sapling after 16, so a lone
  dark oak sapling doesn't eat it all).
- **A furnace** near the table: they burn logs into charcoal and keep about 32 in the chests.
- **A builder waiting for stripped logs:** they strip up to 64 at a time.
- **A Field Marker** (a stick, wheat seeds and paper): mark two corners and hand it over, and they keep that ground
  planted with saplings from the chests in a neat grid (three blocks apart, four for dark oak, which they plant in 2 by
  2 squares) and fell what grows there, up to 48 blocks from the table.

## How the lumberjack fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Toolsmith](toolsmith.md) makes a new axe when the chests run out, and a
  [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md) mends worn ones. The [Composter](composter.md) makes the bone meal for the saplings.
- **Feeds:** the [Porter](porter.md) carries logs to the storehouse (but leaves 32 saplings of each kind). A
  [Carpenter](carpenter.md) turns the logs into whatever a [Builder](builder.md) is short of: give a builder only logs
  and the carpenter makes the rest.
- **Stops when:** there's no axe in a chest nearby ("needs an axe in a chest nearby"), or no grown trees within reach.

## Trading

Lumberjacks buy sticks and apples, and sell oak and spruce logs, dark oak saplings, an iron axe and, at Master, cherry
saplings.
