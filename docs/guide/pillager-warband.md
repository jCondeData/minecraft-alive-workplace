The pillager warband is the raider the walls were built for: an army in tents, with rams and ladders, that comes to
take your town.

## The War Camp

By a Town or a City in the plains, meadows, forests, taiga, snowy lands or savanna, a warband may raise its War Camp 80
to 104 blocks out: a ring of sharpened logs with a gate, three canvas tents, the Warlord's dark tent with the loot chest,
a ravager pen, a siege yard of logs and ladders, and a pole flying the ominous banner. Chat says so: "War drums beyond
Thornholm: Warlord Grath Ironmaw and his pillager warband have raised a war camp 92 blocks south".

## The Warlord and his band

The **Warlord** is a vindicator in iron with the ominous banner on his head and 40 more health, his name floating over
him. His band: pillagers with crossbows, who carry the ladders, and vindicators with axes. The camp starts 10 strong,
grows by 2 a day up to 18, and up to 10 of them stand at the camp at once.

## Its raids are sieges

The warband's raids come from the camp by night, and on a village with walls they're full [sieges](sieges.md): ladders
against the walls, rams at the gate. Every raid brings **ravager rams**: one at a Town, two at a City, and an evoker
too at a City.

At a City the Warlord leads the siege himself: "Warlord Grath Ironmaw himself leads 9 of his warband against Thornholm!"
Kill him at the walls and the camp breaks: its band at the camp scatters and its chest is yours, though the raiders
already at your walls fight on. If he lives through the night, he goes home at dawn.

## How it fits in

- **What stops it:** walls and a [Gatehouse](gatehouse-build.md) (better, a Gatehouse II), archers on battle stations,
  and guards on the walkway to throw ladders down. Then take the fight to the camp with a [war party](war-party.md).
- **What it affects:** the village's gates and walls, which the builders mend first for two days after; its
  [Defense page](defense-page.md), which shows the camp and its strength.
- **The chest:** emeralds, iron, a crossbow or two, an ominous bottle, often arrows, an axe or food, and now and then a
  totem of undying.
- **Switched off:** the warband has its own switch under `raiderCultures` in the config file
  (`pillager_warband`). With **Sieges** off its raids are plain raids, with no rams or ladders.
