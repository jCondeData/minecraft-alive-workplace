The guild of the forge.

## Perks

- Armorers, Toolsmiths, Weaponsmiths, Tinkerers and Ball Smiths work **15% faster**.
- Each ingot a [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md) mends with puts back **a third** of the durability instead of a quarter.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** of any of those trades with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: iron and gold from the miners, Master smiths.
- **What it affects**: the village's tools and armor, and how far its iron goes.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
