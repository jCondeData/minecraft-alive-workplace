The Builder is the heart of the mod: a villager who builds whatever blueprint you hand them, block by block, from your
chests.

![A builder at work](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/builders/still-1.jpg)

## Building something

1. **Place the blueprint.** Hold it and right-click the ground where the front should go. You see the building as
   see-through blocks while you hold it; the gold edge is the front. Sneak-right-click the ground to turn it, and
   sneak-right-click the air to pick it back up.
2. **Stock the chests** within 8 blocks of the builder's Blueprint Table. Hold Shift over the blueprint for the full
   list; once placed, its tooltip shows only what's still missing.
3. **Right-click the builder** with the blueprint.

The builder clears the ground first (never your chests), lays a foundation on uneven ground, puts up the walls and roof
from the bottom up, adds the doors, torches, beds and flowers, and finally levels the ground two blocks round the
building. Water and lava are poured from buckets in the chests. When they finish, the blueprint goes back in the chest so
you can build it again.

![Turned and mirrored where it stands](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/placing/still-2.jpg)

**Short of something?** The builder waits and tells you what's missing. Over their head you see the progress, what
they're short of and which chest they're taking from.

![What a build is still missing](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/missing/still-2.jpg)

**Changed your mind?** Place the blueprint over the finished building and **sneak** while you hand it to a builder: they
take it down and put the blocks back in the chests.

**Building in a hillside?** Right-click the air with a placed blueprint to switch off the leveling for that build.

## Builders get better

- **They level up** as they build, like villagers you trade with. A Master builds two and a half times as fast as a
  Novice, and every level unlocks new blueprints to buy.
- **Busy builders queue up** to five more blueprints and build them in order.
- **Idle builders help.** Up to three builders with nothing to do join a build near their table, so a crew of four
  finishes in under a third of the time.
- **They keep up repairs.** Now and then an idle builder walks round what they built and puts back what's missing: a
  creeper's hole, a broken window. Only holes: anything you changed yourself stays.
- **They lay paths.** After a building they lay a dirt path from its door to the village bell or hall.

![A crew of four on one build](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/crew/still-2.jpg)

## Blueprints to build

The **Blueprint Table** lists the server's blueprints with its size and exactly what it needs. **Get Blueprint**
gives you a copy for a Blank Blueprint.

- **Starter builds:** the Starter Cottage, Market Stall, Lookout Tower, Healing Center, Supply Shop, Storehouse, Berry
  Farm and Research Lab. Build a Healing Center or a Supply Shop and a nurse or shopkeeper moves in.
- **Homes:** the Terrace, the Inn and the Stone House, for a village that wants to grow.
- **Sold by builders only:** the Artisan's House and the Burgher's Townhouse aren't in the Table. A builder sells the
  Artisan's House from Apprentice on, and the Townhouse once the village has a household of Artisans or higher; their
  upgrades come from the builder who finishes them.
- **Workplaces:** a Schoolhouse, Library, Ranch, Apiary Garden, Flower Shop, Graveyard, Tinker's Workshop, Nether
  Gate, Barracks and more, each with a spot for its worker.
- **Decorations:** a Well, Street Lamp, Park Bench, Fountain, Gazebo, Market Square and Chapel. Near a Village Hall
  every one makes the village a little happier.
- **Walls and gates:** the Palisade, Palisade Gate, Stone Wall, Wall Tower and Gatehouse (see
  [Threats and defense](defence.md)).

![The Blueprint Table's library](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/table/still-1.jpg)

**Upgrades.** Most buildings have a bigger version: the Starter Cottage II adds an upper story, the III a kitchen wing.
Right-click a finished building with its upgrade and the blueprint lines up exactly over it; the builder only changes
what's new. Once a builder finishes a building that has an upgrade, they sell its blueprint.

## Styles

Sneak-right-click the air with a blueprint (before placing it) to pick a style: **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark
Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. The builder builds it in those blocks and the material list changes
with it. The **Mirror** button flips the build left to right.

![Picking a style](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/style_menu/still-1.jpg)

## Your own builds

- **Scan Tool** (a Blank Blueprint and a spyglass): right-click two opposite corners of something you built, then
  sneak-right-click the air. You get a blueprint of it, and it's in the Blueprint Table for everyone. Rename the tool
  in an anvil first to name the build.
- **Shape Planner** (a Blank Blueprint and a compass): pick a box, cylinder, dome, sphere, cone, pyramid or arch, its
  size, solid or hollow, and a block you carry, then draw it as a blueprint. Great for walls, towers and domes.
- **Builds from the internet:** at the Blueprint Table, **Upload a File** lists the build files in your `blueprints`
  folder. Download a build as a `.litematic` or `.schem` file, drop it in, and upload it for everyone on the server.

![Shape Planner](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/shapes/still-1.jpg)

## Blocks from other mods

Builders understand Chipped, Rechiseled and Supplementaries blocks, and the Cobbleverse pack's building mods. Stock the
plain block (oak planks, stone bricks) and they turn it into the fancy variant the blueprint uses. Storage mod chests
near the table work as supply chests.
