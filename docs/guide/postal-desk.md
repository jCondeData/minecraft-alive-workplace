The Postal Desk is the Postman's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Postman works at a **Mailbox**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding paper.
The Postal Desk is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:postal_desk`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Postal Desk takes it by themselves and becomes a **Postman**: no item needed.
They collect and deliver the mail within 64 blocks; mail going further goes with the night mail and arrives at dawn.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Postal Desk to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## How it fits in

- **The post office's lockers.** Right-click a Postal Desk to collect every parcel waiting for you: the ones on their way with the night mail, and the ones for players who have no [Mailbox](mailbox.md).
- **Courier routes.** With no mail to carry, a postman hauls between your chests with a [Delivery Note](delivery-note.md).
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Postal Desk or a Mailbox); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
