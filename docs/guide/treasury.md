A working village earns its keep. Every morning a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) puts by its
**takings**, and you collect them at the hall.

## How much

- **A fifth of an emerald a worker a day**, at the default [Daily Takings](settings-quests-and-treasury.md).
- **Half again as much** in a well-kept village, **half as much** in a badly kept one ([Village needs](village-needs.md)).
- **A quarter more for each [rank](ranks.md)** above Hamlet.

So twenty busy workers in a happy Town bring in several emeralds a day.

## Collecting it

Open the hall: its name icon (top left) shows what's in the treasury. **Click it** to take the whole emeralds. With
CobbleDollars installed, you're paid in CobbleDollars at the [CobbleDollar Rate](settings-cobbledollar-rate.md).

The treasury holds up to **a stack of emeralds a rank**, so don't leave it too long. Days the hall wasn't loaded still
count, up to three of them.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** workers (give more villagers jobs: [Giving villagers jobs](jobs.md)), the village's upkeep and its rank.
- **Spend it on:** mercenaries at the hall, blueprints on [market day](market-days.md), or the scholars'
  [research](research.md), which costs emeralds.
- **Turned off:** the [Village Treasury](settings-quests-and-treasury.md) switch.
