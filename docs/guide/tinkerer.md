The Tinkerer makes the redstone and iron parts a build needs, and patches up the village's iron golems between jobs.

## Getting a tinkerer

Stand a villager by a **smithing table** and sneak-right-click them with **redstone**. (An iron ingot at a smithing table
picks the [Toolsmith](toolsmith.md).)

## What they do

- **For the builders:** when a builder nearby is waiting for a redstone or iron part (pistons, rails, hoppers, lanterns,
  iron bars and the like), the tinkerer makes it with the crafting table's recipes and carries it over. When the iron
  isn't there yet, they fire raw ore into ingots in their bench's little forge first, a coal or charcoal for every
  eight.
- **For the golems:** an iron golem of the village that's lost a quarter of its health or more gets patched up with iron
  ingots from the village's stores, as you would.

Over their head: how many parts they've made, and when they're off to mend a golem.

## How the tinkerer fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Miner](miner.md)'s ore and an [Armorer](armorer.md)'s ingots, from the storehouse.
- **Feeds:** the [Builder](builder.md), and keeps the village's golems standing, which helps the [Guard](guard.md)s.
- **Faster with:** the **Craftsmanship** research (see [Scholar](scholar.md)).
- **Stock orders:** they keep the storehouse in the parts you ordered on its board (see [Porter](porter.md)).

## Trading

Tinkerers buy raw iron and redstone, and sell rails, lanterns, pistons, powered rails, a hopper and, at Master, a
crafter.

See also [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).
