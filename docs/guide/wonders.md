A **Wonder** is a village's life's work: a huge build raised in four stages by the village's builders, fed by rare
things found all over the world, and changing what the village (or its whole realm) can do while it stands. There are
eight. Expect one to take a group about a month of evenings.

## The Wonders page

Click the Wonders tab on the Village Hall. Each Wonder is a card: its name and what it does, then **Needs**, each line
ticked or crossed with how far along the village is ("Finished Chapel: 2 of 3"), and the cap ("Wonders: 0 of 1 for a
Town"). A Wonder going up shows its stage. "What next?" on the hall names the Wonder closest to ready ("The Great
Cathedral can be begun").

Every Wonder needs a **Town** (the Colossus a **City**), the **Master Architect**, and a second Legend of its own. On a
server without Legends, a villager stands in for each: a Master builder for the Master Architect, and for the others
what the Wonder's page says (a Master Cleric for the Seer, say). Some ask for more: finished Chapels, research, a
Gatehouse, the coast.

**How many**: a Hamlet or Village none, a Town one, a City two. One of each kind per village, and one going up at a
time.

## The Wonder Plan

With every line ticked, click the card to **draw up the plans**: 32 emeralds (from the treasury if it has them, else
from you), and you get a **Wonder Plan**, a rolled blueprint with a gold seal. Only the hall's owner and their friends
may.

![The Wonder Plan in hand: the whole tower see-through](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/wonders_page/still-2.jpg)

Hold it and you see the whole Wonder as see-through blocks. Right-click the ground to place it (its front faces you),
sneak-right-click the ground to turn it, and sneak-right-click the air to pick it up again or, before placing, to
mirror it. It must stand with its middle within 64 blocks of the hall and every corner within 96, and never over a
finished building. A book and quill in your other hand writes down the whole Wonder's material list.

## Laying the Foundation Stone

With the plan placed, right-click one of the village's builders with it. A **Foundation Stone**, a carved stone with
a brass plaque, goes down just outside the Wonder's front corner, everyone on the server hears that you have begun it,
and the village's chronicle notes it. The chests within 8 blocks of the stone are the Wonder's stockpile: stock them
there. If the builder can't take it on (no bench, they work for another village, no room for the stone) you are told
why.

Right-click the stone at any time for the stage it is on, how far along it is and what the chests are still short of.
To abandon the Wonder, sneak and break the stone (you must be the owner or an operator): what's built stays, and the
plan comes back to you.

## The crew

Every builder of the village with no build of their own joins the Wonder's crew, up to twelve (the **Builders per
Wonder** setting). The stone and the Wonder's card on the hall name who leads it (the Master Architect, when he is one of the village's builders) and how many are on it. Click
**[Call every builder]** under the stone's status (owner or operator) to bring in the busy ones too, as they finish what they're on. Each
builder takes a column of the Wonder and works it from the bottom up, so nothing goes up with nothing under it, and
domes, towers and statues are reached by [scaffolding](scaffolding.md).

## Building it

A Wonder goes up in four named stages, one after another. Besides its blocks, each stage
asks for goods: two or three hard things **found by exploring**, one of each (a Heart of the Sea from buried treasure,
an Echo Shard from an ancient city, a Heavy Core from a trial chamber, a Nether Star from the Wither), and one rare good
from an upgraded workshop, like Vintage Wine or Gold Circlets. Each Wonder's page lists them. A Wonder is the one build
that needs the whole village, and a few expeditions.

Each found thing has a **rare worker** who can find it for you: a Legend such as the Pathfinder or the Old Sage, or a
Master Netherworker, Sifter or Fisherman. While they live in the village they search for it whenever someone is
playing, and after their days are up they bring it: "The Pathfinder found what the Great Library needed: Dune Armor
Trim Template". You don't have to wait for them: bring it yourself and the line is in. Every rare worker who settles is
one less expedition.

![The stone: the sherd in, the Pathfinder searching](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/wonder_finds/still-1.jpg)

![The stone's screen: flowers and potions in, the books short](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/wonder_goods/still-1.jpg)

Right-click the Foundation Stone to see each stage's goods: "0 of 1 Dune Armor Trim Template · found in a desert
temple's chests · the Pathfinder is searching: day 4 of 9", "0 of 1 Illuminated Book · Printer", and "no Printer in the
village" when nobody there makes them. A stage starts only when all its goods are in the chests by the stone. The village sees
to it: the goods are on the Storehouse's requests board, porters carry them over first, the workshops make what's short,
and the hall's daily quest asks for the workshop line furthest behind. You can hand goods
in yourself: right-click the stone holding them, or click the line on the board.

Then, by day (at night it waits for the morning), the stage is **dedicated**: the bell rings, the villagers off work
gather at the stone, the Bard plays and the guards stand in line, the goods are taken and the chronicle says so. Abandon
the Wonder before that and the goods you handed in for the stage come back.

The builders then raise the stage, a part at a time. Ask one of them (sneak and right-click him) and he says he is
building, for example, "Stage 1 part of the Great Cathedral".

## When it's finished

![The festival's fireworks over the folly](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/wonder_finished/still-4.jpg)

When the last stage is done, everyone on the server hears of it ("Pinevale has finished the Great Cathedral!"). That
evening the village holds a festival, with a bigger firework show than usual: gold rockets go up round the square and
from the top of the Wonder itself. The village's chronicle records it, and so do the chronicles of the villages in its
realm (or, without a realm, the villages its caravans trade with). On the Village Map it gets a gold banner with its
name. Its powers come on.

## Keeping it up

Once a day a finished Wonder counts how much of it still stands. Copper that has weathered green, or been waxed, still
counts as the copper it was. Right-click its Foundation Stone, or look at its card on the hall's Wonders page: "Standing:
97% of it. Its powers are on."

If less than nine tenths of it stands (a creeper, a raid, blocks someone took), it is **damaged**: its powers stop, and
the stone and the card say "Damaged: 84% of it stands (90% needed)". The village's builders mend it before anything
else, from the chests by the stone, putting back only the missing blocks. Once enough stands again, its powers come
back. A server owner can set how much must stand with the "Wonder Standing" setting.
