Give your village its own **colors**. Design a banner on a loom as you like, then craft it with a gold ingot: you get a
**Village Banner** of the same design.

## Raising the colors

Right-click the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) with it: "These are now the colors of Thornholm". The banner isn't used
up, and the chronicle notes who raised the new colors. Right-click the hall with another Village Banner to change them.
On any other block it places an ordinary banner of its design, as a banner does.

## Where the colors show

- **Over the doors**: a builder hangs a wall banner over a finished building's front door when a banner of the colors'
  base color is in their chests.
- **On the shields**: guards' plain shields come out painted in the colors.
- **On the hall's pages**: the trade routes page shows every village by its banner, and the
  [Book of Edicts](edicts.md) under the village's own.
- **At festivals**: a banner in the colors within 16 blocks of the festival square makes the festival's good mood
  stronger (20 instead of 15) and longer (3 days instead of 2): "enjoyed the festival under the village's colors".

![A guard with his shield painted in the colors](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/village_banner/still-2.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a loom, dyes and a gold ingot; banners of the base color in the builders' chests.
- **What it affects**: your [builds](builder.md), your [guards](guard.md), your
  [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) and [moods](moods.md).
- **What stops it**: with the **Village Banners** setting off none can be crafted or set, and the colors show nowhere
  (they stay saved).
