A round tower under a copper dome on a raised terrace, its great telescope leaning out of a slit of tinted glass. Round
it: a star-chart room, the Seer's lodging, and a courtyard with a sundial and an orrery, a gold sun with five planets
hung round it on chains.

## What it needs

A **Town** and the **Seer** (on a server without Legends, a Master Cleric stands in), and the Master Architect. It
asks for nothing else. See [Wonders](wonders.md) for drawing up the plans.

## While it stands

- **The Sky Chart.** The hall's map button also draws, on a second empty map, a chart of the land about 6,000 blocks
  across round the village, with a marker on every village, outpost, temple, witch hut, igloo, monument, mansion,
  shipwreck, ruined portal, trail ruin, ancient city, trial chamber and stronghold the Observatory has found, and a
  red marker on each bandit camp. Hover the chart to read the nearest ones' names and where they are. The Observatory
  searches one kind of place an in-game hour, and does it all again every week.
- **The Coming days.** The hall's calendar page shows what's ahead and when: the next festival and market day, weddings
  due, caravans on the road and when they arrive, a traveler expected at the inn, the full moon, the Legends the Seer
  foretells, the bandit camps and where, and the night's raid risk.

If the Observatory is damaged both stop until it's repaired; what it found is kept.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a Master [Cleric](jobs-crafts.md), rare things found by exploring (a rare worker in the village
  finds each faster), and the best goods of upgraded workshops.
- **What it affects**: what you know of the land around the village and of the days ahead.
