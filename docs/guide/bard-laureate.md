"They write every village they love a song of its own, and the fields go quicker when they sing."

## How they come

Throw a [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) that **30 villagers** come to, and at the fireworks the **Bard Laureate** may
join the crowd as a guest. A child of two schooled Master Bards may also grow up as the Laureate.

## What they do

- **The anthem.** When they settle they compose the village's anthem from its name: sixteen notes on one instrument.
  It rings out over the hall at every festival, rank-up, wedding and Legend arrival, and from the hall's Anthem page,
  and the hall's owner gets a written copy, "The Anthem of" your village.
- **Work songs.** Twice a day, mid-morning and mid-afternoon, they walk to where work is busiest and sing for two
  minutes with notes rising round them. Workers within 16 blocks work a quarter faster while they sing, and everyone who
  hears is 5 happier for the day.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: festivals with a big crowd, so a big, happy village.
- **What they affect**: festivals, weddings, [moods](moods.md) and work speed (capped by **Fastest Work Pace**).
- **What stops them**: a strike; the anthem they wrote stays.
