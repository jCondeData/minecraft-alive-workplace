The quick way to hand the village a pile of loot: drop it in, and a porter takes it to the storehouse.

## Getting one

Craft it from a **barrel** and a **hopper**, anywhere in the grid. It's in the mod's creative tab too.

## Using it

Right-click it to open it and put anything in: the loot from a trip, a stack of logs, whatever you're carrying. Put it
within 48 blocks of a [Storehouse](storehouse.md), and its porter empties the Drop Box first and carries everything to
the store.

## How it fits in

- **Feeds** the storehouse, and so every builder and crafter who takes from it.
- **Needs a porter**: no storehouse nearby, and things stay in the box.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself and everything inside.

More in [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
