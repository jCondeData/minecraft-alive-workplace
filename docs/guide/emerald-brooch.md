An emerald set in gold, pinned at a Noble's collar. No Noble household goes without one.

## Getting it

An Apprentice [Jeweler](jeweler.md) makes it from an **emerald** and **3 gold nuggets**, and sells it for 9 emeralds.

## What it's for

A need of the [Nobles](noble.md): each Noble household takes one from the village store every 16 days. A Burgher
household can't rise to Noble without it, and a Noble one that goes three dawns without comes back down.

## How it fits with the rest

Each brooch eats an emerald, so a healthy [treasury](treasury.md) or a busy [shop](shopkeeper.md) helps keep the
Jeweler in stones. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
