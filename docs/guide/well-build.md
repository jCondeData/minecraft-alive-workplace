The Well is the classic village centerpiece: a ring of stone bricks and polished andesite round real water, with a
spruce frame over it and a lantern hanging on a chain above the water. Two little flower pots sit on the rim.

## Building it

Take a Well blueprint from the Blueprint Table, place it, and stock the chests by the builder's table with what the
blueprint's tooltip lists. Most of it is plain stone, so it is a cheap first decoration for a young village. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for placing and stocking.

**Well II.** Right-click the finished Well with the Well II blueprint and the builder adds only what's new: a spruce
roof over the well, benches, more lanterns, a barrel and a hay bale. It stands 7 tall instead of 5.

## How it fits in

- **Beauty.** Finished within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach, the Well adds 2 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md), and Well II adds 3 instead. Every point of beauty makes the village's
  [wellbeing](village-needs.md) a little higher.
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md) ("decorations near home").
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) as a decoration.
- **Market day.** Traveling traders on [market day](market-days.md) sometimes sell decoration blueprints like this one.
- **What breaks it.** If blocks go missing, an idle builder puts them back when [Builder Repairs](settings-village-life-switches.md)
  is on. A Well a player built by hand counts for nothing: only a builder's finished build adds beauty.
