The Nether Brazier is the Netherworker's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Netherworker works at a **cartography table**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding netherrack.
The Nether Brazier is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:nether_brazier`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Nether Brazier takes it by themselves and becomes a **Netherworker**: no item needed.
Within 32 blocks of a Nether portal, with three rations (bread, cooked meat) in the chests, they step through the portal and come back a few minutes later with the Nether's goods.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Nether Brazier to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** rations; a pickaxe, an axe, a sword or a fire resistance potion in the chests brings back more.
- **A bright fire.** The brazier gives off light.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with a pickaxe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Nether Brazier or a cartography table); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
