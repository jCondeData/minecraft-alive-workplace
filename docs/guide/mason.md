The Mason is vanilla's mason, now cutting stone for your builders.

## Getting a mason

A villager without a job takes a free **stonecutter** as a Mason, as in vanilla. If a villager at a stonecutter has
another job, sneak-right-click them with a **clay ball** to make them a Mason again.

## What they do

When a builder nearby is waiting for something a stonecutter makes (stone bricks, stairs, slabs, walls), the mason cuts
it from the village's stone and carries it to the builder's chests. They're the village's crusher, glassblower and kiln
too:

- **Crushing:** cobblestone into gravel, and gravel into sand, at the stonecutter.
- **Firing**, with a **furnace by the stonecutter**: sand into glass, and stone, smooth stone, terracotta and bricks
  from clay. Each coal or charcoal fires eight.

Over their head: how many blocks they've cut, and what they're working on.

## How the mason fits in

- **Fed by:** the [Miner](miner.md)'s stone, from their chests or the storehouse.
- **Feeds:** the [Builder](builder.md). Their gravel is good for a [Sifter](sifter.md), if you move some to the sifter's
  chest.
- **Works beside:** the [Carpenter](carpenter.md), who makes the wooden things.
- **Faster with:** the **Craftsmanship** research (see [Scholar](scholar.md)).
- **Stock orders:** they also keep the storehouse in the stone you ordered on its board (see [Porter](porter.md)).
