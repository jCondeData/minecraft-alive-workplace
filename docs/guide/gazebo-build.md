An open timber gazebo on a cobblestone floor: dark oak posts holding up a stepped dark oak roof, benches inside, a
lantern in the middle and a couple of flower pots. A lovely spot in a park or by the water.

## Building it

Take a Gazebo blueprint from the Blueprint Table and place it on flat ground; the builder levels round it when it's
done. Stock mostly dark oak. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Beauty.** A finished Gazebo within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach adds 3 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md).
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) as a decoration.
- **Market day.** Traveling traders on [market day](market-days.md) sometimes sell decoration blueprints like this one.
