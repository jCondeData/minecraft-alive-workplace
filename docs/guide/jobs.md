# Giving villagers jobs

Every worker in Alive Workplace is an ordinary villager with a job block. Here's how they get one, where they keep their
things, and every job there is.

![One composter, four jobs: each item picks a different one](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/stations/still-1.jpg)

## How a villager gets a job

**Vanilla jobs work as in vanilla.** Put the block near a villager without a job and they take it.

**Most of our jobs share a vanilla block.** To pick one of ours, stand the villager within about 4 blocks of the block
and **sneak-right-click them holding the job's item**. A villager who already works there switches the same way, and the
block's own job comes back with its own item (wheat for a Farmer, coal for an Armorer). You're told what happened
("Dara took the Miner job at the Blast Furnace."), or what's missing.

Hold Shift over a workstation in your inventory to see every job it gives and the item for each.

**Hiring.** Picking a job isn't hiring. A vanilla worker whose job we've extended (an Armorer, a Toolsmith...) is hired
with that job's item on a villager who already has the job: hired, they work with your own workers; unhired, they work
for their village.

## Chests

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** of a workstation are that worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come
out of them, and their work goes into them.

**Villages share.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests: a builder short of
stone takes it from the miner's chests. Only workers who answer to the same people share: yours and your friends', or
village workers nobody has hired.

## What are they doing?

A line over a worker's head says what they're doing or waiting for. Sneak-right-click a worker with an empty hand for
their full status and how to stop them. Workers level up as they work, like villagers you trade with, and every level
makes them faster. Villages keep working while you're away, as long as someone is online.
