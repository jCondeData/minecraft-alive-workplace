The Librarian is vanilla's librarian, with an enchanting table to look after: they enchant the village's weapons, armor
and tools.

## Getting a librarian

A villager without a job takes a free **lectern** as a Librarian, as in vanilla. A lectern has other jobs too (paper
picks the [Scholar](scholar.md), a book the [Teacher](teacher.md)); **lapis lazuli** brings the Librarian back.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a librarian with lapis to hire them: they then enchant your own workers' gear.

## What they do

- **Enchanting.** With an **Enchanting Table** near their lectern and lapis in their chest (or the storehouse), they walk
  up to workers whose gear isn't enchanted yet (a guard's weapon, bow and armor, a miner's pickaxe, a lumberjack's axe)
  and enchant it. The stronger the librarian, the stronger the enchantment: a Master enchants like a full enchanting
  table. **Bookshelves** round the table add to it, placed the way your own table wants them: a Library III's ring of
  fifteen gives a Novice the power of a Journeyman.
- **For the builders.** When a builder nearby is waiting for books, bookshelves, lecterns or paper, the librarian makes
  them.

Over their head: how much they've enchanted or made, and when they're fetching lapis.

## How the librarian fits in

- **Feeds:** the [Guard](guard.md)s, [Miner](miner.md)s and [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md)s (enchanted gear), and the
  [Builder](builder.md).
- **Works beside:** the [Carpenter](carpenter.md), who leaves the bookish things to them.
