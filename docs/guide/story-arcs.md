Now and then a village lives through a tale of its own. A **story arc** comes in chapters: each one is told in chat
as it begins, written in the hall's chronicle, and brings its own quests, places and people. The villagers talk about
it while it runs, and when it ends the village remembers who helped.

![The chapters told in chat as they begin](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/story_arc/still-1.jpg)

## Following a story

Open the hall's quest journal (the Quests button on the Village Hall's screen) and pick the **Story** tab. It shows
the tale the village is living through, chapter by chapter: the ones done are ticked, the one happening now shows its
quest, and the day of the tale is at the top. A short story running beside the big one is listed under "Also
unfolding". With no story, the tab says so.

A chapter moves on when its quests are done, or when something happens out in the world: a banner taken, a wreck
found, a captive walked home. Some chapters have a time limit, and a story can end badly.

## When stories come

A village has one big story at a time, and at least 8 days pass between two of them (the first one waits 8 days too).
Most big stories start by chance on a quiet day, once the village is big enough; the Lost Caravan starts when a caravan
goes missing. At most three big stories run on the whole server at once.

The eight **reform stories** are short side stories: each one starts when an [edict](edicts.md) is in force, and runs
beside the big story without waiting its turn.

## What helping earns

Everyone who did a story's work (handed in a step, took a banner, walked a captive home) gains **standing** in the
village, and the big stories give an **honor** as well, a title the village knows you by. The chronicle names the
helpers, and the village's [quests](quests.md) and [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) often follow.

## For server owners

Whoever runs the server can switch story arcs off in the [settings](settings.md), and set the days between two stories
and how many run at once.
