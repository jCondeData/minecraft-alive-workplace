The crown keeps the roads between its villages.

## Realm edicts

A village that leads a realm proclaims **realm edicts** in its [Book of Edicts](edicts.md): a third row holds the
realm's slots (one for a Barony or County, two for a Duchy, three for a Kingdom, and one more with a finished Royal
Hall II at the capital). A realm edict holds in **every member village**. Only the capital's owner and their friends may
proclaim or lift one, and like any edict it stands at least 3 days. A realm edict's reform is a quest at the capital
**and** a count across the realm while it's in force; when both are done, it's reformed for good.

## What it does

While the Royal Highway is in force, **caravans between member villages carry 2 more stacks and arrive 25% sooner**.
The realm treasury pays **an emerald a day for each route between members**. If it can't pay, the edict lapses.

## Reforming it

**Pave the highway**: bring 128 stone bricks to the capital, and let 12 caravans run between members while it's in
force. After that the caravans stay quick and the treasury pays no more upkeep.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: [caravan routes](trade-and-caravans.md) between your villages, and the realm treasury.
- **What it affects**: how much and how fast your villages supply each other.
- **Good with**: the [Common Market](common-market.md), for a realm that trades a lot.
