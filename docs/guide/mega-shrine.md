With **Mega Showdown** on the server, the Blueprint Table lists the Mega Shrine with the other decorations. Without it
the shrine isn't listed, because its blocks don't exist.

## The build

Mega Showdown's pedestal stands on a chiseled meteorite plinth, on a two-step dais of Mega meteorite bricks edged with
deepslate brick steps. Four posts of deepslate brick wall carry chiseled meteorite caps crowned with amethyst, soul
lanterns light the top step, and a stair climbs to the pedestal from the front. Like every decoration, it makes the
homes near it a little happier (beauty 2).

## The festival show

On a [festival](festivals-and-markets.md) day, for the festival's first hour and a half, a village with a finished shrine holds a
show there:

- the villagers off work gather in front of the shrine instead of at the square;
- a village [trainer](trainer.md) climbs to the pedestal (one who can Mega Evolve first, else the nearest) and Mega
  Evolves their ace: a flash, a burst of light and a beacon chord, and the players near read it in chat;
- every villager watching within 12 blocks gets **+3 mood** ("watched a Mega Evolution at the shrine") for as long as
  a festival's good mood lasts.

The show is held once a festival. Afterwards the crowd goes back to the square for the dancing and the fireworks.

The pedestal has to be standing: if it is broken, the shrine holds no show until it is put back.

## Switch

`villageMegas` (on). Off: the shrine isn't listed and holds no show; a shrine already built stays as it is.
