A **guild** brings a village's workers of one kind together under a Guild Master, and once it has a hall of its own,
every member works better.

## Starting a guild

Craft a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md) and sneak-right-click a **Master** villager with it. They become the **Guild
Master** of their trade's guild, and everyone is told ("Ada is now the Guild Master of the Builders' Guild in
Thornholm!"). The guild's perks wait for a home: give a [builder](builder.md) the [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md)
blueprint, and when it's finished, "The Builders' Guild is founded in its Guildhall": the perks are in force.

Each guild needs a Guildhall of its own. When a new one is finished, the oldest chartered guild without one claims it.

## How many

Your village needs **Village** rank for its first guild: a Village may have 1, a Town 2, a City 3 (the
**Guilds per Rank** setting can raise that). One guild per trade.

| [Herders' Guild](herders-guild.md) | Shepherds, Butchers, Ranchers | 15% faster; every herd may be 4 bigger |
| [Scholars' Guild](scholars-guild.md) | Scholars, Teachers, Librarians, Cartographers | 15% faster; research costs a quarter less |

## The guild row

The [Book of Edicts](edicts.md) shows your guilds on its last row: each Guild Master, the members, whether it's founded
("its Guildhall stands, its perks are in force") or still waiting for a finished Guildhall. With more than five, a
**More guilds** button pages through them.

## When the Guild Master dies

The most experienced member takes over, and the village is told. A guild with no member left is dissolved.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Masters ([work pace](work-pace.md) and levels), a builder for the Guildhall, and the village's
  rank ([ranks](ranks.md)).
- **What it affects**: every member's job; see each guild's page.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that's no longer finished (a creeper, say) pauses its perks until it's repaired; with
  the **Guilds** setting off, charters are refused and perks stop, but guilds stay and come back when it's on again.
