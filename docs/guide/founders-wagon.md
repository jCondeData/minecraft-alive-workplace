A covered wagon full of settlers, sent by the [Founder](founder.md): "make camp where a new village should stand".

## How you get it

While the Founder lives in your village (and isn't on strike), every 7 days the hall's owner is handed a **Founder's
Wagon**, and the chronicle notes it. Its tooltip says which village it comes from.

## Using it

Find the spot for a new village and right-click open ground. Settlers make camp there with a Blueprint Table and
supplies, and with them a **Village Hall** named after the village they left ("New Thornholm") and blueprints in your
village's own styles. Set the hall down and give the builder a blueprint, and a new village begins.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: a Founder who has a home, their books and a happy village.
- **What it affects**: how many villages you have, each with its own [Village Hall](village-hall.md).
