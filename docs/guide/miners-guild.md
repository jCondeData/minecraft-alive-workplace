The guild of those who dig, sift and brave the Nether.

## Perks

- Miners, Sifters and Netherworkers work **15% faster**.
- Their **pickaxes and nether gear wear half as fast**, so the chests need fewer new tools.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Miner, Sifter or Netherworker with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a
builder finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master diggers, and the Toolsmith who keeps them in pickaxes.
- **What it affects**: stone, ore and nether goods for the builders and smiths.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
