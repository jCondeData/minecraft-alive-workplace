# /workplace cancel

```infobox
type: Command
Syntax: /workplace cancel <build>
Who: the player who started it, their friends, or an operator
Stops: a build, a quarry, or a farmer's, fisherman's, orchard keeper's or lumberjack's work area
Easiest: the cancel button in /workplace sites
categories: Commands, Builder
related: command-sites.md, builder.md, jobs-land.md, command-friend.md
```

Stops a piece of work. You hardly ever type it: click the cancel button next to a build in
[/workplace sites](command-sites.md) (or a worker's status) and the game runs it for you with the right id.

## What it stops

- **A build:** "Canceled the build of Fountain." What's built stays, and the blueprint is still placed there: hand it
  back to the builder to carry on, or click the ground to move it.
- **A take-down:** "Canceled the take-down of Fountain." What's still standing stays, and the blueprint is still placed
  there: hand it back to the builder (no need to sneak) to carry on taking it down, or click the ground to move it,
  which makes it a plain blueprint again.
- **A quarry:** "Stopped the quarry."
- **A field, fishing spot, orchard or tree farm:** the farmer, fisherman, orchard keeper or lumberjack is let go from
  it.

## Who may

Only the player who started the work, their [friends](command-friend.md) and operators. Anyone else is told "Only the
player who started this build (or an operator) can cancel it."

## How it interacts

- See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for moving or restarting a build, and [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md)
  for quarries, fields and tree farms.
