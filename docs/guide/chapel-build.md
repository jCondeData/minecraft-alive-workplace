A stone chapel with a bell tower and a slate spire, tall windows down both sides: the finest of the decorations, and where the village's couples marry.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

**Weddings.** Two villagers who court for two days marry, and with a finished Chapel the wedding is held there (without one, at the village's bell), with fireworks and the whole village in a good mood. See [Village life](village-life.md).

**Beauty.** Near a Village Hall the Chapel adds 4 to the village's beauty, more than a fountain or a gazebo, and each point makes the village a little happier. Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it count it among the decorations near home, which lifts their mood. The hall's **What next?** page asks for decorations while the village's beauty is under 3.

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a light blue banner (wells, fountains and the like).
