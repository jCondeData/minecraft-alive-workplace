Out of the sand comes an old king who won't stay dead, and his raiders who won't burn in the sun.

## The Tomb Camp

By a Town or a City in the desert or the badlands, the desert raiders may open their Tomb Camp: a half-buried
sandstone tomb with a stepped roof and a doorway between chiseled pillars, a gold-capped obelisk, sun-bleached awnings
over a fire pit, and bones half sunk in the sand. Inside, by candlelight, the Pharaoh's sarcophagus holds the loot. Chat
says so: "Sand on the wind beyond Thornholm: Pharaoh Neferkaru has risen from an old tomb..."

## The Pharaoh and his raiders

The **Pharaoh** is a husk in a golden helmet and chestplate with a golden sword and 50 more health. He leads the raids on
a City himself. **While he lives, a husk that falls within 24 blocks of him gets up again** three seconds later, at half
health, in a burst of sand. Each rises only once, and none rises once he's dead.

His raiders, all called "Desert raider": husks, husks in gold armor with iron swords, and skeleton archers in gold
helmets. The camp starts 10 strong and grows by 2 a day up to 16.

## Sand ramps

Desert raiders don't use ladders: the husks pile sand against your wall, a block every half second, step by step, until
the ramp is level with the top. Then they walk up and drop inside. Chat warns you: "Sand is piled against the walls of
Thornholm!" When it's over the whole ramp is gone; nothing else is touched.

Husks don't burn, so a desert siege doesn't end at dawn: they stay until **noon**.

## How it fits in

- **What it brings:** while their lair stands nearby, a [drought](drought.md) is twice as likely.
- **What stops it:** archers on the walls to stop the ramp builders, and killing the Pharaoh first to end the curse.
  A [war party](war-party.md) can break the camp.
- **The sarcophagus:** gold, emeralds, a golden apple, gold nuggets, bones, lapis or an enchanted book, now and then a
  diamond, and one time in five the dune armor trim.
- **Switched off:** `desert_raiders` under `raiderCultures` in the config file. With **Sieges** off, no sand ramps.
