# M31 design note: Quests become stories (1.5)

Written for ROADMAP 31.1 (2026-10-05). The roadmap items 31.2 to 31.22 hold the full specs and their tests; this note
is the overview the owner reviews and the map the lanes build from. Lanes don't wait for the owner's reply; anything
he changes goes back into the items.

**In one line:** the village's people become people you know. Each named villager keeps a friendship with each player
(gifts, favours, and heart events that tell their life story) and asks friends for help with their own goals; each
village gives each player a standing and a title; a bounty board hangs up wanted posters; and four story arcs run in
chapters over days. Everything a player can be asked to do is a data file, and nothing waits forever on a player.

## What you'll see (the short version)

- **Hearts on a villager.** Look at a named villager from up to 6 blocks and the action bar reads
  "Dara ♥♥♥♡♡♡♡♡♡♡": ten hearts of 100 points each, yours alone (another player sees their own). Trading with her,
  finishing a quest she posted, killing a zombie that just hit her, coming to her wedding: each puts points on, and
  when they go up, little hearts puff over her head. Hitting her takes 50 off. Hearts never fade, so nobody has to
  visit to keep them.
- **Wrapping and giving a gift.** Craft paper, string and any dye into 4 **Gift Wrap**; put Gift Wrap and any one item
  in the crafting grid and you get a wrapped **Gift** (its tooltip says "From Jesse", never what's inside).
  Right-click Dara with it: she unwraps it (the item's particles, a paper rustle), says over her head and in your chat
  how she likes it ("A cake! You remembered."), and her hearts change: loved +80, liked +45, neutral +20, disliked
  −20, hated −40. The item goes into her chests. One gift a day per villager and two a week; on her **name day**
  (every 28 days; the hall's tooltip says when) a gift counts three times. A gift is never a job's item and is given
  with a plain right-click, so it can't open her trades or change her job by accident.
- **A heart event.** At 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 hearts she has something to tell you. The next time you're within 8 blocks
  while she's off work she walks up, faces you and tells it in three to five lines over her head, a line every 3
  seconds, each also in your chat in grey. Walk away halfway and she starts again next time. At 2 hearts it's **Where
  I come from** ("My mother Ilse kept the bees, and my father Corwin built the first wall..."); at 10 she gives you a
  keepsake, "Dara's Lucky Pick". Each one goes into the chronicle ("Dara told Jesse how she came to Thornholm") and
  into her **life story**, a page you open by shift-clicking her on the hall's list.
- **A personal request, asked and done.** Once you have 3 hearts, a villager may come up one morning and ask: "I want
  to make Journeyman before the next festival. Would you trade with me now and then?" Your chat shows the request
  with **[I'll help]** and **[Not now]**. Accepted, it sits in the journal's Personal tab, on her life-story page and
  on her hall tooltip. When she reaches Journeyman she thanks you, everyone who helped gets +150 friendship and the
  emeralds go to whoever finished it. Miss the deadline and she's glum for a day and doesn't ask again for 3 days.
- **The journal and its tracker bar.** The hall's Quests page becomes a journal with four tabs: **Village** (today's
  daily quests), **Personal**, **Story** and **Bounties**. Each quest shows who asked, a line per objective with its
  progress, the reward and the days left. Click it to hand in what it asks for; shift-click to **Track** it: a boss
  bar at the top of your screen, for you only, "Follow the tracks (2/5)", filling as you go. `/workplace quests`
  lists your quests in chat with a clickable [Track]. The Village Ledger opens the journal from anywhere.
- **A title in chat.** Deeds earn you a standing in each village: Stranger, **Friend** (50), **Hero** (300), **Lord**
  (1000, in a Town or bigger). A new title is told to the whole server ("Jesse is now a Hero of Thornholm!") with a
  sound, and from then on your chat lines read "[Hero of Thornholm] Jesse: ...". Friends get cheaper trades (up to
  40% off with hearts and title together) and festival gifts.
- **The bounty board.** A notice board on two posts, its face showing 0 to 4 posters. From Village rank the village
  pins up a wanted man: "**Grimwald the Red**, Crossbow Ace, last seen 160 blocks north-east, 15 emeralds". Take it
  and you get a Wanted Poster and a map to his hideout; his band waits there once you come near. Bring back his
  Captain's Insignia for the reward. With Cobblemon, a second kind: a huge, mean **Rogue Ursaring** roaming the woods.
- **One arc, chapter by chapter: The Bandit King** (in a Town that has broken up 2 bandit camps, 8% a day):
  1. *Smoke on three hills.* Chat and the chronicle: "Three columns of smoke rise round Thornholm." Three camps stand
     80 to 104 blocks out, their chiefs now "Captain of the Bandit King", each flying a black banner with a red skull.
     Villagers mutter "All the camps fly the same banner now." Break the three banners (each drops a named banner).
  2. *The King's fort.* The next dawn a palisade goes up 110 to 150 blocks out; the next, watchtowers and a stable; the
     next, the King's keep. Meanwhile, win allies (at least one): a neighbouring village's pledge (3 of its guards),
     24 emeralds for 6 mercenaries, or bread for **Pell**, a deserter by the ashes of the first camp, who tells you of
     a postern gate left open.
  3. *The storm.* Raise a Rally Banner within 48 blocks of the fort; your guards and allies follow. The King, in a
     golden crown, has a boss bar and 150 health; at half health he blows a goat horn and 4 bandits pour out of the
     keep. Until he falls his raiders come every second night.

  Ending: his treasure chest (with the King's Crown), a festival the next evening, +150 standing to everyone who
  fought, the honour **Kingslayer**, and no bandit camps for 10 days. If he still stands after 10 days he takes a third
  of the treasury and rides off, leaving the fort a ruin.
- **The other arcs.** **The Sickness** (a Grey Cough spreads; three far herbs make the cure; nobody dies of it), **The
  Lost Caravan** (a caravan goes missing; follow the trail to a stockade and walk the carter Tobin home) and, with
  Cobblemon, **The Professor's Thesis** (three catches for a field study, then a published book with you as
  co-author). When M30's reforms are on, each reform becomes a short side story of its own (The Shift Bell, ...).

## Technical detail

### Where it lives

A new core package `story/` (add it to `docs/agent/layout.md`): `Stories` (the saved data and the hall's round),
`QuestFiles` (loader of `quests/`), `Quest` (an open quest), `Objectives` and `Rewards` (the toolboxes, one small
class per type with its own reader, like `rules/`), `Arcs` (loader of `arcs/` and the arc round), `ArcEffects`,
`Friendship`, `Gifts` and `Tastes` (loader of `villager_tastes/`), `HeartEvents` (loader of `heart_events/`),
`LifeStory`, `PersonalRequests`, `Reputation`, `Bounties` (loader of `bounties/`), `QuestTracker` (the boss bars),
`QuestJournal` (the hall's page), `JobFamilies`, and `RoguePokemon` (the extension point; `compat/cobblemon/
CobblemonRogues` fills it). Conditions go in the shared `rules/` package (it exists: 29.2 created it). The chat
title goes through a new `Platform.onChatDecorate` (Fabric's message decorator); the discount through a mixin at the
end of `Villager.updateSpecialPrices`. All class names are proposals; the items may rename them.

Nothing scans per tick. Quests move on events (kills, hand-ins, battles, a trade, a gift) or in the hall's round
(`VillageNeeds.CHECK_EVERY`, 600 ticks). The arc engine checks its waiting placements and spawned mobs every 40 ticks
with one distance check per player each. The hearts look-up runs every 10 ticks per player, one ray along their look.

**The expansion gate (B76).** `Expansions` gains `M31 = false`, and `Expansions.milestoneOf` lists every key of the
config table below except `villageQuests`. While the gate is shut, nothing new reaches a player: the quest engine runs
(it carries today's daily quests, which look and behave exactly as now), but the journal shows today's page (no tabs,
no Track), and gifts, hearts, requests, titles, bounties and arcs are off. Items and blocks are registered always
(registry ids never change), as M30's are; while their switch is off they do nothing (a Gift is handed back
unopened, the board shows no posters).

### Data formats

All under `data/<namespace>/`, one entry per file, loaded through `Platform.get().onDataReload` like `edicts/`,
`tonics/` and `ranch/PokemonChores`. As there: a malformed file is skipped with **one** log line naming it and its
field (added to `tools/modtest/allow.txt`); `"enabled": false` at the same path switches one of ours off; texts are
text components (`{"translate": ...}` in ours, a plain string allowed in a pack's); item fields take an id or a
`#tag`; entity fields an id, a `#tag` or `"monster"` (anything vanilla counts as an enemy, as `VillageQuests.onKill`
does today); files may carry `"fabric:load_conditions"` (checked through `Platform`); `/reload` picks up changes.
Conditions are the shared `rules/` conditions; an unknown type is an error, so a typo never makes something free.

**Every open quest saves a snapshot of what it asks and pays** (objectives and rewards resolved when it was posted),
so changing or removing a file never changes a quest already up, and old quests can be moved in exactly (below).

#### `quests/<group>/<id>.json` (31.2)

The file's id is its path: `aliveworkplace:daily/want_iron_ingot`. Fields:

| Field | Meaning | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `giver` | `hall` (the daily board), `villager` (a personal request), `bounty` (the board), `arc` (only started by an arc) | required |
| `name` | the quest's title in the journal and the tracker | the first objective's line |
| `weight` | how likely among the files that may be posted | 1 |
| `priority` | higher is chosen first when its conditions hold (the old order: a worker's request, then food) | 0 |
| `conditions` | `rules/` conditions; villager ones read the giver (personal requests) | none |
| `objectives` | in order; all must be done | required |
| `rewards` | paid when done | none |
| `days` | days it stays up, or `"festival"` (before the next festival at least 2 days away) | 3 |
| `repeatable` | whether a player who finished it may get it again (`false`: once per player per village) | true |
| `asks` | personal requests: the lang key of the ask, with the giver, the goal and the deadline as arguments | |

The daily want, as it ships:

```json
{
  "giver": "hall",
  "weight": 1,
  "objectives": [{"type": "bring", "item": "minecraft:iron_ingot", "count": 8}],
  "rewards": [{"type": "money", "emeralds": 5}],
  "days": 3
}
```

A personal request (31.9, `quests/personal/taste_of_home_desert.json`):

```json
{
  "giver": "villager",
  "name": {"translate": "quest.aliveworkplace.personal.taste_of_home"},
  "asks": "quest.aliveworkplace.personal.taste_of_home.ask",
  "weight": 3,
  "conditions": [
    {"type": "hearts_at_least", "hearts": 3},
    {"type": "villager_type", "type_id": "minecraft:desert"}
  ],
  "objectives": [{"type": "bring", "item": "minecraft:rabbit_stew", "count": 1, "to": "giver"}],
  "rewards": [
    {"type": "money", "emeralds": 4, "rank_factor": false},
    {"type": "friendship", "points": 150, "to": "giver", "who": "helpers"},
    {"type": "chronicle", "kind": "friend", "line": "chronicle.aliveworkplace.personal.taste_of_home"}
  ],
  "days": "festival"
}
```

The daily files, with today's numbers (31.2): `daily/worker_request` (priority 2; `bring_request`; reward
`{"type":"money","emeralds":2,"per_count":8,"max":10}`, today's `2 + count/8`, at most 10), `daily/food` (priority 1;
condition `food_below` 16; bring 16 bread; 4), `daily/slay` (weight 10; `kill` 8 `monster` in the village; 6),
`daily/battle` (weight 10; condition `cobblemon`; `battle` 1; 8) and the ten wants, weight 1 each (`want_white_wool`
16 for 3, `want_iron_ingot` 8 for 5, `want_oak_log` 32 for 3, `want_glass` 16 for 3, `want_torch` 32 for 2,
`want_leather` 8 for 3, `want_coal` 16 for 3, `want_bread` 16 for 3, `want_book` 4 for 4, `want_gold_ingot` 4 for 5).
Slay 10, battle 10 and the wants' 10 give today's one-in-three odds of each kind. The board posts one a morning (before
3000 day ticks), three open at most, money times `VillageRanks.questRewardFactor` (a quarter more a rank) unless a
reward says `"rank_factor": false`.

**Conditions** (in `rules/`; new here unless marked): `rank_at_least`, `villagers`, `job_level`, `finished`,
`research_levels` (all existing); `food_below` (`count`), `cobblemon`, `chance` (`chance`, 0 to 1, rolled with the
round's random), `quest_done` (`quest`, by anyone in this village), `not` (`condition`); for arcs `camps_broken`
(`count`, from `aliveworkplace_stories`) and `config` (`switch`: an existing config switch is on); for personal requests
`hearts_at_least` (`hearts`), `job` (`job`), `job_family` (`family`), `level_below` (`level`), `villager_type`
(`type_id`), `home_tier_below` (`tier`), `no_bed`; and from 31.12 `title_at_least` (`title`). Conditions about a
villager or a player read them from a context the caller passes (`Condition.progress` gains an overload with an optional
villager and player; the village conditions ignore it); with no villager in the context, a villager condition is not
met.

**Objectives** (31.2 unless marked): `bring` (`item`, `count`, `to`: `giver`'s chests, `hall` (the store, as now) or the
worker's station), `bring_request` (resolved at posting to the item, count and station of a worker's plain-item request,
as `VillageQuests.make` does today), `kill` (`entity`, `count`, `where`: `village` or `anywhere`), `battle` (`count`),
`wait` (`days`); 31.3 `reach` (`place`, `radius`); 31.4 `talk` (`villager`: an arc role); 31.9 `level_up`, `home`,
`battle_days` (`days`: wins on that many days), `pokemon_near_station` (by `work/Partners`); 31.10 `deliver`, `revive`,
`pet_near_home`, `schooled`, `build` (`blueprint`), `fetch`; 31.13 `insignia`; 31.14 `rogue`; 31.19 `follow_trail`;
31.21 `catch`, `show`, `battle_leader`; plus `flag` (an arc flag is set) and `break` (`block` at an arc place's key,
`count`: the camps' war banners), used by arc chapters. An objective that can't be resolved when the quest would be
posted (no worker's request, no trainer for `battle`, no place found for `reach`) makes that file unavailable that
morning, so the board picks another.

**Rewards** (31.2 unless marked): `money` (`emeralds`, optional `per_count`/`max`, `rank_factor` default true; paid in
CobbleDollars through `work/Money` when that's the currency), `item`, `loot_table`, `chronicle` (`kind`, `line`),
`village_mood` (`points`, `days`, `reason`), `treasury` (`emeralds`); 31.3 `map`; 31.5 `friendship` (`points`, `to`:
`giver` or named arc roles, `who`: `finisher` or `helpers`); 31.11 `reputation` (`points`, `who`), `honour` (`id`);
31.22 `reform_edict`. Money and items go to the finisher; friendship, reputation and honours to whoever the reward
names (`helpers`: every player credited with progress on any objective).

A chapter: `name`, `intro` (lang keys, told to players in and near the village and written in the chronicle as STORY),
`on_start` effects, `quests` (file ids or quests written inline, which are then `giver: arc`), `complete` (`"all"`,
`"any"`, or a list of the quest ids that must be done; `[]` means none), `needs_flags` (flags that must be set too; a
placed build's last stage sets `<key>_done`), `delay_days` before the next chapter, `time_limit_days` and `on_fail`, and
`chatter` lines (village news in `people/Chatter` while it runs). A trigger is `conditions` and a daily `chance`, or an
`event` (only `caravan_lost` today, rolled by `hall/Caravans` with `lostCaravanChance`). `roles` name the people and
mobs an arc needs (a village's healer, a new villager such as Pell, Doctor Wren, Tobin or Thessaly, or a mob a `spawn`
names, such as `king`) so effects and objectives can point at them (`role:pell`). Effects: `announce`, `chronicle`,
`place` (one template, or `stages` placed one `every` dawn, at a spot found by `rule`: `ring` [min, max] round the hall,
`road` (a fraction along a caravan road), `biome` (a tag, within 3000 blocks), or `after` another key with a distance),
`spawn` (a `group` from the arc's spawn file, or written inline: entity, name, gear, health, boss bar, `restrictTo`
radius), `give_map`, `village_mood`, `festival`, `flag` (optionally for `days`), `start_quest`, `end_arc`, `herb_patch`
(31.17), `ruin`, and every quest reward. Arcs marked `threat` don't start in a village set At peace or at threat level 0
once 32.21 is on `main`.

#### `villager_tastes/<id>.json` (31.6)

```json
{
  "for": {"family": "aliveworkplace:mining"},
  "loved": ["minecraft:amethyst_shard", "minecraft:gold_ingot", "minecraft:netherite_scrap"],
  "liked": ["minecraft:iron_ingot", "minecraft:coal", "minecraft:raw_copper"],
  "disliked": [],
  "hated": []
}
```

`for` is `{}` (everyone), `{"family": ...}`, `{"job": ...}` or `{"trait": "glutton"}`. For an item, the most specific
file that names it wins: job, then family, then trait, then everyone; among files of the same level, a data pack's
wins over ours (the later pack in the list), then the one whose id sorts first. An item nobody names is neutral. Ours:
`everyone`, the twelve families and the eight traits, as 31.6 lists them.

**Job families** are fixed in code (`story/JobFamilies`), since tastes, heart events, keepsakes and festival gifts all
use the same twelve: `building` (Builder, Carpenter, Mason, Tinkerer, Leatherworker), `mining` (Miner, Armorer,
Toolsmith, Weaponsmith, Sifter, Netherworker), `land` (Lumberjack, Orchard Keeper, Farmer, Florist, Beekeeper,
Composter), `animals` (Shepherd, Butcher, Rancher, Fisherman), `kitchen` (Chef), `learning` (Scholar, Teacher,
Librarian, Cartographer), `healing` (Nurse, Cleric, Undertaker), `arms` (Guard, Fletcher), `trade` (Shopkeeper,
Innkeeper, Ferryman, Postman, Porter), `music` (Bard), `pokemon` (Trainer, Trainer Leader, Move Tutor, Ball Smith,
Pokémon Trader, Fossil Scientist) and `none` (jobless and nitwits). Jobs not listed (later ours, or another mod's) have
no family: everyone's and their trait's tastes apply, and their 4-heart event is the `none` one.

```json
{
  "hearts": 2,
  "group": "where_i_come_from",
  "weight": 1,
  "when": [{"type": "born_here"}],
  "lines": [
    "heart.aliveworkplace.come_from.born.1",

```

One heart level holds one event a player hears (`group`); its variants are files whose `when` holds for the villager,
the most specific first (the most `when` entries), then by `weight`. `when` facts: `born_here` (`PARENTS`),
`traveller` (`HEAD_START`), `revived`, `before_hall` (none of these), `job_family`, `married`, `courting`, `widowed`,
`parent`, `trait`, `mood_reason`, `rank_at_least`, `level_below`, `home_tier_below`, `no_bed`, `hungry`, `ill`,
`raid_within_days`, `camp_near`. Lines get these arguments, in order: the villager, the player, the village, the
mother, the father, the partner (or late partner), the first two children, the day hired or wed. `keepsake` is an
item stack with its name and lore keys, given once per player (31.8).

### The hall's old daily quests move into the engine (31.2)

Today the hall block entity saves `quests` (a list of `VillageQuests.Quest`: `id`, `kind` BRING/SLAY/BATTLE, `item`,
`count`, `progress`, `reward` (already times the rank factor), `posted` (game time), `poster`, `deliver_to`, and
`reform` for M30's reform steps), with `lastQuestDay` and `questsDone` beside it. From 31.2:

1. **On the hall's first round after loading** (it has the level then; the block entity's `load` doesn't), every quest
   in the hall's `quests` whose id isn't already in `aliveworkplace_stories` is moved in: BRING with `deliver_to` →
   file `daily/worker_request`, objective `bring` to that station; BRING bread without a station → `daily/food`;
   another BRING → `daily/want_<item path>` (or `daily/legacy` if no such file); SLAY → `daily/slay` (`kill`, 8
   `monster`, in the village); BATTLE → `daily/battle`. Progress, poster and `posted` are kept; the reward becomes
   `money` with `rank_factor: false` (it's already scaled); `due` is `posted + 72000` (today's `LASTS`). A quest with
   `reform` keeps its `reform` field, its place on the page and no `due` (M30's steps never expire; `Reforms.round`
   looks for them in the engine from now on). Then the hall's list is set empty and the chunk marked dirty.
2. Matching by id makes this safe to repeat: if the server stops between the saved data and the chunk being written,
   the next load moves nothing twice, and quests already moved aren't lost.
3. `lastQuestDay` and `questsDone` stay on the hall (the rank and the hall's numbers read them); the engine writes them.
4. The `quests` field stays readable for good (`VillageQuests.Quest.CODEC` doesn't change). `VillageQuests` keeps
   `handIn`, `onKill`, `onTrainerBeaten`, `describe`, `task`, `daily` and `MAX_OPEN`/`LASTS`, passing calls on to the
   engine, so the quest tests in `VillageHallGameTests`, `ReformGameTests` and the edict tests pass unchanged.
5. A downgrade to a jar before 1.5 finds an empty list and posts new quests the next morning; nothing breaks.

A kill or a battle counts, as now, toward the oldest open daily quest of that kind in the nearest village, and toward
every reform step and arc or personal quest whose current objective matches.

### The gift gesture

- **Wrapping.** Gift Wrap: paper, string and any dye (`#c:dyes`), shapeless, makes 4, in the recipe book. The Gift: a
  special recipe (`aliveworkplace:gift_wrapping`, like vanilla's map cloning): exactly one Gift Wrap and one other
  item (not a Gift, not Gift Wrap); the item is kept whole in `aliveworkplace:wrapped`. The "From" line is stamped in
  `Item.onCraftedBy`, since a recipe doesn't know who crafts (a Crafter block's Gift says "From someone"). A Gift stacks
  to 1. Special recipes can't show in the recipe book, so Gift Wrap's tooltip says "Craft with any one item to wrap it".
- **Giving.** A right-click on a villager with a Gift in the main hand, sneaking or not, handled in `story/Gifts`
  through `Platform.onUseEntity`, which runs before vanilla's trading. A Gift is never a job's item
  (`Stations.picksAny` is false for it), so it can't pick a job, and the click returns SUCCESS so trades never open.
  An off-hand Gift does nothing. Legends' guests and captives keep their own click (it's handled first, as now).
- **Who takes it.** A named villager in a hall's village. Unnamed ones shake their head (vanilla's "no" and the
  head-shake) and the Gift stays in your hand. With `friendship` off (or the gate shut), the Gift is handed back
  unopened with a line. Over the limits (one a day, two a week per villager per player; weeks are days 1 to 7, 8 to
  14, ...) it's refused with a line saying when.
- **What happens.** Points by taste (loved +80, liked +45, neutral +20, disliked −20, hated −40), three times on the
  name day (both ways). +2 standing (at most +10 a day per village). A Bottle o' Enchanting is liked and gives a
  worker 15 XP. The item goes into the worker's supply chests (`SupplyContainers`), else the village store
  (`VillageNeeds.store`), else drops at their feet.
- **The name day** is the day where `(Chronicle.day + floorMod(uuid.hashCode(), 28)) % 28 == 0`; the hall's tooltip
  shows "Name day: in 5 days".

### Other decisions taken (not the owner's)

1. **`villageQuests` isn't behind the M31 gate**: the daily quests exist today, so shutting the gate must not switch
   them off. Everything else of the milestone is gated (B76), including the journal's tabs and Track.
2. **Open quests are snapshots** of their objectives and rewards; a changed or removed file never changes a quest
   already up. Old quests move in by id, once, with their progress (above).
3. **Daily odds come from weights and priorities**, not a special case: worker's request first, food second, then
   slay 10, battle 10 and the wants 1 each, which is exactly today's choice.
4. **An objective that can't be resolved hides its file for the morning** (no request, no trainer, no place found),
   rather than a `cobblemon`-style condition per case; `cobblemon` stays as a plain condition for files that want it.
5. **Villager and player conditions** go into `rules/` through a context overload, not a second package.
6. **Job families are code, not data** (twelve fixed groups used by four systems); a pack targets a job directly.
7. **Who counts as "named"**: a villager in a hall's village with a custom name (ours or a Name Tag's).
8. **Helpers** are the players credited with progress on any objective; money and items go to the finisher (as today),
   friendship, standing and honours to the helpers.
9. **Personal request deadlines** "before the next festival" use the next festival at least 2 days away; a declined
   request stops that villager asking that player for 3 days (they may ask someone else); a missed one costs 30 to
   each player who accepted.
10. **A village's first arc** waits `arcCooldownDays` from the day the engine first sees its hall, so a live server
    doesn't get three arcs the morning 1.5 arrives. Event arcs (the Lost Caravan) skip the cooldown but not the
    one-arc-per-village rule or `arcsAtOnce`.
11. **Tracking** is one quest per player, kept in the overworld's `aliveworkplace_stories`, so it survives a restart
    and a change of dimension. In the journal, a click hands in (a `bring`) or tracks (anything else); a shift-click
    always toggles Track.
12. **New saved field `hired_day`** (not in the roadmap): the 2-heart "came as a traveller" variant names the day they
    were hired, which nothing saves today. Older hires get the lines without a day.
13. **The wedding day** comes from `Couples.Partner.since` (it's set to the wedding day when they marry), not from the
    chronicle, which keeps only 100 lines.
14. **Widowed before 1.5**: `WIDOWED_DAY` still counts as widowed for heart events (the lines say "my late partner"
    without a name); "Bring back my love" needs `late_partner`, so it's offered only to those widowed from 1.5 on.
15. **The hearts line is shown at any hearts** (also ♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡♡), so a player learns a villager can be befriended.
16. **Names in `captains.json` aren't translated**, like villager names; the rest of every new text is in `en_us.json`.
17. **Chronicle icons**: STORY a book, FRIEND pink petals, TITLE a golden helmet, BOUNTY a crossbow.
18. **"Nothing waits forever on a player"** holds for every new quest (each has `days` or a time limit); M30's reform
    steps keep their own rule (they wait while the edict stands), as M30 designed them.
