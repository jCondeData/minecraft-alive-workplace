# Old houses renewed

```infobox
type: Mechanic
expansion: M27
image: https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/renewal/still-3.jpg
caption: An old plains house, renewed as a Stone House in Cherry
Where: zones with Renew switched on
Which houses: houses no builder built, made of village blocks, with no chest
How often: one at a time, at most one every 2 days in a village
Homes become: a Starter Cottage, Stone House or Terrace with at least as many beds
Workshops become: their job's building (an armorer's house becomes a Smithy)
Its people: keep their beds and their jobs
categories: Steward, Builder
related: plan-zones.md, steward.md, stewards-desk.md, city-plan.md
```

Turn **Renew** on for a zone of the [City Plan](city-plan.md) and the [Steward](steward.md) slowly rebuilds the old
village houses in it, one by one, in the zone's style. A tumbledown plains village becomes a neat town without anyone
moving out.

## Which houses

He looks for houses no builder built: a bed or a workstation with the house around it. It counts as an old village
house only if it's built of the blocks vanilla villages use (cobwebs, mossy and cracked blocks of abandoned ones
included), holds no chest or anything like it, lies wholly inside the renew zone, and no player has placed or broken a
block in it. The desk lists them ("Old houses: 4, 3 can be renewed") and says why each of the others is kept: it holds
a chest, it isn't built of village blocks, a player built there, it reaches out of the zone, it's too big for a house,
or not all of it is loaded. **Show me** outlines them in the world, orange for those that can be renewed.

![The old houses on the desk](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/old_houses/still-2.jpg)

## How a house is renewed

One proposal, "Renew the old house 14 blocks west as a Stone House (Cherry)", does it all:

1. the builders take the old house down, and its blocks go to the store;
2. then they build the new one on the cleared plot, its front where the old door was.

A home becomes a Starter Cottage, Stone House or Terrace with at least as many beds; a worker's house becomes the
building of that job. The villagers who slept there sleep in the new beds, and the worker keeps the job and takes the
new workstation. The chronicle notes each house renewed.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Ask me first** asks for each house; **Run the village** renews by itself, but only in zones whose Renew switch
  you turned on ([the desk](stewards-desk.md)).
- **Never** touches a house with a chest, or one a player changed.
- **Stopped by** the **Steward Renewals** setting (off: houses are listed, never renewed), or the zone's Renew switch.
- The new building counts toward the village's [rank](ranks.md) like any finished building.
