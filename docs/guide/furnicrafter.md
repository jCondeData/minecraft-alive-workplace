The Furnicrafter makes the village's CobbleFurnies furniture. With the CobbleFurnies mod installed, a villager without
a job standing by a **Furni Crafter** takes it as their workstation: you'll know her by the red bobble beanie, the
walnut leather apron with a Poké Ball stitched on it, the yellow tape measure round her neck and the little hammer at
her belt.

## Getting a Furnicrafter

Craft a Furni Crafter (planks, shears and an iron axe), place it in the village and have a villager without a job
nearby. They walk over and take the job by themselves, the way a farmer takes a composter.

## What she makes

- **For the builders.** When a builder near her is held up because the build wants a CobbleFurnies piece (a furnished
  home's Oak Chair, a lamp, a table), she gathers the materials from that builder's chests and the village's store,
  makes the piece at the Furni Crafter and takes it to the builder's chest. The builder carries on.
- **For the store.** Order a piece at the [Storehouse](storehouse.md) (its stock orders) and she keeps it in stock, as
  long as the store has the materials.

She uses only what can be spared: never what the rest of a build still needs, and never what the Storehouse keeps for
its other orders. If a piece needs sticks and the store only has planks, she cuts the sticks first. With materials for
fewer pieces than were asked for, she makes as many as she can.

## Trading

The Furnicrafter buys oak planks and white wool, and sells CobbleFurnies pieces for emeralds: Oak Chairs as a Novice,
then a Red Lamp, an Oak Table and an Oak Cabinet, a White Sofa, and as a Master the Poké Ball Desk.

## Good to know

- **Without CobbleFurnies** there is no Furnicrafter, and a world that had one loads her as a villager without a job.
- With the **Furnicrafters** setting off, a villager without a job no longer takes a Furni Crafter, and a Furnicrafter
  already hired stands idle.
