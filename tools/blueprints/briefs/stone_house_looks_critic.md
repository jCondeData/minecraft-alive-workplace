## Desert notes
1. L1 back/right: flat sandstone planes: window surrounds with brows or sills one block proud, corner pilasters, the frieze a proud course.
2. L1 front/left/back: the cube plus slotted tower reads as a watchtower: bring in Level I mud brick or an arcade, a low wall-court or an awning by the door.
3. L1 front: the door is a narrow slot under the oriel: a visible chiseled surround and a step or awning.
4. L1 left: one clean flue stack with a proper breast, no stray pier or stair pieces at its foot.
5. Every level: beam ends read as crates (shorter ends); add planting (dead bush, cactus pots, a tree in a planter).

## Snowy notes
1. L1/L3 left and right: the stepped block gables should be a clean rake (stairs/slabs or a verge board), keeping the pitch.
2. L1/L3 front: jagged snow ledges round the dormer: give the dormer its own gabled roof, run the courses evenly past it.
3. L1/L3 back/top: snow on every tread makes stripes: snow on the ridge, the eave courses and a few treads only.
4. L1/L3 side walls: the calcite/diorite checkerboard is noise: one wall stone with dressed quoins.
5. L1 right: shutters, a window box or a wood stack on the side wall.

## Savanna (SHIP), optional polish
- Upper-storey acacia posts one block proud of the mud; a lighter dressing course at the sills on the L1 back.

## Taiga (SHIP, narrow), optional polish
- L2/L3 right: a shuttered window or a firewood stack under the catslide; L3 back: a window box or log-end brackets; L1 left: one tidy firewood stack.

## Desert notes (round 2)
1. L1 back/right/left: the windcatcher stands four blocks over the coping at the back corner (keep-and-turret): cut it to about two, or move it off the corner.
2. L3 right and left: brows and sills that wrap onto the pier between the volumes read as loose steps: stop them at the window width.
3. Every level: one planting bigger than a pot that shows from 20 blocks (a tree in a planter at the arcade, or the terrace azalea up where it shows).

## Snowy notes (round 2)
1. L1/L3 front: the dormer needs a visible roof (snow on its ridge or a contrasting verge) and no one-wide calcite column above its face.
2. L3 front: no lone snow strip mid-slope or broken snow piece over the wing's eave: a snow line runs a whole course or isn't there.
3. L1/L3 every side: the ground drift one continuous skirt like the snowy cottage's, or none (the spaced pads read as stepping stones).
