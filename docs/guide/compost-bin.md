The Compost Bin is the Composter's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Composter works at a **composter**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding bone meal.
The Compost Bin is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:compost_bin`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Compost Bin takes it by themselves and becomes a **Composter**: no item needed.
The village's scraps (seeds, saplings, leaves, rotten flesh) go in, and bone meal comes out: more of it than a vanilla composter gives.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Compost Bin to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

## How it fits in

- **Feeds** the farmers, florists and lumberjacks with bone meal.
- **Research helps.** The village's Green Thumb research makes bone meal faster.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Compost Bin or a composter); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
