Built something you love? The Scan Tool turns it into a blueprint, so the village's builders can build it again.

## Getting one

Craft a [Blank Blueprint](blank-blueprint.md) and a **spyglass** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

1. Right-click a corner block of the build, then the opposite corner. The tooltip shows the size you've marked. A
   chest, furnace, trapdoor or other block you'd normally open counts as a corner too: the click marks it and leaves
   it shut.
2. Sneak-right-click the air to save it: you get a [Blueprint](blueprint.md) of it, and it takes a Blank Blueprint
   from your inventory.
3. Want a name? Rename the Scan Tool in an anvil first, and the build is called that.

Sneak-right-click a block to start over.

## How it fits in

- **Shared.** What you save goes into the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md)'s library for everyone on the server.
- **Builders build it** like any other blueprint, from the chests round their table.
- **Frames, paintings and armor stands come along**, empty: the builder hangs each one up again with an item frame,
  painting or armor stand from the chests, but never copies what a frame holds or a stand wears. Villagers, animals,
  dropped items and minecarts aren't scanned.
