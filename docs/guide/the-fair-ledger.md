Under the [Tithe](tithe.md) everyone pays, and nobody sees where it goes. A Librarian asks to keep the books:
"Everyone pays the tithe and nobody knows what it buys. Let me keep a fair ledger, day by day, and people will pay it
without grumbling."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Ask the librarian about the tithe.
2. **The First Step.** A ledger needs books to write in: bring **24 books and quills**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **192 gold ingots**, to show the treasury is good for it.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: a ledger needs a keeper who can stand up for it. Beat one of the village's
   trainers in a Pokémon battle; without Cobblemon or a trainer, slay 32 monsters instead.

Each step pays emeralds ("Gold in the strongroom, written down.").

## How it ends

The Tithe is **reformed** for good: the tithe comes out of the villagers' takings, not your price, so the treasury keeps
its tenth and prices are back to normal. The hall's owner gets **The Fair Ledger** of the village, a written book of
the treasury's takings day by day for the last 7 days. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while the [Tithe](tithe.md) is in force and the village has a Librarian or Scholar.
- The book shows what your [treasury](village-hall.md) really takes in, day by day.
- With Cobblemon, the village's [trainers](pokemon.md) set the last step.
