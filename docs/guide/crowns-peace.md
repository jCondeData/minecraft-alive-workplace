The crown's peace over every member village.

## What it does

A realm edict (see [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md) for how realm edicts work). While the Crown's Peace is in force,
**no other village may declare a feud on a member**, and **bandits never make camp within 128 blocks of one**. The
price: **every member's takings are 10% lower**.

## Reforming it

**Clear the roads**: slay 16 monsters round any member village, and let 7 days pass without a raid on any member while
it's in force. After that the peace holds and the takings are whole again.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it stops**: [bandit camps](defence.md#bandit-camps) near your villages, and feuds from other realms.
- **What it costs**: the daily takings of every member's [treasury](village-hall.md).
