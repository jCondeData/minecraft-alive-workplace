The Move Tutor teaches your Pokémon the moves it can learn but won't pick up by leveling. They need **Cobblemon**
installed.

## Getting a move tutor

Stand a villager by a **Training Post** (a target block on planks) and sneak-right-click them with a **book**. (A block of
gold picks the [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md).)

## Lessons

Right-click the tutor with an empty hand (sneak to trade instead). Pick one of your Pokémon: you see how many moves it can
learn here, each with its power, accuracy and price, and which ones need a more experienced tutor. Click a move once to
choose it, again to pay and teach it. If your Pokémon already knows four moves, the new one waits on the moves page of its
summary for you to swap in.

A new tutor teaches weaker moves. Every lesson they give earns them experience, and as they rank up they teach stronger
ones, until a Master teaches everything. Stay close: walk more than 8 blocks away and the lesson screen closes. Tutors
sleep at night.

![A lesson](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/tutor/still-2.jpg)

## How the move tutor fits in

- **Gets you ready for:** the village's [Trainers](trainer.md) and its [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md).
- **Ranks up with:** every lesson anyone takes.

## Trading

Tutors buy paper, books and books and quills, and sell books, bottles o' enchanting and, at Master, a name tag.
