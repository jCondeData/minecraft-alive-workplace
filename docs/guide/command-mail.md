Where's my parcel? `/workplace mail` lists every parcel on its way to or from you, under "Mail on its way:": waiting
for a postman to pick it up, in a postman's bag, or traveling overnight. With nothing in the post you get "No mail on
its way to or from you."

## How it interacts

- **Postmen** carry the mail from mailbox to mailbox (see [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md)).
- **Far mail** goes beyond the postman's round, the [Postman Round](settings-reach-and-range.md) setting (64 blocks
  normally), and arrives at dawn.
