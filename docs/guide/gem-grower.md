The Gem Grower tends the crystals that grow round her stonecutter and picks them when they're ripe, without ever
breaking what they grow on.

## Getting a grower

Stand a villager by a **stonecutter** and sneak-right-click them with an **amethyst shard**. Left alone, a jobless
villager by a stonecutter still becomes a [Mason](mason.md). Put a chest by the stonecutter for her picking. The
[Gem Grotto](gem-grotto-build.md) is built for her.

## Her gem beds

She finds the beds within 16 blocks of her stonecutter by herself:

- **Amethyst**: budding amethyst. Only full clusters are picked; the budding block is never broken.
- **Tumblestones** (with Cobblemon): Tumblestone, Sky Tumblestone and Black Tumblestone, planted from the chests
  against lava or magma, the way Cobblemon grows them; full clusters picked.
- **Type Gems** (with Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer): each of the 18 types' Gem Block set against a Deepslate Crystal Core; ripe
  clusters picked and the Gem Block kept. With glass in the chests she also makes Blank TMs from shards, up to 8.

Sneak-right-click her with an empty hand to pick which beds she keeps; with none picked, she keeps every bed she has the
makings for.

## Trading

Amethyst shards (4 for an emerald) and glass bought (Novice), tumblestones and tinted glass (Apprentice), a spyglass or
shards bought, and the **Gem Grotto** blueprint for 12 emeralds (Journeyman), Type Gems (Expert, Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer), amethyst
blocks (Master).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Fed by**: budding amethyst (from a geode), tumblestones and magma or lava, and Rock or Steel Pokémon pastured
  nearby, who tap the ripe clusters loose quicker ([Pokémon partners at work](partners-at-work.md)).
- **Feeds**: amethyst, tumblestones and Type Gems into her chests, where the village's store and you can reach them.
- **Stops her**: her stonecutter gone, no chest, no beds within 16 blocks, or the **Gem Growers** setting off.
