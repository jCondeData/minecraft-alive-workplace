Sometimes a Master is taken by a **strange mood**. They stop everything, claim their workstation and ask for rare
things. Bring them, and they make something wonderful and become a [Legend](legends.md).

## When it happens

Once a day, in a happy village with no mood already on, no raid and no festival, a Master whose trade a Legend comes
from (a Master [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md) for the [Golem Smith](golem-smith.md), a Master [Chef](chef.md) for the
[Grand Chef](grand-chef.md)) may be seized, one time in 8, if the village meets that Legend's needs and the Legend's
place is free. The [Founder](founder.md)'s mood is different: it always comes, at the first rise to City.

## What you do

The Master stands at their workstation under a purple line, "Taken by a strange mood". You, the chronicle and the
village's players are told what they want: three rare materials, one of each, in a chest by their workstation within
**3 days**.

- **Success**: they make a **Masterwork**, the Legend's item with a made-up name, lore naming the
  maker, the village, the day and the materials, and a glint. It goes to whoever brought the most. The Master becomes the
  Legend. Hung in an item frame in the village, a Masterwork is worth 3 beauty.
- **Failure**: they sulk for 7 days, unhappy and working at half pace, with "Sulking" over their head, and no strange
  mood comes to that village for 10 days.

![The Masterwork made: a new Legend](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/strange_mood/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Masters, a happy village ([moods](moods.md)), and rare materials from your miners and adventures.
- **What it affects**: which Legends you get, and your [decorations](decorations.md) (a Masterwork on display).
- **What stops it**: a raid, a festival, an unhappy village, or the **Strange Moods** setting turned off (a mood
  already on is called off).
