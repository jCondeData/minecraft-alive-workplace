# Cobbleverse + Alive Workplace

**The Cobbleverse Pokémon pack, plus Alive Workplace: villagers with real jobs.** Hand a builder a blueprint and they
build it from your chests. Farmers, porters, miners, guards and dozens more work the village, and with a Village Hall it
grows into a hamlet, a town, a city and a realm, all the way to building a Wonder.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · Fabric · for a friends' server or single player**

## What's added to Cobbleverse

- **Alive Workplace**: builders and the Blueprint Table (with a 3D preview and five building styles), 30+ village
  jobs, the Village Hall and its ranks, festivals, caravans between villages, threats and walls, Legends, realms, and
  Wonders. Pokémon help at work: partners from your pastures speed up the jobs that suit their type.
- **A questbook (FTB Quests)** that walks you through it in order: Founding → The Hamlet Grows → A Village → A Town →
  A City → Wonders. Build quests tick themselves when your builder finishes the building.
- **Xaero's Minimap and World Map** in place of the FTB map (FTB Chunks stays for claims).

## Getting started

Put a **Blueprint Table** next to a villager without a job: they become your builder. Open the **questbook** and follow
the first chapter: a Starter Cottage, then the Village Hall.
