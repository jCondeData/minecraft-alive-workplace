# Realm Festival

```infobox
type: Edict
expansion: M30, M33
Kind: a realm edict, proclaimed by the capital
Boost: every member holds its festival on the capital's day, and the good mood lasts three days
Cost: 10 emeralds a member for each festival, from the realm treasury
Reform: Lanterns for the square: 32 lanterns, and 3 realm festivals held
Reformed: the realm still feasts together, and the treasury pays nothing more
categories: Edicts, Festivals
related: edicts.md, festivals-and-markets.md, royal-highway.md
```

The whole realm feasts on one day.

## What it does

A realm edict (see [Royal Highway](royal-highway.md) for how realm edicts work). While the Realm Festival is in force,
**every member village holds its festival on the capital's day**, and the festival's **good mood lasts three days**.
The realm treasury pays **10 emeralds a member** for each festival.

## Reforming it

**Lanterns for the square**: bring 32 lanterns to the capital, and hold 3 realm festivals while it's in force. After
that the realm still feasts together and the treasury pays nothing more.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: the realm treasury, filled by the members' levy.
- **What it affects**: every member's [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) and mood.
