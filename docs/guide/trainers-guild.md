```infobox

How you start it: a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md) on a Master of a member trade

related: guilds.md, pokemon.md, trainer.md
```

The guild of the village's Pokémon people. It's there only with Cobblemon installed.

## Perks

- [Move Tutors](move-tutor.md)' lessons and [Fossil Scientists](fossil-scientist.md)' revivals cost **a fifth less**.
- [Trainers](trainer.md) and [Trainer Leaders](trainer-leader.md) **rank up a quarter faster**.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** of any of those trades with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master trainers and tutors.
- **What it affects**: what you pay for moves and fossils, and how fast the village's trainers grow ([Pokémon](pokemon.md)).
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
