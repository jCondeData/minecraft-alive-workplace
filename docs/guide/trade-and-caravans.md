# Trade and caravans

Villages help each other out, and you can run a shop of your own.

## Caravans between villages

Villages with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) can supply each other. Click the **minecart** on the hall's screen to
open its **trade routes**: every other village with a hall within 2048 blocks, and what its workers are waiting for.
Click one to send it caravans.

Once a day a caravan takes that village what it's waiting for from your storehouse's chests (keeping 16 of everything
for yourselves) and it arrives in their storehouse after a trip as long as the road. Both villages' chronicles note the
caravans. A Hamlet can send caravans to three villages, and every rank above it adds one more.

![The trade routes page](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall_pages/still-2.jpg)

## Your own shop

Put down a **Shop Counter**, and a villager becomes your shopkeeper: set your prices, fill the chests, and other players
buy from you while you're away. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

## Buying from the villages

- **Market days** bring travelling traders and their blueprints: see [Festivals and market days](festivals-and-markets.md).
- **Builders** sell blueprints, and more as they level up.
- **Innkeepers** bring skilled travellers you can hire.
- **Ferrymen** sell tickets between villages.
- With Cobblemon, **Pokémon Traders** swap Pokémon with you: see [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

## Mail

Send items and letters to other players through a **Mailbox**, carried by the village's postman: see
[Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
