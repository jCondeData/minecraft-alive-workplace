An obsidian Nether portal in a blackstone arch, soul lanterns and soul campfires round it, and a **cartography table** for the village's Netherworker.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest. Put a **flint and steel** (or a fire charge) in the chests too: when the builder finishes, they light the portal.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Nether Gate, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Nether Gate II** puts a gatehouse roof over the portal and adds a storehouse of chests and barrels and a nether wart garden.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the cartography table with **netherrack** and they become the **Netherworker**. With three rations (bread, cooked meat) in the chest they step through the portal and come back a few minutes later with the Nether's goods; leave them a pickaxe, an axe, a sword or a fire resistance potion and they bring back more. Left alone, a jobless villager takes the table as a cartographer. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with an orange banner (workshops).
