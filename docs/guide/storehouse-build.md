A granary with stone below and a loft with a hoist above: a **Storehouse** block inside and eight chests round it. This is where the whole village's goods end up.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). A Novice **porter** sells it for 6 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Storehouse Shed, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Storehouse II** adds an open shed on the east with eight more chests (16 in all).
- **Storehouse III** adds a stone warehouse behind the bays, for 44 chests in all.

## What it does for the village

A villager without a job who comes near takes the Storehouse block and becomes the **Porter**. The porter walks round the village's workers and carries what they make to these chests: the miner's ore, the lumberjack's logs, the farmer's harvest. A builder short of stone finds it here, and right-clicking the Storehouse shows the requests board: what the workers nearby are waiting for. The Village Hall's **What next?** page asks for a storehouse until the village has one. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a yellow banner (stores, shops and markets).
