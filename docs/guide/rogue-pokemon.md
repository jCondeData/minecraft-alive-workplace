With Cobblemon, half the posters on a [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md) aren't bandits at all: they're on a **rogue**, a
wild Pokémon grown huge and mean, roaming 150 to 250 blocks from the hall. "Wanted: Rogue Ursaring. A huge, mean wild
Pokémon, level 50. Beat it in battle or catch it."

## Which Pokémon

It goes by the land round its spot:

| Where | Rogues |
|---|---|
| forests | Ursaring, Scyther, Pinsir |
| plains and meadows | Tauros, Kangaskhan, Rapidash |
| mountains and peaks | Onix, Golem, Aggron |
| deserts and badlands | Sandslash, Krookodile, Flygon |
| swamps and jungles | Tangrowth, Toxicroak, Swampert |
| beaches and stony shores | Kingler, Crawdaunt, Barbaracle |
| snowy lands | Mamoswine, Abomasnow, Beartic |
| savannas and taigas | Donphan, Rhydon, Lycanroc |

Its level is 35 in a Village, 50 in a Town and 65 in a City (with Radical Cobblemon Trainers, your level cap plus 5).
On the newer Cobblemon that has Alphas, it's one of them.

## Hunting it

Take the bounty on the journal's Bounties tab for the [Wanted Poster](wanted-poster.md). The rogue appears when someone
first comes within about 96 blocks of its spot, and stays until it's claimed. It **glows for the players who took its
bounty**, for them only, within 32 blocks.

**Beat it in a battle or catch it**, and the bounty goes to you straight away, once, with +40
[standing](reputation.md). If you caught it, it's yours, name and all. The chronicle says so ("Jesse caught Rogue Onix
and was paid 24 emeralds."). Unclaimed after 7 days, it wanders off in a puff.

![Beaten: the bounty paid](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/bounty_rogue/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** your village's [rank](ranks.md) sets its level and its reward.
- **What it gives:** emeralds, standing, and a strong Pokémon for your team (see [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md)).
- **What stops it:** without Cobblemon, or with **Rogue Pokémon Bounties** off in the settings, no rogue is posted; open
  rogue bounties still pay.
