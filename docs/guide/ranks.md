A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) climbs from **Hamlet** to **Village**, **Town** and **City** as it
grows. The rank goes by three things: how many people live there, how many buildings its builders have finished, and
how much its scholars have found out.

## What each rank needs

| Rank | Villagers | Finished buildings | Research levels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hamlet | any | any | any |
| Village | 10 | 5 | none |
| Town | 20 | 12 | 3 |
| City | 35 | 25 | 7 |

Hover over the hall's name icon to see what the next rank still needs. A rank up is celebrated with fireworks over the
hall, everyone nearby is told, and the chronicle remembers it.

## What a rank brings

- [Quests](quests.md) pay a quarter more a rank.
- [Caravans](trade-and-caravans.md) can go to one more village a rank.
- More traders come on [market day](market-days.md): a third from Town rank.
- The village can [grow](babies-and-growth.md) ten villagers bigger a rank.
- The [treasury](treasury.md) takes a quarter more a rank and holds a stack of emeralds more.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** [babies](babies-and-growth.md) and new villagers, builders' finished buildings (see
  [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md)) and [research](research.md).
- **Draws trouble:** from Village rank, [bandit camps](bandit-camps.md) can go up near the village.
