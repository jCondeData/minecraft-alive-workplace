The Ball Smith turns apricorns and metal into Poké Balls. They need **Cobblemon** installed.

## Getting a ball smith

Stand a villager by a **smithing table** and sneak-right-click them with any **apricorn**. (An iron ingot picks the
[Toolsmith](toolsmith.md), redstone the [Tinkerer](tinkerer.md).) Put a chest within 8 blocks of the table. Some villages
already have a **Ball Workshop**.

## What they do

They turn the apricorns and ball metals in the chests into Poké Balls with Cobblemon's own recipes (so a data pack's ball
recipes count too), a batch at a time, and put the balls back in the chests. The metal sets how hard a ball is to make:
**copper** for Poké Balls and Premier Balls, **iron** for Great Balls, **gold** for Ultra Balls, **diamonds** for the
rarest. A smith makes balls up to their own level, so they learn the harder ones as they rank up. They never make a
Master Ball.

They take turns between the kinds they can make and stop making a kind once the chests hold 64, so your apricorns aren't
all turned into one ball.

**Orders.** Sneak-right-click them to pick which balls they make. With none picked they make whatever the chests have
the makings for; with some picked, only those.

![Choosing which balls to make](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/smith_orders/still-1.jpg)

Over their head: how many balls they've made and which one now; "needs apricorns and ball metal in a chest" when the
chests run dry.

## How the ball smith fits in

- **Fed by:** the [Orchard Keeper](orchard-keeper.md), who picks apricorns, and the [Cartographer](cartographer.md), who
  finds some on expeditions; an [Armorer](armorer.md)'s iron and gold.
- **Feeds:** you, with all the balls you can throw.

## Trading

Ball smiths buy copper ingots and apricorns, and sell Poké Balls, Great Balls, Ultra Balls, a special ball now and then
and, at Master, Premier Balls.
