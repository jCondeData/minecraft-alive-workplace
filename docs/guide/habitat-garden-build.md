A wild garden in a leafy hedge, round a **mossy center stone**: stepping stones from the gate to the stone and on to a
**Pasture Block** set in the east hedge, a pond with lily pads and ferns on the west, a **Saccharine tree** at the back,
and tall grass, ferns and flowers everywhere else.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** when Cobblemon is installed: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. A Journeyman [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md) sells it for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes it, they sell the upgrade too (the Blueprint Table has it as well):

- **Habitat Garden II** runs the garden on behind, through a gap in the back hedge, to a second pond and a keeper's hide
  on spruce stilts: a ladder up, a slit window over each pond and a lantern.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click the villager by its Pasture Block with a **honey bottle** and she becomes its
[Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md), with a Saccharine tree right there to slather. With Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer, an Expert keeper
puts the village's own Habitat Block under the center stone ([The village's habitat](village-habitat.md)); taking the
garden down removes it. Like every finished building, it counts toward the village's [rank](ranks.md).
