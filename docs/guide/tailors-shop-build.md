A timber shop of dark oak and white plaster under a spruce roof. The door sits between two banners, and a bay window
beside it shows two dress forms and a bolt of cloth. Inside: the loom, a counter, bolts of wool in seven colors, and a
fitting room in a lower wing behind.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**; a [Tailor](tailor.md) who reaches Journeyman also sells it, for 12 emeralds.

## Building it

Place the blueprint, stock the chests by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists (hold Shift over it), and
hand it to the builder. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

**Tailor's Shop II** adds a cutting room story above (a cutting table, bolts of cloth on racks) and a drying loft in the
roof, open at the front behind a rail, with dyed wool hung from the rafters. A ladder runs up through both floors.

## What it does for the village

Its loom is a **Tailor's** workstation: sneak-right-click a villager by it with string. Villages sometimes grow a small
Tailor's Shop of their own, with a Tailor already living in it.

## How it fits with the rest

The Tailor sews the [Work Clothes](work-clothes.md), [Fine Clothes](fine-clothes.md) and
[Noble Robes](noble-robes.md) the classes need. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
