Clothes for being seen in: colored cloth with a touch of gold. A Burgher wouldn't be caught without them.

## Getting it

An Apprentice [Tailor](tailor.md) sews a set from **4 dyed wool** (any colors but white, mixed if you like), a
**string** and **2 gold nuggets**. With dye in the chests, the Tailor dyes white wool themselves. A Tailor sells them
for 6 emeralds.

## What it's for

A need of the [Burghers](burgher.md): every Burgher household takes a set from the village store every 8 days. An
Artisan household can't rise without them, and a Burgher one that goes three dawns without comes back down.

## How it fits with the rest

Wool from a [Shepherd](shepherd.md), dye from a [Florist](florist.md)'s flowers, gold from a [Miner](miner.md). See
[Luxuries](luxuries.md).
