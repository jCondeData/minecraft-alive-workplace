The Shop Counter turns a villager into your own shopkeeper: you set the prices, stock the chests, and other players buy
from you while you're away.

## Getting one

Craft it from an **emerald** on top and **planks**, a **chest** and **planks** in the row below. It's in the mod's
creative tab too.

## Running a shop

Place it and the shop is yours; a villager without a job nearby takes it by themselves and becomes its
**Shopkeeper** (or, with `randomStationJobs` on, sometimes the counter's other job, the Innkeeper; hand them an
emerald to make them the Shopkeeper). Open the counter to set your prices: the top slot of each column is what one sale hands over, the slot
below it the price. Put the goods in chests within 8 blocks (and no more than 4 above or below it). Other players buy by trading with the shopkeeper, and the
payment lands in those chests. **Sneak-right-click** the counter to see your sales, whatever
you have in your hand.

With CobbleDollars installed, emerald prices are paid in CobbleDollars (100 per emerald) straight to you, and a
[Price Tag](price-tag.md) in the price row sets a price in CobbleDollars directly. Only you, your friends and
operators can change the prices.

## Other jobs at the counter

Stand a villager within about 4 blocks and sneak-right-click them with:

- **a bed**: an **Innkeeper**, who brings travelers to stay while there's a free bed nearby, ready to be hired;
- **a Poké Ball** (with Cobblemon): a **Pokémon Trader**, who swaps Pokémon with you;
- **an emerald**: back to a Shopkeeper.

## How it fits in

- **Feeds** your chests with the takings.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself and the items in its price list. The shopkeeper goes the
  vanilla way: one who has leveled up keeps the job and looks for another counter.
