Now and then, in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), a grown villager falls **ill**. You'll notice: they
sneeze, walk slowly, and [chatter](chatter.md) things like "*cough* ...I don't feel so good."

## Being ill

- The ill **work at half pace**, and their [mood](moods.md) drops by 20.
- They get better by themselves after **three days**.
- Or at once, when a **Nurse** gives them a remedy from her chest: a honey bottle, a bucket of milk, or a potion of
  healing or regeneration. Keep the Nurse's chest stocked (see [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md)).

## Who falls ill

A well-kept villager has a 2% chance a day. A **hungry** villager is likelier to, and one **without a bed** likelier
still. So a full store and a bed for everyone are the best medicine.

## How it interacts

- **Fed by:** hunger and homelessness ([Food and diet](food-and-diet.md), [Village needs](village-needs.md)).
- **Affects:** the [work pace](work-pace.md) (half pace, and the cap on bonuses doesn't shield them) and
  [moods](moods.md).
- **Prevented by:** the scholars' **Medicine** [research](research.md), which makes villagers fall ill less often.
- **Turned off:** the [Sickness](settings-village-life-switches.md) switch.
