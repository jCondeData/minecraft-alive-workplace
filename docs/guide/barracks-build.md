A low stone barracks with two **grindstones** (guard posts), bunks, chests, an anvil and two **Training Dummies**.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Barracks, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Barracks II** adds a wing with two more grindstones and two more bunks.
- **Barracks III** digs an **armoury and lock-up** under the barracks, down a ladder under a hatch by the east wall: a cell behind iron bars, an anvil, chests of kit and hay targets. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was; the workers' chests stay where they were.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by a grindstone with a **sword** and they become a **Guard** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a grindstone as a weaponsmith, who mends gear and arms the guards). Guards fight any monster within 24 blocks of their post, keep the night watch and take the best weapons and armor from the chests. On quiet days they spar with the Training Dummies by their post and level up. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a black banner (towers, barracks and gates).
