The guild of the forest and the river.

## Perks

- Lumberjacks, Fletchers and Fishermen work **15% faster**.
- Their **axes and fishing rods wear half as fast**.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Lumberjack, Fletcher or Fisherman with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a
builder finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master woodsmen, and saplings for the tree farm.
- **What it affects**: logs for the builders, arrows for the guards and fish for the store.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
