In survival, Cobblemon's **Habitat Block** only turns up in its own habitat structures; nobody can craft one. A village
with a seasoned [Habitat Keeper](habitat-keeper.md) gets one of its own.

## Founding it

Once the keeper reaches **Expert** in a village with a finished [Habitat Garden](habitat-garden-build.md), she places
one Habitat Block under the garden's mossy center stone. It still looks like moss. Its Pokémon suit the land round the
village: flowery plains, berry patches in a forest, snowy burrows in the tundra, a desert oasis, tide pools on a beach,
and a calm garden anywhere else. The chronicle notes it ("founded the village's own habitat").

One per village. It belongs to the village, so [Village protection](village-protection.md) covers it, and taking the
Habitat Garden down removes it, with nothing dropped.

## Tending

Each morning the keeper visits the natural Habitat Blocks within 64 blocks of her pasture, and the hall's list posts
who's about today ("Lush Cenote, today: Lotad, Wooper, Goomy").

## How it fits with the rest

- **Needs Cobblemon 1.8.0 or newer**: on an older Cobblemon nothing is placed, and the hall says so.
- **Stopped by**: the **Village habitats** setting off (no village gets one).
