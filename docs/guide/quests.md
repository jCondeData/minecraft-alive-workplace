A village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) asks for your help, and pays for it.

## Where to find them

Each morning the village puts up a quest at the hall: three at most, each up for three days. Players nearby are told
when a new one goes up. Open the hall and click the **Quests** button (the map icon) to see them.

![The hall's quests](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/hall_quests/still-1.jpg)

## What they ask

- **Bring something**: what one of the workers is waiting for, food when the store runs low, or something the village
  can use. Click the quest to hand it in: it goes straight to the worker who asked, or to the store.
- **Clear out monsters**: slay 8 monsters round the village.
- **Beat a trainer** (with Cobblemon): win a battle against one of the village's trainers.

Monsters and battles count anywhere in the village.

## Rewards

Whoever finishes a quest gets its reward in emeralds (in CobbleDollars when the pack has them). Quests pay a quarter
more for every rank the village has, so a City's quests are worth the most.

## Need ideas?

The hall's **What next?** page (the compass) is the village's own to-do list: what it lacks most, each with how to put it
right. Doing those is the fastest way to a bigger, happier village.
