# Drought and the Well Keeper

When the rain stays away for five days, a village can fall into a **drought**. Villages in the desert, the savanna and
the badlands know it best: there it can come any dry day, and it's likelier still while desert raiders camp nearby.
Everyone in the village reads "A drought has come to Oakvale: the crops wilt and the wells run low." It lasts three to
five days, and the first rain ends it.

## What a drought does

Around the Village Hall the crops, melon and pumpkin stems and berry bushes grow at a quarter of their usual pace. The
farmland dries out, even next to water, the cauldrons lose a level of water every day, and the villagers grumble that
"the wells are low". Build the village a **Well** or a **Fountain** and they stop worrying about the wells.

## The Well Keeper

Stand a villager by a **cauldron** and sneak-right-click them with a **shovel**: they become the village's **Well
Keeper**. (Leather at the same cauldron still makes a Leatherworker.) In a drought they fill a bucket at the nearest
well, river or pond and carry it to your fields. Every bucket soaks nine blocks of farmland, and the crops on them grow
at their full pace for the rest of the day. Keep a pond or a stream within reach of their cauldron, and the fields
close to it.

Between droughts the Well Keeper keeps the village's cauldrons full and wets any farmland that has dried out. They
also read the wells and the sky: with a Well Keeper in the village, you hear of a drought a day before it comes.

With **Cobblemon**, keep Water-type Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the cauldron: they help fill and pour the buckets
faster.

## The Well House

The **Blueprint Table** has a **Well House** for your builder to put up: a roofed well on a stone platform, its shaft
down to water, with the Well Keeper's cauldron, a water trough and benches. Stand a villager by its cauldron and hand
them a shovel, and the village has its Well Keeper. A drought can come to any village, so when yours has no Well
Keeper, **What next?** at the hall suggests the Well House.

## Settings

**Well Keepers** in the settings screen turns the job off (Well Keepers already hired stand idle). To turn droughts off
altogether, set `drought` to `false` under `disasters` in the config file.
