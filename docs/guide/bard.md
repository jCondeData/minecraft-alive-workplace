The Bard gives the village its music.

## Getting a bard

A jukebox never takes a villager by itself. Stand a villager by one and sneak-right-click them with any **music disc**.

## What they do

Every morning and evening the bard plays at the jukebox. With **music discs** in a chest within 8 blocks, they play them
one after another, going round all the discs there (the discs stay in the chest), with notes floating over their head.
No discs? They make up a little tune on the harp.

Over their head: what they're playing.

## How the bard fits in

- **Fed by:** your music discs. A bard sells some themselves as they level up.
- **Their build:** a finished **Bandstand** near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) counts as a decoration, and a village
  with decorations near its homes is a happier one (see [Village life](village-life.md)).

## Trading

Bards buy string, and sell note blocks, a goat horn and, from Journeyman up, the discs "cat", "blocks" and, at Master,
"otherside".

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
