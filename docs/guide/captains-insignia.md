Proof the job is done: the badge a wanted captain wore, "Taken from Grimwald the Red".

## Getting it

Beat the captain a bounty was posted on (see [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md)). He drops it where he falls.

## Handing it in

Right-click the [Bounty Board](bounty-board.md) or the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) of the village that posted him
with it in either hand, or use the hand-in button on the journal's Bounties tab. The reward goes **to whoever hands it
in**, once: a second insignia for the same bounty pays nothing. You also earn +40 [standing](reputation.md), and the chronicle says who
brought him in.

## How it fits with the rest

You don't need to have taken the bounty to claim it: a friend can hunt him with you, and whoever holds the insignia
gets paid. Every claim is a line in the village's chronicle, and a bounty paid is a bounty off the board.
