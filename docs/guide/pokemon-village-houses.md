With Cobblemon, villages that grow in new land sometimes grow houses for the Pokémon jobs, in the village's own style
(plains, desert, savanna, taiga or snowy), each with exactly one job block and its villager already at work:

- a **Pokémon Center**: a counter with a Healing Machine and a PC, and a [Nurse](nurse.md);
- a **Camp Kitchen**: a Campfire Pot and a [Camp Cook](camp-cook.md);
- a **Berry Nursery**: paired beds, a composter and a [Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md);
- a **Daycare**: a Pasture Block and a [Daycare Keeper](daycare-keeper.md);
- a **Gem Grotto**: a stonecutter, an amethyst niche and tumblestone ledges on magma behind glass (no open lava in a
  village), and a [Gem Grower](gem-grower.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **Ready to visit**: trade with the workers straight away, and heal your team at the Center.
- **Only new land**: villages already generated don't change. The **Pokémon village houses** setting off stops new
  villages from growing them (from the next server start).
- Your own village can build the same kinds of building from blueprints: the
  [Camp Kitchen](camp-kitchen-build.md), [Berry Nursery](berry-nursery-build.md), [Daycare](daycare-build.md),
  [Gem Grotto](gem-grotto-build.md) and [Pokémon Center](pokemon-center-build.md).
