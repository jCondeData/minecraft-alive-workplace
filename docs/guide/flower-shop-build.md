The glasshouse florist: the shop is a glasshouse. A tall glazed gable faces the street on a low brick wall, with
green copper glazing bars and a copper ridge; inside, one aisle runs from the front door to the composter between two
raised beds of tall flowers, with shelves of potted flowers, hanging pots and lanterns. A brick parlor with the counter
and a chest stands against the front corner, the stove that keeps the glass warm sends its chimney up the brick back
wall, and a fenced cutting yard with a little pond lies behind the parlor.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Flower Shop, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Flower Shop II** adds a second, lower glass range across the yard, closing it into a courtyard of beds, with its own
  doors and a glazed lantern on the big ridge.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the composter with a **small flower** and they become the **Florist** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a composter as a farmer). With bone meal in the chest they grow the biome's flowers on the grass round the composter, pick them for the dyers and builders, and fill any empty flower pots nearby. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a lime banner (farms, ranches and gardens).
