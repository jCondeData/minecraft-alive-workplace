Raise the Rally Banner and your guards follow you anywhere, and fight whatever goes for you.

## Getting one

Craft **any banner** and an **iron ingot** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

1. Sneak-right-click up to twelve guards with it to enlist them. The tooltip counts them.
2. Right-click the air to raise it: while it's raised, they follow you and fight whatever goes for you.
3. Right-click the air again to lower it, and they go home to their posts.

## How it fits in

- **Breaking up a bandit camp.** Take your guards along to bring down the chief.
- **Your guards' gear** still comes from the chests at their posts, so arm them well before you set off.
