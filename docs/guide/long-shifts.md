The simplest bargain in the [Book of Edicts](edicts.md): your people work longer days, and they don't like it.

## What it does

While Long Shifts is in force, **every job in the village works 20% faster**: builders lay blocks sooner, miners dig
faster, crafters turn out more. In return **every grown villager is 10 less happy**, and their mood shows "long shifts"
as the reason.

The speed adds to the village's other speed-ups (research, Pokémon partners, levels), but never past the fastest pace
the server allows (the **Fastest Work Pace** setting).

## Reforming it

While it's in force, the Toolsmith asks for a **Shift Bell**: 24 clocks, then 128 gold ingots, then 300 bread, one step
a day ([The Shift Bell](the-shift-bell.md)). When it's done, the bell rings the work day in and out beside the hall and
nobody is unhappy about the long shifts any more: the 20% stays.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it affects**: every [job](jobs.md), and so everything the village makes, builds and carries.
- **What it costs**: a less happy village, so pair it with things that cheer them up: [Free Bread](free-bread.md),
  [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md), decorations (see [Village life](village-life.md)).
- **Good with**: a big build going up, when speed matters most.
