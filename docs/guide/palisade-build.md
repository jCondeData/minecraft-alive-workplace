A wall of sharpened spruce logs with a walkway along the inside, reached by ladders. Each blueprint is a seven-block
stretch, so a builder (or several) can ring a whole village in sections. It's the cheapest wall there is: almost all
spruce logs.

## Building it

Take a Palisade blueprint from the Blueprint Table. Stand inside the village and place each section facing out, end to
end; put a [Palisade Gate](palisade-gate-build.md) where the road goes through and a
[Palisade Tower](palisade-tower-build.md) at the corners. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

**Palisade II.** Right-click a finished section with the Palisade II blueprint: the builder adds a plank fighting step
behind the wall and, in front, a ditch of coarse dirt set with pointed dripstone stakes.

## How it fits in

- **Against raids.** A wall stands between [raiders](raids.md) or [bandits](bandit-camps.md) and the villagers' homes,
  and a [Palisade Gate](palisade-gate-build.md) in the line shuts at night. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).
- **No beauty.** Walls don't add to the village's [beauty](decorations.md), and wall sections are too thin for a mark on
  the [village map](village-map.md).
- **Repairs.** If raiders or creepers knock a section about, an idle builder puts it back when
  [Builder Repairs](settings-village-life-switches.md) is on.
