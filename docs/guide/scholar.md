The Scholar researches for the village. Every level they finish is a bonus for everyone who lives there.

## Getting a scholar

Stand a villager by a **lectern** in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and sneak-right-click them with
**paper**. (Lapis brings the [Librarian](librarian.md) back, a book picks the [Teacher](teacher.md).) Put a chest within
8 blocks of the lectern.

## Choosing what to research

Sneak-right-click the scholar with an empty hand for the village's **research tree**, and click a topic to research it
next. The scholar takes its cost (paper, books, emeralds) from the chest and works it out at the lectern. Several
scholars in one village share the work. The research is kept in the Village Hall.

![The research screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/scholar/still-4.jpg)

| Topic | What each level does | Comes after |
|---|---|---|
| Swift Hands | every job 5% faster | |
| Hearth | the village's wellbeing 10% higher | |
| Drill | guards hit 10% harder | |
| Kinship | one more Pokémon partner per worker | Swift Hands I |
| Logistics | [porters](porter.md) carry 3 more stacks | Swift Hands I |
| Craftsmanship | [carpenters](carpenter.md), [masons](mason.md), [tinkerers](tinkerer.md), [chefs](chef.md) and the other crafters 15% faster | Swift Hands I |
| Lore | children who went to school start as Journeymen ([Teacher](teacher.md)) | Hearth I |
| Medicine | villagers a third less likely to fall ill ([Nurse](nurse.md)) | Hearth I |
| Commerce | one more trader on market days, mercenaries 3 emeralds cheaper | Hearth I |
| Green Thumb | [composters](composter.md) need a layer less for a bone meal | Hearth I |
| Architecture | the scholars draw up the Town Hall blueprint | Swift Hands II and Hearth I |
| Fortification | [guards](guard.md) turn aside one blow in ten | Drill I |
| Warding | explosions (creepers, TNT, fireballs) no longer break blocks in the village | Fortification I |
| Expeditions | [cartographers](cartographer.md) and [netherworkers](netherworker.md) back 20% sooner | Logistics I |

Over their head: what they're researching, or what they need to start; "nothing to research: sneak-right-click to
choose" when nothing is picked.

## How the scholar fits in

- **Fed by:** paper and books (a [Librarian](librarian.md) makes them for builders; buy them or make your own), and
  emeralds.
- **Feeds:** the whole village, through the topics above.
- **Stops when:** there's no Village Hall nearby to keep the research, or the chest is short of the next level's cost.

## Trading

Scholars buy paper and books, and sell books, a compass, a spyglass, bookshelves, bottles o' enchanting and, at Master,
an enchanting table.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
