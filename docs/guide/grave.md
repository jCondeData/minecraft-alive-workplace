A grave is a mound with a headstone, left where a villager fell. It holds everything they were, so they can come back.

## Where graves come from

When a grown villager with a job or a name dies, a grave appears where they fell (on the nearest open, solid spot
within 3 blocks). A villager a zombie turns into a zombie villager leaves no grave: cure them instead.

**Right-click a grave** to read who lies there: "Here lies Mira, Journeyman Builder."

## Bringing them back

An **Undertaker** (a villager at a brewing stand you gave a golden apple or a totem) looks after the graves within 32
blocks of their workstation. With a golden apple, a healing potion or a totem of undying in their chest, they go to the
nearest grave and, after a little while at the graveside, bring back whoever lies there: on their feet beside it, with
their job, level, trades and name as they were. The grave is gone once they're back.

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** the undertaker's chest. Without a golden apple, potion or totem, the undertaker asks for a golden apple,
  and a [Storehouse](storehouse.md)'s board shows it.
- **Breaking it.** It gives nothing back, and whoever lay there can no longer be brought back. Leave it be until your
  undertaker gets to it.
