The way in through a [Palisade](palisade-build.md): the same sharpened spruce logs, with a three-wide spruce gate in
the middle and a lantern either side.

## Building it

Place it in line with your palisade sections, where the road crosses the wall, facing out like the rest. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Shut at night.** In a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) and at least one guard, the gates of every
  finished Palisade Gate (and Gatehouse) are shut at nightfall and opened again in the morning. You can always open
  them yourself.
- **A day's warning.** When the village has been warned of a [raid](raids.md) coming that night, the gates shut from
  noon.
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) with the towers, barracks and gates.
- **No guard, no closing.** Without a guard in the village, nobody shuts the gates. See
  [Threats and defense](defence.md).
