With the **Waystones** mod on the server, every village with a Village Hall gets a waystone of its own. The village's
Steward proposes a Waystone Plaza near the hall once, and the builder raises it like any other build. Without
Waystones the plaza is never proposed.

## The build

A small paved square with a two-step dais in the middle and the Waystones mod's own waystone on top, a lamp post on
each corner, a bench on each side facing the stone and potted plants by the steps. It is drawn in the village's own
style: cobbles and plain stone in the plains, sandstone and a sandy waystone in the desert, mud bricks and terracotta
in the savanna, polished deepslate and a deepslate waystone in snowy villages, mossy stone and a mossy waystone in the
taiga.

## The waystone

The stone is a real waystone. Walk up to it and use it to discover it, then travel to it from any other waystone the
usual way. It carries the village's name, and when you rename the village (a Name Tag or an anvil on the Village
Hall) the stone is renamed with it.

If the stone is broken, the builder puts it back when they look over the village's buildings, like any hole in a
finished build, and the new stone takes the village's name again. They need a waystone of the same kind in their
chest to do it.

## Your waystones on the hall's Map

Hover over the **Map** button on a Village Hall to see the waystone network: every one of your villages that has its
waystone, this one first, then the others nearest first, each with how far it is and which way ("Brookmere, 178
blocks north-east"). Each says whether you have discovered its stone (green), not yet (yellow: go and use it once), or
that its waystone is broken (red: the builder will put it back). Without Waystones on the server the Map doesn't show
this list.

## Switch

`villageWaystones` (on). Off: the plaza isn't proposed; stones already standing stay as they are.
