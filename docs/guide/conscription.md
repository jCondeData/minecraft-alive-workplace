# Conscription

```infobox
type: Edict
expansion: M30
Boost: in a raid every grown villager who isn't ill fights beside the guards, with a stone sword
Cost: all work stops during the raid and until noon the next day; conscripts can fall
Reform: [The Militia Drill](the-militia-drill.md): 32 iron swords, 32 shields, 48 monsters slain
Reformed: everyone still fights, but work stops only near a raider
categories: Edicts, Defense
related: edicts.md, the-militia-drill.md, defence.md
```

Every villager a soldier when the horn sounds.

## What it does

While Conscription is in force, when a raid comes **every grown villager who isn't ill takes up a stone sword** and
fights beside the guards, going for raiders within 24 blocks. The swords are made for the raid and are gone when it
ends: none ever comes out of your chests, and none is left over.

The cost: **all work stops** while the raid lasts **and until noon the next day**, and the conscripts are villagers,
not guards: they **can fall** like anyone else.

## Reforming it

While it's in force, a Guard asks to drill the militia: 32 iron swords, then 32 shields, then 48 monsters slain
([The Militia Drill](the-militia-drill.md)). After that everyone still fights, but work stops only within 24 blocks of a
raider, and the morning after is a normal day.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it helps**: a small village with few [guards](defence.md#guards) against a big [raid](defence.md#raids).
- **What it costs**: a lost morning of every [job](jobs.md), and maybe villagers.
- **Good with**: research that makes guards hit harder, and walls to keep the fight at the gate.
