The Physician's House is a white ward for the plague days, with the [Physician](physician.md)'s brewing stand at the
back.

## What it looks like

A white plastered ward in a spruce frame under a dark oak roof. Inside is the quarantine ward: four beds, two down each
side, each between white wool screens, and at the back the Physician's brewing stand on a counter of herbs. Out front, a
herb garden either side of the path and lamp posts at the gate.

## Building it

Take the Physician's House from the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md), place it and stock the chests by the builder's
table with what its tooltip lists. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md). It's a single build with no upgrade.

## Putting it to work

Stand a villager by its brewing stand and sneak-right-click them with a **glistering melon slice**: the village has its
Physician. The house has the brewing stand and no other job block. Put a chest by the stand for their
[Remedies](remedy.md).

## How it fits in

- **What it answers:** the [plague](plague.md), which only strikes a Town or a City. A Town with no Physician gets a
  tip in **What next?** at the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) to build it.
- **The beds:** four more beds help the village too: a plague is likelier where there are more villagers than beds.
- **The village map:** it's marked under care on the [village map](village-map.md).
- **Not the same as the Clinic:** the [Clinic](clinic-build.md) is the [Nurse](nurse.md)'s, for everyday illness and
  wounds.
