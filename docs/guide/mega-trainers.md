With **Mega Showdown** on the server, some village trainers carry a Mega Stone: one of their Pokémon holds it, and in a
battle against you they Mega Evolve it on the first turn they can.

## Who Mega Evolves

- **Master** trainers, the top rank.
- **Trainer Leaders of a City.** Once your village grows into a City, its [Trainer Leader](trainer-leader.md) gets a
  Mega Stone too. A Town's Leader doesn't, so growing your village makes its Leader tougher.
- **The Festival Cup's finalists.** On the Cup's day, the two trainers who reach the final each bring a Pokémon with
  its Mega Stone (one that fits the Cup's theme). Beat everyone else and you face a Mega in the final.
- **The Stadium Day's visitors.** The trainers who come to a Stadium for its day are champions of their villages, and
  they Mega Evolve when you challenge them.

## The Mega Bracelet

Every trainer who can Mega Evolve wears a **Mega Bracelet**: a gold band round the left wrist with the rainbow Key
Stone set in it. Look at their crossed arms before you challenge them: no bracelet, no Mega.

![The Leader Mega Evolves in the battle](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/mega_trainers/still-2.jpg)

## Settings

On the settings screen, **Village Megas** turns this on or off. Off, only Masters Mega Evolve and nobody wears a
bracelet. Without Mega Showdown nothing changes at all.
