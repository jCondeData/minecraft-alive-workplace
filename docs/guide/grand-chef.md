"Their banquets fill the tables, the hearts and, before long, the cradles."

## How they come

Stock the store with **eight kinds of meal**. Then the **Grand Chef** may come three ways: a Master [Chef](chef.md) taken by
a [strange mood](strange-moods.md) (asking for three of a golden apple, a glistering melon slice, a golden carrot,
honeycomb, glow berries, chorus fruit and a pufferfish, and making a Midsummer Cake), a visit to the inn, or a child of
two schooled Master Chefs growing up.

## What they do

- **Banquets.** Every 5 days, after work, the Grand Chef calls the village to the Market Square (or round the hall). At
  supper each grown-up eats two meals from the store, of as many kinds as it has. Everyone who came is 20 happier for 3
  days, and for 3 days babies come twice as often. No banquet on a festival's day; it waits for the next.
- **A faster kitchen.** Every Chef in the village works a quarter faster.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your [farmers](farmer.md), cooks and the [Storehouse](storehouse.md): a bare store means
  "the store was bare, and nobody ate".
- **What they affect**: [moods](moods.md), [babies](babies-and-growth.md), and how fast the store empties.
- **What stops them**: a strike, or an empty store.
