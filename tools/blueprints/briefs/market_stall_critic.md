## Verdict: BACK

1. **Interior, cut2 front/top of II and III:** the storerooms are bare chest boxes. Put stock and a work spot in them
   (barrels or crates along the free walls, a packing or tally table by the door, a shelf with goods), and join the
   stall's aisle behind the Shop Counter to the storeroom with a door in the shared wall at x 7-8, so the Shopkeeper
   does not walk out through the gate and round the front to reach his own stock.
2. **Interior, market-stall-cut2_front (Level I):** the counter shows only carpet, two pots and four wool blocks, and
   the back wall of the aisle is bare. Fill the counter and the slab shelf behind it with several kinds of goods (the
   OLD stall had produce, flowers and barrels on both); keep within the woodwork and one-barrel budgets.
3. **Back views, all three sheets:** the back is a flat pink panel (I) or flat walls (II, III) with a hay bale and a
   pumpkin. Give the back face frame posts proud of the infill, a window or a back display shelf, and on II/III break
   the long back wall of the storeroom.
4. **Left/right and top views, Level I:** from the sides the stall reads as a closed cottage and the roof ends flush at
   front and back. Carry the display round one side (a side counter or crates under an extended eave) and give the
   gables a one-block verge at front and back.
5. **Front view, sheet 3:** III looks the same as II from the street; its only addition is hidden at the back. Show
   the growth on the street side (a second display, a storeroom front window with goods, or a sign), and keep Level I's
   block count near the OLD's while doing the above.
