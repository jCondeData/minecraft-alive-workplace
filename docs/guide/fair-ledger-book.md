A written book, signed by the Village Clerk: proof of where the village's money goes.

## Getting it

It's the prize at the end of [The Fair Ledger](the-fair-ledger.md), the story that reforms the [Tithe](tithe.md). When
the last step is done, the hall's owner gets the book (if they're not online, whoever finished the story does), and the
chronicle notes that the village keeps a fair ledger now.

## What's in it

One page: "The Fair Ledger of Thornholm. The treasury's takings:", then a row per day for the last 7 days, each in
emeralds to the hundredth ("Day 41: ... emeralds"), and the week's total ("In all: ... emeralds").

## How it fits with the rest

It's a snapshot of your [treasury](treasury.md) the day the story ended: the takings the
[Village Ledger](village-ledger.md) lets you collect, written down. Read it to see how well the village earns once the
Tithe comes out of the villagers' takings instead of your prices.
