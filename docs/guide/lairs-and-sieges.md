Raiders don't just turn up any more: they make camp, they have a leader with a name, and a walled village gets a
proper siege.

## Lairs and their captains

Now and then raiders make camp 80 to 104 blocks from the Village Hall, on dry, fairly flat, open ground. The captain
has a name, and it floats over his head and is in every message about the camp: "Smoke rises beyond Oakvale: Chief Harl
Ashgrave and a band of raiders have made camp..." While the camp stands, the village's night raids come from it,
twice as often. Each kind of raider has a camp of its own: bandits a bandit camp, a pillager warband a war camp,
desert raiders an old tomb, drowned pirates a pirate ship, piglins a blackstone outpost, witches a coven hut.

The camp has a strength: it grows a little every day, and a raid takes its raiders from it. Those who survive a raid go
back; the dead are gone, and so is anyone you kill at the camp. **Kill the captain** and the camp is broken up: the
band scatters, the chest is yours, the chronicle names who did it, and no camp comes for a few days.

## The Defense page

Click the guards icon (the iron sword) on the Village Hall. The Defense page shows the camp, its captain, its strength
and roughly where it is ("south-east, about 90 blocks"), the last three attacks, and the village's gates: open or shut,
the portcullis up or down, and "Under siege" while one is on. On a warned day it also shows the attack that's coming.

## Scouts

Sneak-right-click a guard with a **spyglass** and they become a **Scout** (a bow, shield or healing potion turns them
back). Click "Scout the lair" on the Defense page and the nearest free scout walks out by day, looks the camp over
through the spyglass from about 24 blocks away and comes home. The Defense page then gives the camp's exact place, its
captain and strength, and in the chest by the scout's grindstone is a **Scouting Report**: a map with a red X on the
lair. A scout not home within a day is lost. When raiders flee at dawn, a scout trails them home and the lair is found
at once. A Scout at a Guard Post near a finished Lookout Tower or Wall Tower also sees raids coming, and counts the
raiders.

## Sieges

A raid on a village with at least one finished wall or gate build is a **siege**: "Oakvale is under siege! The gates
are shut and the portcullis is down." The gates shut at once and every finished Gatehouse drops its portcullis. A ram
(a ravager, or raiders with axes) goes for the nearest gate: a Palisade Gate holds about 10 seconds, a Gatehouse about
a minute. A **Gatehouse II** has a barbican with a second gate and portcullis, and raiders have to break both, so it
holds several times as long. Only gates the builders made can break; a gate you placed yourself is never hit.

Raiders with ladders set them against the walls and climb over. A guard on the walkway within 2 blocks of a ladder's
top throws it down.

When a siege begins (or at dusk on a day the village was warned), the guards go to their **battle stations**: archers
up on the towers and walkways on the raiders' side, knights just inside the gate the rams go for, medics behind them.
From a station an archer shoots 24 blocks.

![The gate holds](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/siege_gate/still-3.jpg)

**The morning after**, the chronicle has a siege report: how many came and fell, whether the gate held, and the hero of
the night. Players who killed a raider in a siege that was fought off are Heroes of the Village for a day, and the
whole village is 10 happier. For 2 days the builders mend walls, gates and towers first.

## How it fits with the rest

- [Walls and gates](defence.md#walls-and-gates) from the Blueprint Table turn raids into sieges; their II upgrades
  hold longer.
- [Guards](defence.md#guards) of every kind have a job in a siege, and a Scout finds the lair for them.
- Breaking up a camp is how the [Bandit King](the-bandit-king.md) story begins.
- The Threat Level setting and Peaceful difficulty still decide whether raiders come at all (see [Settings](settings.md)).
