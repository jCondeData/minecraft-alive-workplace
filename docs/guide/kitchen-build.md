A cozy village house of white walls in an oak frame under a spruce roof, with a bed and a campfire, and for its worker a **smoker** and a chest: everything one worker needs to live and work in the same place.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). Newly explored villages often have one of these houses already, with its worker inside.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the smoker with **raw meat**, **raw fish** or a **potato** and they become the **Chef** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a smoker as a butcher). With the makings in the chest the chef cooks bread, cookies, pies, cake, stews and everything a smoker cooks, a batch at a time, and the food feeds the village. See [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md) and [Village life](village-life.md).

**A home.** Its one bed is the worker's own. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a purple banner (other buildings).
