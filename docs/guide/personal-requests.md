A villager who counts you a friend may come to you with something of their own. They walk up and say it over their
head and in your chat:

> Dara: Jesse, the festival's nearly on us, and I'd dearly love to stand there having made Journeyman. Trade with me a
> while, or spare me a Bottle o' Enchanting. Would you?

The next line says what they want and what it pays, with **[I'll help]** and **[Not now]**. Not now costs nothing:
"Another time, then." I'll help opens the request: it shows on the **Personal** tab of the
[quest journal](quest-journal.md), on their life story page and on their tooltip at the hall.

## What they ask

| Request | Who asks | Done when | Time | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Next level before the festival | a worker below Master | they reach their next level (trade with them, or give a Bottle o' Enchanting as a [Gift](gift.md)) | before the next festival | 6 emeralds |
| A home of my own | someone with no home, or a tier I one | they sleep in a finished home of tier II or better | 12 days | 8 emeralds |
| A taste of home | anyone | you bring a dish of their home biome: a pumpkin pie on the plains, rabbit stew in the desert, 16 sweet berries in the taiga... | 3 days | 4 emeralds |
| The tools of my trade | a Miner, Lumberjack, Farmer, Fisherman, Guard, Cartographer, Netherworker or Sifter | you bring the tool: a diamond pickaxe, a diamond axe, a spyglass... | 5 days | 5 emeralds |
| A letter to family | anyone with a relative in a village within caravan range | you carry their [Sealed Letter](sealed-letter.md) there and the reply back | 7 days | 4 emeralds |
| Bring back my love | a widow or widower whose late partner's grave is in the village | an [Undertaker](undertaker.md) revives that grave; they marry again then and there | 10 days | 6 emeralds |
| A cat by the door | anyone with a bed and no pet by it | at dawn, a cat or tamed wolf is within 10 blocks of their bed | 4 days | 3 emeralds |
| Off to school | a parent of a child who hasn't been to school | the child is schooled by a [Teacher](teacher.md) | 10 days | 4 emeralds |
| A chapel wedding | one of a courting couple, with no Chapel in the village | a [Chapel](chapel-build.md) is finished; they marry in it at once, with a feast | 5 days | 6 emeralds |
| The lost heirloom | anyone | you find the [heirloom](lost-heirloom.md) where it was lost and bring it back | 10 days | 8 emeralds |
| Help me train (Cobblemon) | a Trainer | you beat them in battle on three different days | 7 days | friendship |
| A Pokémon friend (Cobblemon) | a worker whose job Pokémon help with | a Pokémon of a helping type is pastured near their workstation at dawn | 5 days | friendship |

Things to bring are handed in from the Personal tab with a click, as on the hall's quests.

![The journal's Personal tab: the pie to hand in](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/personal_request/still-3.jpg)

## Helping together

Other players with 3 hearts with that villager can join from the Personal tab, and anyone with the hearts who hands
something in is a helper too. When it's done, **every helper** gets the reward and 150 friendship, the villager thanks
them, and the chronicle says "Jesse helped Dara: Next level before the festival".

Miss the time and every helper loses 30 friendship, the villager is glum for a day ("Let down: nobody helped in time")
and asks nobody for anything for 3 days.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** [friendship](friendship.md), and the village itself: requests come from what a villager lacks (a
  home, a level, a pet, a school).
- **What it affects:** each helper earns 25 [standing](reputation.md) in the village; a home, a Chapel or a schooled
  child stay with the village for good.
- **What stops it:** only a named, awake grown-up with a friend within 24 blocks asks, one request at a time. With
  **Personal requests** off in the settings, nobody asks.
