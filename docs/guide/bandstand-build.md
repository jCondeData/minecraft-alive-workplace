An open eight-sided bandstand on a stone base, with birch posts and railings under a dark prismarine roof. In the
middle stands a **jukebox** with two note blocks beside it: a stage made for the village's Bard.

## Building it

Take a Bandstand blueprint from the Blueprint Table and give it room: it's 11 blocks across. It needs a fair amount
of stone bricks, birch and dark prismarine. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **The Bard.** The jukebox is the Bard's workstation. A villager who takes it up with a music disc becomes the
  village's Bard and plays every morning and evening (see [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md)).
- **Beauty.** A finished Bandstand within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach adds 3 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md).
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) as a decoration.
