Every named villager in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) keeps a friendship with each player: ten hearts,
yours alone. Look at Dara from up to 6 blocks and your action bar reads "Dara ♥♥♥♡♡♡♡♡♡♡". Another player sees their
own.

## Making friends

| You... | Friendship |
|---|---|
| trade with them | +5, once a day |
| hand in something toward a quest they posted | +10, once a day |
| finish a quest they posted | +40, once a day |
| kill a monster that just hurt them | +15 |
| come to their wedding | +30 |
| are at a festival they came to | +10, once a day |
| give them a [Gift](gift.md) | +20 to +80 by how they like it (three times on their name day) |
| hear one of their [heart events](heart-events.md) to the end | +20 |
| finish one of their [personal requests](personal-requests.md) | +150 |

When your hearts go up, little hearts puff over their head. **Hitting** them costs 50 (at most once a minute). Hearts
never fade, so nobody has to visit to keep them.

![The hall: your hearts and her best friends](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/friendship/still-3.jpg)

On the hall's list, a villager's tooltip shows your hearts and their two best friends among the players.

## What friends get

- **Stories.** At 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 hearts they have something to tell you: their [heart events](heart-events.md), and
  at 10 a [keepsake](keepsakes.md).
- **Requests.** From 3 hearts they may ask you a favor: a [personal request](personal-requests.md).
- **Cheaper trades.** 5% off at 4 hearts and 5% more for each heart after, 30% at most; your [title](reputation.md) in
  their village adds more, up to 40% in all.
- **Festival gifts.** At a festival, everyone with 6 hearts or more with you brings you a gift from their trade.
- **A namesake.** The first baby of a couple where a parent has 8 hearts with you takes your name.
- **Letters.** At 10 hearts a villager writes to you once a week, with a gift, through the village's post.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** trading, [quests](quests.md), defending the village, [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md),
  [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) and gifts.
- **What it affects:** prices, [standing and titles](reputation.md), and every story a villager tells.
- **What stops it:** a villager without a [name](names.md) or a hall keeps no friendships. With **Friendship** off in
  the settings, no points move and no hearts show; what was earned is kept.
