Draw a road on the [City Plan](city-plan.md) and the village's builders lay it: a gravel lane between two cottages, a
stone street through the middle of town or a broad avenue up to the hall, with lamps along it and steps where the
ground rises.

## Drawing a road

On the plan's screen pick **Road**, choose its width (**Lane** 1 block, **Street** 3, **Avenue** 5), click points
along it and double-click (or press Enter) to end it. Right-click a road to take it off. A road takes the style of the
zone it starts in:

| Style | Looks like |
|---|---|
| As drawn | dirt path, with coarse dirt and gravel at the edges |
| Stonework | stone bricks with cracked ones, cobblestone edges |
| Sandstone | smooth and cut sandstone |
| Dark Oak | cobbled and polished deepslate |
| Cherry | polished diorite with stone brick edges |
| Apricorn (Cobblemon) | bricks with mud brick edges |

A road you draw counts as approved: the [Steward](steward.md) builds it without asking.

## How it's built

The Steward finds a way over the ground along your line: it keeps the road's width clear, goes round buildings and
anything a player placed, and climbs at most one block at a time. The way is cut into stretches of up to 24 blocks, and
the nearest free [builder](builder.md) takes each one as an ordinary build. At most two stretches are open at once, and
none while a building of the village waits for a builder, so in a busy village roads come last. A finished road goes
in the hall's chronicle ("finished a Stonework road, 40 blocks long").

- **Lamps**: streets and avenues get the style's **Street Lamp** every 16 blocks on alternate sides, and one at every
  crossing, never in front of a door. Lanes get a lantern post every 12 blocks. Lamps count as street lamps for the
  village's beauty and light the beds near them.
- **Steps**: a one-block rise becomes stairs across the road's width; crossings are paved square.
- **Water and drops**: a [bridge](bridges.md).
- **Lanes to every door**: each newly finished building gets a lane from its door to the nearest road.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Needs**: a Steward and his builders; with the **Stewards Build Roads** setting off, roads stay drawn on the plan
  but aren't built.
- **Leaves your things alone**: a road goes round a player's fence or house and leaves it standing.
- **Doesn't change the rank**: roads aren't buildings, so they never raise the village's [rank](ranks.md) or show as
  buildings on its map.
- **Plots face roads**: the Steward turns new buildings to face the nearest road ([Zones and plots](plan-zones.md)).
- **Trade**: roads also run [to the villages you trade with](roads-between-villages.md).
