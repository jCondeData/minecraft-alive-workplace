Black sails on the horizon: a Town by the sea may find a pirate ship anchored off its beach.

## The Pirate Ship

By a Town or a City with an ocean or a beach within 48 blocks of its hall, drowned pirates may anchor 40 to 70 blocks
out, in water at least 5 deep, bow toward the village: a two-masted dark oak sloop with black sails, a crow's nest,
gunports down both sides and the Jolly Roger over the quarterdeck. Chat says so: "Black sails off Thornholm: Captain Silas
Brine has dropped anchor 52 blocks south with his drowned crew..."

## The crew

**Captain Silas Brine** is a drowned in a deep red coat and a black cap, with a trident and 40 more health. He stays in
his cabin at the stern. His crew, all called "Drowned pirate": drowned with tridents, plain drowned, and skeleton
**gunners** in black caps with Flame bows. The ship starts 8 strong and grows by 1 a day up to 14.

On a raid night the crew wades in from the water off the village's beach nearest the ship, never from the land side, so
a sea wall or a harbor gate is what holds them. At dawn they go back into the sea.

## Fire arrows

The gunners' burning arrows can set alight the wooden blocks your builders built: a [village fire](fire.md). A
[Firewarden](firewarden.md) is good company for a seaside town.

## Sinking the ship

Kill the captain and his chest is set on a little raft of logs on the water between the ship and the village, and the
ship burns and sinks over ten seconds, from the mastheads down. Only the ship's own blocks go; the sea comes back where
the hull was. The crew scatters and no new lair comes for five days.

## How it fits in

- **What stops it:** a wall along the shore, guards near the beach, and a boat out to the captain's cabin.
- **The chest:** gold, emeralds, a trident, nautilus shells, often gold nuggets, fish, prismarine shards, a map or an
  enchanted book, now and then a diamond, and one time in ten a heart of the sea.
- **Switched off:** `drowned_pirates` under `raiderCultures` in the config file. The `mobGriefing` game rule off keeps
  their arrows from lighting anything.
