A fenced garden laid out for crossing berries: farmland beds in pairs with a water channel between the two beds of each
pair, edged in mud brick, a path up the middle to the **composter**, and at the back a potting bench under a little
lean-to roof with pots, saplings, a barrel and the breeder's chest.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table** when Cobblemon is installed: pick it and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank
Blueprint. A Journeyman [Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md) sells it for 10 emeralds.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests
by your builder's Blueprint Table with what it lists, and right-click the builder with it. See
[The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes it, they sell the upgrade too (the Blueprint Table has it as well):

- **Berry Nursery II** adds a greenhouse behind, glass between spruce posts under a glass roof, with four more beds in
  two pairs, all within reach of the composter.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click the villager by its composter with **any Cobblemon berry** and she becomes its
[Berry Breeder](berry-breeder.md): the paired beds are just what she needs to cross two berries. Like every finished
building, it counts toward the village's [rank](ranks.md).
