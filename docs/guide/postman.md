The Postman carries mail between players' mailboxes, and when there's no mail, hauls things between your chests.

## Getting a postman

Craft a **Mailbox** (iron nuggets round a chest, on a fence) and place it. A mailbox never takes a villager by itself:
stand a villager by it and sneak-right-click them with **paper**.

## Mail

Your mailbox is yours: mail for you arrives there (the red flag goes up), and only you, your friends and operators can
open it. To send something, open your mailbox, put items in the top row, type a player's name and press **Send**; write
in the **Letter** line to send a letter too. `/workplace mail` shows where your parcels are.

The postman collects parcels from the mailboxes near their own and carries each one to its player's mailbox when that's
on their round (64 blocks). Mail going farther (another village, another dimension) is handed in and goes with the
**night mail**, arriving at the next dawn. With a **Flying** Pokémon in a Pasture Block nearby, far parcels go by
**air mail** and arrive at once.

![The mailbox screen](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/mail/still-1.jpg)

## Courier routes

Craft a **Delivery Note** (paper, a feather and an ink sac). Right-click the chest to take from, then the one to bring to.
To carry only certain things, hold an item in your other hand and right-click the air to add it to the note's list (up
to nine); with no list they carry everything but tools, weapons and armor. Hand the note to a postman: when there's no
mail, they walk the route. A postman keeps up to four routes; hand them a blank note to end them all.

Over their head: how many deliveries, and whose mailbox they're collecting from or delivering to.

## How the postman fits in

- **Works beside:** the [Porter](porter.md), who carries the workers' goods to the storehouse; a courier route moves
  whatever you like between any two chests.
- **Helped by:** a Flying partner (see [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md)).
