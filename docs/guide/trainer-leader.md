The Trainer Leader is the village's best trainer: the one to beat. Like every trainer, they need **Cobblemon**
installed.

## Getting a leader

Stand a villager by a **Training Post** (a target block on planks) and sneak-right-click them with a **block of gold**.
Some villages already have a **Leader's Hall**.

## Challenging the leader

Right-click the leader with an empty hand to battle. A leader battles at **Expert** strength from the very start, pays **three times** a trainer's prize, and takes **one challenge a day** from each player ("come
back tomorrow!").

A village has one leader: if there's another leader within 64 blocks with more experience, they take the challenges, and
the other tells you to go to them.

![The battle starts](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/leader/still-2.jpg)

## How the leader fits in

- **After:** the village's [Trainers](trainer.md), who start as Novices.
- **Before the battle:** a [Move Tutor](move-tutor.md) can teach your Pokémon a move it won't learn by leveling, and a
  [Nurse](nurse.md) heals your team.

## Trading

At Expert, a leader sells the **Arena** blueprint for 20 emeralds.
