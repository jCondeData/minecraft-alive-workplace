Two sliders: how long the village calendar's seasons last, and how fast all the bonuses together may make a worker.
See [Settings](settings.md) for where to change them.

## Days per Season

Normally **16**: the days in each of the village calendar's four [seasons](seasons.md), so a year is 64 days. Each
season has its festival day in the middle. Shorter seasons turn the calendar faster; the village's regular festivals
still come every eight days.

## Fastest Work Pace

Normally **200**: how fast all the bonuses together can make a worker, in percent of the usual pace. 200 is twice as
fast. The bonuses are Pokémon partners, a well-kept village, research, traits and a happy mood. Sickness and bad moods
still slow a worker down after the cap, and a worker's level doesn't count towards it (a Master is faster on top). At
100, no bonus speeds anyone up at all. More in [Work pace](work-pace.md).

## How they interact

- The seasons show on the hall's calendar page (see [Festivals and market days](festivals-and-markets.md)).
- A lower Fastest Work Pace makes [research](research.md), [moods](moods.md), [traits](traits.md) and partners matter
  less once a worker has a few of them.
