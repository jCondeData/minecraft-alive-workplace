A straw-thatched barn of compost bins, the Bin Barn, with a **composter** and its chest in the dry at the front.

## What it looks like

A steep roof of golden straw (bamboo mosaic, a hay ridge) over packed-mud walls in a dark oak frame on a rubble plinth
(rebuilt from scratch; a Compost Yard already standing keeps its old look until refreshed). The barn's front opens on a
packed yard as three bins behind spruce boards, the heaps graded from fresh green (moss and leaves) through turning brown
(mud and rooted dirt) to ripe black (podzol). Over the middle bin a hoist gable juts out on a beam, with a loft door, a
hoist arm and a lantern; under its overhang stand the composter and the scrap chest, with lanterns hung on chains above.
Behind the bins, a turning aisle under its own lower roof, reached by a door at the west end, holds the straw for the
heaps and a bone-meal chest.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Compost Yard, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Compost Yard II** leans a riddling shed on the barn's east side under its own pent roof: two more chests against the barn wall, a pile of riddled fine compost gone to mushrooms, a potting bench with saplings in pots, and a bed of ferns and lilies of the valley grown in the finished compost.

## What it does for the village

Sneak-right-click a villager by the composter with **bone meal** and they become the **Composter** (left alone, a jobless villager takes a composter as a farmer). The village's scraps (seeds, saplings, leaves, rotten flesh) go in, and more bone meal comes out than a vanilla composter gives, for the farmers, florists and lumberjacks. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a lime banner (farms, ranches and gardens).
