The Undertaker brings the village's dead back to life, just as they were.

## Getting an undertaker

Stand a villager by a **brewing stand** and sneak-right-click them with a **golden apple** (an enchanted one works too)
or a **totem of undying**. Put a chest within 8 blocks of the stand.

## Graves

A grown villager with a job or a name who dies leaves a **grave** where they fell: a mound with a headstone. Right-click
it to read who lies there. The grave holds everything they were: job, level, trades, name. A villager a zombie turns into
a zombie villager leaves no grave: cure them instead.

## What they do

With a **golden apple**, a **healing potion** or a **totem of undying** in the chest, the undertaker takes it to the
nearest grave within 32 blocks, spends a little while at the graveside, and whoever lies there gets up again, with their
old job, level, trades and name. Without one, they ask for a golden apple on the Storehouse's requests board.

Meanwhile they tidy up: each grave lying about the village (a street, a field, a doorstep) is laid to rest in a row of
the village's [Graveyard](graveyard-build.md), and whoever lies there can still be brought back from it. A village with
a Village Hall and no Graveyard gets a small plot of three rows near the hall. The `gravesToGraveyard` setting turns
this off.

Over their head: how many they've brought back, and whether they're going to a grave, bringing someone back, or what
they need.

## How the undertaker fits in

- **Fed by:** a [Cleric](cleric.md)'s healing potions if you move some over, golden apples and totems from you.
- **Keeps:** a Master [Builder](builder.md) or a seasoned [Guard](guard.md) lost to a raid isn't lost for good. Couples
  mourn when one of them dies (see [Village life](village-life.md)), so a quick return matters.
- **Stops when:** there's nothing in the chest to bring someone back with, or no chest by the brewing stand.

## Trading

Undertakers buy bones and rotten flesh, and sell candles, lilies of the valley, soul lanterns, mossy stone bricks, a
golden apple and, at Master, a totem of undying.

See also [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).
