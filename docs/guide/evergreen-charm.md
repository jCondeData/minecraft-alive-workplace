A gold-and-green leaf pendant that keeps a good villager with you for good. One charm, one villager.

## Getting it

Craft a **totem of undying**, a **golden apple**, **2 emeralds** and a **heart of the sea** together, in any
arrangement. The recipe unlocks when you hold a totem or a heart of the sea.

## Using it

Sneak-right-click an [elder](elders.md) with it.

- **An elder with a good trait** takes it: a Master of their trade, someone Gifted or a Legend, or an elder who has been
  happy (mood 80 or more) for 20 days running. Totem sparkles, a cheer, "Bram (a Master of their trade) takes the
  Evergreen Charm and will never leave us." The charm is used up, the chronicle remembers who gave it, and their card on
  the hall's list wears a gold leaf badge: "Ageless: will never leave us".
- **Anyone else** turns it down and you keep it, with the reason over your hotbar: not an elder yet ("grown 30 of 120
  days"), not good enough yet ("Happy days so far: 19"), or already ageless.

![45 elder days on, at nightfall: Dara's grave, and Bram still here](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/ageless_elder/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What it changes:** only the passing. An ageless elder still walks slower, still has their quiet old age and still
  retires.
- **When it's needed:** the hall's card warns from ten days before an elder's time comes. A villager who already passed
  can still come back through an [Undertaker](undertaker.md), and then take a charm.
- **Settings:** with **Evergreen Charms** off the charm is refused and kept; with **Elders Pass Away** off nobody needs
  one, so it's refused too. Elders already ageless stay ageless.
