The Training Post is where the village's Pokémon trainers work: the trainers you battle, the village's leader and the
move tutor.

## Getting one

Craft it from a **target block** on top of **three planks**. It's in the mod's creative tab too.

## The jobs

A villager without a job nearby takes it by themselves and becomes a **Trainer**: right-click them to battle. Every
trainer starts as a Novice with two young Pokémon and ranks up as people battle them. Beating one pays CobbleDollars
(emeralds without CobbleDollars), once a day per trainer.

Stand a villager within about 4 blocks and sneak-right-click them with:

- **a block of gold**: the **Trainer Leader**, who battles at Expert strength from the start, pays three times the
  prize and takes one challenge a day from each player;
- **a book**: a **Move Tutor**, who teaches your Pokémon moves they won't get from leveling;
- **a Poké Ball**: back to a Trainer.

## How it fits in

- **Needs Cobblemon.** Without it nobody battles or teaches here.
- **Quests** may ask you to beat one of the village's trainers.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself. A trainer who has leveled up keeps the job and looks for
  another post.
