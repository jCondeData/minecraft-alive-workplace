A stone fountain: a wide basin of polished andesite and stone bricks with a chiseled column in the middle and water
spilling down into the pool. It's all stone, so it is easy to stock, and it is one of the prettiest things a small
village can have.

## Building it

Take a Fountain blueprint from the Blueprint Table, place it in an open square and fill the chests with stone. The
builder places the water too. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md).

## How it fits in

- **Beauty.** Finished within a [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s reach, it adds 3 to the village's
  [beauty](decorations.md), as much as the Gazebo or the Bandstand.
- **Moods.** Villagers whose bed is within 16 blocks of it are in a better [mood](moods.md).
- **The village map.** It shows on the [village map](village-map.md) as a decoration.
- **Market day.** Traveling traders on [market day](market-days.md) sometimes sell decoration blueprints like this one.
