The Fossil Lab is the Fossil Scientist's old workstation. You can't craft it any more, and it's not in the creative tab. Today the Fossil Scientist works at a **Cobblemon's Fossil Analyzer**: stand a villager within about 4 blocks of one and sneak-right-click them holding a fossil.
The Fossil Lab is kept so that a world that already has one keeps working, and an operator can still hand one out with
`/give @p aliveworkplace:fossil_lab`.

## What it does

A villager without a job who stands near a Fossil Lab takes it by themselves and becomes a **Fossil Scientist**: no item needed.
Right-click the scientist holding a fossil and pay 8 emeralds: they revive it while they work, and the Pokémon joins your party (or your PC) when it's done.

Chests and barrels within **8 blocks** are the worker's supply chests: their tools and materials come out of them,
and their work goes into them. Right-click the Fossil Lab to see how many supply chests it has. More on the job in
[Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md).

## How it fits in

- **Needs Cobblemon**: fossils are Cobblemon's.
- **A soft glow.** The lab gives off a little light.
- **Shared stock.** Workers whose workstations are within 48 blocks of each other share their chests, as long as they
  answer to the same people.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with a pickaxe; it drops itself, so you can move it. A worker who has leveled up keeps the job
  and takes a free block of their job nearby by themselves (a Fossil Lab or a Cobblemon's Fossil Analyzer); one who hasn't worked yet may go
  back to having no job, as in vanilla.
