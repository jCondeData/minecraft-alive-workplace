Twice the parties, if you can pay for them.

## What it does

While Festival Season is in force, the village holds **a festival every 4 days instead of every 8**, with all the
feasting, fireworks and good mood a festival brings. Each one **costs the treasury 3 emeralds, and 1 more for every 4
villagers**. When the treasury can't pay, the festival is called off and the villagers are disappointed. The hall's
festival icon shows the next one, its price and what the treasury holds.

## Reforming it

While it's in force, the Bard asks for a **festival fund**: 24 cakes, then 256 firework rockets, then 48 note blocks
([The Festival Fund](the-festival-fund.md)). After that the villagers put by for their own festivals, one every 4 days,
and the treasury pays nothing.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: the [treasury](village-hall.md): taxes, the [Tithe](tithe.md), the market.
- **What it affects**: the village's mood, festival after festival (see
  [Festivals and market days](festivals-and-markets.md)).
- **What breaks it**: an empty treasury. A called-off festival leaves the village worse off than none.
