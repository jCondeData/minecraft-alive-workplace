A wrapped present. Its tooltip says who it's from ("From Jesse"), never what's inside.

## Giving it

Right-click a named villager with it. They unwrap it (a paper rustle, bits of the item flying), say what they think
over their head and in your chat ("Cake! You remembered."), and your [hearts](friendship.md) with them move:

| They... | Friendship |
|---|---|
| love it | +80 |
| like it | +45 |
| don't mind it | +20 |
| dislike it | -20 |
| hate it | -40 |

Hearts puff if they liked it; they shake their head if they didn't. The item goes into the chests by their
workstation, or the village store, or their pockets.

A second gift the same day, or a third the same week, is turned down and stays in your hand.

## What they like

Everyone loves **cake, pumpkin pie, golden apples and diamonds**, and likes bread, cookies, honey bottles, emeralds and
small flowers. Nobody wants dirt, gravel, cobblestone or bones, and rotten flesh, spider eyes, poisonous potatoes and
pufferfish are an insult. Beyond that, each trade has its own loves: a builder loves blueprints and spyglasses, a miner
amethyst and gold, a farmer golden carrots and honeycomb, a scholar enchanted and written books, a guard shields and
crossbows, a bard music discs. Traits count too: a Glutton loves any food, a Frugal villager emeralds (and dislikes
cake), a Clever one books and clocks.

A **Bottle o' Enchanting** is liked, and a villager with a trade learns from it (a child or a nitwit just keeps it).

![The hall: when her name day is](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/gifts/still-4.jpg)

## Name days

Every villager has a **name day** every 28 days. That day a gift counts three times (a hated one costs three times as
much), and they add "And on my name day, too!". The hall's list says when: "Name day: today", "tomorrow" or "in 6
days".

## How it fits with the rest

- **Who takes gifts:** named villagers in a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md). Anyone else shakes their
  head and you keep the Gift.
- **What it feeds:** [friendship](friendship.md), and through it [heart events](heart-events.md),
  [personal requests](personal-requests.md) and cheaper trades. A gift they don't dislike also earns a little
  [standing](reputation.md) in the village.
- **What stops it:** with **Friendship** off in the settings, the Gift is handed back unopened.
