A big timber inn: a tavern on the ground floor with a **Shop Counter**, barrels, a cauldron and a brewing stand, and three guest rooms upstairs with six beds.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the Inn, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Inn II** adds a stable on the side, with a **smoker**, a water trough and a second chest.
- **Inn III** digs an **ale and wine cellar** under the inn, down a ladder under a hatch in the taproom's corner: racks of wine casks along both walls, oak ale casks and sacks of malt, bottles in chests and pots, and a tasting table. The builder digs the room out of the ground himself and leaves the building above as it was; the workers' chests stay where they were.

## What it does for the village

Stand a villager by the Shop Counter and sneak-right-click them with a **bed** and they become the **Innkeeper** (left alone, a jobless villager takes the counter as a shopkeeper). Each morning, while there's a free bed nearby, a **traveler** comes to stay. Travelers already know a trade: right-click one to hire them and they join your village at their level. If nobody hires them, they move on after two days. See [Porters, post and services](jobs-services.md).

In Inn II's stable, sneak-right-click a villager by the smoker with a **saddle** or a **golden carrot** and they become a **Rancher**, who breaks in and breeds the horses nearby (see [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md)).

**A home.** The guest beds are beds like any other. A villager with a bed of their own is happier, and a bed in a building of tier II or higher counts as **a fine home** (tier III and up: **a grand home**) on the hall's list, lifting their mood more. Free beds also let the village grow: with a free bed, plenty of food and a fairly happy village, a couple has a baby. See [Village life](village-life.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a white banner (homes).
