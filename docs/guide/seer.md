"They read tomorrow in the stars as others read a ledger, and they are never wrong."

## How they come

Build a [Chapel](chapel-build.md). At midnight under a full moon, one time in two, the **Seer** may walk into it as a
guest. A child of two schooled Master Clerics may also grow up as the Seer. By day they keep to the Chapel; at night
pale motes drift round them.

## What they do

- **Foretelling.** Every dawn the village's players hear, in chat and on the hall's "What next?": whether raiders come
  that night and from which side, the next festival and market day, and tomorrow's Legend guest. The Seer is never
  wrong: the night goes as foretold. "Post guards on the east side and see everyone home before dark."
- **Blessed weddings.** A wedding at the Chapel with the Seer nearby is blessed: the couple is 10 happier for 7 days,
  and their first baby comes within 2 days when a bed is free.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: a finished Chapel, and a happy village.
- **What they affect**: your [guards](guard.md) and [raids](raids.md) (you know which side to defend),
  [festivals and markets](festivals-and-markets.md), [weddings](couples-and-weddings.md) and
  [babies](babies-and-growth.md).
- **What stops them**: a strike; then no foretelling and no blessings.
