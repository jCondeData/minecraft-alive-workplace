A thriving City can send some of its people out to start again somewhere new. The colony is a village of your own,
founded by settlers you've known for days, with a road home.

## 1. The charter

On the City's [Village Hall](village-hall.md), the minecart's **Colonies** tab sells a
[Colony Charter](colony-charter.md) for 32 emeralds; the village's treasury pays what it can and you pay the rest. Choose
the spot on the charter's map, or by standing there, and give the colony a name in an anvil.

## 2. The settlers

Right-click the hall with the charter. Two settlers volunteer: the colony's builder (the lowest-leveled builder if the
village has two or more, otherwise a jobless grown-up who becomes one), and a jobless villager or the lowest-leveled of a
job the village has three or more of. Never a guard, the only worker of a job or a child. A married volunteer brings
their partner.

They pack **64 logs, 64 planks, 64 cobblestone, 32 bread, 16 torches, 12 glass panes and 3 beds** from the chests by the
[Storehouse](storehouse.md). What's missing goes on the village's wants and on the Storehouse's requests board in a
settler's name, so you or a partner village's caravans can bring it. After 3 days they stop waiting and leave with what
there is. Until they leave, **Call the colony off** on the Colonies tab gives you everything back.

The morning they leave, the bell rings, they gather at the hall and walk out toward the spot. The journey takes 3
minutes, and a little more the farther it is.

## 3. The colony is born

The settlers make camp on the nearest open ground within 48 blocks of the spot: the [Colony Camp](colony-camp-build.md),
with the colony's own Village Hall under its awning and a [Travel Post](travel-post.md) at the front. The new hall
carries the charter's name and belongs to you. The builder starts at once on a Starter Cottage and a Storehouse, in the
style most of the mother village's buildings use, and you get a letter saying they've arrived.

If there's no open ground near the spot (all water, a cliff), the settlers come home, with the supplies and the
charter's 32 emeralds, and nothing is counted.

## Sister villages

Mother and colony are **sister villages**: a caravan route both ways that is always on, carries what the other waits for
free, and doesn't count toward either village's route limit. See [Trade and caravans](trade-and-caravans.md).

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** a City with emeralds in its treasury, spare workers and a full Storehouse.
- **Realms:** a colony founded while its mother is in a [realm](realms.md) joins it on arrival; the realm's **Charters**
  research makes charters cheaper and supplies bigger, and the [Royal Charter](royal-charter.md) edict lets Towns found
  colonies and wait half as long.
- **The hall knows:** a village that could found a colony now gets a tip, "Found a colony", in **What next?**.
- **What stops it:** **Colonies** off in the settings screen (a colony already on the road still arrives).
  **Colonies per Village** (3) and **Days Between Colonies** (7) set the limits.
