The Storehouse makes a village's chests into one store. Its porter carries what every worker makes to it, and every
worker nearby can take from it.

## Getting one

Craft it from **three planks** on top and a **barrel**, a **chest** and a **barrel** in the row below. It's in the
mod's creative tab too. Put chests within 8 blocks of it: those are the store.

## The Porter

A villager without a job nearby takes it by themselves and becomes its **Porter**. The porter walks round the
village's workers and carries what they make to the store: the miner's ore, the lumberjack's logs, the farmer's
harvest, the fisherman's catch. Tools, seeds and whatever each worker still needs stay where they are. Whoever places
the storehouse owns it: its porter works for them and their friends. A village's own storehouse works for the village.

## The requests board

Right-click the Storehouse to see what the workers nearby are waiting for (a builder's missing blocks, a miner's
pickaxe, a farmer's seeds) and how much is already in store. Click one to hand over what you have, straight into that
worker's chest.

**Stock orders.** Click the book on the board, hold an item and click it to order it (16, 32, 64...): the carpenters,
masons, tinkerers and chefs keep that many in store, making more when it runs low.

## How it fits in

- **Fed by** every worker's chests, and by [Drop Boxes](drop-box.md) within 48 blocks: the porter empties those first.
- **Feeds** the builders and crafters: a builder short of stone finds it here.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself (the chests round it are ordinary chests and keep what's in
  them).
