When a [road](roads.md) on the City Plan runs into a river, a pond or a ravine, the builders don't stop: they bridge
it.

## What gets built

Where a road meets water, or a drop deeper than 2 blocks, for up to 16 blocks, the builders put up a bridge in the
road's style: a deck level with the banks, rails along both sides, a pillar every 4 blocks going down to the bed (at
most 12 blocks deep) and a ramp of stairs at each end. Villagers walk over it like any road.

## Too wide to bridge

A gap wider than 16 blocks isn't bridged: the road stops at the bank and the [Steward's desk](stewards-desk.md) says
why ("A road stops at a gap 20 blocks wide: a bridge spans 16 at most"). Draw the road round the water, or across a
narrower spot.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Part of a road**: bridges are built a stretch at a time with the rest of the road, by the village's builders, and
  only while the **Stewards Build Roads** setting is on.
- **Roads between villages** bridge rivers the same way ([Roads between villages](roads-between-villages.md)).
- Like roads, bridges don't count as buildings for the village's rank.
