The top of the village: a grand home, every comfort, and a ball at every other festival.

## What a Noble needs

- **a tier III home**: a home with both its upgrades done, or a Manor;
- **fed from the store 3 days running**, on **a varied diet**;
- **a chapel, a school, a clinic and a library**, all near home;
- the village a **Town** or bigger;
- **decorations** near home;
- **Noble Robes** every 8 days, **Vintage Wine** every 2 days and an **Emerald Brooch** every 16 days, from the store.

Three dawns running without one of them and the household comes down to [Burgher](burgher.md). Nobody becomes a Noble
when classes first switch on: Nobles are earned.

**Wants**: an **Illuminated Book** every 16 days and a **Gold Circlet** every 32. Each want had makes the household pay
10% more tax.

## What Nobles give

- **Four times the tax** into the [treasury](village-hall.md).
- **A happier village**: 3% more wellbeing for each Noble household, up to 9%.
- **The Noble's Ball**: every other festival is a ball. "Guests gather at the Manor or the hall after work: wine, the
  best of the store, and gold fireworks at dusk."

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: your [builder](builder.md) (full upgrades or a Manor, and every service building), decorations, a
  Tailor, a Vintner (Vintage Wine ages three days) and a Jeweler.
- **What it affects**: the whole village's mood, the treasury and its [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md).
- **What breaks it**: the village dropping below Town rank, or the store running short of wine.
