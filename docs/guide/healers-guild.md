The guild of those who mend the sick and tend the dead.

## Perks

- Nurses, Clerics and Undertakers work **15% faster**.
- The village's ill **get well in two days** instead of three ([sickness](sickness.md)).
- Nurses and Undertakers **look 48 blocks out** instead of 32.

## Starting it

Sneak-right-click a **Master** Nurse, Cleric or Undertaker with a [Guild Charter](guild-charter.md), then have a builder
finish a [Guildhall](guildhall-build.md).

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it**: Master healers, and their medicines in the chests.
- **What it affects**: how long sickness slows the village, and how many [graves](grave.md) are tended.
- **What stops it**: a Guildhall that isn't finished any more pauses the perks until it's repaired; if the Guild Master dies, the most experienced member takes over.
