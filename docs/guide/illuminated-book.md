"The Chronicle of Thornholm, illuminated and bound in gold": the whole story of your village, from the day the hall
went up, in one gilded book.

## Getting it

A Journeyman [Printer](printer.md) makes it from a **book**, **2 gold nuggets**, a **lapis lazuli** and a **glow ink
sac**, and sells it for 8 emeralds. Like the [Gazette](village-gazette.md), a copy is written from the nearest
[Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s chronicle: one you buy is filled in where you stand the first time you read it.

## What it's for

- **A want of the Nobles.** A [Noble](noble.md) household takes one from the village store every 16 days. It isn't a
  need, but every want a household has makes it pay 10% more tax.
- **A keepsake for you.** Read your village's history from the first day.

## How it fits with the rest

Glow ink sacs come from glow squid, so a Printer near deep, dark water is a happy one. See [Luxuries](luxuries.md).
