The Toolsmith is the village's blacksmith: when a worker's tool breaks and there's no spare, they make a new one.

## Getting a toolsmith

A villager without a job takes a free **smithing table** as a Toolsmith, as in vanilla. If a villager there has another
job, sneak-right-click them with an **iron ingot**.

**Hiring.** Sneak-right-click a toolsmith with an iron ingot to hire them: they then make tools for your own workers,
from the iron and stone in your storehouse.

## What they do

When a worker is waiting for a tool (a miner's pickaxe, a lumberjack's axe, a fisherman's rod), the toolsmith makes one
with the game's recipe and takes it to that worker's chests. **Iron** if the storehouse or the smelters have it, else
**stone**. **Diamond** only from the toolsmith's own chest: your diamonds go into tools only when you put them there.
In between they go about a vanilla toolsmith's day.

Over their head: how many tools they've made, and whom they're making one for; "nobody's waiting for a tool" when all's
well.

## How the toolsmith fits in

- **Fed by:** an [Armorer](armorer.md)'s ingots and the storehouse's stone.
- **Feeds:** the [Miner](miner.md), the [Lumberjack](lumberjack.md), the [Fisherman](fisherman.md) and every other
  worker who wears out a tool. You see what they're waiting for on the Storehouse's requests board (see
  [Porter](porter.md)).
- **Works beside:** the [Weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md), who mends worn tools instead of making new ones.

See also [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).
