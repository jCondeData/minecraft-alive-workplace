The Quarry is a pit the [Miner](miner.md) works like a farmer works a field: they dig it out, and what they dig slowly
grows back, so the village has stone and ore for good.

## What it looks like

A curb of cobblestone and stone brick with a spruce fence round the pit, open at the front under a spruce hoist with a
lantern on a chain, and a gate on the east side. Inside the gate a flight of stone steps runs down the side of the pit
to a lantern-lit landing four blocks down. East of the pit stands the Miner's shed: spruce walls between log posts on a
cobblestone footing, a dark oak roof, and inside the blast furnace, a double chest and a crafting table. The pit itself
is the ground you build it on.

## Building it

Take the Quarry from the [Blueprint Table](blueprint-table.md) (its Jobs line says it brings a Miner), place it and
stock the chests by the builder's table with what its tooltip lists. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md). The
builder digs the steps out and leaves the pit's ground as it is.

## Putting it to work

Stand a villager by the shed's blast furnace and sneak-right-click them with **a pickaxe**: they're the Quarry's Miner.
No [Quarry Marker](quarry-marker.md) needed: within a few seconds they take the pit and start digging it out layer by
layer, cutting steps down its walls as they go, and bring what they find to the shed's chests.

## The pit grows back

What the Miner digs out grows back slowly, from the bottom of the pit up: mostly stone, some coal and copper, now and
then iron, redstone and gold, and rarely a diamond, with deepslate in the bottom layers. When the Miner has dug to the
bottom they wait at the shed until a layer has grown back, then dig again. A pit dug out all the way takes about a week
of Minecraft days to fill up again. Only the pit grows back: nothing outside it changes, and a block you put in the pit
stays where you put it.

Hand the Quarry's Miner a Quarry Marker and they dig the area you marked instead; when it's done they go back to the
Quarry.

## Settings

- **Quarry Build** (on): off, the Quarry isn't offered and nothing grows back. The Quarry Marker works as before.
- **Quarry Regrowth** (100): how many blocks grow back in a Minecraft day; 0 for none.
