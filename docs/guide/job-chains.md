Needs: raw materials (logs are enough for anything wooden)

The workers who make things for the builders don't stop at one step. Give the village logs, and the carpenter saws
them into planks, cuts the planks into slabs, then makes the barrel or chair the builder was waiting for. Each job
makes its own kind of thing, from the raw materials up.

## Who makes what

- **Carpenter:** woodwork. Planks, slabs, stairs, doors, fences, barrels, and the furniture mods' pieces that are made
  on a crafting table (Handcrafted's chairs need only oak logs).
- **Mason:** stone. Cut at the stonecutter, and fired in a furnace beside it: cobblestone into stone, stone into stone
  brick stairs.
- **Librarian, Tinkerer, Toolsmith and Dyer:** their own goods, as before, but now from further down: a dyer crushes
  poppies into dye and then dyes the wool.
- **Furnicrafter:** CobbleFurnies' furniture at its Furni Crafter, with the planks and sticks sawn from logs first.

Several jobs can fill one builder's list at the same time: the carpenter makes the woodwork while the dyer makes the
red wool.

![Two jobs filling one builder's list](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/job_chains/still-3.jpg)

## What you see

Over their head: how many things they've made, and what they're fetching, making ("making 24 Oak Planks (step 1 of
3)") or carrying. What's made goes straight into the builder's chests. A big order is made a bagful at a time until
there's enough. After the builders, they fill the Storehouse's orders, then anything another worker is waiting for,
like a miner's ladders.

They check everything before they take anything. If something can't be made, nothing is cut up for nothing: the line
over their head says what's short, like "short of 1 Oak Log to make 3 Barrel", and so does the builder's own line.
Put the logs in the Storehouse and they're on it. Something no job makes stays in the builder's list of what it needs.

If the server stops halfway through, they carry on from the step they were at.

## The Crafter is retired

There used to be a Crafter, a villager at a Crafter's Workbench who made everything. The jobs above do that work now.
The workbench can't be crafted any more and no villager takes it. A Crafter on your server becomes jobless (whatever
they were carrying drops at their feet), and any Crafter's Workshop already built stays as a plain building.

## Switching it off

The **Crafting chains** setting (on by default once Furnished Homes is out) turns the chains off: each job then makes
what it can in one go, as it did before.

See also [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).
