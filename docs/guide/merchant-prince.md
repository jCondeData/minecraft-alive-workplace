"Feed them, and they will make a rich village richer: a bank in the hall, fairs in the square and fair prices on every
road."

## How they come

Once your [treasury](treasury.md) has taken in 500 emeralds and your village runs three
[caravan routes](trade-and-caravans.md), look along the coast: by a shipwreck within 1500 blocks a castaway may have made
camp on the beach. Hand them a cooked meal, and they come to your hall the next morning as a guest.

## What they do

- **The bank.** The treasury earns 2% a day on what it holds, and holds twice as much. The hall's **Bank** page opens
  for players too: keep up to 10 stacks of emeralds there, earning 5% a week, and take them out any time (even after the
  Prince is gone).
- **Trade fairs.** Every 10 days a fair fills the [Market Square](market-square-build.md) (or the ground round the
  hall): six traveling traders, a stall for every village you trade with, bunting and fireworks, and every trade there
  and with your villagers 10% cheaper for the day.
- **Caravan pay.** Every stack your caravans bring to a village that was waiting for it earns the treasury an emerald.
- **Wider prices.** The village's [price board](price-board.md) compares every village within caravan range, not
  only the ones you trade with; its caravans take goods for sale only to the partner that pays most for them; and its
  board sells to you for only 5% more than it pays, instead of 10%.
- By day the Prince keeps to the hall and the Market Square.

![A trade fair: six traders, bunting and fireworks round the hall](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/legend_merchant_prince/still-3.jpg)

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds them**: your [shops](shopkeeper.md), [market days](market-days.md) and caravans, which fill the treasury.
- **What they affect**: the treasury, your own emeralds, and every trade on fair day.
- **What stops them**: a strike; the bank stops paying interest, but what you put in can still be taken out.
