Do things for a village and it remembers. Each player has a **standing** in each village, and a line above your hotbar
says when it moves: "+10 standing in Thornholm (now 60)".

## Titles

At **50** standing you're a **Friend** of the village, at **300** a **Hero**, and at **1000**, once the village is a
Town or bigger, a **Lord**. The first time you reach a title, the whole server hears it in gold, with a fanfare:

> Jesse is now a Friend of Thornholm!

and the village's chronicle keeps a line. Your title goes before what you say in chat ("[Hero of Thornholm] anyone seen
my horse?"): the one you hold where you stand, else your best anywhere.

![A chat line with the title before it](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/titles/still-2.jpg)

## Earning and losing it

| Earned by | Standing |
|---|---|
| finishing one of the hall's daily quests | +10 |
| a villager's own request done (every helper) | +25 |
| finishing a bounty | +40 |
| breaking up a bandit camp (whoever brought its chief down) | +40 |
| fighting in a raid (your third raider) | +20, once a raid |
| a story chapter you helped with | +50 |
| a story's ending | +150 in ours |
| coming to a festival | +5 a festival day |
| a gift the villager doesn't dislike | +2, at most +10 a day |

| Lost by | Standing |
|---|---|
| hitting a villager | -10 |
| killing an iron golem in the village | -100 |
| killing a villager | -150 |
| killing a guard | -200 |

Drop under a title's number and it's gone; only you are told. Win it back and only you hear it again.

## What a title is worth

- **Cheaper trades.** In its village a title takes 5% (Friend), 10% (Hero) or 15% (Lord) off on top of your
  [friendship](friendship.md) discount, up to 40% in all.
- **Hero of the Village II** at festivals, for Heroes and Lords.
- **Honors** like Kingslayer and Wayfinder, from the stories you see through, listed with your standing for good.

## How it fits with the rest

- **What feeds it:** [quests](quests.md), [requests](personal-requests.md), [bounties](bounty-board.md),
  [raids](raids.md), [bandit camps](bandit-camps.md), [festivals](festivals-and-markets.md) and
  [stories](story-arcs.md).
- **What it affects:** prices, festival blessings, and who the village thinks most of: hover the name tag at the top of
  the [Village Hall](village-hall.md)'s screen for your standing, your honors and the three best regarded players.
- **What stops it:** with **Reputation and titles** off in the settings nothing is earned or lost (saved standings are
  kept); with **Titles in chat** off, chat is left alone but titles still count.
