A straw-stuffed sack on a post for the village's guards to practice on.

## Getting one

Craft it from **wool** on top, a **hay bale** with a **stick** on each side, and a **stick** underneath. It's in the
mod's creative tab too.

## What it does

Put it within 12 blocks of a guard's post (their grindstone). On quiet days, by day, a guard who isn't an Expert yet
spars with it between fights and gains experience, up to Expert. At night they stay by their post instead.
You can give it a whack too: it thuds and puffs straw, the same as when a guard hits it.

![Sparring with the Training Dummy](https://jcondedata.github.io/minecraft-alive-workplace/guard/still-4.jpg)

## How it fits in

- **Affects** your guards: better guards hit harder and last longer in a raid.
- **It's straw.** It burns, so keep it away from lava and fire.
- **Breaking it.** Mine it with an axe; it drops itself.

More in [Threats and defense](defence.md).
