The Glassblower makes the village's glass for your builders: he fires it from sand at his own kiln and makes the panes,
stained glass, tinted glass and bottles they wait for.

## Getting a Glassblower

Craft a **Glass Kiln** (eight bricks round a furnace with an iron ingot on top) and put it in your village. A villager
without a job takes it by themselves. A plain furnace is never a Glassblower's: villagers leave your furnaces alone.

The Blueprint Table has a **Glassblower's Workshop**: a brick glasshouse with colored panes in its front windows, the
Glass Kiln inside between two chests, and a kiln house with a smoking chimney. A village's Steward plans one once it has
eight villagers and no Glassblower.

## What he makes

When a builder nearby is waiting for glass, he fetches the makings from the builder's chests (and the village's), makes
it at his kiln and carries it to the builder's chest:

- **Glass**, fired from **sand**. Each **coal or charcoal** fires eight.
- **Glass panes**, made from the glass he fires: give a builder sand and coal, and he makes the rest.
- **Stained glass** and **stained glass panes**, with the [Florist](florist.md)'s **dyes**.
- **Tinted glass**, from glass and amethyst shards.
- **Glass bottles**, for the Storehouse when it orders them.

Over his head: how many things he's made, and what he's working on.

## When something is missing

The builder's line says what's short, word for word: with sand but no coal, "Can't be made yet: short of 1 Coal to make
1 Glass Pane". A missing dye is named as the dye, the way you'd hand it over.

With no Glassblower in the village the line says "Hire a Glassblower to make Glass Panes". A [Mason](mason.md) no
longer fires glass, but he does crush cobblestone and gravel into the sand a Glassblower needs.
