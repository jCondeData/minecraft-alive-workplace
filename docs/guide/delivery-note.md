# Delivery Note

```infobox
type: Item
Recipe: paper, a feather and an ink sac, anywhere in the grid
Used by: Postman
Carries: everything but tools, weapons and armor, or only the item you pick
Stack size: 1
Found in creative tab: yes
categories: Items, Jobs
related: mailbox.md, jobs-services.md, storehouse.md
```

A Delivery Note sets up a courier route: a postman with no mail to carry hauls things between two of your chests.

## Getting one

Craft **paper**, a **feather** and an **ink sac** together, anywhere in the grid.

## Using it

1. Right-click the container to take from, then the one to bring to.
2. Want just one thing moved? Hold that item in your other hand and right-click the air: the note carries only that.
   Otherwise it carries everything but tools, weapons and armor. A note carries up to 9 kinds; do the same with a
   kind it carries to take it off. Sneak and right-click a block to clear the two containers.
3. Hand the note to a Postman (a villager at a [Mailbox](mailbox.md) you gave paper).

## How it fits in

- **Mail first.** The postman runs the route when there's no mail to deliver.
- **Protected villages.** In someone else's [protected village](village-protection.md) the note can't mark their
  chests, and a postman won't carry a route you set out of it, unless the owner made you a friend.
- **Handy for** moving a farm's harvest to a kitchen, or a quarry's stone to a builder's chests.
