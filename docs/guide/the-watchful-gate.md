With [Open Gates](open-gates.md), strangers come and go at all hours, and bandits notice. A Guard asks you to help keep
watch: "Open gates bring trade, and trouble. Help me arm the watch, and I know an old soldier who'd come to keep it."

## The story

1. **The Ask.** Hear the guard out about the gates.
2. **The First Step.** Iron for the gate's bars and the watch's gear: bring **192 iron ingots**.
3. **The Second Step.** A day later: **256 arrows** for the watch on the walls.
4. **The Third Step.** A day later: clear the roads of the monsters that slip in at night: **slay 40 monsters**.

Each step pays emeralds, and the guards grow bolder ("Arrows on the walls. Let them come.").

## How it ends

Open Gates is **reformed** for good: travelers and traders keep coming, and bandits are no likelier to make camp than
usual. An **old soldier** comes to the gate: a new villager who becomes a Guard at Expert, in iron armor with an iron
sword. Everyone who helped gains 60 standing.

## How it fits with the rest

- It only starts while [Open Gates](open-gates.md) is in force and the village has a guard.
- The iron comes from your [miners](jobs-land.md), the arrows from the [fletcher](jobs-crafts.md).
- The old soldier joins your [guards](defence.md#guards) at once, a strong one.
