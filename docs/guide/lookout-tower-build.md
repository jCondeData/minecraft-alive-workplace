A square stone-brick tower with a ladder up the middle and a lookout platform at the top: the first thing that makes a village look like it can look after itself.

## Getting the blueprint

It's in the **Blueprint Table**: pick it there and press **Get Blueprint**, which takes a Blank Blueprint (paper and blue dye make two). A Journeyman **builder** sells it for 12 emeralds. On market day (a village with a Village Hall and a Market Square) a traveling trader may sell it too, for 6 emeralds, sometimes already in another style.

## Building it

Hold the blueprint and right-click the ground where its front should go (the gold edge is the front), stock the chests within 8 blocks of your builder's Blueprint Table with what the blueprint lists when you hold Shift over it, and right-click the builder with it. Want it in another look? Sneak-right-click the air before you place it to pick **Stonework**, **Sandstone**, **Dark Oak**, **Cherry** or, with Cobblemon, **Apricorn**. See [The Builder and blueprints](builder.md) for the rest.

## Upgrades

Once a builder finishes the tower, they sell its upgrade's blueprint as well (the Blueprint Table has it too). Right-click the finished building with the upgrade and it lines up exactly over it; the builder only builds what's new. The Village Hall's **What next?** page reminds you of upgrades waiting.

- **Lookout Tower II** raises the tower to 23 blocks under a pointed dark oak roof, with a **bell** at the top and a **grindstone** and chest at the foot of the ladder: a guard's post.
- **Lookout Tower III** adds a guardhouse beside the tower: a second grindstone, an anvil and two bunks.

## What it does for the village

Stand a villager by a grindstone and sneak-right-click them with a **sword** and they become a **Guard**, fighting any monster within 24 blocks of their post; put weapons and armor in the chest and they wear the best of it. **Ring the bell** and the guards rush to it while everyone else runs home. See [Threats and defense](defence.md).

Near a [Village Hall](village-hall.md) every finished building counts toward the village's rank (a Village needs 5 of them, a Town 12, a City 25, besides villagers and research), and a Village Map drawn at the hall marks it with a black banner (towers, barracks and gates).
