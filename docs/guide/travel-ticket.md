A ticket to one Travel Post, sold by the ferrymen.

## Getting one

Right-click a Ferryman (the villager at a [Travel Post](travel-post.md)), pick any other post you know and pay the
fare: an emerald for every 256 blocks (16 at most; 8 to another dimension). The ticket is named for where it goes ("Ticket to Riverside"), and its tooltip
says where that post stands.

## Using it

Use it within 16 blocks of **any** Travel Post and you arrive at the ticket's post. Keep a few for the trip home.

## How it fits in

- **Only posts you know.** Ferrymen sell tickets to the posts you've right-clicked, so add every post you pass.
