With the **CobbleDollars** mod installed (it comes with the Cobbleverse pack), the villagers deal in CobbleDollars as
well as emeralds. The **CobbleDollar Rate** says what an emerald price comes to in CobbleDollars. Without CobbleDollars
the setting does nothing.

## What it changes

Normally **100**: a price of 3 emeralds is 300 CobbleDollars. It's used wherever the mod turns emeralds into
CobbleDollars: lessons from the move tutor, shop prices and ferry fares, and what the village pays you, such as quest
rewards and the [treasury](treasury.md) when you collect it.

## How it interacts

- Raise it if CobbleDollars are easy to come by on your server, lower it if they're scarce.
- Prices in emeralds don't change; only what they're worth in CobbleDollars does. See
  [Pokémon and villagers](pokemon.md) and [Trade and caravans](trade-and-caravans.md).
