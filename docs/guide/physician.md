The Physician is the village's doctor for the bad days: when a plague goes round a town, they're the one knocking on
every door.

## Getting a Physician

Stand a villager by a **brewing stand** and sneak-right-click them with a **glistering melon slice**. The brewing stand
is shared: a glass bottle there still makes a [Cleric](cleric.md), and the [Nurse](nurse.md) and
[Undertaker](undertaker.md) work at one too. The [Physician's House](physicians-house-build.md) is built around a
Physician's brewing stand.

## At work

The Physician works the long day shift.

- **They see it coming.** With a Physician in the village, a [plague](plague.md) is foretold at its very first case, a
  day before it spreads: "Mara has seen the first case: a plague will spread in Oakvale."
- **House to house.** In a plague they go to the ill within 32 blocks of their stand, a [Remedy](remedy.md) in hand
  from the chest beside the stand, and cure them at the bedside.
- **No Remedy?** They still visit: they see the patient and send them home to rest. Someone the Physician has seen only
  passes the plague on to the people they live with, not to the whole street.
- **They brew.** Leave a glass bottle, a glistering melon slice, a honey bottle and a fermented spider eye in the chest
  by the stand and they make a Remedy themselves. When they run short they ask for Remedies on the requests board.

## How it fits in

- **What feeds it:** Remedies, or their makings, in the chest by the stand. A [Cleric](cleric.md) or a
  [Beekeeper](beekeeper.md) keeps the honey and potions side of things going.
- **What it answers:** the plague, which strikes a Town or a City, and the hexes of a
  [witch coven](witch-coven.md): a Remedy lifts those too.
- **The Sickness:** in a village with no Nurse, the Physician is the healer of [the Sickness](the-sickness.md): talk to
  them about the Gray Cough, bring them the herbs, and they brew the tonic at their stand and take it round. A Remedy
  only eases the cough for a day.
- **Works with:** the [Nurse](nurse.md), who looks after ordinary illness. Her honey, milk and potions can't touch the
  plague; that's the Physician's work.
- **Helped by:** Fairy- and Psychic-type Pokémon in a Pasture Block near the brewing stand (with Cobblemon): the
  Physician gets round the village faster.
- **The hall knows:** a Town with no Physician gets a tip in **What next?** to build the Physician's House.
- **What stops it:** **Physicians** off in the settings screen. Physicians already hired then stand idle, and the
  brewing stand no longer offers the job.

## Trading

Physicians buy glass bottles and sell a Remedy. Higher up they trade in honey bottles, a glistering melon slice and
spider eyes, and at the top a potion of healing and a golden apple.
