Two villages that trade with each other end up joined by a proper street. Each one's [Steward](steward.md) has his
builders lay their own half, from the end of the village's nearest road towards the other hall.

## How it goes

For every caravan route the village has ([Trade and caravans](trade-and-caravans.md)), it plans a street towards the
point halfway to the other village, in the style of the zone it starts from, up to the **Road to Another Village**
setting (256 blocks) or halfway, whichever is less. When the two halves would end within 32 blocks of each other, both
carry on to meet. Rivers on the way get [bridges](bridges.md).

The way is only planned through land that's loaded: no land is loaded for it, so far from players the work simply
waits until someone is near.

## Milestones

A half that stops short of the other ends at a **milestone**: a stone post with a lantern and a sign reading "To" the
other village and how many blocks it is. Far-apart villages get two halves and two milestones.

When the road is done, both villages' chronicles say so ("The road to Ashford is finished"), or note the milestone.

## How it fits with the rest

- **Faster trade**: caravans on a finished road arrive in **three quarters of the time**, so goods and money move
  sooner. The hall's trade routes list the caravans on the road.
- **Needs**: a trade route, a Steward and free builders in each village (a village without a Steward builds nothing);
  the **Roads Between Villages** setting on.
- **Comes last**: road work waits while any building of the village waits for a builder.
