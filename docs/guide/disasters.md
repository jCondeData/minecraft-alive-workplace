Raiders aren't the only trouble a village can meet. Fire, drought and sickness come too, and each has a villager whose
job is to answer it.

## The three

| Disaster | Comes to | What it does | Answered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Village fire](fire.md) | a village built mostly of wood | the builders' roofs and walls burn | the [Firewarden](firewarden.md) |
| [Drought](drought.md) | any village, after five days without rain (sooner in the desert, savanna and badlands) | crops grow at a quarter of their pace, farmland and cauldrons dry out | the [Well Keeper](well-keeper.md) |
| [Plague](plague.md) | a Town or a City | the ill pass it on and take to their beds | the [Physician](physician.md) |

## How a disaster goes

- **It's news.** Everyone in the village, and the hall's owner wherever they are, reads it when it comes and when it's
  over, and the chronicle keeps both.
- **One at a time.** A village has at most one disaster under way, and after one it gets 7 days' rest.
- **Foretold.** With the right villager at home, you hear a day ahead: the Well Keeper reads the sky before a drought,
  and the Physician sees the plague's first case before it spreads. A fire gives no warning.
- **The hall shows it.** The wellbeing icon on the [Village Hall](village-hall.md) has it in red on its first line
  ("Drought (days left: 2)"), and **What next?** names the job that answers it when the village has none.
- **Houses for the answering jobs.** The [Fire Station](fire-station-build.md), the
  [Well House](well-house-build.md) and the [Physician's House](physicians-house-build.md) each hold their job's
  block. **What next?** suggests the one a village needs before the disaster comes.

## How it fits in

- **What makes them likelier:** wood for fire, dry weather and a [desert raiders](desert-raiders.md)' lair for drought,
  crowding (more villagers than beds), a [witch coven](witch-coven.md)'s hex or a traveler from a faraway land at the
  inn for plague.
- **What softens them:** a finished [Well](well-build.md) or [Fountain](fountain-build.md) keeps villagers from fretting
  about the wells in a drought; **Medicine** research means fewer catch the plague.
- **What stops them:** the hall's **At peace** on the [Defense page](defense-page.md), a
  [Threat Level](threat-level.md) of 0 (at 1 there's no plague), or Peaceful difficulty. Each disaster also has its own
  switch in the config file; see [Siege and disaster settings](settings-sieges-and-disasters.md).
