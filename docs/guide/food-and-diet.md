In a village with a [Village Hall](village-hall.md), villagers eat. Every grown villager has one meal a day from the
village's **store**, and how well they eat feeds into almost everything else.

## The store

The store is the chests by the village's **kitchens** (smokers and Kitchen Stoves, where the Chef's cooking goes) and,
after those, the chests by its **Storehouses**. A villager who hasn't eaten for a day takes a meal from there. Anything
plain to eat will do: bread, baked potatoes, cooked fish, pies, apples, carrots. Golden food, food that makes you ill,
and berries kept back for Pokémon don't count as a meal. Food a builder still needs for a build nearby is left alone.

The hall's top row shows how much food is in store, and its food icon how many kinds of meal there are.

## A varied diet

Villagers remember their last five meals, and when they eat they pick something they haven't had lately if the store
has it. **Three or more kinds** among those meals is a varied diet: their [mood](moods.md) goes up by 10. The same thing
every time is a dull one: down by 10. So a store with bread, baked potatoes, cooked fish and pies keeps the village
happier than one full of bread.

## How it interacts

- **Moods:** fed is +15, hungry is -20, and the diet another 10 either way ([Moods](moods.md)).
- **Village needs:** the share of villagers who are fed is the biggest part of the village's
  [wellbeing](village-needs.md), and so of everyone's [work pace](work-pace.md).
- **Babies:** a baby is only on the way when the store holds at least 16 meals, and the family eats 8 of them
  ([Babies and growth](babies-and-growth.md)).
- **Sickness:** hungry villagers fall [ill](sickness.md) more often.
- **Festivals:** the festival feast is served from the store ([Festivals and market days](festivals-and-markets.md)).
- **Traits:** [Gluttons](traits.md) eat twice a day, the Frugal every other day.
- **Who fills it:** farmers, fishermen, ranchers, chefs and porters. See [Jobs on the land](jobs-land.md) and
  [Crafters and smiths](jobs-crafts.md).
